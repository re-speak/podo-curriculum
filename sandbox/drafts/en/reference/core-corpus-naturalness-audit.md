# Core 71–122 corpus-informed naturalness audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-14
**Source snapshot:** the current `english/tracks/1-core-patterns/table-of-contents.md`
**Scope:** 52 lessons · 104 primary model lines · supporting expressions and pair load
**TOC changes made by this audit:** none

## Verdict

All 52 rows still have a coherent single outcome and two mutually supporting production targets.
The 104 primary model lines are plausible spoken English in the situations claimed. No row earns an
evidence-led rewrite, demotion or removal before native catalog review.

Eleven rows carry an **author watch**. These are not hidden third objectives and do not invalidate
the production pair: they concern a substitution-frame mechanic, a regional choice, a marked
supporting expression or pragmatic delivery that the eventual deck must teach in context. The live
TOC was deliberately left unchanged.

This is **corpus-informed, not corpus-counted**. Cambridge Grammar Today is based on the Cambridge
English Corpus and supplies real-use grammar and register guidance; the EGP supplies learner-corpus
level evidence where an exact use is available. Neither source licenses a claim that every exact
sentence below has a particular spoken frequency. Native review and recorded learner performance
remain separate gates.

## Evidence key and query record

- **P — past, future and modality:** [Should](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/should),
  [Be expressions](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/be-expressions-be-able-to-be-due-to),
  [Tenses and time](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/tenses-and-time), and
  [English Grammar Profile Online](https://englishprofile.org/?menu=egp-online).
- **S — services, requests and politeness:** [Have something done](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/have-something-done),
  [Get](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/get), and
  [Politeness](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/politeness_2).
- **C — comparison and quantity:** [Comparison: adjectives](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/comparison-adjectives-)
  and Cambridge Dictionary's [the more … the more/less](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/morethe-more-less).
- **D — discourse, stance and interaction:** [Discourse markers](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/discourse-markers),
  [Politeness](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/politeness_2), and the
  [Cambridge Grammar Today index](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/grammar/british-grammar/).
- **E — level evidence:** direct EGP searches for `supposed to` and the priority constructions,
  plus CEFR-labelled Cambridge Dictionary entries when the EGP had no exact result. The priority
  dispositions are recorded in [`core-evidence-ledger.md`](./core-evidence-ledger.md).

The query text in the table is the construction or pragmatic move checked, not a claim of a raw
web-hit count. `Pass · watch` means the two primary patterns pass while the note must survive into
deck authoring or native review.

## Disposition

- **Keep all 52 rows unchanged for native catalog review.** This audit found no no-brainer TOC edit.
- Carry the eleven author watches into lesson briefs or native feedback when their rows are selected;
  do not inflate them into third assessed targets.
- Keep Core 76 and 105's productive bands explicitly provisional in the evidence ledger. Natural
  attestation proves that people use the constructions, not that independent production belongs at
  the current curriculum point.
- Re-run the exact row only if native feedback changes a model, frame, expression or communicative
  job. A broad re-audit is unnecessary when the row snapshot is unchanged.
