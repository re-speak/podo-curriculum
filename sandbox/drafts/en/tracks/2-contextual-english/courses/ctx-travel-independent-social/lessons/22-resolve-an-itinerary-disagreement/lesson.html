Today's pattern (今日のパターン)

Meaning & use 意味・使い方Use this to acknowledge someone's reason before stating a different preference.相手の理由を認めてから、異なる自分の希望を伝える表現です。

I see why you want to go, but I prefer staying nearby.行きたい理由は分かりますが、私は近くにいるほうを選びます。
I see why you want the long route, but I prefer the short one.長い経路を選びたい理由は分かりますが、私は短いほうを選びます。
I see why you want the museum, but I prefer the market.美術館へ行きたい理由は分かりますが、私は市場のほうを選びます。

Acknowledge before you contrast (対比の前に相手を認める)

Put “I see why” before the other person's reason and “but I prefer” before your option.相手の理由の前に I see why、自分の希望の前に but I prefer を置きます。

I see why + their reason, but I prefer + my optionKeep both preferences visible両方の希望を示すI see why · you want to gobut I prefer · staying nearby

Say “I see why” or “I see your point” (I see why と I see your point)

Use “I see why” to acknowledge someone's motivation; use “I see your point” to acknowledge their argument.相手の動機を認めるなら I see why、相手の主張を認めるなら I see your point を使います。
