## Supporting content

- **Expressions:** `you know, the one who...` · `whatsisname`
- **Grammar:** the describing part comes **after** the noun in English.
- **Japanese-L1 risk:** **word order re-entry, and the biggest flip in the language.** Japanese pre-modifies — 「電話した人」 puts the whole clause before the noun. `The called person` and `I bought yesterday the book` are predicted. The *concept* is native (連体修飾), so teach the position, not the idea.
