## Supporting content

- **Expressions:** `not really` · `all the time`
- **Grammar:** to ask, put `do` at the front and leave the verb alone.
- **Japanese-L1 risk:** **do-support as an operation, and it is genuinely alien.** Japanese builds questions with a final particle; nothing is inserted and nothing moves. `You drink coffee?` is predicted, and it is the single most persistent A1 error. Teach the *operation*, and drill it with the answer form `Yes, I do` so the auxiliary is heard twice.
