Right now or at the moment? (right now と at the moment)

Both mean now; right now is more immediate, while at the moment sounds a little less urgent.どちらも「今」ですが、right now はより即時的で、at the moment は少し切迫感が弱い表現です。

Immediateまさに今I'm working right now.今まさに仕事中です。
