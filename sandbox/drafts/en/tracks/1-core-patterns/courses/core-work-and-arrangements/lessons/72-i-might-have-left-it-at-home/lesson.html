Maybe or might have? (maybe と might have)

Maybe can introduce the whole explanation. Might have builds the possibility into the verb.Maybe は説明全体の前に置けます。might have は可能性を動詞の中に組み込みます。

Sentence opener文全体への前置きMaybe I left it at home.もしかすると家に置いてきました。
