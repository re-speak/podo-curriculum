Spiral review (スパイラル復習)

Keep the modal followed by the plain verb have.法助動詞の直後は原形の have にしましょう。

should の直後は原形。I should have/had called earlier.
shouldn't の直後は原形。I shouldn't had/have waited.
might の直後は原形。I might have/had missed it.
must の直後は原形。It must had/have closed.
