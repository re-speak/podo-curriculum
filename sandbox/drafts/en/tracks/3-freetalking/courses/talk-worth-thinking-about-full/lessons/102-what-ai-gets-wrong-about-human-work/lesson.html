What AI gets wrong about human work (AIが人の仕事を誤解するところ)

Let's examine where AI misses the human context around work. Please read the title aloud.AIが仕事を取り巻く人間の文脈をどこで見落とすか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Capability is not the whole job (能力だけでは仕事にならない)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A system may execute a task while missing the institutional and human context that gives it meaning.システムは作業を実行できても、その意味を支える組織や人間の文脈を見落とすことがあります。
The measurable output may conceal coordination, trust, timing, and judgment.測定できる成果の裏には、調整、信頼、タイミング、判断が隠れていることがあります。
Fluency and speed can make a contextually poor answer look more reliable than it is.流暢さと速さによって、状況に合わない答えが実際以上に信頼できそうに見えることがあります。
Human oversight matters, but it should not be treated as error-free by definition.人による監督は重要ですが、それだけで誤りがないと考えるべきではありません。
Responsible use begins by identifying the tool's blind spot rather than assuming general intelligence.責任ある利用は、万能な知能を想定せず、その道具が見落としやすい点を特定することから始まります。
Performance improves when the task is well specified and the output can be independently verified.作業が明確に定義され、出力を別の方法で確認できるほど、性能は上がります。
Optimization is less useful when the disputed issue is which value or trade-off should govern the decision.どの価値やトレードオフを判断の基準にするかが争点なら、最適化の力は限られます。
Delegation changes effort and visibility, but accountability still needs a named person.作業を委ねれば労力や見通しは変わりますが、結果に責任を持つ人は明確にする必要があります。accountability説明責任
Automation may redistribute tasks, status, and entry routes more unevenly than predictions of total replacement suggest.自動化は、仕事がすべて置き換わるという予測より不均等に、作業、立場、仕事への入り口を変えるかもしれません。
A useful evaluation names the task, the failure cost, the reviewer, and the person accountable for the result.役立つ評価では、作業、失敗の代償、確認者、結果に責任を持つ人を明確にします。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Something AI does badly (AIが苦手なこと)

WARM-UP 1What is one thing AI does badly when it tries to do human work?AIが人の仕事をしようとするとき、うまくできないことを一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which job gives a clear example?
  • Would you trust AI more with numbers or feelings?

Sarcasm or sadness (皮肉、それとも悲しみ)

WARM-UP 2Which is harder for AI to understand: sarcasm or sadness?AIにとって理解しにくいのは、皮肉と悲しみのどちらですか？

Follow up
  • What clue would a person notice?
  • Could AI misunderstand 'I'm fine' in a text message?

A mistake that starts funny (最初は面白く見える間違い)

QUESTION 1Which AI mistake can look funny until it affects a real decision?実際の決断に影響するまでは面白く見えるAIの間違いは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who could be affected by that mistake?
  • Have you ever seen AI give a strange answer?

A boring task AI can help with (AIが手伝える地味な仕事)

QUESTION 2Which boring part of human work should AI help with?人の仕事の地味な部分で、AIに手伝ってほしいものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How could a person check the result?
  • Would you rather let AI sort emails or write a complaint reply?

A mistake that can hurt someone (人を傷つける可能性のある間違い)

QUESTION 3Which AI mistake could seriously hurt someone?AIのどんな間違いが、人を深く傷つける可能性がありますか？

Follow up
  • Who could be hurt?
  • Should AI choose who gets a job by itself?

A person must be responsible (責任を持つ人が必要なこと)

QUESTION 4Which decisions need a person who can explain the result and accept responsibility?結果を説明し、責任を引き受ける人が必要なのは、どんな決断ですか？

Follow up
  • What could go wrong without a person in charge?
  • Who should have the final say in a hiring decision?

A human skill that will matter more (もっと大切になる人間の技能)

QUESTION 5What human skill will matter more as AI improves?AIが進歩するほど、どんな人間の技能がより大切になりますか？

Follow up
  • Why will that skill matter more?
  • Which matters more with AI: asking good questions or checking answers?

Expecting too much from AI (AIに期待しすぎること)

QUESTION 6What do people often expect AI to do better than it can?人はAIに、実際よりもうまくできると何を期待しがちですか？

Follow up
  • What example shows the real limit?
  • Which does AI do better: summarizing text or understanding feelings?
