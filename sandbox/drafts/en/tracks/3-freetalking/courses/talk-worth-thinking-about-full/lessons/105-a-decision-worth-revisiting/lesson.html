A decision worth revisiting (見直す価値のある決断)

Let's compare one decision with a realistic alternative. Please read the title aloud.一つの決断と現実的な別の選択を比べましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

A different choice is not a simple rewrite (別の選択だけでは書き換えられない)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Revising one decision need not erase the value of the path that actually occurred.一つの決断を見直しても、実際に歩んだ道の価値まで消す必要はありません。
A choice may have been reasonable under the evidence, pressure, and options then available.当時の根拠、圧力、選択肢の中では、その選択が合理的だった可能性があります。
Hindsight can convert uncertainty into a false sense that the outcome was predictable.結果を知った後では、当時は不確かだったことまで予測できたように感じられます。
A counterfactual path changes a network of consequences, not one isolated result.反実仮想の道では、一つの結果だけでなく、結果のつながり全体が変わります。counterfactual反実仮想
Useful regret extracts a principle for future action without demanding endless punishment.役立つ後悔は、自分を責め続けず、将来の行動に使える原則を取り出します。
Regret becomes corrosive when we second-guess the past without gaining new action or understanding.新しい行動や理解につながらないまま過去の判断を疑い続けると、後悔は心をすり減らします。
The claim of no regrets may express acceptance, identity, defense, or a genuinely low level of regret.「後悔はない」という言葉は、受容、自分らしさ、防御、または本当に後悔が少ないことを表す場合があります。
Acceptance of the path is compatible with admitting that a particular decision was poor.歩んだ道を受け入れることと、特定の決断がよくなかったと認めることは両立します。
A low-stakes decision can expose the same pattern of judgment as a life-changing one.小さな決断からも、人生を変える決断と同じ判断の型が見えることがあります。
Counterfactual reflection earns its value when it improves a future choice rather than rewriting identity.反実仮想の振り返りは、自分自身を書き換えるのでなく、次の選択を改善するときに価値を持ちます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A decision worth revisiting (見直す価値のある決断)

WARM-UP 1Would you rather change a movie ending or a real historical decision?映画の結末と現実の歴史上の決断なら、どちらを変えたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which ending or decision would you change?
  • Would you make a sad movie ending happy?

A choice that works out well (結果的にうまくいく選択)

WARM-UP 2Can a choice look wrong at first but work out well?最初は間違って見える選択が、結果的にうまくいくことはありますか？

Follow up
  • What's a simple example?
  • Does a good result mean it was a good choice?

What shows a good choice (よい選択を示すもの)

QUESTION 1When people disagree, which better shows a good choice: fewer problems or happier people?意見が分かれるとき、問題が減ることと、人が幸せになることのどちらが、よい選択だったと分かりやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Can luck create a good result?
  • Can a good choice still end badly?

What was known then, or what happened later? (当時の情報か、その後の結果か)

QUESTION 2When judging a decision, which matters more: what was known then or what happened later?決断を評価するとき、当時分かっていたことと、その後起きたことのどちらがより大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does that matter more to you?
  • Should a doctor be judged by the decision or only the result?

One solution, another problem (一つを解決して別の問題)

QUESTION 3Can solving one problem create a new one?一つの問題を解決すると、新しい問題が生まれることはありますか？

Follow up
  • What's a simple example?
  • Can saving money now create a bigger cost later?

Why it made sense (当時は合理的だった理由)

QUESTION 4Why can a bad decision make sense at the time?悪い決断でも、そのときには納得できることがあるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • What information was available then?
  • Which matters more under pressure: more time or better information?

Looking back to decide better (次の決断のために振り返ること)

QUESTION 5When does looking back help with the next decision?振り返ることが、次の決断に役立つのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What future action could looking back change?
  • Is it useful to think about a small mistake after the mistake is fixed?

A fixable mistake (直しやすい間違い)

QUESTION 6Which is easier to fix: being late or sending the wrong message?遅刻と、間違ったメッセージを送ることでは、どちらが直しやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What would you do first?
  • Which mistake gets worse if you wait?
