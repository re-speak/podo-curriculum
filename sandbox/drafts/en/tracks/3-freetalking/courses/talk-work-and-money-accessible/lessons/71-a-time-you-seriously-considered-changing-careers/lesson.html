A time you seriously considered changing careers (キャリア変更を真剣に考えたとき)

Let's talk about why people consider changing careers. Please read the title aloud.人がキャリア変更を考える理由について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

A near-change reveals what a decision must protect (変えかけた経験から守るべきものが見える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
People may change careers by changing field, role, or working pattern.分野、役割、働き方を変えることで、キャリアを変える人もいます。change careersキャリアを変える
A career change can also mean moving between study, care, paid work, and retirement.キャリア変更には、勉強、ケア、有給の仕事、退職の間の移動も含まれます。
Thinking seriously about change does not require taking action.真剣に考えても、実際に行動するとは限りません。
The attraction of a new path may coexist with real risk.新しい進路の魅力と現実的なリスクは両立します。
Staying can be an active decision, not a failure of courage.続けることも、勇気がないのではなく、積極的な判断になり得ます。
A change can be gradual instead of complete.変更は、一度に全部行うのではなく、段階的にもできます。
Privacy matters when a career decision could affect security or identity.キャリアの決定が安全や自分の立場に影響する場合、プライバシーが大切です。
Advice should not pretend constraints are simple.助言で、制約を単純に扱うべきではありません。
A decision can remain unresolved for a valid reason.正当な理由で、判断がまだ決まらないこともあります。
The key question is what a change is meant to improve.大切なのは、変更によって何をよくしたいかです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A possible change (考えた変更)

WARM-UP 1What makes someone seriously consider changing careers?人が本気でキャリアチェンジを考えるのは、何がきっかけですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of change was considered?
  • Was it a change of field, role, schedule, or something else?

What pushed the question (考えるきっかけ)

WARM-UP 2Which pressure most often pushes someone toward a career change?人をキャリアチェンジに向かわせることが多いプレッシャーは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Was the pressure temporary or ongoing?
  • Which part needed to improve?

What pulled the other way (新しい方向の魅力)

QUESTION 1What makes a different career direction attractive?別のキャリアの方向が魅力的になるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Was it the work, meaning, freedom, or conditions?
  • Which attraction was strongest?

What was at risk (懸念したこと)

QUESTION 2What makes a career change difficult or risky?キャリアチェンジを難しくしたり、リスクのあるものにしたりするのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which risk was most concrete?
  • Could any part be reduced?

What happened (その後)

QUESTION 3Why do people postpone a career change?なぜ人はキャリアチェンジを延期するのですか？

Follow up
  • Which responsibility or uncertainty causes the delay?
  • What new evidence could change the decision?

A smaller test (小さく試す)

QUESTION 4What smaller experiment could test the new direction?新しい方向を試すために、どんな小さな実験ができますか？

Follow up
  • What would the person learn?
  • What limit would keep the test safe?

Who knew (誰に話したか)

QUESTION 5Who should someone talk to before changing careers?キャリアを変える前に、誰と話すとよいですか？

Follow up
  • What could that person help clarify?
  • Which details would be inappropriate to share?

What a change should improve (変更でよくしたいこと)

QUESTION 6Before changing careers, what should a person be able to name clearly?キャリアを変える前に、何を明確に言えるとよいですか？

Follow up
  • What problem should the change solve?
  • What value should it protect?
