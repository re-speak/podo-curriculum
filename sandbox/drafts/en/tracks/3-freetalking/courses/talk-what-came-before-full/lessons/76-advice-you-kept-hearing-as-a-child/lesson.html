Why repeated advice stays with us (繰り返された助言が残る理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Adults often repeat advice because protection feels more urgent than explanation.大人は、説明することより守ることを急ぎ、同じ助言を繰り返すことがあります。
A child may absorb the wording years before grasping the reason behind it.子どもは、その理由を理解する何年も前から、言い回しだけを覚えていることがあります。
Some advice addresses practical risks, from crossing roads to managing money.道路を渡るときの確認からお金の管理まで、現実的な危険に関する助言もあります。
Other warnings reveal as much about the adult's history as the child's situation.別の忠告には、子どもの状況と同じくらい大人自身の過去が表れています。
Without a clear reason, repetition can sound like nagging rather than care.理由が明確でなければ、繰り返しは思いやりより小言のように聞こえます。
Even sound advice can lose force when repetition makes it background noise.妥当な助言でも、繰り返されて当たり前になると力を失うことがあります。background noise意識されないもの
The meaning may become obvious only after experience recreates the original risk.経験によって同じ危険に直面して初めて、その意味が明らかになることがあります。
People may later hear themselves passing the same warning to a younger person.後になって、自分が同じ忠告を年下の人にしていると気づくこともあります。
Some inherited advice deserves revision because the world or the value behind it changed.時代や価値観が変わり、受け継いだ助言を見直す必要がある場合もあります。
Revisiting the words helps us separate useful care from fear we need not inherit.その言葉を振り返ることで、役立つ思いやりと受け継ぐ必要のない不安を分けられます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The repeated line (何度も聞いた言葉)

WARM-UP 1What advice do adults keep passing on to younger people?大人が若い世代に伝え続けている助言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who usually gives that advice?
  • Do adults repeat 'be careful' or 'work hard' more often?

The exact words (実際の言葉)

WARM-UP 2What's a piece of advice whose exact words have stayed with you?正確な言い方が今も心に残っている助言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Did anyone explain what it meant?
  • Do lines like 'be kind' or 'work hard' come to mind?

Advice people ignore (無視されがちな助言)

QUESTION 1Which childhood warning do people often understand only after ignoring it?無視した後になって初めて意味が分かることの多い子どもの頃の忠告は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why do people ignore it?
  • Which is easier for children to ignore: advice about money or health?

Why repeat it? (繰り返す理由)

QUESTION 2Which childhood rule is still worth passing on?今も伝える価値がある子ども時代のルールは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why is it still useful?
  • Would 'tell the truth' or 'clean up after yourself' be a good example?

Advice that makes sense later (後で分かる助言)

QUESTION 3Which childhood advice becomes more convincing with age?年齢を重ねるほど納得できるようになる子どもの頃の助言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why can the advice make more sense later?
  • Would advice about money or health be a good example?

Passing it on (受け継ぐ言葉)

QUESTION 4Which childhood advice would you rewrite before passing it on?子どもの頃の助言で、次の人に伝える前に言い換えたいものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What part would you keep?
  • Would you change advice about school, manners, or safety?

A reminder without nagging (口うるさくない注意)

QUESTION 5How can adults give reminders without sounding like they are nagging?大人は、どうすれば小言に聞こえずに注意できますか？

Follow up
  • Would a calm voice or a short message help more?
  • When should the adult stop repeating it?

Advice for adults (大人への助言)

QUESTION 6What advice do adults ignore even though they know it is true?本当だと分かっていても、大人が無視しがちな助言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would adults benefit more from advice about rest, money, or kindness?
  • Why might they ignore it?
