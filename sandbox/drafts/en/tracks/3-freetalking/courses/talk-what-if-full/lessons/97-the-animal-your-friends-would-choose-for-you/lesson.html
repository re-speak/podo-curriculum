What an animal comparison can symbolize (動物の比較が象徴するもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Animal comparisons compress a complicated personality into one memorable image.動物の比較は、複雑な性格を一つの覚えやすいイメージにまとめます。
To symbolize a trait is to stand for it through an image rather than describe it literally.特徴を象徴するとは、文字どおり説明するのではなく、イメージを通してその特徴を表すことです。
An animal may represent loyalty in one context and dependence in another.ある動物が一つの文脈では忠実さを、別の文脈では依存を表すことがあります。
Familiar observers may recognize consistent habits that are invisible from inside our own routine.身近な観察者は、自分の日課の中からは見えない一貫した習慣を認識することがあります。
Different observers may emphasize different traits because they see a person in different settings.人を見る場面が異なるため、観察者によって重視する特徴も変わります。
Self-image may emphasize aspiration, while other people's choice reflects observed behaviour.自己イメージは理想を強調し、ほかの人の選択は観察した行動を反映することがあります。
A useful comparison can reveal a mismatch without reducing a person to a fixed label.役立つ比較は、人を固定したラベルにせずに、見方のずれを表せます。
Rejecting an animal clarifies which trait or stereotype feels fundamentally inaccurate.ある動物を否定することで、どの特徴や固定観念が根本的に違うかが明確になります。
A fair comparison should follow real traits supplied by the person rather than a guess based on appearance.公平な比較では、見た目の推測ではなく、その人が実際に示した特徴を使うべきです。
A good metaphor invites correction and explanation rather than claiming the final truth about a person.よい比喩は人について最終的な真実を主張せず、訂正や説明を招きます。metaphor比喩

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your animal (自分を表す動物)

WARM-UP 1Which animal best represents your personality?どの動物があなたの性格を最もよく表しますか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a dog, cat, fox, or bird?
  • Where does the comparison stop working?

Your friends' choice (友人が選ぶ動物)

WARM-UP 2Which animal would your friends choose for you?友達はあなたにどの動物を選ぶと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Would they choose a dog, cat, or bird?
  • How is a dog's personality different from a cat's?

Your own choice (自分で選ぶ動物)

QUESTION 1Which animal quality would you like to develop more in yourself?どんな動物の性質を、自分の中でもっと伸ばしたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a dog's loyalty, a cat's independence, or a bird's freedom?
  • When would that quality help?

What friends notice (友人が気づくこと)

QUESTION 2What might friends notice in your habits that you overlook in yourself?自分では見落としていても、友人が習慣の中で気づくことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which habit might they notice?
  • Do people notice a person's voice or body language first?

Not that animal (絶対に違う動物)

QUESTION 3Which animal is least like you?あなたに最も似ていない動物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would a loud, slow, or highly social animal feel least like you?
  • Could one trait still fit?

A busy day or a day off (忙しい日か休みの日か)

QUESTION 4Which animal fits you on a busy day?忙しい日の自分には、どの動物が合いますか？

Follow up
  • Which trait does that animal show?
  • On a busy day, do you move quickly or slowly?

Three real traits (三つの本当の特徴)

QUESTION 5I'll share three real traits about myself. Which animal fits them?私が自分の本当の特徴を三つ話します。どの動物が合いますか？

Follow up
  • Which trait has the strongest influence on your choice?
  • What does the animal comparison fail to capture?

Beyond one animal (一つの動物を超えて)

QUESTION 6What important part of someone could never fit one animal comparison?一つの動物のたとえでは決して表せない、人の大切な部分は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could one animal show both kindness and ambition?
  • Would using two animals be fairer?
