WHAT IF

What you would really do if you won the lottery (宝くじに当たったら本当にすること)

Let's imagine how a large lottery win could change everyday life. Please read the title aloud.宝くじで大当たりしたら、日常がどう変わるか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What a sudden win reveals (突然の大当たりで見えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A sudden jackpot creates immediate choices long before it provides a sensible plan.突然の大当たりは、現実的な計画を与えるより先に、すぐ決めるべき選択を生みます。jackpot大当たり
The first imagined action often exposes what currently feels most urgent.最初に思い浮かぶ行動には、今もっとも切迫していることが表れやすいです。
Some people would share the news immediately, while others would protect their privacy.すぐ知らせる人もいれば、自分のプライバシーを守ろうとする人もいます。
Financial freedom does not automatically settle whether paid or unpaid work still gives life structure.経済的に自由になっても、有給・無給の仕事が生活に必要な形を与えるかは自動的には決まりません。
A first purchase is only one response; waiting, helping, or changing nothing are equally possible.最初に買うだけでなく、待つ、誰かを助ける、何も変えないという答えもあります。
A decision made during the initial excitement may look different once the shock has passed.最初の興奮の中で決めたことは、衝撃が過ぎると違って見えることがあります。
Regret may come from acting too quickly, waiting too long, or not changing anything at all.急ぎすぎたこと、待ちすぎたこと、何も変えなかったことのどれも後悔になり得ます。
The unfamiliar life might become ordinary within weeks, or remain unsettled for years.慣れない生活が数週間で日常になる場合も、何年も安定しない場合もあります。
Money can remove practical limits without supplying purpose, trust, or direction.お金は現実的な制約を減らせますが、目的や信頼、進む方向までは与えません。
The deeper question is which life the money should make possible rather than which object it should buy.本質的なのは、何を買うかではなく、そのお金でどんな人生を可能にしたいかという問いです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The first move (最初の行動)

WARM-UP 1Imagine receiving a life-changing lottery prize tomorrow. Which first decision would matter most?明日、人生を変えるほどの宝くじに当たったと想像してください。最初のどの決断が最も重要ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you deliberately postpone?
  • Which concern makes that the most sensible first move?

Why first (それを最初にする理由)

WARM-UP 2Which priority should guide the first decision after an unexpected financial windfall?予想外の大金を得た後、最初の決断を導くべき優先事項は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which immediate need or value would it serve?
  • What information would you want first?

Who knows (誰に知らせるか)

QUESTION 1Would you disclose the jackpot to anyone?大当たりしたことを誰かに知らせますか？

Follow up
  • Who would have a legitimate reason to know?
  • Which consequence could careful disclosure prevent?

Work after the win (当選後の仕事)

QUESTION 2Which parts of your current paid work, study, care, or routine would you keep?今の有給の仕事、勉強、ケア、または日課のどの部分を続けますか？

Follow up
  • Which structure would you create?
  • What would remain meaningful without financial pressure?

Buying or not buying (買うか買わないか)

QUESTION 3What, if anything, would become your first purchase?最初の買い物になるものがあるとすれば、何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of life would it change?
  • Which non-purchase use would come first?

A possible regret (後悔する可能性)

QUESTION 4Which choice could produce regret, if any?後悔につながる選択があるとすれば、どれですか？

Follow up
  • Which later consequence would create it?
  • Which principle would keep the decision defensible?

A new normal (新しい日常)

QUESTION 5Would the jackpot create a different normal, or would you preserve most of your current life?大当たりで新しい日常が生まれますか、それとも、今の生活の大部分を保ちますか？

Follow up
  • Which unfamiliar part would become routine first?
  • Which part would you intentionally preserve?

What money cannot choose (お金では決められないこと)

QUESTION 6Which important part of life would remain unresolved by the jackpot?大当たりしても解決されない大切なことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What non-financial resource would it still require?
  • Could money reduce the difficulty without resolving it?
