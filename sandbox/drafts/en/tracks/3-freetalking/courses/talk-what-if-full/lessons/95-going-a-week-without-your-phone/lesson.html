What a phone-free week would test (携帯電話なしの一週間で試されること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A digital detox is a deliberate period in which discretionary digital-device use is reduced.デジタルデトックスとは、自由に減らせるデジタル機器の利用を意識的に減らす期間です。
For some people, much phone use is convenient or recreational rather than essential.人によっては、携帯電話の多くの利用は必要不可欠ではなく、便利さや娯楽のためです。
For others, a phone provides accessibility, medical support, safety, paid work, or care coordination.別の人には、アクセシビリティ、医療支援、安全、有給の仕事、ケアの調整に必要な道具です。accessibilityアクセシビリティ
Here, what breaks first refers to the first routine or system that fails, not physical damage.ここで「最初に駄目になるもの」とは、物理的な破損ではなく、最初に機能しなくなる日課や仕組みを指します。
Modern infrastructure has concentrated navigation, payment, tickets, authentication, and communication in one device.現代の仕組みでは、移動、支払い、切符、認証、連絡が一つの機器に集中しています。
The hardest loss may be one function, one person, or one source of reassurance rather than the device itself.最も困るのは機器そのものではなく、一つの機能、一人との連絡、安心感かもしれません。
Fewer alerts could improve concentration, sleep, presence, or boredom tolerance.通知が減ると、集中、睡眠、目の前への注意、退屈への耐性がよくなる可能性があります。
A workable experiment may rely on replacement devices, printed information, or agreed human support.実行できる実験には、代わりの機器、紙の情報、合意した人の支援が必要な場合があります。
A period offline can distinguish essential access from convenient or habitual use.オフラインの時間を過ごすと、必要な利用と、便利さや習慣による利用を区別できます。
A responsible digital detox distinguishes discretionary habits from access and care needs.責任あるデジタルデトックスは、自由に減らせる習慣と、アクセスやケアの必要を区別します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Seven days (七日間)

WARM-UP 1Which challenge would define a week without discretionary phone use?携帯電話の自由に減らせる利用をやめた一週間では、どんな課題が中心になりますか？

Follow up
  • Which routine would require the strongest replacement?
  • Which condition could make the challenge workable?

What stops working (最初に機能しなくなること)

WARM-UP 2Which routine or system is most dependent on discretionary phone access?どの日課や仕組みが、携帯電話の任意の利用に最も依存していますか？

Follow up
  • Is that function essential, accessible support, or convenience?
  • Which boundary keeps the experiment workable?

What you would miss (恋しくなるもの)

QUESTION 1Which function, connection, or reassurance would be hardest to replace during a reduced-use week?利用を減らす一週間で、代わりを用意するのが最も難しい機能、つながり、安心は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which practical or emotional need does it meet?
  • Which alternative could preserve the essential benefit?

What might improve (よくなりそうなこと)

QUESTION 2Which part of life might improve during a phone-free or reduced-phone week?携帯電話なし、または利用を減らした一週間で、生活のどの部分がよくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which current pressure might decrease?
  • Which observable change would count as improvement?

A past break (過去に離れた時間)

QUESTION 3Which setting makes a deliberate break from optional phone use most realistic?携帯電話の任意の利用を意識的に休むことが、最も現実的になる場面はどこですか？

Follow up
  • Which feature of that setting supports a deliberate pause?
  • What duration would preserve the benefit without causing disruption?

Making it work (実行するための支え)

QUESTION 4Which tools, access arrangements, and boundaries would make a reduced-use experiment responsible?利用を減らす試みを責任あるものにするには、どんな道具、利用環境、境界が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which replacement system would provide that support?
  • How would the arrangement preserve safety and accessibility?

One use to keep (残す一つの使い方)

QUESTION 5Which phone function should remain available during a deliberately reduced-use week?携帯電話の利用を意識的に減らす一週間でも、どの機能は使えるようにしておくべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which need makes continued access proportionate?
  • Which discretionary use could be reduced without affecting that need?

After the week (一週間のあと)

QUESTION 6Which discretionary phone habit offers the greatest opportunity for sustainable change?携帯電話の自由に減らせる習慣のうち、持続的な変化につながる可能性が最も高いものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which existing boundary deserves to remain?
  • Which modest change could be sustainable?
