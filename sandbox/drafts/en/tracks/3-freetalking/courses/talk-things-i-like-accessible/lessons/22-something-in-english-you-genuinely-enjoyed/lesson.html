THINGS I LIKE

Something in English you genuinely enjoyed (心から楽しめた英語のもの)

Let's trace one enjoyable experience in English and what it led to. Please read the title aloud.英語で楽しめた一つの体験と、その後につながったことを話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Enjoyment before perfect understanding (完全に分からなくても楽しめること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Many people first connect with English through entertainment, not study.英語に最初に親しむきっかけは、勉強より娯楽から生まれることがよくあります。
A song, film, or game can feel exciting even when much of the language is unclear.言葉の多くが分からなくても、歌や映画、ゲームにわくわくすることがあります。
Enjoyment gives us a reason to return to the same words.楽しさがあると、同じ英語に繰り返し触れる理由ができます。
Repeated scenes or lyrics slowly make unfamiliar expressions easier to recognize.場面や歌詞を繰り返すうちに、知らない表現も少しずつ分かるようになります。
Understanding one small part can feel like a real breakthrough.一部分を理解できると、本当の突破口のように感じられます。breakthrough突破口
That moment can change English from a school subject into something personal.その瞬間、英語が学校の科目から自分の興味へ変わることがあります。
People often explore more after finding one creator, genre, or community they like.好きな作り手やジャンル、仲間を一つ見つけると、さらに広く探すことがあります。
Subtitles and translations can support enjoyment without replacing the English.字幕や翻訳は、英語そのものを失わずに楽しむ助けになります。
The best starting point is different for each person's interests.よい入り口は、その人の興味によって変わります。
A personal recommendation works best when it explains why the experience is enjoyable.よいおすすめは、なぜ楽しめそうかを伝えます。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The first enjoyable thing (最初に楽しめたもの)

WARM-UP 1What was the first thing in English you actually enjoyed — song, film, game, anything?歌や映画、ゲームなど何でもよいので、英語で初めて本当に楽しめたものは何でしたか？

Follow up
  • What do you remember about it?
  • What made it feel different from studying?

The kind of thing (どんなもの？)

WARM-UP 2Was it something you watched, listened to, read, or played?それは見たもの、聴いたもの、読んだもの、遊んだもののどれでしたか？

Follow up
  • How did you find it?
  • Did anyone share the experience with you?

Your age then (そのときの年齢)

QUESTION 1How old were you when you found it?それを見つけたとき、何歳くらいでしたか？

Follow up
  • What else were you interested in then?
  • How did English fit into your life at that time?

How much you understood (理解できた量)

QUESTION 2How much of the English did you understand?その英語をどのくらい理解できましたか？

Follow up
  • Which part was clear first?
  • What helped with the difficult parts?

What came next (その次)

QUESTION 3What did you do after you enjoyed it?それを楽しんだあと、何をしましたか？

Follow up
  • Did you look for anything similar?
  • Which action kept your interest going?

What brought you here (今につながったもの)

QUESTION 4How did that experience lead to the English you use now?その体験は、今使っている英語にどうつながりましたか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the connection is strongest?
  • What might have happened without it?

A good starting point (よい入り口)

QUESTION 5What helps someone enjoy English before they understand everything?すべて理解する前でも英語を楽しむには、何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • How much support is useful?
  • What can make the experience feel like work?

Your recommendation (自分のおすすめ)

QUESTION 6What would you recommend to someone starting English now?今英語を始める人に、何をすすめますか？

Follow up
  • Why would that be a good gateway?
  • Who might prefer a different starting point?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
