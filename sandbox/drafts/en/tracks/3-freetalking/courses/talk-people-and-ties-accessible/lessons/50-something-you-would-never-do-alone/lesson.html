Where the solo line comes from (一人で行動する境界)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Being alone feels different in different activities.一人でいる感覚は、することによって変わります。
Eating alone may feel ordinary while travelling alone feels difficult.一人での食事は普通でも、一人旅は難しく感じる人がいます。
The line depends partly on safety, cost, and unfamiliarity.その境界には、安全、費用、慣れているかどうかが関係します。
It also depends on whether the activity is meant to be shared.その活動を誰かと分かち合いたいかどうかも関係します。
Doing something alone can bring freedom and control.一人で行動すると、自由に決められます。
It can also make every problem feel like one person's responsibility.一方で、問題を全部一人で引き受けるように感じることもあります。
A small successful attempt can expand a person's comfort zone.小さな成功で、安心してできる範囲が広がることがあります。comfort zone安心してできる範囲
A bad experience can move the line in the other direction.悪い経験で、その境界が逆に狭くなることもあります。
Another person's courage can make a solo activity seem possible.ほかの人が一人で行動するのを見ると、その活動が自分にもできそうに感じます。
The useful question is not whether solo is better, but where the choice feels right.大切なのは一人がよいかではなく、どこまでなら自分に合うかです。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Where's your line? (どこまで一人で？)

WARM-UP 1Eating alone, cinema alone, travelling alone — where's your line?一人で食事、映画、一人旅、どこまでなら一人でできますか？

Follow up
  • Which of the three feels easiest?
  • Which one crosses your line?

A recent example (最近の例)

WARM-UP 2What's the boldest thing you've done alone—or one solo activity you'd realistically try?一人でした最も思い切ったこと、または現実的に一人で挑戦してみたいことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • For a real experience, where did it happen?
  • For a possible activity, what would make it manageable?

What happened (そのとき)

QUESTION 1What happened in that real experience—or what might happen in the imagined one?実際の経験では何が起きましたか、または、想像した挑戦では何が起きそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which moment would feel hardest?
  • What could make it satisfying?

Freedom or pressure (自由か負担か)

QUESTION 2What is the best and hardest part of doing that alone?それを一人でする一番よい点と、一番難しい点は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which decisions become easier?
  • Which problem would you rather share?

Someone braver (もっと勇気のある人)

QUESTION 3Do you know anyone braver about doing things alone—or can you use a public or fictional example?一人で行動することに自分より勇気がある人を知っていますか、または、公に知られた人や架空の人物を例にできますか？

Follow up
  • If someone comes to mind, what do they do differently?
  • If nobody does, what would someone confident doing things alone do differently?

A moving line (変わった境界)

QUESTION 4Has your line for doing things alone changed?一人でできることの境界は変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what moved it?
  • If not, what has kept it stable?

A smaller step (小さな一歩)

QUESTION 5What smaller solo activity could prepare you for the next one?次の挑戦に備えるために、どんな小さなことなら一人でできますか？

Follow up
  • How would you reduce the risk?
  • When would you know you were ready?

What would push it (境界を広げる条件)

QUESTION 6What would push your line further?何があれば、一人でできる範囲が広がりますか？

Follow up
  • Would money, information, or practice matter most?
  • Which activity would still remain outside it?
