Why some advice stays (助言が心に残る理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Advice often stays because of when we heard it, not only what it said.助言は内容だけでなく、聞いたタイミングによって心に残ります。
A simple sentence can land differently during a difficult decision.難しい決断のときには、簡単な一言も違って響きます。
The speaker matters when we trust their experience or intentions.相手の経験や意図を信頼していると、その人の言葉は大切になります。
We may understand the words immediately but need years to understand their value.言葉はすぐ分かっても、その価値を理解するまで何年もかかることがあります。
Good advice usually leaves room for the listener's real situation.よい助言は、聞く人の実際の状況に合わせる余地があります。
Bad advice may sound confident while ignoring cost, risk, or personality.悪い助言は自信があるように聞こえても、費用や危険、性格を無視することがあります。
Ignoring advice does not always mean it was wrong.助言に従わなくても、その助言が間違っていたとは限りません。
Following advice also does not prove it caused the result.助言に従ったからといって、それが結果の原因だとは限りません。
When we pass advice on, we also pass our interpretation of it.助言を人に伝えるとき、自分なりの解釈も一緒に伝えます。
The best test is whether the advice helps someone see a choice more clearly.よい助言かどうかは、誰かが選択をはっきり見られるようになるかで分かります。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Words that stayed (残っている言葉)

WARM-UP 1What's one thing someone said to you that you still repeat?誰かに言われたことで、今も繰り返している言葉は何ですか？

Follow up
  • If something comes to mind, who said it?
  • If nothing does, what familiar saying or general example could you use?

The wording (言葉)

WARM-UP 2What were the exact words—or how would you put the idea now?正確にはどんな言葉でしたか、または、今ならその考えをどう表しますか？

Follow up
  • Which word carries the main point?
  • Would another wording change the meaning?

The source (言った人)

QUESTION 1Who gave you the advice—or where did you hear it?誰からその助言をもらいましたか、または、どこで聞きましたか？

Follow up
  • Why did that source matter?
  • Would the same words work from someone else?

The moment (関係する場面)

QUESTION 2What was happening when the advice became relevant—or when might it matter?その助言が関係するようになったとき、何が起きていましたか、または、どんな場面で関係しそうですか？

Follow up
  • For a real case, what made the timing important?
  • For a familiar example, when might the advice be needed?

Understanding later (あとで分かること)

QUESTION 3Did you understand advice you actually heard then—or when might a familiar example become clear?自分が実際に聞いた助言は当時理解できましたか、または、身近な例ならいつ意味が分かりそうですか？

Follow up
  • For a real case, what changed your understanding?
  • For a familiar example, which experience might make it clearer?

Did it help? (役に立つ？)

QUESTION 4How has the advice helped someone—or how might it fail to help?その助言は人にどのように役立ちましたか、または、どのように役立たない可能性がありますか？

Follow up
  • For a real case, which choice did it affect?
  • For a familiar example, where might its limit be?

Passing it on (人に伝える)

QUESTION 5Who, if anyone, would you pass on the advice to?その助言を人に伝えるなら、誰に伝えますか？

Follow up
  • Who might benefit from it—or might nobody benefit?
  • What would you adapt—or what makes it too specific to share?

Advice ignored (従わなかった助言)

QUESTION 6Have you ever ignored advice—or can you use a familiar example?助言に従わなかったことがありますか、または、身近な例を使えますか？

Follow up
  • If it happened to you, what stopped you from following it?
  • If it is a familiar example, why might someone choose differently?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
