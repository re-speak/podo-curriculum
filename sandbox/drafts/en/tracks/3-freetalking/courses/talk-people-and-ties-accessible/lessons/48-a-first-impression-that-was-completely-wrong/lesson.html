Why first impressions change (第一印象が変わる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A first impression forms before we know much about a person.第一印象は、相手をよく知る前に生まれます。
A voice, expression, or short reply can feel like strong evidence.声や表情、短い返事が、確かな証拠のように感じられることがあります。
But the person may be tired, nervous, busy, or out of place.しかし、その人は疲れていたり、緊張していたり、忙しかったりしただけかもしれません。
One unusual moment can therefore create an unfair picture.そのため、普段と違う一瞬から、不公平なイメージができることがあります。
Repeated contact gives us more situations to compare.何度も会うと、違う場面での行動を比べられます。
A generous action or honest conversation may change the picture quickly.親切な行動や正直な会話で、印象がすぐ変わることもあります。
Sometimes the change is slower and comes from a pattern.一方で、何度かの行動を見て、ゆっくり印象が変わることもあります。
A positive first impression can also turn out to be wrong.よい第一印象も、間違っていたと分かることがあります。turn out実際には〜だとわかる
Remembering our mistakes can make us slower to judge others.自分の思い違いを覚えておくと、人をすぐに判断しにくくなります。
Other people are also forming incomplete first impressions of us.同時に、ほかの人も私たちについて不完全な第一印象を作っています。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Someone unexpected (意外だった人)

WARM-UP 1Has anyone turned out completely different from how they first seemed?最初の印象とまったく違う人だったと分かったことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, who was the person to you?
  • If not, which familiar or fictional example could you discuss?

At first (最初は)

WARM-UP 2What did you first think about that person or example?その人、または例について、最初はどう思いましたか？

Follow up
  • Which detail created that impression?
  • How certain did it feel?

The evidence (変えた出来事)

QUESTION 1What happened that changed the first impression?何が起きて、第一印象が変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • Was it one moment or several?
  • What did the person do differently?

How long (かかった時間)

QUESTION 2How long did it take for the impression to change?印象が変わるまで、どのくらいかかりましたか？

Follow up
  • Why did it take that long?
  • Did anyone see the person differently sooner?

What you missed (見落としていたこと)

QUESTION 3Why was the first impression wrong?なぜ最初の印象は間違っていたのですか？

Follow up
  • What context did you not have?
  • Was any part of the first impression still fair?

The other direction (逆の変化)

QUESTION 4Has a good first impression ever become worse?よい第一印象が、あとで悪くなったことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what changed it?
  • If not, what warning sign might people miss?

Judging quickly (すぐ判断すること)

QUESTION 5When is a quick first impression useful?すぐに持つ第一印象が役立つのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • When can it become unfair?
  • What should make us pause?

People's view of you (自分への第一印象)

QUESTION 6What, if anything, do people get wrong about you at first?人は最初、自分について何か誤解しやすいですか、それとも、特にありませんか？

Follow up
  • If something is misread, what creates that impression?
  • If not, what do people usually understand correctly?
