What repair after conflict requires (対立のあとの修復に必要なこと)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Arguments do not all need the same kind of repair.けんかはすべて同じ方法で仲直りできるわけではありません。
A small misunderstanding may need only a quick clarification.小さな誤解なら、すぐに説明するだけでよいことがあります。
A hurtful action may need time, responsibility, and a real apology.傷つける行動には、時間、責任、具体的な謝罪が必要な場合があります。
Contacting first does not automatically mean admitting all the blame.先に連絡しても、すべて自分が悪いと認めることにはなりません。
It can simply mean that the relationship matters more than the silence.沈黙を続けるより、関係を大切にしたいという意味にもなります。
Some people need a pause before they can speak calmly.落ち着いて話すために、少し時間が必要な人もいます。
Too much silence can make the original problem harder to name.沈黙が長すぎると、最初の問題を言葉にしにくくなります。
A good repair names the problem without trying to win it again.よい仲直りでは、もう一度勝とうとせずに問題を言葉にします。
Some relationships recover, while others need a new boundary.元に戻る関係もあれば、新しい境界が必要な関係もあります。
Making up is successful when the next interaction can be more honest.次の会話をもっと正直にできるなら、仲直りは成功です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Who moves first? (先に動く人)

WARM-UP 1When you argue with someone close, who contacts who first?親しい人とけんかしたとき、どちらから先に連絡しますか？

Follow up
  • If no close relationship or argument comes to mind, what seems fair in an ordinary imagined disagreement?
  • In a real or imagined case, what makes the first move difficult?

How long (かかる時間)

WARM-UP 2How long, if at all, do you need before you can talk calmly?落ち着いて話すまでに時間は必要ですか、必要なら、どのくらいですか？

Follow up
  • If you take a pause, what do you do during it?
  • If you do not, what helps you speak calmly right away?

The first message (最初のメッセージ)

QUESTION 1What would you say when you reach out in the first message?最初に連絡を取るメッセージで何と言いますか？

Follow up
  • Would you name the argument immediately?
  • How would you avoid sounding defensive?

A real or safe example (実際または話せる例)

QUESTION 2What was a recent small disagreement—or what everyday example are you comfortable discussing?最近の小さな意見の違い、または差し支えなく話せる日常の例は何ですか？

Follow up
  • If it was real, what was the disagreement really about?
  • If it is a general example, what might happen afterward?

What repairs it (修復に役立つこと)

QUESTION 3What makes an apology feel real?どんな謝罪なら本気だと感じますか？

Follow up
  • How specific should it be?
  • What action should follow the words?

Still unresolved (まだ解決していないこと)

QUESTION 4Can some arguments remain unfixed without ending a relationship?解決しないけんかがあっても、関係は続けられますか？

Follow up
  • Which differences can people accept?
  • When is a new boundary needed?

Not every repair (仲直りしない選択)

QUESTION 5When is making up not the right goal?仲直りを目標にしないほうがよいのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What would need to change first?
  • How can someone end contact respectfully?

The right way (よい方法)

QUESTION 6What's the right way to make up after an ordinary argument?普通のけんかのあと、どのように仲直りするのがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Who should speak first?
  • How would you know the repair worked?
