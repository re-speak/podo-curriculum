How small talk opens a door (雑談が会話を始めるしくみ)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Talking to a stranger begins with very little information.知らない人との会話は、相手の情報がほとんどない状態で始まります。
A useful opening therefore comes from the situation around both people.そのため、二人がいる状況から話題を選ぶと始めやすくなります。
A delay, event, menu, or seat can provide a safe first topic.待ち時間や行事、メニュー、席などが安全な最初の話題になります。
The best question is easy to answer and not too personal.よい質問は答えやすく、個人的すぎません。
The reply shows whether the other person wants to continue.返事から、相手が会話を続けたいか分かります。
A short answer is not always rejection; the person may be tired or busy.短い返事でも、拒絶とは限りません。疲れていたり、忙しかったりする場合があります。
One gentle follow-up is usually enough to test the conversation.会話が続くか確かめるには、軽い追加質問が一つあれば十分です。
If it still does not move, a polite ending protects both people.それでも続かなければ、丁寧に終えることでお互いに気まずくなりません。
People who seem good at small talk are often good at noticing signals.雑談が上手な人は、相手の反応によく気づいています。small talk雑談
A failed opening is normal and does not need to become a bigger failure.会話が始まらないことは普通で、大きな失敗にする必要はありません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Your opening (最初の一言)

WARM-UP 1You're seated next to someone you've never met. What do you ask?会ったことのない人の隣に座っています。何を聞きますか？

Follow up
  • Which shared detail could you mention?
  • How would you keep it easy to answer?

The setting (場面)

WARM-UP 2Where would that opening work best?その最初の一言は、どんな場所で使いやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What do both people already share there?
  • Where would it feel less natural?

What to avoid (避けたい話題)

QUESTION 1What would you avoid asking a stranger?知らない人には何を聞かないほうがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Why could it feel too personal?
  • Would the setting ever change that?

What has worked (うまくいった一言)

QUESTION 2Has an opening ever worked well for you—or in a conversation you observed?自分で、または見かけた会話で、最初の一言がうまくいったことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what made the reply easy?
  • If not, which familiar example might work?

A dead end (続かなかった会話)

QUESTION 3What kind of opening makes a conversation die quickly?どんな最初の一言だと、会話がすぐ終わりますか？

Follow up
  • Is the problem the topic or the delivery?
  • What might save it once?

Someone skilled (会話が上手な人)

QUESTION 4Do you know someone good at small talk—or can you use a familiar public or imagined example?雑談が上手な人を知っていますか、または、公に知られた人や想像の例を使えますか？

Follow up
  • If a person or example comes to mind, what signals do they notice?
  • If none does, what would a skilled speaker notice?

When it fails (失敗したとき)

QUESTION 5What do you do when your opening does not work?最初の一言がうまくいかないとき、どうしますか？

Follow up
  • Would you try one more question?
  • How would you end politely?

A useful starter (使えるきっかけ)

QUESTION 6What conversation starter could you actually use this week?今週、実際に使えそうな会話のきっかけは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Where could you use it?
  • What answer would help you continue?
