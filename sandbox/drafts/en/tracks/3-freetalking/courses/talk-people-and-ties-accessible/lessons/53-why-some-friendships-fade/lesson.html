Why connections fade (つながりが薄れる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Most social circles change even when nobody plans the change.人間関係は、誰も変えようとしなくても変わっていきます。
A move, new job, family duty, or health issue can reduce contact.引っ越し、仕事、家族の世話、健康の問題で連絡が減ることがあります。
The friendship may fade before either person notices a clear ending.どちらも終わりに気づかないまま、友情が薄れることがあります。fade薄れていく
Distance matters, but different routines can create distance in the same city.距離は関係しますが、同じ街でも生活習慣の違いで遠くなることがあります。
People also change what they need from friendship.人は友情に求めるものも変わります。
That change is not always a rejection of the other person.その変化は、相手を拒絶することとは限りません。
Sometimes one person did cause harm, and distance protects the other.誰かが傷つけたために、距離を置く必要がある場合もあります。
At other times, looking for fault creates a story that was never there.一方で、責任を探すことで、実際にはなかった対立を作ってしまうこともあります。
Some faded friendships can be renewed with one honest message.薄れた友情も、正直なメッセージ一つで戻ることがあります。
Others can be valued as part of the past without being restarted.再開しなくても、過去の大切な関係として残せる友情もあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Then and now (当時と今)

WARM-UP 1How many people did you see socially five years ago, compared to now?5年前と今では、個人的に会う人の数はどのくらい違いますか？

Follow up
  • If a personal comparison works, would you say more, fewer, or about the same?
  • If it does not, what familiar or imagined change in a social circle could you compare?

What changed (変わったこと)

WARM-UP 2What changed in your real comparison—or in social life generally?自分の比較、または一般的な人間関係で、何が変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • For your own comparison, was the change sudden or gradual?
  • For a general example, which routine might change first?

Who dropped off (疎遠になった人)

QUESTION 1Did you drift apart from anyone—or can you use a familiar example of two friends?誰かと疎遠になりましたか、または、二人の友人についての身近な例を使えますか？

Follow up
  • For a real connection, what changed around the two people?
  • For a familiar example, what commonly reduces contact between friends?

Fault or change (責任か変化か)

QUESTION 2In that real or familiar example, was anyone at fault, or was life changing?その実際または身近な例では、誰かに責任がありましたか、それとも生活が変わっただけですか？

Follow up
  • In that example, what evidence would support blame?
  • Which part could happen without anyone choosing it?

The main cause (主な原因)

QUESTION 3Which matters most: distance, time, or people changing?距離、時間、人の変化のうち、どれが最も大きいですか？

Follow up
  • Can two causes reinforce each other?
  • Which one is easiest to overcome?

Normal or a problem (自然か問題か)

QUESTION 4Is a fading friendship normal, or is it a problem?友情が薄れるのは自然なことですか、それとも問題ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes the difference?
  • When does acceptance become avoidance?

Reach out? (連絡する？)

QUESTION 5When is it worth contacting someone again?もう一度誰かに連絡する価値があるのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What could the first message say?
  • How could you keep it low-pressure?

Overall (全体として)

QUESTION 6Overall, what should people do when a friendship fades?全体として、友情が薄れたとき、人はどうすればよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which relationships deserve effort?
  • Which ones can remain in the past?
