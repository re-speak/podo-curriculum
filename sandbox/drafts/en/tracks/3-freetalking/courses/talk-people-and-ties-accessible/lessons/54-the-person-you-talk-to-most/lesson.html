What frequent contact reveals (頻繁な会話から分かること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The person we talk to most is not always the person we feel closest to.いちばんよく話す人が、いちばん親しい人とは限りません。
Work, home, and daily routines shape who is available.仕事、家庭、日課によって、話せる相手が決まります。
Some frequent conversations are practical rather than personal.よくする会話でも、個人的ではなく用事だけの場合があります。
Others move easily between small updates and serious worries.日常の小さな話から深い悩みまで、自然に話せる関係もあります。
Messaging makes frequent contact possible without sharing a schedule.メッセージなら、同じ時間が空いていなくても頻繁に連絡できます。
Calls or face-to-face talks may carry more tone and detail.電話や対面の会話では、声の調子や細かな情報が伝わります。
The main person can change when a job, home, or relationship changes.仕事、住む場所、関係が変わると、よく話す相手も変わります。
Losing regular contact can reveal how much support it provided.日常の連絡がなくなると、それがどれだけ支えだったか分かることがあります。
Staying in touch requires a rhythm both people can maintain.連絡を続けるには、二人が無理なく続けられる頻度が必要です。stay in touch連絡を取り続ける
The best measure may be not frequency but whether real things can be said.大切なのは頻度より、本当のことを話せるかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Your last message (最後のメッセージ)

WARM-UP 1Who did you last message, and what about?最後に誰に、何についてメッセージを送りましたか？

Follow up
  • If it was automated or a group, what was the contact for?
  • If no recent message comes to mind, what other contact or familiar example could you use?

Most often (いちばんよく連絡する相手)

WARM-UP 2Is there one person, group, or service you contact most, or is your contact spread out?いちばんよく連絡する人、グループ、サービスがありますか、それとも、連絡先は分かれていますか？

Follow up
  • If one contact stands out, is that because of closeness, need, or routine?
  • If not, what determines who or what you contact on a given day?

How often (頻度)

QUESTION 1How often are you in touch with that main contact—or across your contacts overall?その主な相手、または連絡先全体とは、どのくらいの頻度でやり取りしますか？

Follow up
  • If one contact stands out, who usually starts?
  • If contact is spread out, what sets the rhythm?

What you discuss (話す内容や目的)

QUESTION 2Across that contact pattern, what do you discuss—or what is the contact used for?その連絡の形では、何について話しますか、または、その連絡先を何に使いますか？

Follow up
  • For a conversation, which topic comes up most?
  • For an automated contact or service, what purpose does it serve?

What you share (話す内容や用件)

QUESTION 3With that person, group, service, or other contact, what feels easy to discuss or handle?その人、グループ、サービス、またはほかの連絡先とは、どんな話題や用件なら扱いやすいですか？

Follow up
  • If you discuss things, which topics feel comfortable?
  • If not, which task or need is easy to handle through the contact?

Has it changed? (変化)

QUESTION 4Has the way you communicate changed over time?連絡のしかたは、時間とともに変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • If it changed, what caused the shift?
  • If it did not, what has kept it stable?

Five years ago (5年前)

QUESTION 5Five years ago, was there one main person, group, or service—or were contacts spread out?5年前に、主に連絡する人、グループ、サービスが一つありましたか、それとも、連絡先は分かれていましたか？

Follow up
  • If one contact stood out, what role did that person, group, or service play then?
  • If not, what shaped the mix of contacts—or what familiar historical example could you use?

Staying in touch (連絡を続ける)

QUESTION 6What helps two people stay in touch without making it a duty?義務に感じずに二人が連絡を続けるには、何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • How much silence is comfortable?
  • What kind of message is easy to answer?
