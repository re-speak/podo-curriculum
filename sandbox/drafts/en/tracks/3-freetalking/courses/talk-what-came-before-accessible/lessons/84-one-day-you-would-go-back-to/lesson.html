What we seek in one past day (過去の一日に求めるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Wanting to revisit a day does not always mean wanting to change it.一日に戻りたいからといって、変えたいとは限りません。
Someone may want to notice details they missed the first time.最初に見落とした細部に気づきたい人もいます。
A joyful day can be worth reliving without any regret.後悔がなくても、幸せな一日をもう一度体験したいことがあります。reliving再体験すること
A difficult day may hold an unanswered question.つらい一日には、答えのない疑問が残っていることがあります。
Changing one event could also change later events.一つの出来事を変えると、その後も変わるかもしれません。
Watching without acting may still be emotionally difficult.何もしないで見るだけでも、気持ちの上では難しいかもしれません。
The imagined visit needs a clear boundary: one day and one purpose.想像の旅には、一日と一つの目的という境界が必要です。
Some people would choose a public day rather than a private memory.個人的な記憶ではなく、公の一日を選ぶ人もいます。
Others would not return because the present matters more.今のほうが大切で、戻らない人もいます。
The choice reveals what kind of understanding feels unfinished.選んだ一日は、どんな理解がまだ終わっていないかを表します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One day only (一日だけ)

WARM-UP 1You can visit one day in the past. Which day do you choose?過去の一日を訪れられます。どの日を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that day matter?
  • Where would you arrive?

The chosen day (選んだ一日)

WARM-UP 2What is happening on that day?その日には何が起きていますか？

Follow up
  • Who is present?
  • Which moment would you watch first?

Why return (戻る目的)

QUESTION 1What would you hope to understand by seeing that day?その日を見ることで、何を理解したいですか？

Follow up
  • Which question matters most?
  • What might the present-day visitor misunderstand?

Change or observe (変えるか見るか)

QUESTION 2Would you only observe, or would you change something?見るだけにしますか、それとも何かを変えますか？

Follow up
  • What could intervention change?
  • What could observation reveal?

Someone there (その日にいる人)

QUESTION 3Who would you most want to see that day?その日に最も会いたいのは誰ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you notice about them?
  • What might surprise you?

What to say (伝える言葉)

QUESTION 4What would you ask that person?その人に何を尋ねますか？

Follow up
  • Why that question?
  • Would the person understand your concern?

Would it help? (役に立つか)

QUESTION 5How might you feel after returning to the present?現在に戻ったあと、どんな気持ちになるでしょうか？

Follow up
  • What would feel clearer?
  • What might become harder?

Stay in the present (今に残る理由)

QUESTION 6What is one good reason not to go back?過去へ戻らないほうがよい理由を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • What deserves attention now?
  • Can acceptance be more useful than an answer?
