Why a place can be worth revisiting (もう一度行く価値が生まれる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A return visit is different from seeing a place for the first time.再訪は、初めてその場所を見るのとは違います。
The visitor already knows what is worth their time.訪れる人は、何に時間を使う価値があるか知っています。
A place may be attractive because of atmosphere rather than famous sights.有名な名所ではなく、雰囲気が魅力の場所もあります。
Food, weather, people, or pace may shape the memory.食べ物、天気、人、過ごす速さが思い出を作ります。
Returning with different people can create a different trip.違う人と戻ると、別の旅行になることがあります。
Returning alone can reveal what belonged to the place itself.一人で戻ると、その場所自体の魅力が分かることがあります。
Change may improve the place or remove what made it special.変化によって場所がよくなることも、特別さが失われることもあります。
Memory can make a return impossible to judge fairly.思い出のため、再訪を公平に判断できないこともあります。
A good return does not have to copy the first visit.よい再訪は、最初の旅行と同じである必要はありません。
The strongest reason to return is often one unfinished curiosity.また行きたい一番の理由は、まだ知りたいことがあるからです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Worth revisiting (再訪したい場所)

WARM-UP 1Which place would you happily return to, even if the second visit felt different?二回目の訪問で感じ方が違っても、喜んで戻りたい場所はどこですか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a restaurant, park, or city?
  • What would you do first there?

Worth a second visit (もう一度行く価値)

WARM-UP 2What can a second visit reveal that the first visit misses?最初の訪問では気づかない、二回目だから分かることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Does the place need to change?
  • Would a favourite restaurant or park be worth a quick return?

The place or your past self (場所か昔の自分か)

QUESTION 1When you miss a place, do you miss the place itself or who you were then?ある場所が恋しいとき、恋しいのはその場所ですか、それともそこにいた頃の自分ですか？

Follow up
  • What detail brings back the old feeling?
  • Would it feel the same if you returned now?

Return alone or together (一人か誰かと再訪するか)

QUESTION 2For a return visit, would you rather go alone or share it with someone?再訪するなら、一人で行くのと誰かと一緒に行くのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • What would you want to do first on the return visit?
  • Would you rather revisit a restaurant or a park?

Would it be the same? (同じかどうか)

QUESTION 3What might change more on a return visit: the place or the visitor?再訪でより大きく変わっているのは、場所と訪れる人のどちらでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Which change matters most?
  • Would the place feel more different after one year or ten years?

The first thing to do (最初にすること)

QUESTION 4What is worth repeating on a return visit?再訪したとき、もう一度する価値があることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would you repeat a meal, walk, or photo?
  • What might feel different the second time?

Memory versus reality (記憶と現実)

QUESTION 5How can you enjoy a return visit when the place has changed?場所が変わっていても、どうすれば再訪を楽しめますか？

Follow up
  • What expectation could ruin the visit?
  • Would trying one new thing help?

A feeling to bring home (日常に持ち帰りたい気持ち)

QUESTION 6What part of a favourite place would improve your ordinary week?お気に入りの場所のどんな部分が、普段の一週間をよりよくしてくれますか？

Follow up
  • Would you bring home its food, pace, or atmosphere?
  • How could you add a small part of it at home?
