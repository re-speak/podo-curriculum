Why some teachers remain memorable (記憶に残る先生の理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A memorable teacher is not always the kindest or most popular one.記憶に残る先生が、いつも最も優しく人気があるとは限りません。
Students may remember a teacher's voice, rules, humour, or patience.生徒は先生の声、規則、ユーモア、忍耐強さを覚えていることがあります。
Strictness can feel frightening at the time and fair later.厳しさは当時怖く感じても、後で公平だったと思えることがあります。
It can also remain unfair, especially when it humiliates a student.一方、生徒に恥をかかせる厳しさは、不公平なまま残ることもあります。
A small act of attention can matter more than a dramatic speech.小さな気づかいが、大げさな言葉より大切なこともあります。
A teacher may notice an ability before the student does.先生が、生徒本人より先に能力に気づくこともあります。
Lessons about effort or fairness may last longer than subject knowledge.努力や公平さについての教えは、教科の知識より長く残ることがあります。
Students also remember teachers through stories shared by classmates.生徒は、同級生と共有した話を通して先生を覚えていることもあります。
Time can soften fear without making every action acceptable.時間が恐怖を和らげても、すべての行動が許されるわけではありません。
Remembering a teacher can clarify what good teaching means to us.先生を思い出すと、自分にとってよい指導とは何かが分かります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A memorable teacher (記憶に残る先生)

WARM-UP 1Which teacher—real or fictional—stands out to you most, and why?実在でも作品の中でも、特に印象に残る先生は誰ですか、なぜですか？

Follow up
  • What makes that teacher memorable?
  • What kind of student did they teach?

Who they were (どんな先生か)

WARM-UP 2What did that teacher teach, or what role did they have?その先生は何を教えていましたか、それとも、どんな役割でしたか？

Follow up
  • About how old was the student?
  • What was the setting like?

One clear moment (一つの場面)

QUESTION 1What did the teacher do that stands out?その先生のどんな行動が強く印象に残っていますか？

Follow up
  • What happened right before it?
  • How did the student respond?

Fear or gratitude (怖さと感謝)

QUESTION 2Did the student feel scared, grateful, both, or neither?その生徒は怖さ、感謝、その両方、またはどちらでもない気持ちでしたか？

Follow up
  • How did that feeling change over time?
  • Which moment best explains the feeling?

Beyond the subject (教科以外の学び)

QUESTION 3What did the student learn from that teacher besides the subject?その生徒は教科以外に、その先生から何を学びましたか？

Follow up
  • Does that lesson still matter?
  • Was it taught directly or by example?

Looking back (振り返って)

QUESTION 4Looking back, does the teacher seem fairer, less fair, or unchanged?振り返ると、その先生はより公平に、より不公平に、それとも同じように見えますか？

Follow up
  • What changed in the later judgment?
  • What still feels the same?

What you would say (今伝えるなら)

QUESTION 5What would you want that teacher to know if you met today?今その先生に会うとしたら、何を知ってほしいですか？

Follow up
  • Would it be thanks, a question, or criticism?
  • How might the teacher respond?

A difficult teacher (苦手だった先生)

QUESTION 6What makes a teacher difficult to learn from?どんな先生だと学びにくいですか？

Follow up
  • Can strict teaching still work?
  • Which behaviour crosses a line?
