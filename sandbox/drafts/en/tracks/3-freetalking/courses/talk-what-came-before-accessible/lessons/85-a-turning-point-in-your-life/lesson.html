How a turning point becomes visible (転機が見えるまで)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A turning point changes which future becomes possible.転機は、どんな将来が可能かを変えます。
The moment may look ordinary while it is happening.その瞬間は、起きている間は普通に見えることがあります。
A decision, accident, meeting, loss, or opportunity can redirect a life.決断、事故、出会い、喪失、機会が人生を変えることがあります。
Some changes are chosen, while others require adaptation.自分で選ぶ変化もあれば、対応を求められる変化もあります。
Other people may influence the choice without deciding it.ほかの人が、決めるのではなく選択に影響することもあります。
The alternative path is always partly imagined.別の道は、いつも一部は想像です。
Luck and decision often work together.運と決断は、しばしば一緒に働きます。
A positive outcome does not make the transition easy.よい結果でも、変化が簡単だったとは限りません。
A difficult outcome does not prove the original choice was foolish.難しい結果でも、最初の選択が愚かだったとは限りません。
Naming a turning point helps organize a complicated story.転機と名付けると、複雑な話を整理できます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A small choice, a new direction (小さな選択、新しい方向)

WARM-UP 1Think of a small choice that changed someone's life. What happened?誰かの人生を変えた小さな選択を考えてください。何が起きましたか？

Follow up
  • What changed afterward?
  • Could accepting one invitation change a life?

An ordinary turning point (日常の転機)

WARM-UP 2What's an ordinary decision that can change a person's future?人の将来を変えることのある普通の決断は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why can that decision matter so much?
  • Does the result appear quickly or slowly?

Noticing a turning point (転機に気づくこと)

QUESTION 1Why are turning points hard to notice while they happen?転機は、起きている最中には気づきにくいのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • What can make an ordinary moment matter later?
  • Can a new class or one invitation become a turning point?

A small opportunity (小さな機会)

QUESTION 2What small opportunity can open a completely new path?全く新しい道を開くことのある小さな機会は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why might someone almost miss it?
  • Could a class, trip, or new friendship open a new path?

Someone who changes a life (人生を変える人)

QUESTION 3Who can quietly change the direction of another person's life?誰が、ほかの人の人生の方向を静かに変えることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Could a friend, teacher, or stranger do it?
  • Would they know they changed the person's life?

The role of luck (運が果たす役割)

QUESTION 4How much of a turning point comes from luck?転機のどのくらいが運によるものですか？

Follow up
  • What can the person control?
  • Would meeting the right person be luck or choice?

Changing direction later (後で方向を変えること)

QUESTION 5When is it worth changing direction after years on one path?何年も一つの道を進んだ後でも、方向を変える価値があるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What makes staying feel safer?
  • Would boredom or a new opportunity be enough reason to change?

Time for a new direction (新しい方向へ進むとき)

QUESTION 6How can someone tell that it may be time for a new direction?新しい方向へ進む時期かもしれないと、どう判断できますか？

Follow up
  • Who might notice first?
  • Would one bad week be enough reason to change?
