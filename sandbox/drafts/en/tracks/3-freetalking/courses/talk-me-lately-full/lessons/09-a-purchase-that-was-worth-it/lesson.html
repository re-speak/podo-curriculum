A purchase that was worth it (買ってよかったもの)

Tell the story of a purchase and explain why it was worth the money.買い物の話をして、お金を出す価値があった理由を説明しましょう。

Describe what you bought買ったものを説明する
Explain the decision買うと決めた理由を話す
Judge its real value実際の価値を考える

What makes a purchase worth it? (買い物の価値)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A purchase can feel worthwhile for reasons that have little to do with its original price.買い物に価値を感じる理由は、最初の値段とはあまり関係がないことがあります。
Something used every day may have a low cost per use, even when it was expensive.高価でも毎日使うものなら、一回あたりの費用は低くなるかもしれません。cost per use一回あたりの費用
A product can also earn its place by solving a small but recurring problem.商品は、小さいけれど繰り返し起きる問題を解決することで価値を持つこともあります。
The excitement of buying something often fades, while genuine usefulness remains.何かを買ったときの興奮は薄れがちですが、本当の使いやすさは残ります。
Quality may mean durability, comfort, saved time, or simply fewer daily frustrations.品質とは、耐久性、快適さ、時間の節約、日々の小さなストレスの減少を意味することがあります。
A high price does not prove value, but reliable performance can justify paying more.高い値段だけで価値は決まりませんが、安定した性能なら多く払う理由になります。
Subscriptions, maintenance, storage, and accessories can become hidden costs after the purchase.定期契約、手入れ、保管、付属品は、購入後の見えにくい追加費用になることがあります。hidden costs見えにくい追加費用
Timing matters too: the right product bought at the wrong moment can still create regret.タイミングも重要で、よい商品でも悪い時期に買えば後悔につながることがあります。
Regretted purchases often begin with an impulse or an expectation the product cannot meet.後悔する買い物は、衝動や、商品では満たせない期待から始まることがよくあります。
Repair options and resale value can extend a product's useful life.修理の選択肢や再販価値は、商品の役立つ期間を延ばすことができます。resale value再販価値
Some purchases matter because they create convenience; others carry a memory or a sense of confidence.便利さを生む買い物もあれば、思い出や自信につながる買い物もあります。
The thing someone calls their best purchase often reveals what they want more of in daily life.一番よかった買い物には、その人が日常生活で何をもっと求めているかが表れます。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答える必要はありません。一つの答えが深くなったら、それが今日の成功です。

Your best buy (買ってよかったもの)

WARM-UP 1What's the best thing you've bought this year?今年買ったものの中で、一番よかったものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What do you use it for?
  • How quickly did you know it was a good purchase?
  • What did you use before you bought it?

The purchase (買ったとき)

WARM-UP 2When did you buy it?いつ買いましたか？

Follow up
  • Where did you buy it?
  • Were you already looking for it, or did you find it unexpectedly?
  • What do you remember about choosing it?

The decision (決めた理由)

QUESTION 1What made you decide to buy it?何がきっかけで買うことにしましたか？

Follow up
  • What problem were you trying to solve?
  • Which other option did you consider?
  • What finally convinced you?

In your routine (日常の中で)

QUESTION 2How has the purchase changed your daily routine?その買い物で、日常の習慣はどう変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • How often do you use it now?
  • What takes less time or effort because of it?
  • Who else has noticed the change?

Price and value (値段と価値)

QUESTION 3How much did the price influence your decision?値段は購入の決断にどのくらい影響しましたか？

Follow up
  • Did it feel expensive at the time?
  • Would a cheaper version have been enough?
  • What cost did you avoid because you bought it?

A regret (後悔した買い物)

QUESTION 4Tell me about something you bought that was not worth the money.お金を出す価値がなかったと思う買い物について教えてください。

Follow up
  • What expectation did it fail to meet?
  • Did you return, sell, or keep it?
  • What did that purchase teach you?

The limit (価値がなくなる条件)

QUESTION 5What change would make your best purchase stop feeling worth it?どんな変化があれば、その一番よかった買い物に価値を感じなくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Would you keep it if it became less reliable?
  • How much extra cost would be too much?
  • Could another product replace it now?

Buy it again? (もう一度買う？)

QUESTION 6How would you decide whether to buy the same thing again today?今日、同じものをもう一度買うかどうか、どのように決めますか？

Follow up
  • Who would you recommend it to?
  • Who would probably not need it?
  • What would you check before buying the next version?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。
