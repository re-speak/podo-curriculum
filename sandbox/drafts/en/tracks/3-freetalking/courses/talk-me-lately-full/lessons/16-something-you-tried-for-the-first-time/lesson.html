What first times teach us (初めての体験が教えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Daily routines create useful stability, but they can gradually narrow the range of experiences we consider.日課は生活を楽にしますが、自分の世界を小さくすることもあります。
A first attempt forces us to pay attention because familiar shortcuts no longer work.初めての挑戦では、慣れたやり方が通用しないため、もう一度よく注意することになります。
Before a new experience, we build expectations from other people's stories and incomplete information.新しい体験の前には、人の話や不完全な情報から予想を作ります。
Nervousness does not always signal danger; it can show that the outcome matters to us.緊張は必ずしも危険のサインではなく、結果が自分にとって大切だという表れの場合もあります。
Clear guidance or a supportive companion can reduce the uncertainty of a first attempt.分かりやすい説明や支えてくれる人がいると、最初の一歩が楽になります。
The actual experience often differs from our imagined version in both easier and harder ways.実際の体験は、想像より楽な点も難しい点もあることがよくあります。
Feeling like a beginner can be uncomfortable when we are used to being capable in daily life.普段はできることが多い人にとって、初心者の感覚は居心地が悪いことがあります。beginner初心者
A second attempt reveals whether the activity has lasting value or was exciting only because it was novel.二度目に試すと、その活動に長く続く価値があるのか、新しいから楽しかっただけなのかが分かります。
Remembering recent firsts reminds us that personal growth does not end at a particular age.最近の初めてを思い出すと、成長は特定の年齢で終わるわけではないと分かります。
One successful first experience often expands the range of things we are willing to try next.初めての体験がうまくいくと、次に挑戦してみようと思えることの幅が広がります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

A recent first (最近の初めて)

WARM-UP 1Have you tried anything for the first time recently?最近、初めて試したことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, when did you do it?
  • If not, what counts as a recent first for you?

Feeling new (初めての感覚)

WARM-UP 2When did you last feel like a beginner?最後に初心者のように感じたのはいつですか？

Follow up
  • What made the situation unfamiliar?
  • How did you respond to that feeling?

Why people try (試す理由)

QUESTION 1What can finally persuade someone to try something new?人がついに新しいことを試すきっかけは何だと思いますか？

Follow up
  • How much does encouragement matter?
  • Can a long delay make the first step harder?

Expectation and reality (予想と実際)

QUESTION 2How closely do new experiences usually match your expectations?新しい体験は、普段、予想どおりですか？

Follow up
  • What tends to be easier than expected?
  • What tends to be more difficult?

Trying again (もう一度)

QUESTION 3What makes a first experience worth repeating?もう一度やってみたいと思うのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What would you change the second time?
  • How might a companion change it?

The first before that (その前の初めて)

QUESTION 4Can you remember another first-time experience?ほかの初めての体験を思い出せますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, how was it different?
  • If not, why are first times hard to remember?

Staying a beginner (初心者でいること)

QUESTION 5How do you feel when you're not good at something yet?まだ上手にできないとき、どんな気持ちになりますか？

Follow up
  • What helps you continue?
  • When do you decide to stop?

The next first (次の初めて)

QUESTION 6Is there anything you'd like to try next?次にやってみたいことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what is stopping you now?
  • If not, what makes your current routine work well?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
