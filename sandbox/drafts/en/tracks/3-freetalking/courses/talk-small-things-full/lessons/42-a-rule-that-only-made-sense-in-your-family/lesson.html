Every home makes a small culture (どの家庭にも小さな文化がある)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Every household develops rules that appear self-evident to the people living within it.どの家庭にも、そこで暮らす人には自明に見える決まりが生まれます。
Some rules respond to a practical constraint that is invisible outside the household.家庭の外からは見えない実際的な制約に対応する決まりもあります。
Other rules survive as habits long after their original purpose has vanished.別の決まりは、最初の目的が消えたあとも習慣として残ります。
Children naturally mistake household customs for universal rules.子どもは家庭の習慣を、誰にでも共通する決まりだと思い込みがちです。
One visit, meal, or sleepover can suddenly expose how local a familiar rule is.一度の訪問や食事、泊まりで、慣れた決まりがどれほどその家だけのものか突然分かります。
Discovering the difference can produce amusement, embarrassment, defensiveness, or relief.違いに気づくと、面白さ、恥ずかしさ、反発、安心などが生まれることがあります。
We may reproduce a household rule automatically even after rejecting the logic behind it.背景の理屈を否定したあとでも、家庭の決まりを無意識に再現することがあります。
A rule that seems bizarre elsewhere may become entirely reasonable once its context is known.ほかでは奇妙に見える決まりでも、その背景が分かれば完全に合理的になることがあります。
Carrying a rule into the next household is a deliberate choice rather than an inherited obligation.決まりを次の家庭へ持ち込むことは、受け継いだ義務ではなく、意識的な選択です。inherited受け継いだ
The rules retold years later become part of the story a family tells about itself.何年後も語られる決まりは、その家族が自分たちについて語る物語の一部になります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

A household rule (家庭の決まり)

WARM-UP 1What was normal in your house that you found out wasn't normal anywhere else?自分の家では普通だったのに、ほかでは普通でないと知ったことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, what was the rule?
  • If none does, what rule from another household surprised you?

How it worked (決まりの内容)

WARM-UP 2How did that real or borrowed rule work?自分の家庭、またはほかの家庭で知ったその決まりは、どのような内容でしたか？

Follow up
  • Who was expected to follow it?
  • What happened if someone forgot?

When you found out (気づいた時)

QUESTION 1When did you discover that other households worked differently?ほかの家庭は違うと、いつ気づきましたか？

Follow up
  • If there was a moment, what happened?
  • If there was not, how did you gradually learn the difference?

Your reaction (自分の反応)

QUESTION 2How did you react to the difference?その違いにどう反応しましたか？

Follow up
  • Did the rule seem strange at first?
  • What explanation changed your view?

Do you still do it? (今もする？)

QUESTION 3Do you follow any part of that rule?その決まりの一部に従っていますか？

Follow up
  • If you do, what makes sense about it?
  • If you do not, did you stop—or have you never followed it?

Another home's rule (ほかの家の決まり)

QUESTION 4Which rule in another household has struck you as strange?ほかの家庭の決まりで、変わっていると感じたものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, what context might explain it?
  • If none does, which area of home life probably varies most?

Carry it forward (受け継ぐ？)

QUESTION 5Which household rule would you choose to carry forward?どの家庭の決まりを、これからも受け継ぎたいですか？

Follow up
  • Why is it worth keeping?
  • How would you explain it to a new person?

Known for (覚えられる決まり)

QUESTION 6What small rule should define a household?家庭は、どのような小さな決まりで知られるとよいですか？

Follow up
  • What value would the rule express?
  • How could it stay flexible?
