Our real stress habits (本当のストレス習慣)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Advice about stress is usually much easier to offer than to apply under pressure.ストレスへの助言は、重圧の中で実行するよりも、人に伝えるほうがはるかに簡単です。
Under pressure, we default to familiar behaviours that demand almost no decision-making.重圧がかかると、判断をほとんど必要としない慣れた行動に頼りがちです。
A coping response may provide immediate relief while quietly creating a later cost.ストレスへの対処は、すぐ楽にしてくれる一方で、後の負担をひそかに生むことがあります。coping responseストレスへの対処
We may procrastinate, snack, clean obsessively, vent, withdraw, or overwork.先延ばしにしたり、間食したり、過度に掃除したり、不満を吐き出したり、引きこもったり、働きすぎたりします。procrastinate先延ばしにする
The same behaviour can regulate stress in one context and deepen avoidance in another.同じ行動でも、ある状況ではストレスを整え、別の状況では回避を深めることがあります。
We sometimes report the healthy response we admire rather than the behaviour we actually repeat.実際に繰り返す行動ではなく、自分が理想とする健康的な反応を話してしまうことがあります。
Naming the unflattering truth makes the actual pattern visible enough to change.格好のよくない事実を言葉にすると、本当のパターンが変えられるほどはっきり見えてきます。
A modest substitute is usually more sustainable than an idealized stress-management routine.理想化されたストレス管理の習慣より、控えめな代替行動のほうが続けやすいものです。
Even retired coping habits can reappear when pressure becomes unusually intense.やめたはずの対処習慣でも、重圧が特に強くなると再び現れることがあります。
Improvement may mean recognizing the pattern sooner rather than eliminating it forever.改善とは、その習慣を永遠になくすことではなく、より早くパターンに気づくことかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The honest answer (正直な答え)

WARM-UP 1What do you really do when you're stressed—not what you should do?ストレスがあるとき、するべきことではなく、実際には何をしますか？

Follow up
  • What appears first in your behaviour?
  • What helps you stay steady?

When it appears (現れやすい時)

WARM-UP 2When does that reaction show up most often?その反応は、どのようなときにいちばん現れやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What level of stress brings it out?
  • When does the pattern stay quiet?

Help and cost (助けと代償)

QUESTION 1What does the habit help with, and what does it make worse?その習慣は何の助けになり、何を悪化させますか？

Follow up
  • How long does the relief last?
  • What cost appears later?

What people say (人が話すこと)

QUESTION 2How accurately do people describe their own coping responses under stress?人は、ストレスがあるときにすることを、普段どのくらい正直に話すでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Which response sounds better than it really is?
  • Why might someone keep the real habit private?

Then and now (以前と今)

QUESTION 3Compare how you handle stress now with five years ago.今のストレスへの対処を、5年前と比べてください。

Follow up
  • Which part has improved?
  • Which part has stayed stubborn?

What makes it worse (悪化する条件)

QUESTION 4In which situation is your stress habit most counterproductive?どのような状況で、そのストレス習慣はいちばん役に立たなくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Which responsibility becomes harder?
  • Who else can be affected?

A realistic alternative (現実的な代わり)

QUESTION 5What would you realistically like to do instead?代わりに、現実的には何をしたいですか？

Follow up
  • What is the smallest possible version?
  • What would make it easier to choose?

Notice it sooner (早く気づく)

QUESTION 6What signal could help you recognize stress sooner?どのようなサインがあれば、もっと早くストレスに気づけますか？

Follow up
  • What could you do at that first sign?
  • Which sign is easiest to ignore?
