BALANCE GAME

Being rich and anonymous or respected on an ordinary income? (裕福で無名、それとも普通の収入で尊敬？)

Let's compare private wealth and respect on an ordinary income, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.無名で裕福な生活と普通の収入で得る尊敬を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Keeping wealth secret can make honest conversations about money harder.富を秘密にすると、お金について正直に話すことが難しくなる場合があります。
Respect based on a title can disappear faster than respect based on contribution.肩書きによる尊敬は、貢献による尊敬より早く失われることがあります。
Anonymity can protect privacy without removing responsibility.無名でいることは、責任をなくさずにプライバシーを守れます。anonymity無名でいること
Public recognition changes who can approach a person and how easily they can be left alone.人に知られると、近づいてくる人や、一人でいられる時間が変わります。
Wealth can create privacy and choice without creating trust or respect.富はプライバシーや選択肢を増やしますが、信頼や尊敬までは生みません。
Respect can feel meaningful while also creating pressure to keep meeting expectations.尊敬は意味を感じさせますが、期待に応え続ける圧力にもなります。
An ordinary income can still support a secure life when needs and public services are stable.必要なものや公共サービスが安定していれば、普通の収入でも安心して暮らせます。
Anonymity protects people from unwanted attention but can limit influence.無名でいると不要な注目を避けられますが、影響力は小さくなる場合があります。
Anonymous giving can help others without turning generosity into status.名前を出さずに人を助ければ、善意を地位に変えずにすみます。
Both money and reputation are fragile if a life depends on them alone.お金も評判も、それだけに人生を頼ると不安定です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Private pride (人に知られない誇り)

WARM-UP 1What can feel worth doing even when nobody knows?誰にも知られなくても、価値があると感じることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why is it worth doing without praise?
  • Which feels better: private pride or public praise?

Helping without credit (評価を求めない助け)

WARM-UP 2What's one good reason to help someone anonymously?名前を出さずに誰かを助けるよい理由は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who could benefit?
  • Would you donate anonymously if you could?

Wealth or respect (富か尊敬)

QUESTION 1Would you rather be rich and unknown or respected on an average income?裕福で無名の生活と、平均的な収入で尊敬される生活のどちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of that life matters most to you?
  • Would you prefer a quiet day alone or being thanked in public?

Keeping wealth private (富を秘密にする生活)

QUESTION 2What would an ordinary day look like if you were rich but kept your wealth private?裕福でもそのことを秘密にしていたら、普通の一日はどんな一日ですか？

Follow up
  • What would stay normal?
  • Would you tell one close person about the money?

Respected and judged (尊敬と評価)

QUESTION 3Would being respected still appeal if people constantly judged you?いつも人から評価されるなら、尊敬される生活は魅力的ですか？

Follow up
  • Which judgment would feel hardest?
  • Would public respect be worth losing some privacy?

Respect from strangers (知らない人からの尊敬)

QUESTION 4Would public respect matter if the people closest to you did not admire you?親しい人に尊敬されなくても、世間からの尊敬は大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Why might public respect still feel valuable?
  • Which matters more: respect from family or from strangers?

Privacy or recognition (プライバシーか評価)

QUESTION 5Which is harder to live without: privacy or recognition?プライバシーと人からの評価のうち、ないとより困るのはどちらですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that hard to live without?
  • Would you leave social media for a month to protect your privacy?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • Does your tutor value privacy or recognition more?
  • Would the choice change if fame meant no privacy?
