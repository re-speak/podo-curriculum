BALANCE GAME

Endless summer or endless winter? (終わらない夏、それとも冬？)

Let's compare endless summer and endless winter, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.終わらない夏と終わらない冬を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Humidity can make moderate heat feel harder than a higher dry temperature.湿度が高いと、それほど高くない気温でも厳しく感じることがあります。
Cold becomes more dangerous when wind, ice, or poor housing limits movement.寒さは、風、氷、住まいの状態で移動が難しくなると、より危険になります。
Seasonal extremes can make either option difficult rather than pleasant.季節の極端さによって、どちらも快適ではなく大変になることがあります。seasonal extremes季節の極端さ
One permanent season would also change food, water, plants, and local work.一つの季節が続けば、食べ物、水、植物、地域の仕事も変わります。
Extreme heat and extreme cold can both become health risks.厳しい暑さも寒さも、健康上の危険になることがあります。
Buildings and public transport change how difficult a season feels.建物や公共交通によって、季節の大変さは変わります。
Long summer days create more outdoor time, but they can also disturb sleep.夏の長い日は外で過ごす時間を増やしますが、睡眠を妨げることもあります。
Winter can offer quiet and snow activities while limiting movement.冬には静けさや雪の活動がありますが、移動が制限されることもあります。
Energy cost matters when one season never ends.一つの季節が続くなら、冷暖房のエネルギー費用が重要になります。
The best climate depends on what people must do, not only what weather they enjoy.よい気候は、好きな天気だけでなく、そこで何をする必要があるかで決まります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A difficult weather day (大変な天気)

WARM-UP 1What makes a very hot or very cold day difficult?暑い日や寒い日を大変にするものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Does travel make it worse?
  • Does sleep change?

Perfect weather (完璧な天気)

WARM-UP 2What would you do outside on a day with perfect weather?完璧な天気の日なら、外で何をしたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which outdoor place would you visit?
  • How many hours would you stay outside?

One season forever (一つの季節)

QUESTION 1If one season had to last all year, would you choose summer or winter?一つの季節が一年中続くなら、夏と冬のどちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which activity shapes your answer?
  • What is the best part of the other season?

Everyday life (毎日の生活)

QUESTION 2How would everyday life change if your season never ended?選んだ季節が終わらなければ、日常はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • What would happen to clothing?
  • What would happen to outdoor plans?

Perfect indoors (家の中は快適)

QUESTION 3Would your answer stay the same if every building and train were comfortable?建物や交通機関がいつも快適でも、答えは同じですか？

Follow up
  • Would daylight matter more?
  • Would energy cost still matter?

Hours of daylight (日の長さ)

QUESTION 4Would equal daylight in both seasons change your answer?夏と冬の日の長さが同じなら、選択は変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Does light affect your mood?
  • Does it affect your sleep?

When you switch (答えが変わる条件)

QUESTION 5What weather condition would make you switch seasons?どんな天候なら、反対の季節を選びますか？

Follow up
  • How long could you accept it?
  • What would you do in that weather?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor whether they would choose endless summer or endless winter, and why.チューターなら終わらない夏と終わらない冬のどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Follow up
  • Which weather burden matters most to your tutor?
  • Would better housing change their season?
