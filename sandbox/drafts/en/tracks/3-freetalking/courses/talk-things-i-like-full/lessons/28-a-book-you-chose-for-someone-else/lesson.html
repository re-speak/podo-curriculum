Choosing a book for someone (誰かのために本を選ぶこと)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Giving a book away requires a careful guess about another person's interests, time, and attention.本を贈るときは、相手の興味だけでなく、その本を読む時間や集中力まで慎重に考える必要があります。
A book that mattered deeply to us is not automatically appropriate for someone else.自分にとって大切な本が、必ずしも相手に合うとは限りません。
The most thoughtful choice often grows out of a conversation or concern the two people have shared.よい選択は、二人が以前に話したことや共有した関心から生まれることが多いです。
A brief personal note can explain the connection without telling the recipient how to read the book.短いメモなら、読み方を押しつけずに、なぜその人を思い出したか伝えられます。
Repeatedly asking whether the recipient finished the book can turn a gift into an obligation.読み終えたか何度も聞くと、贈り物が義務のようになることがあります。obligation義務
For some people, the recommendation itself is more valuable than receiving another physical object.人によっては、本という物より、おすすめそのもののほうがうれしい場合があります。
A well-chosen book can continue a conversation long after the moment of giving.よく選ばれた本は、贈ったあとも長く会話を続けてくれます。
Accepting that we may never know whether it was read is part of giving without conditions.読まれたか分からないことを受け入れるのも、条件をつけずに贈ることの一部です。
A book that helped us may reach another person through an entirely different idea or moment.自分を助けた本が、別の人にはまったく違う考えや瞬間を通して届くことがあります。
Choosing a book for someone reveals our view of that person as much as our own taste.誰かのために本を選ぶと、自分の好みだけでなく、その人をどう見ているかも表れます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

A book to give away (人に贈った本)

WARM-UP 1Have you ever bought a book to give away?誰かに贈るために本を買ったことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, did you write a note inside?
  • If not, which book would you most like to give someone?

A book you received (もらった本)

WARM-UP 2Has anyone ever chosen a book for you?誰かがあなたのために本を選んでくれたことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what did their choice say about you?
  • If not, who would understand your taste well enough?

The book and person (本と相手)

QUESTION 1Tell me about the book you chose or would choose for someone else.誰かのために選んだ本、または選びたい本について話してください。

Follow up
  • Who is the person?
  • What would be the occasion?

Did they read it? (読んでくれた？)

QUESTION 2If you gave the book, do you know whether they read it?その本を贈ったなら、相手が読んだか知っていますか？

Follow up
  • If they read it, what did they say?
  • If you have not given it, what response would show that your choice worked?

Why that book (その本を選んだ理由)

QUESTION 3Why does that book seem right for them?なぜその本が相手に合うと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Which part makes you think of them?
  • What other book could also fit?

What it did for you (自分への影響)

QUESTION 4What has the book meant to you, if you have read it?自分で読んだことがあるなら、その本は自分にとってどんな意味がありましたか？

Follow up
  • If you read it, which idea stayed with you?
  • If not, why do you still trust the recommendation?

Choosing well (よい選び方)

QUESTION 5What makes a book a good gift?どんな本がよい贈り物になりますか？

Follow up
  • How much should the giver explain?
  • When is a recommendation better than a gift?

A book for me (私に選ぶ一冊)

QUESTION 6Ask me what I enjoy reading, then choose a book for me.私がどんな本を読むのが好きか聞いてから、私に一冊選んでください。

Follow up
  • Why does it fit what I told you?
  • What would you ask after I read it?
