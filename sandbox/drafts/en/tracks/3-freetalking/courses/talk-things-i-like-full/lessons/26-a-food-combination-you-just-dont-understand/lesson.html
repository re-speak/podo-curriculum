When two foods meet (二つの食べ物が出会うとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A food pairing can feel completely ordinary in one culture and deeply strange in another.ある文化では普通の食べ合わせが、別の文化ではとても奇妙に感じられます。
Sweet and salty flavors often work together because contrast makes both more noticeable.甘さと塩気は、対比によって互いをはっきりさせるため、よく合います。
Texture can be just as decisive as flavor when two foods are brought together.二つの食べ物を合わせるとき、食感も味と同じくらい重要です。
A combination can sound worse than it tastes because its description creates resistance before the first bite.説明を聞いただけで、ひと口目の前に抵抗感が生まれ、実際の味より悪く聞こえる組み合わせもあります。resistance抵抗感
Trying one well-made version provides better evidence than judging the whole idea from a poor example.上手に作られた一つを試すほうが、よくない一例から全体を判断するより、よい根拠になります。
An acquired taste may become enjoyable only after familiarity replaces the initial surprise.慣れて好きになる味は、最初の驚きが薄れてから楽しめるようになります。
People often defend familiar combinations because they carry memory and identity as well as flavor.なじみのある組み合わせには、味だけでなく思い出や自分らしさも含まれています。
Refusal may come from smell, texture, or a specific ingredient rather than simple closed-mindedness.食べ物を断る理由は、偏見ではなく、匂いや食感、特定の材料かもしれません。
Trust in the person offering the food and the setting can change our willingness to experiment.すすめる人への信頼や場面によって、試す気持ちは変わります。
A fair food conversation makes room for genuine curiosity as well as a firm personal limit.公平な食べ物の会話では、好奇心にも、はっきり断ることにも余地があります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Pineapple on pizza (パイナップルのピザ)

WARM-UP 1Pineapple on pizza — yes or no?パイナップルのピザは、ありですか、なしですか？

Follow up
  • Would you eat one slice right now?
  • Would your answer change if someone else ordered it?

Have you tried it? (食べたことは？)

WARM-UP 2Have you actually tried pineapple on pizza?実際にパイナップルのピザを食べたことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what was the taste like?
  • If not, what do you imagine would be wrong?

Why your side (その立場の理由)

QUESTION 1What matters most in your answer: taste, texture, or the idea itself?答えを決めるうえで、味、食感、組み合わせのイメージのどれがいちばん大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which detail is hardest to accept?
  • What detail might work better than expected?

Your own combination (自分の組み合わせ)

QUESTION 2What food combination do you like that other people find strange?自分は好きでも、ほかの人が変だと思う食べ合わせは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, how did you first try it?
  • If none does, which unusual pairing would you still consider trying?

Someone else's choice (ほかの人の選択)

QUESTION 3What food pairing have you seen someone enjoy but could not watch?誰かが喜んで食べていても、自分には見ていられなかった食べ合わせは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, what exactly bothered you?
  • If none does, which pairing would be hardest to watch?

A changed mind (変わった好み)

QUESTION 4Is there a food combination you changed your mind about?考えが変わった食べ合わせはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what caused the change?
  • If not, which opinion has stayed strongest?

A firm no (絶対に無理)

QUESTION 5What food combination would you refuse to try?どんな食べ合わせなら、試すのを断りますか？

Follow up
  • Is the limit about one ingredient?
  • Could any setting change your answer?

One fair chance (一度だけ試すなら)

QUESTION 6Which strange combination would you give one fair chance?変わった食べ合わせを一つだけ公平に試すなら、何にしますか？

Follow up
  • Who would you trust to make it?
  • What would count as a successful first bite?
