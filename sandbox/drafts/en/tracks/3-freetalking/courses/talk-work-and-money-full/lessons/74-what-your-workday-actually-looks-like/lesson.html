The work behind the results (結果を支える仕事)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A carefully described two-hour window can expose the structure and interruptions of an ordinary routine.2時間を丁寧にたどると、普段の流れの構造や中断が見えてきます。
Visible output often depends on preparation, verification, coordination, maintenance, and recovery work.見える成果は、準備、検証、調整、保守、復旧の仕事に支えられることがよくあります。
Unpaid care, household administration, and emotional coordination often remain absent from formal accounts of work.無給のケア、家庭の事務、感情面の調整は、正式な仕事の記録から抜け落ちがちです。
Preventive work is least visible precisely when it succeeds and nothing goes wrong.予防の仕事は、成功して問題が起きないときほど見えにくくなります。preventive予防の
Monitoring, anticipating, waiting, and deciding not to intervene can all be consequential work.監視、予測、待機、介入しない判断も、結果に影響する仕事です。
A useful account can describe the type and purpose of hidden work without naming clients, colleagues, or sensitive facts.顧客、同僚、機密情報を明かさず、見えない仕事の種類と目的を説明できます。
Attempts to make hidden labor visible can fail if documentation becomes another unsupported burden.見えない仕事を可視化する試みも、記録が新たな無支援の負担になれば失敗します。
The least visible work varies across employment, study, care, volunteering, and household life.雇用、学習、ケア、ボランティア、家庭生活では、最も見えにくい仕事が異なります。
Continuity improves when pending checks, risks, and dependencies are communicated rather than left implicit.保留中の確認、リスク、依存関係を暗黙にせず伝えると、仕事の継続性が高まります。
A fuller account of labor may alter judgments about time, skill, staffing, and responsibility.仕事の全体像を知ると、時間、技能、人員、責任に対する評価が変わる場合があります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A two-hour window (二時間の様子)

WARM-UP 1Choose any recent two-hour window. Which activities filled it?最近の二時間を一つ選んでください。どんな活動がその時間を占めましたか？

Follow up
  • Which activity would have been visible to an observer?
  • What made that period typical or unusual?

Walk through the period (時間の流れ)

WARM-UP 2Which sequence best represents a typical two-hour period?よくある二時間を最もよく表す活動の流れは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which activity normally determines the sequence?
  • Which interruption most often changes the plan?

What takes longest (最も時間がかかること)

QUESTION 1Which activity consumes the largest share of a typical routine?よくある日課の中で、最も大きな時間を占める活動は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Does the duration come from execution, waiting, verification, or coordination?
  • Would the reason be visible to an outsider?

A part you value (大切に思う部分)

QUESTION 2Which part of a routine is most likely to feel satisfying, frustrating, or neutral?日課のどの部分が、満足、不満、または無感情に感じられやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Which feature of the activity produces that response?
  • Would a modest change alter the experience?

What would surprise an outsider (外からは意外なこと)

QUESTION 3Which preparation, verification, care, or coordination happens behind the scenes and would surprise an outsider?表に見えないところで、外の人が驚きそうな準備、検証、ケア、調整は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which feature keeps the work out of view?
  • Which problem does that hidden work prevent?

How the routine changed (変わった日課)

QUESTION 4How do ordinary work, study, or care routines evolve over time?仕事、勉強、ケアの普通の日課は、時間とともにどう変化しますか？

Follow up
  • Which activity or responsibility tends to shift most?
  • Which feature remains relatively stable?

What to remove (減らしたいこと)

QUESTION 5Which routine element should be removed, reduced, redesigned, or deliberately retained?日課のどの要素をなくす、減らす、設計し直す、または意識して残すべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which outcome would improve?
  • Which necessary function or protection already works?

See the whole day (一日全体を見る)

QUESTION 6Which question would give an outsider a fairer account of the visible and hidden work in this routine?外の人が、この日課の見える仕事と見えない仕事をより公平に理解するには、どんな質問が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Whose perspective would be most informative?
  • Which mistaken assumption might the answer correct?
