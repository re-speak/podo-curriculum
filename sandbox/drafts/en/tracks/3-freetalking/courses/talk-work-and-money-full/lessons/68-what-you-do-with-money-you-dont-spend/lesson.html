WORK AND MONEY

What you do with money you don't spend (使わなかったお金でしていること)

Let's talk about what money can do besides pay for things. Please read the title aloud.物を買う以外に、お金にできることについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Money can serve more than one purpose (お金には複数の役割がある)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Beyond immediate spending, money may be reserved, shared, invested, used to reduce debt, or kept liquid.すぐに使う以外にも、お金は貯蓄、分配、投資、債務の削減、流動性の確保に充てられます。
Any discussion of financial choices must acknowledge that essential costs may leave no surplus.金銭的な選択を話す際は、必要経費のあとに余裕がない場合も認める必要があります。surplus余裕資金
People may set aside even a modest reserve for flexibility when an expected or unexpected need appears.少額でも備えとして取っておくと、予想内外の必要が生じたときに選択の余地が生まれます。
Financial support may reflect generosity, obligation, reciprocity, or collective responsibility.金銭的な支援は、寛大さ、義務、互恵、共同責任を示すことがあります。
No single allocation is right for every income level, household, risk, or stage of life.どの収入、世帯、リスク、人生段階にも当てはまる配分はありません。
Comparing financial choices by purpose and trade-off can be more informative than comparing balances.残高を比べるより、目的とトレードオフでお金の選択を比べるほうが有益なことがあります。
Financial guidance can become harmful when it ignores constraints, risk tolerance, or local rules.制約、リスクの許容度、地域の規則を無視した金融助言は有害になり得ます。
Allocating a fixed amount across competing goals can expose priorities and opportunity costs.限られた金額を複数の目的に配分すると、優先順位と機会費用が見えてきます。
A change in dependants, work, health, or security can reorder financial priorities.扶養、仕事、健康、安心の変化によって、金銭的な優先順位が入れ替わる場合があります。
A financial action is best understood through the need or value it is meant to serve.金銭的な行動は、それが支えようとする必要や価値から理解するのが適切です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Beyond spending (使う以外)

WARM-UP 1Which purposes can money serve besides immediate spending?目の前で使う以外に、お金はどんな目的に使えますか？

Follow up
  • Is that purpose regular or occasional?
  • Which long-term option would carry the greatest value?

One purpose (一つの目的)

WARM-UP 2Which non-spending purpose deserves the highest priority?使う以外の目的で、最も優先すべきなのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which need or value does it serve?
  • Is its benefit immediate, future, or both?

When it began (始めた時期)

QUESTION 1Which condition most often makes setting money aside realistic?お金を取っておくことを現実的にする条件は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which event or need can prompt the first step?
  • Which condition makes it sustainable rather than temporary?

How it is going (今の状況)

QUESTION 2What determines whether a financial approach remains viable?お金の扱い方が実行可能であり続けるかを決めるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which condition has supported it?
  • Which constraint has limited or prevented it?

Where the idea came from (考えのきっかけ)

QUESTION 3Which influence most strongly shapes how people allocate money they do not spend?人が使わないお金をどう配分するかに、最も強く影響するのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of that influence is useful?
  • How has the resulting approach evolved?

Would it suit someone else (人に合うか)

QUESTION 4Which financial practices should be recommended only conditionally?どの金融行動は、条件付きでのみ勧めるべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which circumstances make it appropriate?
  • Which constraint or risk rules it out?

A financial boundary (しないこと)

QUESTION 5Which financial action should require the strictest safeguards?どの金融行動に、最も厳しい保護策が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which risk, duty, or value justifies the boundary?
  • How can the principle be explained without disclosing private finances?

More than money (お金以外)

QUESTION 6Which valuable form of support cannot be reduced to a financial transfer?金銭の受け渡しだけでは表せない大切な支援は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What distinctive need does that support meet?
  • Can financial resources still help make it possible?
