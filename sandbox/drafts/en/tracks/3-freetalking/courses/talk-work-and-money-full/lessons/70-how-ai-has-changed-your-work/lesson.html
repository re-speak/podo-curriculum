How AI has changed your work (AIが仕事を変えたこと)

Let's talk about how AI changes tasks and responsibility. Please read the title aloud.AIが作業と責任をどう変えるかについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

A tool matters when a routine actually changes (道具の意味は習慣の変化に表れる)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Access, relevance, preference, and policy mean that AI has not altered every person's routine.利用環境、必要性、好み、規則によって、AIが全員の日常を変えたわけではありません。
Evaluating one concrete task is more informative than making a broad claim about AI.AIについて広く主張するより、具体的な作業一つを評価するほうが有益です。
Time saved has value only when quality, judgment, privacy, and responsibility remain acceptable.時間短縮の価値は、質、判断、プライバシー、責任が保たれて初めて成り立ちます。
Relevant use may occur in employment, education, domestic administration, accessibility, or creative activity.AIの活用は、雇用、教育、家庭の事務、アクセシビリティ、創作などに見られます。
When people hand over part of a task to AI, they still need to review consequential output.作業の一部をAIに任せても、重要な出力を確認する責任は残ります。
Confidential, identifying, or sensitive material may require strict limits or complete exclusion.機密情報、個人を特定する情報、センシティブな内容には、厳しい制限や完全な除外が必要な場合があります。
Verification, correction, prompting, and policy compliance can offset some apparent efficiency.確認、修正、指示作成、規則への対応によって、見かけの効率が相殺される場合があります。
Even without direct use, AI can change a routine through institutions, colleagues, products, and public services.AIを直接使わなくても、組織、同僚、製品、公共サービスを通じて日常が変化することがあります。
As tools take over execution, human attention may shift toward framing, verification, and accountability.道具が実行を担うと、人の注意は問題設定、検証、説明責任へ移ることがあります。accountability説明責任
Rejecting automation can be reasonable when human contact, learning, authorship, or trust is central.人との関わり、学習、作者性、信頼が中心なら、自動化しない選択も合理的です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One changed task (変わった作業)

WARM-UP 1What's one task AI has changed—or could change—in your daily life?AIによって変わった、または変わりそうな日常の作業を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • What part would change first?
  • If none comes to mind, would planning or writing be easier to imagine?

Before AI (AIを使う前)

WARM-UP 2Think of one task people now do with AI. How did they do it before?今はAIを使う作業を一つ考えてください。以前はどうしていましたか？

Follow up
  • Which step became shorter?
  • What still takes the same effort?

Speed or quality (速さか質か)

QUESTION 1For a task you know, is AI more useful for speed or quality?知っている作業では、AIは速さと質のどちらに役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What result shows that?
  • What if it improves neither?

Work it created (増えた作業)

QUESTION 2What new checking or correction work can AI create?AIはどんな検証や修正の作業を生み出すことがありますか？

Follow up
  • Who is responsible for checking or fixing the output?
  • When would using AI create more checking than it saves?

A clear boundary (明確な境界)

QUESTION 3What task, decision, or information should never be handed to AI?この状況で、どの作業、判断、情報をAIの利用範囲外にすべきですか？

Follow up
  • What makes that task too risky for AI?
  • Which is most sensitive: a password, a medical record, or a hiring decision?

Who remains responsible (残る責任)

QUESTION 4Who should be responsible for checking AI-assisted work?AIを使った仕事の確認責任は、誰が持ち続けるべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which claim in the result needs checking first?
  • Which final choice should a person make?

A skill to protect (守りたい人の力)

QUESTION 5Which human skill is most at risk when people rely heavily on AI?AIに大きく頼るとき、どの人間の力が最も失われやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What would people lose if the skill weakened?
  • How could schools or workplaces protect it?

A useful trial (試す価値のある使い方)

QUESTION 6What's one small, low-risk way to test an AI tool?AIツールを試す、小さくてリスクの低い方法は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What clear result would count as success?
  • Which personal or company information must stay out?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
