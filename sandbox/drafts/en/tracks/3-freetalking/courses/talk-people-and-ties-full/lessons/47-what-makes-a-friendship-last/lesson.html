What keeps a friendship going (友情を続けるもの)

Long friendships endure without remaining static.長い友情は、変わらないからではなく、変化しながら続いていきます。static変化しない
Jobs, homes, routines, and priorities can all shift over time.仕事、住む場所、生活習慣、優先事項は、時間とともに変化します。
A friendship often lasts because both people allow the relationship to adapt.双方が関係の変化を受け入れることで、友情は続きやすくなります。
Regular contact can help, yet frequency alone does not define closeness.定期的な連絡は役立ちますが、頻度だけで親しさが決まるわけではありません。
Some friends resume an easy connection even after months of little contact.何か月もほとんど連絡しなくても、自然に関係を再開できる友人もいます。
Shared history provides context that a newer relationship cannot yet offer.共有してきた歴史は、新しい関係にはまだない理解を与えてくれます。
History alone cannot sustain a friendship when attention and care become one-sided.ただし、関心や思いやりが一方通行になれば、共有した歴史だけでは友情を支えられません。
Clear boundaries can protect a friendship from unspoken resentment.明確な境界線は、口に出せない不満から友情を守ることがあります。resentment言えない不満
People can drift apart because their lives diverge, without either person being at fault.どちらにも非がなくても、生活の方向が分かれて疎遠になることがあります。
A durable friendship depends on what both people continue to contribute.長く続く友情は、双方が関係に与え続けるものによって支えられます。

8 QUESTION POOL

A lasting friend (長く続く友人)

WARM-UP 1Think of a friend you've kept for a long time. What are they like?長く付き合っている友人を思い浮かべてください。どのような人ですか？

Follow up
  • If someone comes to mind, which quality do you value most?
  • If nobody does, which quality would matter most in a long-term friend?

How you met (出会い)

WARM-UP 2How did you meet—or how might a friendship like that begin?どのように出会いましたか、または、そのような友情はどのように始まりそうですか？

Follow up
  • For a real friendship, what first gave you something to discuss?
  • For an imagined one, what might begin to create closeness?

Time together (付き合いの長さ)

QUESTION 1How long have you known each other—or what duration would count as a long friendship to you?知り合ってどのくらいですか、または、自分にとってどのくらいなら長い友情だと言えますか？

Follow up
  • For a real friendship, how has the relationship adapted over time?
  • For an imagined one, what would need to remain familiar?

What keeps it going (続く理由)

QUESTION 2What sustains the real friendship—or what would sustain an imagined one?実際の友情は何によって続いていますか、または、想像した友情なら何が支えになりそうですか？

Follow up
  • How do both people demonstrate care?
  • How does the relationship survive busy periods?

A deal-breaker (受け入れられないこと)

QUESTION 3What behaviour could you not tolerate in a friendship?友情の中で、どのような行動なら受け入れられませんか？

Follow up
  • Why would that cross a boundary?
  • Could a sincere apology repair the damage?

Drifting apart (疎遠になる)

QUESTION 4Have you drifted apart from anyone—or observed it happen to others?誰かと疎遠になったこと、またはほかの人に起きたのを見たことはありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, which change mattered first?
  • If not, what commonly causes friends to diverge?

Starting again (関係を戻す)

QUESTION 5When is a friendship worth trying to revive?友情をもう一度よみがえらせる価値があるのは、どのようなときですか？

Follow up
  • Who should make the first move?
  • What would make renewed contact feel natural?

What you bring (自分が与えるもの)

QUESTION 6What do you contribute to a strong friendship?強い友情に、自分は何を与えていますか？

Follow up
  • How might a friend describe that contribution?
  • What would you still like to improve?
