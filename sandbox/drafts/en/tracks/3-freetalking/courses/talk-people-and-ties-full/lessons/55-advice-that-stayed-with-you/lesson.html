Why some advice stays (助言が心に残る理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Advice often endures because of its timing, not merely its wording.助言は言葉そのものだけでなく、聞いたタイミングによって残ります。
An ordinary sentence can acquire unusual force during a difficult decision.難しい決断の中では、普通の一言が特別な力を持つことがあります。
The source matters when we trust the speaker's experience, judgment, or care.相手の経験、判断、思いやりを信頼していると、その言葉は大切になります。
We may understand the literal message immediately while taking years to grasp its value.表面の意味はすぐ分かっても、その価値を理解するまで何年もかかることがあります。
Durable advice usually leaves room for context rather than pretending to be universal.長く役立つ助言は、すべてに当てはまるふりをせず、状況に合わせる余地があります。
Poor advice can sound decisive while ignoring cost, risk, power, or personality.悪い助言は断定的に聞こえても、費用、危険、力関係、性格を無視することがあります。
Advice can be sound even when the listener is not ready or the moment is wrong.聞く人に準備がない、または時期が違えば、よい助言でも従えないことがあります。
Following advice does not by itself prove that the advice produced the outcome.助言に従ったことだけでは、その助言が結果を生んだとは証明できません。
Passing advice on inevitably carries our own interpretation and omissions.助言を人に伝えるとき、自分の解釈や省いた部分も必ず一緒に伝わります。
A useful test is whether the advice clarifies a choice without taking ownership of it away.よい助言かどうかは、相手から決定権を奪わずに選択を明確にできるかで分かります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Words that stayed (残っている言葉)

WARM-UP 1Which advice has stayed with you—or which familiar example could you examine?心に残っている助言はありますか、または、どの身近な例を取り上げますか？

Follow up
  • If advice comes to mind, who gave it?
  • If none does, which familiar saying or general example could you examine?

The wording (言葉)

WARM-UP 2What was the original wording—or how would you formulate the idea now?元はどんな言葉でしたか、または、今ならその考えをどう表現しますか？

Follow up
  • Which phrase carries the central idea?
  • Would different wording alter its force?

The source (言った人)

QUESTION 1Who gave you the advice—or what was its source?誰からその助言をもらいましたか、または、出どころは何でしたか？

Follow up
  • Why did that source carry authority for you?
  • Would the same words work from a different person?

The moment (関係する場面)

QUESTION 2What was happening when the advice became relevant—or in which situation might it matter?その助言が関係するようになったとき、何が起きていましたか、または、どのような状況で関係しそうですか？

Follow up
  • For a real case, why was the timing significant?
  • For a familiar example, when might the advice be needed?

Understanding later (あとで分かること)

QUESTION 3Did you grasp advice you actually heard immediately—or when might a familiar example make sense in hindsight?自分が実際に聞いた助言はすぐ理解できましたか、または、身近な例ならいつ振り返って意味が分かりそうですか？

Follow up
  • For a real case, which experience changed your interpretation?
  • For a familiar example, what might become clearer with distance?

Did it help? (役に立つ？)

QUESTION 4In what way has the advice helped someone—or in which situation might it fail to help?その助言は人にどのように役立ちましたか、または、どのような状況では役立たない可能性がありますか？

Follow up
  • For a real case, which decision did it influence?
  • For a familiar example, where might its usefulness end?

Passing it on (人に伝える)

QUESTION 5Under what conditions, if any, would you pass on the advice?その助言を人に伝えるなら、どのような条件ですか、それとも、伝えませんか？

Follow up
  • Who might genuinely benefit—or might nobody benefit?
  • How would you adapt it—or what makes it unsuitable to pass on?

Advice ignored (従わなかった助言)

QUESTION 6Have you ever chosen not to follow advice—or can you examine a familiar example?助言に従わないと決めたことがありますか、または、身近な例を取り上げられますか？

Follow up
  • If it happened to you, which circumstance outweighed the advice?
  • If it is a familiar example, why might someone choose differently?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。
