How small talk opens a door (雑談が会話を始めるしくみ)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A conversation with a stranger begins under conditions of limited information.知らない人との会話は、相手についての情報が限られた状態で始まります。
A reliable opening usually draws on the immediate situation both people share.そのため、双方が共有する目の前の状況から始めると自然です。
A delay, event, menu, or seating arrangement can provide a low-risk conversation starter.待ち時間、行事、メニュー、座席などが、負担の少ない会話のきっかけになります。conversation starter会話のきっかけ
The best opening question is answerable without demanding private information.よい最初の質問は、私的な情報を求めずに答えられるものです。
The length and warmth of the reply signal whether the other person wants to continue.返事の長さや温かさから、相手が会話を続けたいか分かります。
A brief answer is not necessarily rejection; the person may be tired, distracted, or occupied.短い返事でも拒絶とは限らず、疲れていたり、気が散っていたり、用事があったりします。
One proportionate follow-up is usually enough to test whether there is mutual interest.お互いに話す気があるか確かめるには、ほどよい追加質問が一つあれば十分です。
If the exchange still stalls, a graceful ending respects both people's attention.それでも会話が止まるなら、自然に終えることで双方の時間を尊重できます。
Skilled conversationalists are often distinguished less by wit than by attention to signals.会話が上手な人は、機知よりも相手の反応への注意によって際立つことが多いです。
An opening that goes nowhere is ordinary and need not become an awkward performance.最初の一言が続かないのは普通で、気まずい失敗に広げる必要はありません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your opening (最初の一言)

WARM-UP 1You're seated beside someone you've never met. How would you open the conversation?会ったことのない人の隣に座っています。どのように会話を始めますか？

Follow up
  • Which shared detail would you use?
  • How would you keep the opening low-pressure?

The setting (場面)

WARM-UP 2Where would a simple opening with a stranger feel most natural?知らない人への簡単な声のかけ方が、最も自然に感じるのはどこですか？

Follow up
  • What can both people notice in that moment?
  • Where would it feel intrusive?

What to avoid (避けたい話題)

QUESTION 1Which topics would you avoid with a stranger?知らない人との会話では、どのような話題を避けますか？

Follow up
  • Why might the topic feel intrusive?
  • Could a different setting make it appropriate?

Easy to answer (答えやすさ)

QUESTION 2What makes a small talk question easy to answer?どんな雑談の質問なら答えやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Should it be general or specific?
  • How can the other person give a short answer?

A dead end (続かなかった会話)

QUESTION 3Which kind of opening makes a conversation die quickly?どのような最初の一言だと、会話がすぐ止まりますか？

Follow up
  • Is the problem the topic or the way it was said?
  • What simple question might save the conversation?

Reading the signals (反応を読む)

QUESTION 4How can you tell that a stranger wants to keep talking?どのような反応から、相手が本当に会話を続けたいと分かりますか？

Follow up
  • What can a brief answer mean?
  • Which sign suggests it is time to end the conversation politely?
