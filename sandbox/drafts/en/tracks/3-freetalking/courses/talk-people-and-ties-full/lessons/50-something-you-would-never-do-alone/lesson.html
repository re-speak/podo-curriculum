Where the solo line comes from (一人で行動する境界)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Solitude feels radically different depending on the activity.一人でいる感覚は、活動の種類によって大きく変わります。
Someone may dine alone comfortably yet find solo travel intimidating.一人での食事は平気でも、一人旅には不安を感じる人がいます。
The boundary often reflects safety, cost, unfamiliarity, and social expectations.その境界には、安全、費用、慣れのなさ、周囲の期待が関係します。
It also depends on whether companionship is central to the activity's meaning.誰かと一緒にいることが、その活動の意味の中心かどうかも関係します。
Acting alone offers autonomy over pace, timing, and decisions.一人で行動すると、速さ、時間、判断を自分で決められます。autonomy自分で決める自由
It can also concentrate every practical problem and decision on one person.一方で、実際の問題や判断がすべて一人に集中します。
One manageable success can expand a person's comfort zone.無理のない一度の成功で、安心してできる範囲が広がることがあります。
A frightening or exhausting experience can contract that boundary again.怖い経験や疲れる経験で、その境界が再び狭くなることもあります。
Seeing someone similar act alone can make the same choice feel attainable.自分に似た人が一人で行動するのを見ると、同じ選択が現実的に感じられます。
The useful question is not whether independence is superior, but where it genuinely serves us.大切なのは自立が優れているかではなく、どこまでなら本当に自分の役に立つかです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Where's your line? (どこまで一人で？)

WARM-UP 1Eating alone, going to the cinema alone, travelling alone—where do you draw the line?一人で食事、映画、一人旅、どこに境界を引きますか？

Follow up
  • Which of the three requires the least courage?
  • Which one lies beyond your current boundary?

A recent example (最近の例)

WARM-UP 2What's the boldest thing you've done alone—or one solo activity you would realistically attempt?一人でした最も大胆なこと、または現実的に一人で挑戦したいことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • For a real experience, where did it take place?
  • For a possible activity, which condition would make it manageable?

What happened (そのとき)

QUESTION 1What happened in the real experience—or what might unfold in the imagined one?実際の経験では何が起きましたか、または、想像した挑戦ではどのように進みそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which moment would test you most?
  • What could make the experience rewarding?

Freedom or pressure (自由か負担か)

QUESTION 2What is the greatest freedom—and the greatest pressure—in doing that alone?それを一人でする最大の自由と、最大の負担は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which decisions become simpler?
  • Which responsibility would you prefer to share?

Someone braver (もっと勇気のある人)

QUESTION 3Do you know anyone more comfortable acting alone—or can you use a public or fictional example?一人で行動することに自分より抵抗がない人を知っていますか、または、公に知られた人や架空の人物を例にできますか？

Follow up
  • If someone comes to mind, which habit makes them seem confident?
  • If nobody does, what would someone confident acting alone do differently?

A moving line (変わった境界)

QUESTION 4Has your boundary for doing things alone shifted over time?一人でできることの境界は、時間とともに変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • If yes, which experience shifted it?
  • If not, what has kept the boundary stable?

A smaller step (小さな一歩)

QUESTION 5Which smaller solo activity could prepare you to go further?さらに進む準備として、どのような小さなことなら一人でできますか？

Follow up
  • How would you make the experiment safer?
  • Which sign would show you were ready?

What would push it (境界を広げる条件)

QUESTION 6What would expand your comfort zone further?何があれば、安心して一人でできる範囲がさらに広がりますか？

Follow up
  • Would resources, information, or practice matter most?
  • Which activity would still remain beyond it?
