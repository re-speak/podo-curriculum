A useful label—or a fixed box? (役立つラベル、それとも固定された箱？)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
MBTI begins with a self-report: people answer questions about how they usually think, decide, and interact.MBTIは自己申告から始まり、普段どのように考え、決め、人と関わるかについて質問に答えます。self-report自己申告
The result places those answers into four pairs of categories and gives a four-letter type.結果は答えを四組の分類に分け、四文字のタイプを示します。
Many people enjoy the description because it gives them clear language for patterns they already notice.自分がすでに気づいている傾向を分かりやすく表す言葉が得られるため、説明を楽しむ人は多くいます。
A broad description can feel personal, especially when someone remembers the matching parts more than the mismatches.合わない部分より合う部分をよく覚えていると、広い説明でも自分だけのもののように感じられます。
This selective attention is sometimes called confirmation bias.このような選択的な注意は、確証バイアスと呼ばれることがあります。confirmation bias確証バイアス
Behavior also changes with context: a person may be quiet at work, talkative with friends, and decisive in an emergency.行動は状況でも変わり、職場では静かでも、友人とはよく話し、緊急時には決断力を見せる人もいます。
Different moods, experiences, and interpretations of a question can lead to a different result later.気分、経験、質問の受け取り方が違えば、あとで別の結果が出ることもあります。
Other people may notice habits that the person answering the test overlooks.本人がテストで見落とした習慣に、周りの人が気づくこともあります。
A type can start a useful conversation about personality without proving every claim in the description.タイプは、説明のすべてを証明しなくても、性格について役立つ会話を始めるきっかけになります。
Problems begin when the label becomes a fixed identity or an excuse for behavior.ラベルが固定されたアイデンティティや行動の言い訳になると、問題が始まります。
Knowing someone's type may shape expectations before that person has a chance to show who they are.相手がどんな人かを示す前に、タイプを知ることで期待が作られることがあります。
MBTI may be most useful as a conversation tool rather than a rulebook for judging people.MBTIは、人を判断する規則集ではなく、会話の道具として使うと最も役立つかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答える必要はありません。一つの答えが深くなったら、それが今日の成功です。

Your type (あなたのタイプ)

WARM-UP 1Tell me your MBTI and how accurate it feels.あなたのMBTIと、どのくらい当たっていると感じるかを教えてください。

Follow up
  • When did you first take the test?
  • Have you ever received a different result?
  • How strongly do you identify with it?

One accurate trait (当たっている特徴)

WARM-UP 2Which part of the description sounds most like you?説明のどの部分が、一番自分らしいと思いますか？

Follow up
  • What real example shows that trait?
  • Does it appear more at work, at home, or with friends?
  • When is that trait most useful?

In real life (実生活では)

QUESTION 1When does your MBTI describe your behavior especially well?どんなときに、MBTIがあなたの行動を特によく表していますか？

Follow up
  • What usually triggers that behavior?
  • Do other people notice it too?
  • How does that behavior affect people around you?

Other people's view (周りの見方)

QUESTION 2How do you think someone who knows you well would react to your MBTI result?あなたをよく知る人は、あなたのMBTI結果にどう反応すると思いますか？

Follow up
  • Which part would they agree with?
  • Which part might they question?
  • Would their view change how accurate the result feels to you?

What it misses (当たっていないところ)

QUESTION 3What does your MBTI description get wrong about you?MBTIの説明は、あなたのどんなところを間違って表していますか？

Follow up
  • Is the description too broad or too fixed?
  • Which situation brings out a different side of you?
  • What would you add to make it more accurate?

Asking other people (人に聞くこと)

QUESTION 4What role, if any, does MBTI play when you first meet someone?初めて会う人との会話で、MBTIを使うとしたら、どんな役割がありますか？

Follow up
  • What, if anything, can it tell you about someone new?
  • When would asking about it feel natural or awkward?
  • What could you ask instead?

Change over time (時間と変化)

QUESTION 5How much can a person's MBTI result change over time?人のMBTI結果は、時間とともにどのくらい変わると思いますか？

Follow up
  • Which life experience might change an answer?
  • Would a changed result surprise you?
  • What part of personality seems more stable?

Labels and treatment (ラベルと接し方)

QUESTION 6In what ways, if any, does knowing someone's MBTI change how you treat them?相手のMBTIを知ることで、接し方が変わるとすれば、どのように変わりますか？

Follow up
  • What expectation might the label create?
  • When could that expectation be helpful?
  • How could the label make you misunderstand the person?
