What repair after conflict requires (対立のあとの修復に必要なこと)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Conflicts vary, so repair cannot follow one universal script.対立の形はさまざまで、修復に一つの決まった台本はありません。
A minor misunderstanding may require little more than timely clarification.小さな誤解なら、適切なタイミングで説明するだけで十分なことがあります。
A harmful action may require time, accountability, and a specific apology.人を傷つけた行動には、時間、責任を引き受けること、具体的な謝罪が必要な場合があります。
Reaching out first is not the same as accepting sole responsibility.先に連絡を取ることは、責任をすべて自分だけで負うこととは違います。reach out連絡を取る
It may simply show that preserving the relationship matters more than prolonging the silence.沈黙を長引かせるより、関係を守るほうが大切だと示すこともあります。
Some people need distance before they can speak without defending themselves.自己弁護ばかりせずに話すために、少し距離が必要な人もいます。
An extended silence can add resentment and make the original issue harder to identify.沈黙が長引くと不満が加わり、もとの問題を見つけにくくなります。
A constructive repair names the disagreement without reopening it as a contest.建設的な修復では、対立を勝負としてやり直さずに問題を言葉にします。
Some relationships recover fully; others require a firmer boundary or an ending.完全に戻る関係もあれば、より明確な境界や終わりが必要な関係もあります。
Repair succeeds when the next interaction can be safer and more honest.次のやり取りをより安心して、正直にできるなら、修復は成功です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Who moves first? (先に動く人)

WARM-UP 1When you argue with someone close, who usually reaches out first?親しい人とけんかしたとき、普段はどちらから先に連絡を取りますか？

Follow up
  • If no close relationship or argument comes to mind, which approach seems fairest in an ordinary imagined disagreement?
  • In a real or imagined case, what makes initiating contact difficult?

How long (かかる時間)

WARM-UP 2How much time, if any, do you need before a calm conversation becomes possible?落ち着いて話せるようになるまでに時間は必要ですか、必要なら、どのくらいですか？

Follow up
  • If you take a pause, how do you use it?
  • If you do not, what makes an immediate calm conversation possible?

The first message (最初のメッセージ)

QUESTION 1How would you word the first message when you reach out after an argument?けんかのあと、最初に連絡を取るメッセージをどのように書きますか？

Follow up
  • Would you identify the disagreement immediately?
  • How would you keep the tone from sounding defensive?

A real or safe example (実際または話せる例)

QUESTION 2What was a recent minor disagreement—or which everyday example are you comfortable examining?最近の小さな意見の違い、または差し支えなく話せる日常の例は何ですか？

Follow up
  • If it was real, what was the underlying disagreement?
  • If it is a general example, what might change afterward?

What repairs it (修復に役立つこと)

QUESTION 3What makes an apology feel credible?どのような謝罪なら信頼できると感じますか？

Follow up
  • How specific should the apology be?
  • Which action should follow the words?

Still unresolved (まだ解決していないこと)

QUESTION 4Can some disagreements remain unresolved without destroying a relationship?解決しない意見の違いがあっても、関係を壊さずにいられますか？

Follow up
  • Which differences can a relationship absorb?
  • When does the disagreement require a new boundary?

Not every repair (仲直りしない選択)

QUESTION 5When is reconciliation not the appropriate goal?仲直りを目標にすることが適切ではないのは、どのようなときですか？

Follow up
  • Which condition would need to change first?
  • How can someone end contact respectfully?

The right way (よい方法)

QUESTION 6What is a fair way to make up after an ordinary argument?普通のけんかのあと、どのように公平に仲直りできますか？

Follow up
  • Who should initiate it?
  • Which sign would show that the repair succeeded?
