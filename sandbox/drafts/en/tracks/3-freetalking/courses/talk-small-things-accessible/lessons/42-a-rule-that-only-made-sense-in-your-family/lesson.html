Every household develops rules that feel obvious inside it.どの家庭にも、その中では当然に感じられる決まりがあります。household rule家庭の決まり
Some rules solve a practical problem that outsiders cannot see.家の外の人には見えない実用的な問題を解決する決まりもあります。
Others continue long after the original reason disappears.もともとの理由がなくなったあとも続く決まりもあります。
Children often assume every home works the same way.子どもは、どの家も同じようにしていると思いがちです。
A visit to another home can reveal the difference suddenly.ほかの家を訪れると、その違いに急に気づくことがあります。
The discovery can be funny, embarrassing, or surprisingly emotional.その発見は、面白かったり、恥ずかしかったり、意外に感情的だったりします。
We may keep a rule even after we stop agreeing with its reason.理由に納得しなくなっても、その決まりを続けることがあります。
Another family's strange rule may be perfectly sensible in context.別の家庭の変わった決まりも、背景を知れば納得できるかもしれません。
Passing a rule on is a choice, not an automatic duty.決まりを次に伝えることは、当然の義務ではなく選択です。
The rules people remember become part of a family's story.覚えている決まりは、その家庭の物語の一部になります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

A household rule (家庭の決まり)

WARM-UP 1What was normal in your house that you found out wasn't normal anywhere else?自分の家では普通だったのに、ほかでは普通でないと知ったことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, what was the rule?
  • If none does, what rule from another household surprised you?

How it worked (決まりの内容)

WARM-UP 2How did that real or borrowed rule work?自分の家庭、またはほかの家庭で知ったその決まりは、どのような内容でしたか？

Follow up
  • Who was expected to follow it?
  • What happened if someone forgot?

When you found out (気づいた時)

QUESTION 1When did you realize other homes were different?ほかの家庭は違うと、いつ気づきましたか？

Follow up
  • If there was a moment, what happened?
  • If there was not, how did you gradually learn the difference?

Your reaction (自分の反応)

QUESTION 2How did you react to the difference?その違いにどう反応しましたか？

Follow up
  • Did the rule seem strange at first?
  • What explanation changed your view?

Do you still do it? (今もする？)

QUESTION 3Do you follow any part of that rule?その決まりの一部に従っていますか？

Follow up
  • If you do, what makes sense about it?
  • If you do not, did you stop—or have you never followed it?

Another home's rule (ほかの家の決まり)

QUESTION 4What rule in another household has seemed strange to you?ほかの家庭の決まりで、変わっていると感じたものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, what context might explain it?
  • If none does, which area of home life probably varies most?

Carry it forward (受け継ぐ？)

QUESTION 5Which household rule would you choose to carry forward?どの家庭の決まりを、これからも受け継ぎたいですか？

Follow up
  • Why is it worth keeping?
  • How would you explain it to a new person?

Known for (覚えられる決まり)

QUESTION 6What small rule should a household be known for?家庭は、どのような小さな決まりで知られるとよいですか？

Follow up
  • What value would the rule express?
  • How could it stay flexible?
