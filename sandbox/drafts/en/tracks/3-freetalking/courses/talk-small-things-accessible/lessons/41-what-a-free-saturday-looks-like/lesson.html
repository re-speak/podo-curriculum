The shape of free time (自由時間の形)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A completely unstructured day can feel restful or strangely difficult.予定がまったく決まっていない日は、休まることもあれば、妙に難しく感じることもあります。unstructured予定が決まっていない
Without a deadline, the morning may start much later than planned.締め切りがないと、朝は予定よりずっと遅く始まることがあります。
One fixed ritual can give the rest of the day a useful shape.一つ決まった習慣があると、残りの一日に形が生まれます。
Doing nothing is different from choosing to rest.何もしないことと、休むことを選ぶのは違います。
A small errand can either start the day or interrupt it.小さな用事は、一日を始めるきっかけにも、邪魔にもなります。
Messages from other people can turn open time into a shared plan.誰かからの連絡で、空いていた時間が一緒の予定に変わります。
The ideal balance between company and solitude changes from week to week.人と過ごす時間と一人の時間の理想的なバランスは、週ごとに変わります。
Sundays can feel different because Monday is already closer.日曜日は月曜日が近いので、土曜日とは違って感じます。
Free days change as our responsibilities and energy change.責任や元気の量が変わると、自由な日の過ごし方も変わります。
What feels missing from a free day can show what we need.自由な日に足りないと感じるものは、自分に必要なものを示します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

A free Saturday (予定のない土曜日)

WARM-UP 1Walk me through a Saturday when you have nothing on.予定のない土曜日を、順に説明してください。

Follow up
  • If that happens sometimes, what do you do first?
  • If it rarely happens, how would the ideal version begin?

The morning (朝の流れ)

WARM-UP 2What happens during the first two hours?最初の二時間は、どのように過ごしますか？

Follow up
  • What time does the day really begin?
  • Which part would you refuse to rush?

What's fixed (決まっていること)

QUESTION 1What stays fixed even on a completely free Saturday?完全に自由な土曜日でも、必ずすることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • If something is fixed, why does it matter?
  • If nothing is, how do you decide what comes first?

What would ruin it (台無しにするもの)

QUESTION 2What would ruin that kind of Saturday?そのような土曜日を台無しにするものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could you recover the day afterward?
  • Which interruption would be acceptable?

Saturday and Sunday (土曜と日曜)

QUESTION 3How does a free Sunday feel different?予定のない日曜日は、どのように違って感じますか？

Follow up
  • When do you start thinking about Monday?
  • Which day is easier to enjoy fully?

Five years ago (5年前)

QUESTION 4What did a free weekend look like five years ago?5年前の予定のない週末は、どのようなものでしたか？

Follow up
  • What has changed most?
  • Which old part would you bring back?

Company or solitude (人と過ごす？一人？)

QUESTION 5How much of the day would you spend with other people, if any?誰かと過ごすなら、一日のどのくらいを一緒に過ごしますか？

Follow up
  • If you want company, what kind fits the day?
  • If you want solitude, what does it give you?

Described and ideal (話した一日と理想)

QUESTION 6How does the free Saturday you described compare with your ideal one?ここまで話した予定のない土曜日は、理想の過ごし方と比べてどうですか？

Follow up
  • If they differ, what would you add or remove?
  • If they match, what makes the day work so well?
