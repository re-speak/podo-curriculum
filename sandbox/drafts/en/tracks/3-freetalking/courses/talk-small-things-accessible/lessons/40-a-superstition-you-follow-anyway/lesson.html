Belief, habit, and luck (信念、習慣、運)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A superstition connects an action or sign with luck without clear proof.迷信は、はっきりした証拠なしに、行動やしるしと運を結びつけます。
People can follow one without fully believing it.人は、完全に信じていなくても迷信に従うことがあります。
The action may be easy, while ignoring it feels strangely risky.従うのは簡単でも、無視すると妙に不安に感じます。
Many superstitions are learned from family, friends, or school.多くの迷信は、家族や友人、学校から学びます。
Some began as practical warnings that lost their explanation.実用的な注意から始まり、理由だけが忘れられたものもあります。
Others survive because the story is memorable and easy to repeat.印象的で繰り返しやすい話だから残るものもあります。
Breaking a superstition can feel like testing luck on purpose.迷信を破ることは、わざと運を試すように感じられます。
Different places give the same number, animal, or object different meanings.同じ数字や動物、物でも、場所によって意味が違います。
Learning the story matters more than laughing at the rule.その決まりを笑うより、背景の話を知るほうが大切です。
Even a skeptic may keep one ritual because it feels familiar.迷信を疑う人でも、なじみがあるという理由で一つの習慣を続けることがあります。skeptic疑う人

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

One superstition (一つの迷信)

WARM-UP 1Is there anything you avoid doing, even though you don't believe in it?信じてはいないのに、避けていることはありますか？

Follow up
  • If there is, what do you avoid?
  • If there is not, which superstition do you hear most often?

The rule (その決まり)

WARM-UP 2How does that real or familiar superstition work?自分が従っている迷信、またはよく知っている迷信には、どのような決まりがありますか？

Follow up
  • What action or sign is involved?
  • What is supposed to happen?

Where it came from (教わった相手)

QUESTION 1Who taught you about it, or where did you hear it?誰に教わったか、または、どこで聞きましたか？

Follow up
  • How old were you then?
  • What explanation were you given?

Belief or habit (信じる？習慣？)

QUESTION 2Do you believe any part of it?その迷信を少しでも信じていますか？

Follow up
  • If you do, which part feels possible?
  • If you do not, why might the habit remain?

Break the rule (破ったら？)

QUESTION 3What would happen if you broke the rule?その決まりを破ったら、どうなるとされていますか？

Follow up
  • Would you feel uneasy anyway?
  • What real consequence, if any, could follow?

A Japanese example (日本の例)

QUESTION 4What's a Japanese superstition an outsider might not know?外国の人が知らなさそうな日本の迷信は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Where might they encounter it?
  • What background would help them understand it?

The strangest one (いちばん不思議なもの)

QUESTION 5What's the strangest superstition you've heard?聞いたことがある中で、いちばん不思議な迷信は何ですか？

Follow up
  • If one comes to mind, what makes it memorable?
  • If none does, what kind of superstition would surprise you?

One to keep (残したいもの)

QUESTION 6Which superstition is worth keeping as a tradition, if any?伝統として残す価値がある迷信があるなら、どれですか？

Follow up
  • What story would survive with it?
  • How would you keep it from becoming harmful?
