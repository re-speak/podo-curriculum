Our real stress habits (本当のストレス習慣)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Advice about stress is often easier to give than to follow.ストレスへの助言は、実行するより人に言うほうが簡単です。
Under pressure, we return to habits that require little thought.プレッシャーがあると、あまり考えなくてよい習慣に戻ります。
A coping response can calm us briefly but create another problem later.ストレスへの対処は、少し落ち着かせても、あとで別の問題を作ることがあります。coping responseストレスへの対処
We may avoid a task, eat, clean, complain, or become very quiet.仕事を避けたり、食べたり、掃除したり、不満を言ったり、黙ったりします。
The same action can help in one situation and hurt in another.同じ行動でも、ある場面では助けになり、別の場面では悪化させます。
People sometimes describe the healthy habit they wish they had.人は、実際の習慣ではなく、できたらよいと思う健康的な方法を話すことがあります。
Honesty makes it easier to see the real pattern.正直に話すと、本当のパターンが見えやすくなります。
A small replacement is often more realistic than a perfect routine.完璧な習慣より、小さな代わりの行動のほうが現実的です。
Old stress habits can return during a difficult period.大変な時期には、昔のストレス習慣が戻ることがあります。
Progress may mean noticing the habit earlier, not removing it completely.改善とは、完全になくすことではなく、早く気づくことかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

The honest answer (正直な答え)

WARM-UP 1What do you really do when you're stressed — not what you should do.ストレスがあるとき、するべきことではなく、実際にすることを教えてください。

Follow up
  • If stress has a clear effect, what appears first?
  • If it rarely changes your behaviour, what helps you stay steady?

How often (頻度)

WARM-UP 2How often does that reaction happen?その反応はどのくらいの頻度で起きますか？

Follow up
  • What level of stress brings it out?
  • When does the pattern not appear?

Does it help? (役立つ？)

QUESTION 1Does it actually help?それは本当に役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • If it helps, how long does the effect last?
  • If it does not, why do you still return to it?

What you tell people (人に話すこと)

QUESTION 2What do you tell people you do when you're stressed? Say whether you have one usual answer, it varies, or the topic has never come up.ストレスがあるときに何をするか、人にはどう話しますか。いつも同じ答えか、その時々で変わるか、それとも一度も話題になったことがないかも話してください。

Follow up
  • If you have a usual answer, how does it compare with what you really do?
  • If it varies, what changes what you say?
  • If the topic has never come up, what would you say if it did?

An old reaction (昔の反応)

QUESTION 3What did you use to do under stress?以前は、ストレスがあるとき何をしていましたか？

Follow up
  • If something changed, what caused the change?
  • If nothing changed, which part has stayed constant?

What makes it worse (悪化する条件)

QUESTION 4Which situation makes your stress habit most unhelpful?どのような状況で、そのストレス習慣はいちばん役に立たなくなりますか？

Follow up
  • What cost appears later?
  • Who, if anyone, is affected by it?

A realistic alternative (現実的な代わり)

QUESTION 5What would you realistically like to do instead?代わりに、現実的には何をしたいですか？

Follow up
  • What is the smallest possible version?
  • What would make it easier to choose?

Notice it sooner (早く気づく)

QUESTION 6What sign could help you notice stress earlier?どのようなサインがあれば、もっと早くストレスに気づけますか？

Follow up
  • What could you do at that first sign?
  • Which sign do you often ignore?
