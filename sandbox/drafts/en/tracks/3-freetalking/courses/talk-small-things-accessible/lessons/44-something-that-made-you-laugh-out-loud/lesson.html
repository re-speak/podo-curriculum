Why laughter is hard to explain (笑いを説明するのが難しい理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Real laughter often arrives before we can explain the joke.本当の笑いは、なぜ面白いか説明する前に出ることがあります。
Timing can matter as much as the words themselves.言葉そのものと同じくらい、タイミングが大切です。
A serious situation can become funny when one small thing goes wrong.真面目な場面でも、小さなことがうまくいかないと面白くなることがあります。
Shared history makes some jokes almost impossible to translate.共通の思い出があると、ほかの人には説明しにくい笑いが生まれます。
Laughter can be contagious, making an ordinary moment funnier.ほかの人が笑うと、普通の場面ももっと面白く感じられます。
Trying too hard to be funny can make a room uncomfortable.面白くしようとしすぎると、その場が気まずくなることがあります。
Different people laugh at stories, language, mistakes, or absurd situations.人によって、物語、言葉、失敗、変な状況など、笑うものが違います。
A joke that works once may fail when it is repeated.一度面白かった冗談も、繰り返すとうまくいかないことがあります。
Explaining a funny moment requires enough context, but not every detail.面白い場面を話すには、十分な背景が必要ですが、細部を全部話す必要はありません。
The funniest person is often the one who notices what everyone else missed.いちばん面白い人は、ほかの人が見逃したことに気づく人かもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

The last real laugh (最後に本当に笑った時)

WARM-UP 1When did you last actually laugh out loud?最後に本当に声を出して笑ったのはいつですか？

Follow up
  • If a moment comes to mind, where were you?
  • If none does, what usually comes closest to making you laugh?

What happened (何が起きた？)

WARM-UP 2What happened in that moment—or in a typical example?その瞬間、または典型的な例では、何が起きましたか？

Follow up
  • What set up the funny part?
  • What happened immediately before the laugh?

Who was there (一緒にいた人)

QUESTION 1Who were you with, if anyone?一緒にいた人がいるなら、誰でしたか？

Follow up
  • If someone was there, did their reaction add to it?
  • If you were alone, what made the moment work without company?

Why it worked (面白かった理由)

QUESTION 2Why was it so funny to you?なぜ自分にはそれほど面白かったのですか？

Follow up
  • Was the timing or the content more important?
  • Would everyone understand the humour?

What usually works (普段笑うもの)

QUESTION 3Does that kind of humour usually work on you?そのような笑いは、普段も自分に合いますか？

Follow up
  • What other example fits your taste?
  • When can the same style fail?

What never works (笑えないもの)

QUESTION 4What kind of humour never works for you?どのような笑いは、自分にはまったく合いませんか？

Follow up
  • What makes it uncomfortable or dull?
  • Can the right person ever make it work?

Retell it (話し直す)

QUESTION 5How would you retell the moment without losing the funny part?面白さを失わずに、その場面をどう話し直しますか？

Follow up
  • Which detail is essential?
  • Which background detail would you cut?

The funniest person (いちばん面白い人)

QUESTION 6Who's the funniest person you know, if anyone?知っている中で、いちばん面白い人がいるなら誰ですか？

Follow up
  • If someone comes to mind, what do they notice well?
  • If nobody does, what quality makes a person funny to you?
