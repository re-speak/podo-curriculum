BALANCE GAME

A hundred friends or three close ones? (友人百人、それとも親しい友人三人？)

Let's compare a hundred friends and three close friends, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.百人の友人と三人の親しい友人を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Weak ties often widen access to information and communities beyond a close circle.広い人間関係からは、さまざまな情報、考え、紹介を得られます。
Intimacy makes subtle changes visible and can support care before an explicit request.親しい友人は、助けを求める前に問題に気づくことがあります。
A reliable inner circle depends less on size than on repeated, mutual attention.親しい仲間の輪は小さいからこそ、繰り返す関わりの中で信頼が育ちます。
Network size creates a maintenance cost because reciprocity and attention are limited resources.どの友情にも気づかいが必要なので、人間関係が広いほど時間の使い方も変わります。
A wide network expands social opportunity while increasing requests, coordination, and claims on attention.多くの人に知られると、誘いが増える一方、応える必要も増えます。
Intimacy depends on sustained mutual attention rather than the number of names in a contact list.親しい友情には、繰り返す時間、気づかい、信頼が必要です。
Social reach does not prevent loneliness when no relationship supports vulnerability or dependable care.知り合いが百人いても、正直に話せる相手がいなければ孤独を感じます。
A small inner circle may offer depth while lacking the range needed for every interest, opportunity, or emergency.親しい友人三人だけで、すべての交流や実用的な必要を満たせるとは限りません。
Transitions increase the value of broad networks because information and belonging must be rebuilt quickly.引っ越し、転職、新しい地域への参加では、広いつながりの価値が高まります。
Healthy social lives often combine a dependable inner circle with looser relationships that provide range and renewal.多くの人には、信頼できる小さな中心と、その周りの広いつながりの両方が必要です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A long conversation (ゆっくり話す)

WARM-UP 1Which quality allows one friendship to support a genuinely long conversation?一人の友人とゆっくり話すなら、何が大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which sign shows that a conversation has built trust?
  • How does mutual attention matter more than its length?

A room of strangers (知らない人の集まり)

WARM-UP 2What makes a large unfamiliar gathering easier to enter?知らない人が多い集まりでは、どう会話を始めますか？

Follow up
  • Which social cue would make the first approach easier?
  • How would the strategy change if nobody knew the host?

Choose a circle (人間関係を選ぶ)

QUESTION 1During a difficult week, would a wide circle of friendly contacts or three close friends matter more?大変な一週間により頼りになるのは、広い友人関係と親しい友人三人のどちらですか？

Follow up
  • Which need—belonging, information, or care—drives the choice?
  • What would the preferred circle fail to provide?

Daily contact (日常のつながり)

QUESTION 2What would disappear from daily life if every relationship had the same level of closeness?その種類の友人関係だけなら、日常はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Which ordinary situation would reveal the limitation first?
  • How would the same social structure affect loneliness?

When the other helps (反対側が必要なとき)

QUESTION 3Which situation gives the rejected kind of relationship a clear advantage?どんなときに、反対の人間関係のほうが役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Which transition gives the rejected network an advantage?
  • Whose needs would make the opposite structure more suitable?

A new city (新しい町)

QUESTION 4After arriving in a new city, would breadth or closeness matter first?新しい町へ引っ越したら、どちらの人間関係が先に役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Would broad contacts or dependable care matter first?
  • At what point would the other kind of friendship become necessary?

What changes the answer (答えを変えるもの)

QUESTION 5Which loss or new opportunity would reverse your preferred social structure?人間関係にどんな変化があれば、反対を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which loss would expose a weakness in the chosen circle?
  • Could one new responsibility reverse the preferred network?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between a hundred friends and three close friends, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターに百人の友人と三人の親しい友人のどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • How does the tutor distinguish a contact from a close friend?
  • Which transition would reverse their preferred circle?
