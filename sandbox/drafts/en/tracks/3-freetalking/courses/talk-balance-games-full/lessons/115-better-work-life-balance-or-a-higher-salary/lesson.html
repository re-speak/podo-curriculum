Better work-life balance or a higher salary? (よりよい生活との両立、それとも高い給料？)

Let's compare better work-life balance and a higher salary, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.よりよい仕事と生活の両立とより高い給料を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Compensation can reduce financial stress while leaving chronic workload and recovery problems unresolved.給料が高いと安心は増えますが、疲れる働き方を続けられるとは限りません。
Schedule autonomy often shapes well-being independently of the number of contracted hours.始業時間や休みを選べることは、労働時間の長さと同じくらい大切です。
Time autonomy includes predictability, boundaries, and the ability to respond to care or health needs.時間の自由とは、仕事が私生活に入る時間を実際に調整できることです。
Temporary imbalance is safer when its purpose, limit, and recovery plan are explicit.忙しい期間を短くするなら、終わる時期と回復する時間が必要です。
Total compensation includes benefits, paid leave, insurance, retirement support, and risk—not salary alone.報酬には、給料だけでなく、休暇、健康支援、退職後の制度も含まれます。
Work-life balance must account for care and domestic labour that continues outside paid employment.労働時間が短くても、介護や家事などの無給の仕事はなくなりません。
Additional earnings may lose practical value when work eliminates rest, relationships, and the time to use them.長時間働くと、得た収入を楽しむ時間がなくなることがあります。
Insufficient compensation can make nominal free time stressful and insecure.給料が低いと、出費の心配で休む時間も落ち着かなくなります。
Career value may come through learning, mentorship, autonomy, or mobility rather than immediate compensation.給料が低い仕事でも、技能、支援、将来の選択肢が増えるなら価値があります。
The decision should identify which financial, health, care, or career need is genuinely time-sensitive.よりよい条件は、後回しにできない必要を守るものです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A well-rested day (しっかり休めた日)

WARM-UP 1Which boundary allows rest to restore attention after work or study?しっかり休めたと感じるには、何が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which boundary prevents work from occupying the day off mentally?
  • How would care or household duties alter the idea of rest?

Time you protect (守りたい時間)

WARM-UP 2Which daily period is most important to protect from work?一日の中で、必ず守りたい自分の時間はいつですか？

Follow up
  • Which relationship or health need depends on that period?
  • What workplace rule could keep it protected?

Time or salary (時間か給料)

QUESTION 1Over the next year, would greater control of your time or a higher income matter more?これから一年では、時間の裁量と高い収入のどちらがより大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which unmet need makes time or compensation more urgent?
  • What minimum condition would the rejected offer still have to meet?

Only one benefit (一つだけ得る)

QUESTION 2What would improve and deteriorate if only the chosen benefit increased?選んだ条件だけがよくなれば、日常はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Which improvement would appear first and which cost would arrive later?
  • Would the benefit remain valuable after a full year?

Nights and weekends (夜と週末)

QUESTION 3How much compensation could justify regularly surrendering evenings and weekends?高い給料でも夜や週末をよく諦めるなら、受け入れますか？

Follow up
  • Which recurring commitment makes those hours irreplaceable?
  • What duration or compensation would make the sacrifice temporary?

Exactly one year (一年だけ)

QUESTION 4Does a fixed one-year horizon make a period of imbalance defensible?ちょうど一年だけなら、高い給料を選びやすくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Which measurable goal would justify the period of imbalance?
  • What recovery plan would be required before accepting it?

A new responsibility (新しい責任)

QUESTION 5Which financial, health, or care obligation would reverse your choice?どんな責任や目標ができたら、反対を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which responsibility creates a genuine deadline for the decision?
  • Would the new choice last after that responsibility ended?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between better work-life balance and a higher salary, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターによりよい仕事と生活の両立とより高い給料のどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • How does the tutor distinguish compensation from sustainable work?
  • Which responsibility would reverse their preferred offer?
