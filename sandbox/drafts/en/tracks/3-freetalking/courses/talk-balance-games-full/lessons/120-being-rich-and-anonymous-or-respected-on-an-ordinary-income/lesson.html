BALANCE GAME

Praise from one person or recognition from many? (一人からの言葉？多くの人からの評価？)

Let’s compare personal praise with public recognition, then explain how the person, audience, and consequences could change the choice. Please read the title aloud.個人的な褒め言葉と公の評価を比べ、誰から、どんな人の前で、何が起こるかによって選択がどう変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why the source and setting matter (誰がどこで褒めるか)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
After finishing a difficult project, one person might receive a thoughtful private message while another hears their name announced on a stage.難しい仕事を終えた後、一人は心のこもった個人的なメッセージを受け取り、もう一人は舞台で名前を呼ばれるかもしれません。
Private praise can be specific because the person giving it knows what the effort actually involved.個人的な褒め言葉は、実際にどんな努力が必要だったか知る人からのものなので、具体的になりやすいです。
Public recognition can introduce someone’s work to new people and create opportunities that private thanks cannot.公の評価は、その人の仕事を新しい人々に知らせ、個人的な感謝だけでは生まれない機会につながることがあります。
The same applause can feel encouraging to one person and exposing to another.同じ拍手でも、励みになる人もいれば、人目にさらされたように感じる人もいます。
Recognition may also create an expectation that the next result must be equally impressive.評価されると、次の成果も同じくらい立派でなければならないという期待が生まれることがあります。
An award for one person can hide the work of a team unless credit is shared carefully.一人への賞は、功績をきちんと分けなければ、チームの仕事を見えにくくすることがあります。
Praise from a trusted person may last because their judgement matters, even when nobody else hears it.信頼する人からの褒め言葉は、ほかの誰も聞いていなくても、その人の判断が大切だからこそ長く心に残ることがあります。
A larger audience brings visibility, but it can also bring criticism from people who know little about the work.多くの人に知られると注目が集まる一方、その仕事をよく知らない人からの批判も集まることがあります。
The best form of recognition can change with the achievement, the audience, and what the person hopes to do next.どんな評価がよいかは、成果の内容、聞く人、そして本人が次に何をしたいかによって変わります。
Someone may prefer quiet thanks for personal effort and still welcome public credit when it helps a shared project.個人的な努力には静かな感謝を好みながら、共同の仕事に役立つなら公の評価を歓迎する人もいます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The praise that matters (最もうれしい褒め言葉)

WARM-UP 1Whose praise means the most to you?誰から褒められるのが、あなたにとって最もうれしいですか？

Follow up
  • A friend, family member, teacher, or manager?
  • What would you want them to notice?

Praise in public (人前での褒め言葉)

WARM-UP 2Would you enjoy being praised in front of a large group?大勢の前で褒められたら、うれしいですか？

Follow up
  • Would a smaller group feel better?
  • What might make it uncomfortable?

A message or an award (メッセージか賞か)

QUESTION 1Would you rather receive a thoughtful message from one person or a public award?一人から心のこもったメッセージをもらうのと、公の場で賞をもらうのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which would you remember longer?
  • Who would you want the message to come from?

What recognition can bring (評価がもたらすもの)

QUESTION 2What could public recognition help you do next?多くの人から評価されると、次にどんなことができそうですか？

Follow up
  • Could it open a new opportunity?
  • Would it motivate you?

Uncomfortable praise (居心地の悪い褒め言葉)

QUESTION 3When can public praise feel uncomfortable?人前で褒められることが、居心地悪く感じられるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Is the attention or the expectation harder?
  • Would private thanks feel better?

Recognition worth sharing (公に評価する価値のある成果)

QUESTION 4What kind of achievement deserves public recognition?どんな成果なら、公に評価されるべきだと思いますか？

Follow up
  • An individual success or a team success?
  • Who should share the credit?

Praise with criticism (批判を伴う評価)

QUESTION 5If recognition brought criticism too, would you still want it?評価と一緒に批判も集まるなら、それでも人に知られたいですか？

Follow up
  • How much criticism would be too much?
  • Would it matter who criticized you?

A reason to switch (褒められ方を変える理由)

QUESTION 6What would make you switch to the other kind of praise?どんな条件なら、もう一方の褒められ方を選びますか？

Answer first, then follow up
  • Would the size of the audience matter?
  • Would the person giving the praise change your answer?
