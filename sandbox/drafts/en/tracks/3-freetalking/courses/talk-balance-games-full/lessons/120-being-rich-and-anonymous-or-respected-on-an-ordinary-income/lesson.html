Let's compare private wealth and respect on an ordinary income, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.無名で裕福な生活と普通の収入で得る尊敬を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Financial anonymity protects privacy while complicating openness in close relationships.富を秘密にすると、お金について正直に話すことが難しくなる場合があります。
Status-based regard is less durable than respect grounded in conduct, competence, or service.肩書きによる尊敬は、貢献による尊敬より早く失われることがあります。
Anonymity can reduce unwanted exposure, although it does not erase accountability for choices that affect others.無名でいることは、責任をなくさずにプライバシーを守れます。
Visibility can expand influence while narrowing control over access, attention, and ordinary privacy.人に知られると、近づいてくる人や、一人でいられる時間が変わります。
Financial security expands private options, but social regard depends on conduct and contribution.富はプライバシーや選択肢を増やしますが、信頼や尊敬までは生みません。
Public regard can affirm contribution and simultaneously impose visibility, scrutiny, and role pressure.尊敬は意味を感じさせますが、期待に応え続ける圧力にもなります。
Income level cannot be judged apart from housing, health care, obligations, and the security provided by institutions.必要なものや公共サービスが安定していれば、普通の収入でも安心して暮らせます。
Remaining unknown reduces exposure while making it harder to mobilise reputation for public impact.無名でいると不要な注目を避けられますが、影響力は小さくなる場合があります。
Private contribution can separate social benefit from recognition, branding, or personal prestige.名前を出さずに人を助ければ、善意を地位に変えずにすみます。
A resilient life needs relationships, purpose, privacy, and security beyond either wealth or public approval.お金も評判も、それだけに人生を頼ると不安定です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Private pride (人に知られない誇り)

WARM-UP 1Which achievement keeps its value without recognition?誰にも知られなくても、価値があると感じることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which value remains even without an audience?
  • How would recognition change the reason for doing it?

Anonymous help (名前を出さない助け)

WARM-UP 2Which contribution would be easier or better if it remained anonymous?名前を出さずによいことをするなら、何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Which form of accountability should remain even without a name?
  • Could anonymity increase trust in the contribution itself?

Wealth or respect (富か尊敬)

QUESTION 1Would you choose private wealth without recognition or public respect on an ordinary income?世間に知られない裕福な生活と、普通の収入で尊敬される生活のどちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which need—security, influence, privacy, or belonging—drives the answer?
  • What hidden cost of the preferred life is easiest to ignore?

An ordinary day (普段の一日)

QUESTION 2Which daily relationships and decisions would change under your chosen life?選んだ生活では、普段の一日はどのようになりますか？

Follow up
  • Which relationship would be most altered by wealth or reputation?
  • How would ordinary decisions reveal the chosen value?

Always being judged (評価され続ける)

QUESTION 3How much public scrutiny would make respect feel like a burden?いつも人から評価されるなら、尊敬される生活を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which kind of scrutiny would turn respect into a burden?
  • Could boundaries preserve influence without constant exposure?

Only family knows (家族だけが知る)

QUESTION 4If financial status stayed inside the family, would anonymity become easier to choose?家族だけが裕福だと知っているなら、選択は変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Would secrecy change trust inside the family?
  • Which responsibility would come with sharing the information?

The price of visibility (知られることの代償)

QUESTION 5At what level of exposure or insecurity would the other life become preferable?どの程度人に知られたら、反対の生活を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which level of visibility removes control over ordinary life?
  • Would financial insecurity or reputational pressure reverse the choice first?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between private wealth and respect on an ordinary income, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターに無名で裕福な生活と普通の収入で得る尊敬のどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • How does the tutor separate respect from public visibility?
  • Which level of security or scrutiny would reverse their answer?
