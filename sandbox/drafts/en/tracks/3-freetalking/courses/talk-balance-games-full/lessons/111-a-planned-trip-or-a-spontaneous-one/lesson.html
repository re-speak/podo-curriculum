A planned trip or a spontaneous one? (計画的な旅、それとも気ままな旅？)

Let's compare a planned trip and a spontaneous trip, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.計画的な旅行と行き当たりばったりの旅行を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Scarcity and dynamic pricing make some transport and accommodation costly to leave open.航空券や人気の部屋は、日程が近づくほど取りにくく、高くなることがあります。
Open time allows an itinerary to respond to weather, fatigue, closures, and information found locally.自由な一日は、天気、体力、現地の情報に合わせて変えられます。
A last-minute plan is easier to defend when safety, access, and the cost of failure have already been managed.直前の計画は、大切な危険への備えができているときにうまくいきます。
Refundability can turn advance booking from a commitment into a flexible option.キャンセルの条件によって、早い計画が自由を守るか、失わせるかが変わります。
Calendars and access rules can constrain an itinerary even when travel and lodging remain flexible.営業日、地域の祝日、入場時間によって、交通より先に旅程が決まることがあります。
Flexible travel becomes attractive when cancellation costs, safety risks, and access barriers are low.方向を変える費用や危険が小さいほど、行き当たりばったりの旅行がしやすくなります。
Constantly deciding where to go, eat, or sleep can turn freedom into decision fatigue.旅行中に選択が多すぎると、決めることに疲れてしまいます。
Planning protects scarce experiences but may crowd out discoveries that only appear on the day.細かい計画はストレスを減らしますが、驚きも減らすことがあります。
Shared travel works best when the group distinguishes non-negotiable bookings from flexible time.同行者とは、何を決め、何を自由にしておくか合意する必要があります。
A hybrid itinerary can secure essential access while preserving room for weather, energy, and discovery.よい旅行の多くは、基本だけ計画し、その周りに自由な時間を残します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A good surprise (よい予想外)

WARM-UP 1What kind of surprise can improve a trip?どんな予想外の出来事が、旅行をよくすることがありますか？

Follow up
  • When would the same surprise be stressful?
  • Would unexpected rain ruin a trip or make it memorable?

The first decision (最初の決定)

WARM-UP 2What would you decide first for a short trip tomorrow?明日短い旅行へ行くなら、最初に何を決めますか？

Follow up
  • What could stay open?
  • Which would you book first: transport or a place to stay?

Plan or decide later (計画か現地で決めるか)

QUESTION 1For a short trip, would you rather follow a plan or make decisions as you go?短い旅行なら、計画に従うのと、その場で決めるのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • What would you leave undecided?
  • Which is worse: missing a booking or missing a surprise?

Too much planning (計画しすぎる)

QUESTION 2What can go wrong when every minute of a trip is planned?旅行の一分一分まで計画すると、何がうまくいかなくなることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the plan would feel restrictive?
  • Would you enjoy a trip with every meal booked?

Only the room is booked (宿だけ予約)

QUESTION 3Would one hotel booking make you feel prepared for a week-long trip?一週間の旅行で宿だけ予約してあれば、準備できたと感じますか？

Follow up
  • What would you do on the first day?
  • Would you feel calmer with transport booked too?

Two travel styles (二つの旅行スタイル)

QUESTION 4Which trip decision should people settle before travelling together?一緒に旅行する前に、どの旅行の決め事を決めておくべきですか？

Follow up
  • How could people compromise?
  • Which causes more arguments: money or timing?

A trip that needs a plan (計画が必要な旅行)

QUESTION 5What kind of trip needs a detailed plan?どんな旅行には詳しい計画が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • What could go wrong without a plan?
  • Which needs more planning: a beach weekend or an international trip?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • What does your tutor always book first?
  • Would they plan more for an expensive trip?
