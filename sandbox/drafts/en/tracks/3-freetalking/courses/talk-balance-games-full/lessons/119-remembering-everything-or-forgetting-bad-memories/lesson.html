BALANCE GAME

Remembering everything or forgetting bad memories? (すべて覚える、それとも嫌な記憶を忘れる？)

Let's compare remembering everything and forgetting bad memories, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.すべてを覚えていることとつらい記憶を忘れることを比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Memory consolidation during sleep preserves selected information rather than every experienced detail.睡眠中に強くなる記憶もあれば、薄れる記憶もあります。
Memory can preserve an event's gist while losing source, sequence, and verbal detail.人は、出来事の正確な言葉や順番より、意味をよく覚えていることがあります。
A memory trigger may reactivate emotion before a person can place the memory in a coherent account.記憶のきっかけによって、出来事全体が分かる前に感情が戻ることがあります。
External records can support continuity and learning without requiring total biological memory.文章や写真なら、完全な記憶がなくても役立つ細部を残せます。
Memory begins with selective attention, so many details are never encoded rather than later forgotten.そもそも何を記憶するかは、そのとき何に注意を向けたかで変わります。
Emotion can preserve the force of an experience while factual detail becomes incomplete.つらい記憶は、普通の細部が薄れても鮮明に感じられることがあります。
Letting details fade may reduce distress while weakening evidence, continuity, or learning.忘れることは心を守りますが、役立つ学びも失うことがあります。
Selective forgetting can support abstraction, although it may also discard evidence that later matters.細かいことを一部忘れると、全体のパターンや学びが見えやすくなることがあります。
Perfect recall would preserve trivial pain and social mistakes alongside valuable knowledge.すべて覚えるなら、失敗や恥ずかしい出来事も鮮明に残ります。
The better question is how to preserve learning and continuity while giving people agency and support around distressing memories.よい選択は、役立つ記憶を守りながら、つらい記憶とともに生きる人を支えるものです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A memory from a photo (写真で戻る記憶)

WARM-UP 1Why can a small visual cue restore a forgotten positive memory?写真を見ると、どんな楽しい記憶が戻ることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Which sensory detail seems to reactivate the memory?
  • How could later knowledge have changed the recalled scene?

A small moment to keep (残したい小さな出来事)

WARM-UP 2What makes an ordinary moment important enough to preserve?長く覚えていたい小さな出来事は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which meaning would make the ordinary detail valuable later?
  • Would an external record preserve what memory might lose?

Perfect recall or relief (完全な記憶か安心)

QUESTION 1Would you prefer perfect recall of ordinary details or the ability to let painful memories fade?日常の細部を完全に覚えるのと、つらい記憶を薄れさせられるのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which benefit depends on continuity and which on relief?
  • What form of support could reduce the cost of either choice?

Memory in learning (学びと記憶)

QUESTION 2Which parts of study and work would improve—and which might become harder—with perfect recall?完全に覚えられたら、学習や仕事はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Would perfect recall improve judgment or only access to detail?
  • Which kind of mistake might become harder to move beyond?

Every feeling stays vivid (すべて鮮明)

QUESTION 3If trivial pain remained as vivid as valuable knowledge, would perfect recall still appeal?失敗や恥ずかしいことも鮮明に残るなら、完全な記憶を選びますか？

Follow up
  • How would unimportant pain compete with useful information?
  • Would control over when memories appear change the choice?

When the lesson fades (学びも薄れる)

QUESTION 4If relief came with losing part of what the experience taught, would it remain worthwhile?つらさを忘れると学びも薄れるなら、選択は変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the lesson could be preserved outside memory?
  • How would agency change the value of forgetting?

A different kind of memory (記憶の種類)

QUESTION 5What difference in usefulness, pain, or identity would reverse your choice?どんな記憶なら、反対の能力を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which distinction between fact, feeling, and identity is decisive?
  • Would the answer differ for recent and distant memories?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between remembering everything and forgetting bad memories, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターにすべてを覚えていることとつらい記憶を忘れることのどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • How does the tutor weigh continuity against relief?
  • Which kind of memory would reverse their answer?
