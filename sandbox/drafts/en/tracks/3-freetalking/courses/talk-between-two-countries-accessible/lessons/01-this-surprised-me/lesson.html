BETWEEN TWO COUNTRIES

Something that surprised you about another culture (異文化で驚いたこと)

Let's talk about something that surprised you about another country.ほかの国について驚いたことを話しましょう。

Tell one clear story一つの出来事を具体的に話す
Say what you expected予想していたことを言う
Explain what changed変わったことを説明する

Why small things surprise us (小さな違いに驚く理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Before people visit another country, they often imagine it through films, social media, and stories from friends.人はほかの国を訪れる前に、映画やSNS、友人の話を通してその国を想像することがよくあります。
These early ideas do not show the whole picture, but they can still feel true.最初にできたイメージは全体を表していなくても、本当のことのように感じられます。
Then daily life may be different from what they expected.すると、実際の日常生活が予想と違うことがあります。
A small thing—how people wait in line, share food, or talk to strangers—can be more surprising than a famous place.並び方や食べ物の分け方、知らない人への話し方など、小さなことのほうが有名な場所より驚くことがあります。
Because it is part of daily life, the detail can feel personal and stay in our memory.日常生活の一部だからこそ、その細かな違いは身近に感じられ、記憶に残ることがあります。
At first, we may think the way we know is normal and the other way is strange.最初は、自分が知っているやり方が普通で、別のやり方は変だと思うことがあります。
Our first reaction can help if we ask a question before we decide.すぐに決めつけず、まず問いを持てば、最初の反応も役に立ちます。
But one experience cannot show a whole country, because people have different habits in different places, age groups, and situations.でも、一つの体験だけで国全体は分かりません。場所や年齢層、状況によって、人々の習慣は違うからです。
Talking with different people or seeing the same situation again can change our first idea.違う人と話したり、同じ状況をもう一度見たりすると、最初の考えが変わることがあります。
If we ask why something surprised us, we can learn about both the new culture and our own expectations.なぜ驚いたのかを考えると、新しい文化と自分の予想の両方について学べます。expectation予想
Comparing both places can also help us notice our own habits at home.二つの場所を比べると、自国での自分たちの習慣にも気づきやすくなります。
Later, the surprise may become curiosity and change how we see daily life at home.あとになると、その驚きが好奇心に変わり、自国の日常生活の見え方まで変えることがあります。curiosity好奇心

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

Your story (あなたの話)

WARM-UP 1Tell me about a time something surprised you about another country—or about someone from there.ほかの国、またはその国の人について、何かに驚いたときのことを教えてください。

Follow up
  • Did it happen in person, online, or through a story?
  • What surprised you most?
  • Had anything like that happened before?

One detail (一つの細部)

WARM-UP 2What small detail from that moment do you remember most clearly?そのときの小さなことで、いちばんはっきり覚えているものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What exactly did you see, hear, or notice?
  • Why did that detail stand out?

What happened? (何が起きた？)

QUESTION 1What happened before you felt surprised?驚く前に、何が起きましたか？

Follow up
  • When did you notice something was different?
  • What happened right after that?

Your expectation (予想していたこと)

QUESTION 2What did you expect to happen instead?その代わりに、何が起きると思っていましたか？

Follow up
  • What was most different from what you expected?
  • Did the difference feel good, bad, or simply new?
  • Did anyone else you know expect the same thing?

Where it came from (予想の出どころ)

QUESTION 3Where do you think that idea came from?その考えはどこから来たと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Did it come from media, another person, or an earlier experience?
  • Why did you believe it?
  • Had you heard a different view before?

In the moment (その場で)

QUESTION 4How did you react at the time?そのとき、どう反応しましたか？

Follow up
  • Did you show your surprise or hide it?
  • What happened because of your response?
  • Would you respond differently now?

What changed? (何が変わった？)

QUESTION 5How did the experience change how you see the country or its people?その経験で、その国や人々への見方はどう変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • What do you notice now that you did not notice before?
  • What part of your old idea has not changed?
  • Do you act differently in similar situations now?

Turn it around (逆に考えよう)

QUESTION 6What part of everyday life in Japan might surprise someone visiting for the first time?日本を初めて訪れる人は、日常生活のどんなところに驚くと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Where would they notice it?
  • What might they expect instead?
  • What should they understand before they react?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。
