What first times teach us (初めての体験が教えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Daily routines become easier, but they can make our world feel smaller.日課は生活を楽にしますが、自分の世界を小さくすることもあります。
Trying something new makes us pay attention again.初めてのことをすると、もう一度よく注意するようになります。
We usually imagine the experience before we actually have it.新しい体験の前には、実際にするより先に頭の中で想像します。
Nervousness can mean that the result matters to us.緊張は、その結果を大切に思っているしるしかもしれません。nervousness緊張
Clear instructions or a supportive person can make the first step easier.分かりやすい説明や支えてくれる人がいると、最初の一歩が楽になります。
The real experience is often different from the version in our head.実際の体験は、頭の中で考えていたものと違うことがよくあります。
Being bad at first can feel uncomfortable when we are used to being capable.普段はできることが多い人にとって、初心者の感覚は居心地が悪いことがあります。
Trying it again shows whether the experience was only exciting because it was new.もう一度すると、新しいから楽しかっただけかどうかが分かります。
Remembering first times helps us notice that we are still changing.最近の初めてを覚えておくと、自分がまだ変わっていると気づけます。
One new experience often points toward the next thing we want to try.一つの新しい体験が、次に挑戦したいことにつながることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A recent first (最近の初めて)

WARM-UP 1Have you tried anything for the first time recently?最近、初めて試したことはありますか？

Follow up
  • What was the experience?
  • How did it feel to be new at it?

Feeling new (初めての感覚)

WARM-UP 2When did you last feel like a beginner?最後に初心者のように感じたのはいつですか？

Follow up
  • What made it feel new?
  • How did you respond to that feeling?

Why people try (試す理由)

QUESTION 1What can finally make someone try something new?人がついに新しいことを試すきっかけは何だと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Does another person's help matter?
  • Can waiting make the first step harder?

Expectation and reality (予想と実際)

QUESTION 2Are new experiences usually what you expect?新しい体験は、普段、予想どおりですか？

Follow up
  • What is often easier?
  • What is often harder?

Trying again (もう一度)

QUESTION 3What makes you want to try something again?もう一度やってみたいと思うのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What would you change the second time?
  • Does doing it with someone help?

The first before that (その前の初めて)

QUESTION 4Can you remember another first-time experience?ほかの初めての体験を思い出せますか？

Follow up
  • Which experience comes to mind?
  • What made that first time memorable?

Staying a beginner (初心者でいること)

QUESTION 5How do you feel when you're not good at something yet?まだ上手にできないとき、どんな気持ちになりますか？

Follow up
  • What helps you continue?
  • When do you decide to stop?

The next first (次の初めて)

QUESTION 6Is there anything you'd like to try next?次にやってみたいことはありますか？

Follow up
  • What would you like to try?
  • What makes now a good or bad time?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
