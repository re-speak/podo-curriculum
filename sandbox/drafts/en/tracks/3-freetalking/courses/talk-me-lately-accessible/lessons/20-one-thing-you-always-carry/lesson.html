Let's use what's in your bag to talk about your daily life. Please read the title aloud.かばんの中身から、日常生活について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

The life inside a bag (かばんの中にある生活)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A bag holds the things we expect to need away from home.かばんには、外出先で必要だと思う物を入れます。
The things we use every day are usually easy to reach.毎日使う物は、たいてい取り出しやすい場所に入っています。
Other items stay there for an emergency that may never happen.ほかの物は、起こらないかもしれない緊急時のために入っています。emergency緊急時
Receipts and forgotten objects can stay in a bag for months because we never decide to remove them.レシートや忘れた物は、出すか決めないまま何か月もかばんに残ることがあります。
A heavy bag can show how many different situations we are trying to prepare for.かばんが重いと、いろいろな状況に備えようとしていることが分かります。
The contents change when our work, health, or responsibilities change.仕事や健康、責任が変わると、中身も変わります。
Phones have replaced many separate tools, but they have also added chargers.スマートフォンは多くの道具に取って代わりましたが、今度は充電器を持つ必要が生まれました。
Some objects are useful mainly because they make us feel prepared.実際に役立つより、持っていると安心する物もあります。
Cleaning a bag forces us to decide what still belongs in our routine.かばんを整理すると、今の生活にまだ必要な物か決めることになります。
One ordinary object can tell a surprisingly detailed story about a person's day.普通の一つの物から、その人の一日について意外に詳しいことが分かります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A bag today (今日のかばん)

WARM-UP 1Which everyday item would be most inconvenient to forget?日常の持ち物で、忘れるといちばん困る物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What need does it meet?
  • How easy would it be to replace?

What you carry (持ち歩く物)

WARM-UP 2What would you carry for an ordinary day out?ふつうの外出なら、あなたは何を持っていきますか？

Follow up
  • Which item would go in a bag rather than a pocket?
  • What could stay at home?

What's inside (入っている物)

QUESTION 1What tends to take up the most space in your bag or pockets?かばんやポケットの中で、何がいちばん場所を取りやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Which category takes the most space?
  • What do you carry without using very often?

What you use (実際に使う物)

QUESTION 2What do you use most when you are away from home?外出中、いちばんよく使う物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would be hard to replace?
  • What saves you the most time?

There for months (何か月もある物)

QUESTION 3Which item are you most likely to carry for months without using?何か月も使わないまま持ち歩きそうな物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why might it stay there so long?
  • What would finally make you remove it?

Always forgotten (いつも忘れる物)

QUESTION 4Is there anything you often forget when you go out?外出するとき、よく忘れる物はありますか？

Follow up
  • What helps you remember it?
  • What do you do when it is missing?

How it changed (変わった持ち物)

QUESTION 5How have phones changed what people carry?スマートフォンによって、人の持ち物はどう変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • Which item has become less necessary?
  • What new item has become common?

What it says (持ち物が語ること)

QUESTION 6What do the things you carry every day say about your day?普段の持ち物から、あなたの一日について何が分かりますか？

Follow up
  • What best shows your usual routine?
  • What might someone get wrong?
