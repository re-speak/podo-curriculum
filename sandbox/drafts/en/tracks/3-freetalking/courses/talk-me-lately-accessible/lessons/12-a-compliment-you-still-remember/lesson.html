Why some praise stays with us (心に残る褒め言葉)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A compliment can be only a few words and still stay with us for years.褒め言葉は短くても、何年も心に残ることがあります。
Specific praise often feels more believable than a general compliment.具体的な褒め言葉は、誰にでも当てはまる一般的な褒め言葉より信じやすく感じます。
It matters when the speaker noticed effort that other people missed.ほかの人が気づかなかった努力を見てくれたとき、その言葉は特に大切になります。
Many people feel awkward and quickly reject praise.褒められると気まずく感じ、すぐ否定してしまう人も多いです。
A simple thank-you accepts the kindness without claiming too much.簡単にお礼を言えば、自慢せずに相手の好意を受け取れます。
Criticism can be easier to remember because our minds watch for danger.人は危険に注意しやすいため、批判のほうを覚えやすいことがあります。criticism批判
Saving a meaningful compliment can balance that negative habit.意味のある褒め言葉を覚えておくと、その悪い傾向とのバランスを取れます。
Giving good praise also requires attention to another person's real work.よい褒め言葉を伝えるには、相手の本当の努力を見る必要があります。
The best time is often soon after the action, while the detail is clear.具体的なことを覚えているうちに伝えると、褒め言葉はより効果的です。
One honest sentence can change how someone sees their own ability.たった一つの正直な言葉で、自分の能力についての見方が変わることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

全部答えなくても大丈夫です。一つの答えをゆっくり広げましょう。

A remembered compliment (覚えている褒め言葉)

WARM-UP 1Is there a compliment you still remember?今も覚えている褒め言葉はありますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, when did you hear it?
  • If not, what kind of praise feels meaningful to you?

Praise you like (うれしい褒め言葉)

WARM-UP 2What kind of praise do you most like hearing?どんな褒め言葉を言われると、いちばんうれしいですか？

Follow up
  • Do you prefer praise about effort or results?
  • Do you like short or detailed comments?

Whose praise matters (心に残る相手)

QUESTION 1Whose praise would stay with you most?誰から褒められると、一番心に残りますか？

Follow up
  • Have they ever praised you?
  • Why does that person's view matter?

Did you believe it? (信じられた？)

QUESTION 2Would you believe that kind of compliment?そのような褒め言葉を信じられると思いますか？

Follow up
  • What would make it believable?
  • What might make it hard to accept?

Your response (そのときの返事)

QUESTION 3How do you usually respond to praise?褒められたとき、普段どう返事をしますか？

Follow up
  • What do you feel but not say?
  • Does your answer change with the person?

What stays longer (長く残るもの)

QUESTION 4Do you remember praise or negative comments better?褒め言葉と批判では、どちらをよく覚えていますか？

Follow up
  • Why do those words stay with you?
  • Do they affect what you do next?

Praise you gave (自分が伝えた言葉)

QUESTION 5Have you given someone a real compliment recently?最近、誰かを心から褒めましたか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what had the person done?
  • If not, what sometimes stops you?

Someone who deserves it (褒めたい人)

QUESTION 6Is there someone you would like to compliment now?今、褒めたい人はいますか？

Follow up
  • If yes, what exactly would you praise?
  • If not, what kind of effort deserves more praise?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
