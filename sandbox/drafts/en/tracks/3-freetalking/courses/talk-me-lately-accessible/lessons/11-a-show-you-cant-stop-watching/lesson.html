Let's talk about what keeps you watching a show. Please read the title aloud.つい見続けてしまう作品について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why we keep watching (見続けたくなる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Streaming gives us more shows than we could ever finish.配信サービスには、見終えられないほど多くの作品があります。streaming動画配信
Choosing what to start can be harder than choosing what to continue.何を見るか決めるほうが、続きを見るか決めるより難しいことがあります。
A strong first episode gives us a question that needs an answer.よい第1話は、答えを知りたくなる疑問を残します。
Characters can keep us watching even when the story moves slowly.物語の進みが遅くても、登場人物の魅力で見続けることがあります。
Recommendations also help because they come with someone else's excitement.人からのおすすめには、その人の熱意も含まれていて、見続ける理由になります。
Binge-watching several episodes can be fun, but it can also steal sleep.何話も一気見するのは楽しい一方、睡眠やほかの予定の時間を奪うこともあります。binge-watch一気見する
Dropping a show halfway is reasonable when it no longer feels rewarding.楽しめなくなった作品を途中でやめるのは、悪いことではありません。
The right show often depends on our mood and available attention.その日に合う作品は、気分や集中できる度合いによって変わります。
A satisfying ending can change how we remember the whole series.結末に満足できるかどうかで、作品全体の記憶が変わることがあります。
Finishing one show usually sends us looking for the next feeling, not the same story.一つ見終えると、同じ物語より、同じ気持ちを味わえる次の作品を探すことが多いです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Watching now (今見ているもの)

WARM-UP 1Are you watching any shows at the moment?今、何か作品を見ていますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of show fits your mood right now?
  • What usually makes you start one?

Your last episode (直近のエピソード)

WARM-UP 2When was the last time you watched an episode of a show?最後に作品の一話を見たのはいつですか？

Follow up
  • What do you remember about that episode?
  • What do you watch when you do not want a series?

How far in (どこまで見る？)

QUESTION 1How far do you usually get before you decide to continue a show?作品を見続けるか決めるまで、普段どのくらい見ますか？

Follow up
  • What helps you decide?
  • Have you ever decided after one episode?

The pull (見続ける理由)

QUESTION 2What usually makes you keep watching a show?普段、どんなことがあると作品を見続けますか？

Follow up
  • Is the story or a character more important?
  • At what point do you get interested?

Finding a show (作品の見つけ方)

QUESTION 3How do you usually find a new show?普段、新しい作品をどうやって見つけますか？

Follow up
  • Do you trust friends or an app more?
  • What makes a show look interesting?

A show you dropped (途中でやめた作品)

QUESTION 4What usually makes someone stop watching a show halfway?作品を途中で見るのをやめるのは、ふつうどんな理由からですか？

Follow up
  • Which problem loses attention fastest?
  • What might make it worth trying again?

Enough is enough (見すぎの境目)

QUESTION 5When does watching another episode become a bad idea?もう1話見るのがよくない判断になるのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What would you need to do the next day?
  • How late is too late for you?

What's next (次に見るもの)

QUESTION 6What kind of show would you like to watch next?次はどんな作品を見たいですか？

Follow up
  • Do you want a similar feeling or something different?
  • Where would you look for ideas?
