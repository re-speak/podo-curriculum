WHAT IF

One thing you would make free forever (一生無料にしたい一つのもの)

Let's choose one free service and explore how life would change. Please read the title aloud.一つのサービスを無料にして、生活がどう変わるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

When free changes behaviour (無料が行動を変えるとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Free access changes more than the price.無料になると、値段以外のことも変わります。
People may use a free service more even when they do not need it.必要がなくても、無料なら使う回数が増えるかもしれません。
The best choice depends on real use, not the highest price.最も高い物より、実際によく使う物のほうがよい選択かもしれません。
Some people would choose for convenience; others for opportunity.便利さで選ぶ人もいれば、新しい機会のために選ぶ人もいます。
A benefit can reach other people without being resold.転売しなくても、無料の利益をほかの人に広げられます。
To resell means to sell something again after receiving it.転売するとは、受け取った物をもう一度売ることです。resell転売する
The boring answer may solve a larger everyday cost.退屈な答えでも、毎日の大きな費用を減らせるかもしれません。
Free use can still cost time, space, attention, or energy.値段がゼロでも、時間、場所、注意、エネルギーは必要です。
Free access can reveal whether scarcity created the original desire.いつでも無料で使えると、もともとの欲求が希少性から生まれたのか分かることがあります。
The choice reveals what we want more of in ordinary life.何を無料にするかで、日常にもっとほしいものが見えます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Free for life (一生無料)

WARM-UP 1If one everyday service were free for life, which would you choose?日常のサービスを一つ一生無料にできるなら、何を選びますか？

Follow up
  • How often would you use it?
  • What makes that service especially useful?

Real use (実際の利用)

WARM-UP 2How often would a free service need to be used to make it valuable?無料のサービスは、どのくらい使えば価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Which service would meet that level of use?
  • What could make frequent use less valuable?

A changed week (変わる一週間)

QUESTION 1How could one free service change an ordinary week?一つの無料サービスで、普通の一週間はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Which day or routine would change most?
  • What would you do with the time or money saved?

Beyond you (自分以外への利益)

QUESTION 2How could other people benefit from one person's free service?一人の無料サービスから、ほかの人はどう恩恵を受けられますか？

Follow up
  • How could you share it legally?
  • What makes the benefit personal?

The boring answer (退屈でも正しい答え)

QUESTION 3Which boring service would be the most sensible one to make free?地味でも、無料にするのが最も賢いサービスは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which long-term cost would it remove?
  • Would practical value change what you preferred?

Reselling (転売)

QUESTION 4When would reselling free access be fair or unfair?無料の利用権を転売することは、どんなときに公平、または不公平ですか？

Follow up
  • What rule would have to allow it?
  • What makes resale unfair or pointless?

Still not free (残る負担)

QUESTION 5What hidden costs can remain when a service is free?サービスが無料でも、どんな見えない負担が残りますか？

Follow up
  • Which hidden cost would matter most?
  • How could a provider reduce that cost?

Paying on purpose (あえて払うもの)

QUESTION 6What would make a paid service better than a free alternative?有料のサービスが無料の代わりよりよくなるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What extra value would payment buy?
  • What makes the free version enough?
