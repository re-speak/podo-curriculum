Let's explore what a week with less phone use would change. Please read the title aloud.携帯電話を減らす一週間で何が変わるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What a phone-free week would test (携帯電話なしの一週間で試されること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A digital detox means choosing a period with less optional device use.デジタルデトックスとは、任意のデジタル機器の利用を減らす期間を選ぶことです。digital detoxデジタル機器から離れること
For some people, a phone is optional entertainment.人によっては、携帯電話は主に娯楽の道具です。
For others, it is an access, safety, work, or care tool.別の人には、アクセス、安全、仕事、ケアのための道具です。
In this topic, what breaks first means what stops working first.この話で「最初に駄目になるもの」とは、最初に機能しなくなるものを指します。
Maps, payments, tickets, and messages have moved into one device.地図、支払い、切符、メッセージが一つの機器に集まっています。
Missing the phone is not the same as missing every app.携帯電話が恋しいことと、すべてのアプリが必要なことは同じではありません。
Less interruption could improve focus or rest.中断が減ると、集中や休息がよくなるかもしれません。
A week without a phone may require another device or another person.携帯電話なしの一週間には、別の機器や人の助けが必要かもしれません。
Time offline can reveal which phone functions are truly important.携帯電話を使わない時間があると、本当に大切な機能が分かります。
A useful experiment removes what is optional and protects what is essential.よい実験は、不要なものを減らし、必要なものを守ります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Seven days (七日間)

WARM-UP 1Which phone feature would you miss most for one week?一週間、携帯電話のどの機能が最も恋しくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Would you miss maps, messages, or payments most?
  • What could you use instead?

How the day begins (一日の始まり方)

WARM-UP 2What, if anything, do you usually use your phone for first?普段、最初にスマートフォンを使うとしたら、何に使いますか？

Follow up
  • Could that first task be done another way?
  • What happens on a day when you do not use it?

Easiest to live without (なくても困りにくい機能)

QUESTION 1Which phone feature would be easiest to live without?なくても最も困りにくいスマートフォンの機能は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you use instead?
  • Why is another feature harder to lose?

What might improve (よくなりそうなこと)

QUESTION 2What might improve, if anything, after three days with less phone use?スマホをあまり使わずに三日過ごしたら、何かよくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would sleep, focus, or free time change?
  • What could become harder with less phone use?

Leaving the phone behind (スマホを別の部屋に置くこと)

QUESTION 3When is it easiest to leave your phone in another room?携帯電話を別の部屋に置きやすいのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Would meals, exercise, or bedtime be easiest?
  • What makes a phone hard to leave?

Reachable in an emergency (緊急時につながる方法)

QUESTION 4How could you stay reachable in an emergency while using your phone less?携帯電話を減らしながら、緊急時に連絡を受けられるようにするにはどうしますか？

Follow up
  • Who would need another way to contact you?
  • Which emergency feature would stay available?

One feature to keep (残したい一つの機能)

QUESTION 5Which phone feature would you keep during a break?携帯電話を休む間でも、どの機能は使えるようにしますか？

Follow up
  • Why should that function remain available?
  • Which optional use could be limited around it?

One habit to change (変えたい一つの習慣)

QUESTION 6Which phone habit would you most like to change?どの携帯電話の習慣を最も変えたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you change late-night use or constant checking?
  • Which phone habit is worth keeping?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
