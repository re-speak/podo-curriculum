The animal your friends would choose for you (自分を知る人が選びそうな動物)

Let's use animal comparisons to explore personality. Please read the title aloud.動物の比較を使って、性格について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What an animal comparison can symbolize (動物の比較が象徴するもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Animal comparisons turn personality into a quick picture.動物の比較は、性格をすぐ分かるイメージに変えます。
To symbolize means to represent an idea or quality.象徴するとは、考えや特徴を表すことです。symbolize象徴する
The same animal can symbolize different things to different people.同じ動物でも、人によって象徴することが違います。
People who know us may notice habits we overlook.自分を知る人は、自分では見落とす習慣に気づくことがあります。
Family, classmates, colleagues, and other observers may notice different traits.家族、同級生、同僚など、見る人によって気づく特徴は異なります。
Our own choice may differ because we value a different side of ourselves.自分の選択は、自分の別の面を大切にするので違うかもしれません。
The difference can be funny without being an insult.違いは、悪口でなくても面白く感じられます。
An animal we could never be shows where the comparison fails.絶対に自分ではない動物から、比較の限界が分かります。
Choosing an animal for someone else requires evidence.ほかの人に動物を選ぶには、根拠が必要です。
The best comparison opens conversation instead of ending it.よい比較は会話を終わらせず、広げます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your animal (自分を表す動物)

WARM-UP 1Which animal best represents your personality?どの動物があなたの性格を最もよく表しますか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a dog, cat, fox, or bird?
  • Where does the comparison stop working?

Your friends' choice (友人が選ぶ動物)

WARM-UP 2Which animal would your friends choose for you?友達はあなたにどの動物を選ぶと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Would they choose a dog, cat, or bird?
  • How is a dog's personality different from a cat's?

Your own choice (自分で選ぶ動物)

QUESTION 1Which animal quality would you like to have more of?どんな動物の性質を、もっと自分にも持ちたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a dog's loyalty, a cat's independence, or a bird's freedom?
  • When would that quality help?

What friends notice (友人が気づくこと)

QUESTION 2What can friends notice about you that you may not notice?自分では気づかなくても、友達が気づくあなたの特徴は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which habit might they notice?
  • Do people notice a person's voice or body language first?

Not that animal (絶対に違う動物)

QUESTION 3Which animal is least like you?あなたに最も似ていない動物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would a loud, slow, or highly social animal feel least like you?
  • Could one trait still fit?

A busy day or a day off (忙しい日か休みの日か)

QUESTION 4Which animal fits you on a busy day?忙しい日の自分には、どの動物が合いますか？

Follow up
  • Which trait does that animal show?
  • On a busy day, do you move quickly or slowly?

Three real traits (三つの本当の特徴)

QUESTION 5I'll share three real traits about myself. Which animal fits them?私が自分の本当の特徴を三つ話します。どの動物が合いますか？

Follow up
  • Which trait has the strongest influence on your choice?
  • What does the animal comparison fail to capture?

Beyond one animal (一つの動物を超えて)

QUESTION 6What important part of a person can an animal comparison miss?動物のたとえでは、人のどんな大切な部分を表せませんか？

Follow up
  • Could one animal show both kindness and ambition?
  • Would using two animals be fairer?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
