# Accepted freetalking editorial decisions · 2026-08

The curriculum owner accepted three previously open product policies. Exact lesson-field changes
are recorded in [accepted-decisions-2026-08.issues.jsonl](accepted-decisions-2026-08.issues.jsonl).

## D01 · Targeted comfort and skip guidance

Applied brief alternatives and skip permission to six personally sensitive prompts at both levels:
light embarrassment, painful childhood language, school nicknames, embarrassing mistakes, a trip
to the past, and a ten-year-future prompt. Ordinary light prompts do not repeat the warning.

Result: 12 tutor-guidance fields across 12 decks.

## D17 · Realistic correction-focused contract

The common option now promises to record important sentences or recurring errors and correct them
at a point that does not break the conversation flow. It no longer implies that every sentence is
transcribed and interrupted immediately.

Result: two common strings propagated to all 182 decks (364 string replacements).

## D18 · Genuine Intermediate simplification

Question count, topic, page IDs and follow-up availability remain unchanged. After checking the
existing level differences, the remaining high-burden prompts were rewritten around one person,
action or concrete situation instead of abstract relationships, conditions or principle-level
argumentation.

Result: 34 fields supporting 15 Intermediate prompts across five themes. The superpower question
also received three concrete follow-ups; deep-topic skip guidance remained intact.
