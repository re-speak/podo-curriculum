Variant 01

Hanji — warm editorial

Korean paper, ink, and a single persimmon seal. Serif Korean type gives the language itself center stage; answers are written on worksheet rules, and dialogues read like a script. Calm and premium — best for adult learners and a "slow study" brand.

Variant 02

Workbook — modern textbook

Swiss discipline: one cobalt accent, mono labels, ghost page numbers, spec-sheet hero. Everything sits on a strict grid, so fifty lessons later every page still feels like the same product. Best if the curriculum should read as a serious, systematic course.

Variant 03

Jua Pop — playful stickers

Rounded Jua type, chunky outlines, hard candy shadows. The lesson feels like a sticker book — high energy and instantly approachable, with color-coded activity pills doing the wayfinding. Best for younger learners or a brand that wants Duolingo-level warmth without the mascot.

Variant 04

Podo App — unified with the product

The lesson speaks the app's exact design language: Pretendard, gray-100 exercise blocks, the segmented lesson progress bar, green unit labels, the blue info panel, and the lime sticker-button as chapter pages. A student moving between app and lesson never changes worlds.

Variant 05

Podo Bold — brand tokens, graphic weight

Same lime-and-ink design tokens as the app, pushed to print weight: every page is a chunky outlined card sitting on a hard shadow (the app's button construction, scaled up), lime kicker pills, and a dark hero and chapter pages shadowed in lime. Louder than Variant 04, still unmistakably the same brand.

How to choose: all five variants carry the exact same lesson structure (kicker → title → one-line instruction → single activity), so switching styles is a pure CSS swap. Hanji slows the student down and flatters the language; Workbook scales cleanest across a long curriculum; Jua Pop maximizes approachability for grammar-shy beginners. Variants 04 and 05 borrow the live app's tokens (Pretendard, #1C1C1C ink, #B5FD4C lime, gray-100 blocks): 04 is the faithful "same product" reading, 05 turns those tokens up to print weight. If brand unification matters most, start from 04 and pull 05's transition pages in for chapter breaks.
