WHAT IF

What you would really do if you won the lottery (宝くじに当たったら本当にすること)

Let's imagine how a large lottery win could change everyday life. Please read the title aloud.宝くじで大当たりしたら、日常がどう変わるか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What a sudden win reveals (突然の大当たりで見えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A sudden jackpot creates immediate choices long before it provides a sensible plan.突然の大当たりは、現実的な計画を与えるより先に、すぐ決めるべき選択を生みます。
The first imagined action often exposes what currently feels most urgent.最初に思い浮かぶ行動には、今もっとも切迫していることが表れやすいです。
Some people would share the news immediately, while others would protect their privacy.すぐ知らせる人もいれば、自分のプライバシーを守ろうとする人もいます。
Financial freedom does not automatically settle whether paid or unpaid work still gives life structure.経済的に自由になっても、有給・無給の仕事が生活に必要な形を与えるかは自動的には決まりません。financial freedom経済的な自由
A first purchase is only one response; waiting, helping, or changing nothing are equally possible.最初に買うだけでなく、待つ、誰かを助ける、何も変えないという答えもあります。
A decision made during the initial excitement may look different once the shock has passed.最初の興奮の中で決めたことは、衝撃が過ぎると違って見えることがあります。
Regret may come from acting too quickly, waiting too long, or not changing anything at all.急ぎすぎたこと、待ちすぎたこと、何も変えなかったことのどれも後悔になり得ます。
The unfamiliar life might become ordinary within weeks, or remain unsettled for years.慣れない生活が数週間で日常になる場合も、何年も安定しない場合もあります。
Money can remove practical limits without supplying purpose, trust, or direction.お金は現実的な制約を減らせますが、目的や信頼、進む方向までは与えません。
The deeper question is which life the money should make possible rather than which object it should buy.本質的なのは、何を買うかではなく、そのお金でどんな人生を可能にしたいかという問いです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The first move (最初の行動)

WARM-UP 1Imagine you win a large lottery prize tomorrow. What would you do first?明日、宝くじで大当たりしたと想像してください。最初に何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Would you call someone, make a plan, or do nothing for a day?
  • What would you avoid doing immediately?

The first call (最初に電話する相手)

WARM-UP 2Whose advice, if anyone, would you want before making a big decision?大きな決断をする前に、相談したい相手はいますか？

Follow up
  • What could that person help you notice?
  • If you asked no one, how would you slow the decision down?

Who knows (誰に知らせるか)

QUESTION 1Who, if anyone, would need to know about the win?当選について、知らせる必要がある人がいるとすれば誰ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would have a practical reason to know?
  • What benefit could privacy provide?

Work after the win (当選後の仕事)

QUESTION 2Which part of your current life would you protect from the changes money could bring?お金による変化から、今の生活のどんな部分を守りたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you keep the same home, hobbies, or daily schedule?
  • How might money still change it?

Buying for someone else (誰かのために買う物)

QUESTION 3What's the first thing you would buy for someone else?ほかの人のために最初に買うものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a gift, a trip, or practical help?
  • If you bought nothing, how else could you share the money?

A possible regret (後悔する可能性)

QUESTION 4Which early decision could create the biggest regret after a lottery win?宝くじに当たった後、どんな早い決断が最も大きな後悔につながりますか？

Follow up
  • What would cause that mistake?
  • Would spending too fast or trusting the wrong person be more dangerous?

Keeping life normal (日常を変えないこと)

QUESTION 5What would you want to keep normal after becoming rich?お金持ちになっても、何を普通のままにしておきたいですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that part worth protecting?
  • Would sudden wealth make ordinary friendships harder?

What money cannot choose (お金では決められないこと)

QUESTION 6Which problem might money make easier without truly solving it?お金で楽にはできても、本当には解決できない問題は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would money solve loneliness or poor health?
  • What else would help?
