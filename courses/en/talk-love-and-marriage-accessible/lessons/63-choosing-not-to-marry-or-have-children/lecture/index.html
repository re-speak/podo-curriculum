LOVE AND MARRIAGE

Choosing not to marry or have children (結婚や子どもを持たない選択)

Let's talk about different choices around marriage and children. Please read the title aloud.結婚や子どもについてのさまざまな選択を話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

More than one life path (一つではない生き方)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Marriage and having children are separate decisions.結婚することと、子どもを持つことは別の決定です。
People may want both, one, neither, or remain unsure.両方、一方だけ、どちらも望まない、またはまだ分からないという人がいます。
Reasons can include desire, health, money, care work, freedom, or values.理由には、希望、健康、費用、人を支える責任、自由、価値観などがあります。
Some reasons are private, and nobody owes a complete explanation.理由には私的なものもあり、すべてを説明する義務はありません。
Family interest can feel supportive to one person and like social pressure to another.家族の関心は、支えに感じる人もいれば、社会的な圧力に感じる人もいます。social pressure社会的な圧力
Relatives may worry about loneliness, regret, or family continuity.家族は、将来の孤独や後悔、家族のつながりが続くかどうかを心配する場合があります。
Those worries can be sincere without deciding what another adult should choose.その心配が本気でも、別の大人の選択を決めるものではありません。
A person's view may change, stay stable, or remain undecided.考えは変わることも、変わらないことも、決まらないままのこともあります。
Housing, work, policy, and support can affect a choice without fully explaining it.住まい、仕事、制度、支援は選択に影響しますが、それだけで理由のすべては説明できません。
Respect begins by asking without assuming that one answer is correct.一つの答えが正しいと決めつけずに聞くことが、敬意の始まりです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The bigger decision (より大きな決断)

WARM-UP 1Which feels like the bigger life decision: marriage or having children?結婚と子どもを持つことでは、どちらのほうが大きな人生の決断だと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that choice feel bigger?
  • When should the two decisions be considered separately?

What feels biggest (いちばん大きいこと)

WARM-UP 2What feels biggest about that decision: daily life, money, freedom, or responsibility?その決断でいちばん大きいのは、日々の暮らし、お金、自由、責任のどれですか？

Follow up
  • Which part is easiest to imagine?
  • Which part do people underestimate?

Marriage without children (子どもを持たない結婚)

QUESTION 1What might make someone want marriage but not children?結婚はしたいけれど、子どもは望まない人には、どんな理由がありそうですか？

Follow up
  • Could freedom, health, or responsibility matter?
  • How might a partner's wishes affect the choice?

Children without marriage (結婚を伴わない子育て)

QUESTION 2What might make someone want children but not marriage?子どもは望むけれど、結婚は望まない人には、どんな理由がありそうですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of support would they need?
  • How would daily life differ?

Family opinions (家族の意見)

QUESTION 3How much should family opinions matter in these decisions?こうした決断に、家族の意見はどのくらい影響してよいと思いますか？

Follow up
  • When does concern become pressure?
  • Which question should relatives avoid?

Now or later (今と将来)

QUESTION 4Which should matter more: what you want now or what you might want later?今の希望と、将来気が変わる可能性では、どちらを大切にすべきですか？

Follow up
  • Is "I haven't decided" a complete answer?
  • What information could help someone decide?

A change of mind (考えが変わるきっかけ)

QUESTION 5What could make someone change their mind about marriage or children?結婚や子どもについて、人の考えが変わるきっかけは何だと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Could money, health, or a relationship change it?
  • Is changing one's mind a problem?

Your life in ten years (10年後の暮らし)

QUESTION 6Imagine your ideal life ten years from now. Who is part of it?10年後の理想の暮らしを想像してください。そこには誰がいますか？

Follow up
  • What does an ordinary day look like?
  • Which part of that life matters most?
