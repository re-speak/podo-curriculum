What makes a wedding feel personal (自分らしい結婚式を作るもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A wedding combines a private commitment with a public event.結婚式は、個人的な約束と、人が集まる行事を組み合わせます。
One small element can carry more meaning than an expensive setting.高価な会場よりも、一つの小さな要素に大きな意味があることがあります。
Guests, cost, family wishes, and tradition can pull the plan in different directions.招待客、費用、家族の希望、伝統によって、計画が別々の方向に引っ張られることがあります。
A wedding tradition is a choice, not an automatic duty.結婚式の習慣は、当然の義務ではなく、選べるものです。wedding tradition結婚式の習慣
Cutting one part may protect the thing that matters most.一つを省くことで、いちばん大切なものを守れる場合があります。
Decisions may involve the couple, their families, or the wider community.決めるときには、二人、家族、周りの人が関わることがあります。
The best wedding memory is not always the most expensive one.いちばんよい結婚式の思い出が、いちばん高価なものとは限りません。
Some people would prefer a small legal ceremony or no wedding event at all.小さな手続きだけの式や、結婚式をまったくしないことを選ぶ人もいます。
A hypothetical wedding can reveal values even for someone with no plans to marry.結婚する予定がなくても、仮定の結婚式から大切な価値観が見えることがあります。
The event feels personal when its choices tell one clear story.選んだものに一つのはっきりした意味があると、式は自分らしく感じられます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One thing to include (欠かせない一つ)

WARM-UP 1If you had a wedding tomorrow, what's the one thing you'd insist on?もし明日結婚式をするなら、絶対に入れたいことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What feeling should it create?
  • Who else would it matter to?

What stays (残すもの)

WARM-UP 2Which wedding tradition is worth keeping?どの結婚式の習慣は残す価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • What does it mean?
  • How could it be updated?

What goes (省くもの)

QUESTION 1Which common wedding tradition would you remove?よくある結婚式の習慣の中で、どれをなくしたいですか？

Follow up
  • What problem would that solve?
  • Who might miss it?

Who decides (決める人)

QUESTION 2Who should have the strongest voice in wedding decisions?結婚式の決定で、誰の意見を最も強く反映すべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which choices can involve family?
  • Which choices belong to the couple?

A memorable wedding (心に残る結婚式)

QUESTION 3What makes a wedding detail memorable?結婚式の一つの演出が心に残るのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Does it need to be expensive?
  • Could it work at a small wedding?

The worst part (大変な部分)

QUESTION 4What part of weddings creates the most unnecessary stress?結婚式のどの部分が、最も不必要なストレスを生みますか？

Follow up
  • Who usually carries the stress?
  • What simple change could reduce it?

Skipping the event (式をしない選択)

QUESTION 5Could a tiny ceremony feel more meaningful than a large wedding?ごく小さな式のほうが、大きな結婚式より意味深く感じられることはありますか？

Follow up
  • What would the smaller event protect?
  • What might be lost?

What the day is for (式の目的)

QUESTION 6What should a wedding be for?結婚式は何のためにあるべきですか？

Follow up
  • Who should feel represented?
  • Which choice would show that purpose?
