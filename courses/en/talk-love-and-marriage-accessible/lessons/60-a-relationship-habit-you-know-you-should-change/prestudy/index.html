Let's talk about relationship habits and how people can change them. Please read the title aloud.人間関係の癖と、それを変える方法について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Changing a relationship pattern (関係の癖を変える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Relationship habits often appear most clearly under stress.関係の中での癖は、ストレスがあるときにはっきり表れることがよくあります。
Some people interrupt; others shut down or ask for reassurance too often.話を遮る人もいれば、黙り込んだり、安心できる言葉を何度も求めたりする人もいます。
We cannot change a habit until we notice when it starts.癖が始まる瞬間に気づかなければ、変えることはできません。
Feedback can be useful without becoming a final judgment about us.相手からの意見は、自分への最終評価にしなくても、役立つことがあります。
Shame can make us defensive instead of helping us change.恥ずかしさが強いと、変わるよりも身構えてしまうことがあります。defensive身構えた
A small replacement action is more useful than a vague promise to be better.もっとよくすると曖昧に約束するより、小さな代わりの行動を決めるほうが役立ちます。
Progress is not perfect, and an old habit may return in a hard week.改善は完璧には進まず、大変な週には昔の癖が戻ることもあります。
The same habit can affect different partners in different ways.同じ癖でも、相手によって受ける影響は違います。
Warning someone about a habit can help, but it does not excuse the harm.自分の癖を前もって伝えることは役立ちますが、相手への影響がなくなるわけではありません。
Looking at our strengths keeps self-improvement from becoming only self-criticism.自分の長所にも目を向けると、改善が自己批判だけになるのを防げます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A habit to improve (改善したい癖)

WARM-UP 1What's one relationship habit you'd like to get better at?人間関係で、もっと改善したい自分の癖は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Is it avoiding hard talks, overthinking messages, interrupting, or something else?
  • Why would you like to change it?

When it appears (癖が出るとき)

WARM-UP 2When does that habit show up most often?その癖が最も出やすいのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Does stress, conflict, or messaging bring it out?
  • What is the first sign?

Helpful feedback (受け止めやすい指摘)

QUESTION 1How could someone point it out without making you defensive?自分が身構えずに受け止められるように、その癖をどう指摘してもらいたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which exact behavior should they mention?
  • When would the conversation go best?

A better response (代わりの行動)

QUESTION 2What could you do instead?代わりに、どんな行動ができますか？

Follow up
  • What is the smallest realistic response?
  • What could remind you in the moment?

The other person's experience (相手への影響)

QUESTION 3How might that habit affect the other person?その癖は、相手にどんな影響を与えるかもしれませんか？

Follow up
  • What might they assume?
  • What might they need from you instead?

Explanation or excuse (説明と言い訳)

QUESTION 4When does explaining the habit start to sound like an excuse?その癖の説明が、言い訳に聞こえ始めるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What responsibility still belongs to you?
  • What action would make the explanation believable?

Falling back (元の癖に戻ること)

QUESTION 5What might make you fall back into the habit?何があると、その癖に戻ってしまいそうですか？

Follow up
  • Does stress or tiredness make it harder?
  • How could you notice it earlier?

A healthy habit (よい習慣)

QUESTION 6Now the opposite: what's one healthy habit you already bring to relationships?では逆に、人間関係ですでにできているよい習慣は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How does it appear in daily life?
  • Why might other people overlook it?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
