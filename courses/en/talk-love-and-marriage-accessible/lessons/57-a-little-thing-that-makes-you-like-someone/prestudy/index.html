Let's talk about a small quality or gesture that can make someone appealing. Please read the title aloud.人を魅力的に感じる小さな特徴や行動について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why small things stand out (小さなことが心に残る理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A small gesture can change how we feel about someone.ささやかな行動一つで、その人への感じ方が変わることがあります。small gestureささやかな行動
Different people notice very different details.人によって、目に留まる細かな点は大きく違います。
Remembering a small detail can feel thoughtful.小さなことを覚えていてくれると、思いやりを感じることがあります。thoughtful思いやりのある
Kindness is often clearest when nobody is trying to impress anyone.誰かによく見られようとしていないときほど、親切さがはっきり表れることがあります。
A laugh, a voice, or an ordinary habit may become surprisingly appealing.笑い方や声、何気ない習慣が、意外と魅力的に感じられることがあります。
What attracts us does not always match what we say we want.実際にひかれる点は、口で言う理想と一致するとは限りません。
One charming moment does not prove someone's whole character.魅力的な一瞬だけで、その人の性格全体が分かるわけではありません。
The same habit can seem sweet in one person and annoying in another.同じ癖でも、ある人なら好ましく、別の人なら気になることがあります。
The small things we value can change as we get older.年齢を重ねると、大切に感じる小さな点も変わることがあります。
A specific example often reveals the value behind a preference.具体的な例を話すと、その好みの背景にある価値観が見えやすくなります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A small attraction (小さな魅力)

WARM-UP 1What small gesture or quality makes someone more attractive to you?どんな小さな行動や特徴が、その人をより魅力的に感じさせますか？

Follow up
  • When do you notice it most?
  • Why do you like it?

What it suggests (そこから感じること)

WARM-UP 2What can a small kind gesture suggest about a person?小さな親切から、その人のどんな人柄が感じられますか？

Follow up
  • Which quality could it show?
  • What else would you want to see before judging?

Saying it aloud (本人に伝える)

QUESTION 1Would you tell someone about a small quality you quietly find attractive?その人のさりげない特徴に魅力を感じると、本人に伝えますか？

Follow up
  • What would make the compliment feel genuine?
  • Which situation could make it uncomfortable?

Noticing it (気づいた場面)

QUESTION 2Where does kindness show most clearly: at home, at work, or with strangers?親切が最もはっきり表れるのは、家、職場、知らない人との場面のどこですか？

Follow up
  • Which action would show kindness there?
  • Who would notice the benefit first?

Looks and behavior (見た目と日頃の行動)

QUESTION 3After you get to know someone, which matters more: looks or everyday behavior?相手を知ってからは、見た目と日頃の行動のどちらが大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which one matters more at first?
  • What habit could change your opinion?

What turns it off (魅力が消えるとき)

QUESTION 4When can a charming habit start to feel annoying?魅力的な癖が、うっとうしく感じ始めるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Would a good intention change how the habit feels?
  • What repeated behaviour could make it irritating?

Changing with age (年齢による変化)

QUESTION 5How have age and experience changed which small qualities you value?年齢や経験によって、大切に感じるさりげない特徴はどう変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • What matters more to you now?
  • Which preference has stayed the same?

What others notice (自分の小さな魅力)

QUESTION 6Which small quality would you be glad to have someone notice in you?自分のどんなさりげない特徴に気づいてもらえたら、うれしいですか？

Follow up
  • How would people notice it in daily life?
  • Would you want them to mention it?
