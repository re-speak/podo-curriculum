The one food you could eat forever (一生食べ続けられる一つの食べ物)

Let's test what makes one food enjoyable over time. Please read the title aloud.一つの食べ物を長く楽しめる条件について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why one food would stop being enough (一つの食べ物では足りなくなる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A one-food thought experiment removes choice and makes repetition the central problem.一つの食べ物だけという想像では、選択肢がなくなり、繰り返しが中心の問題になります。
A dependable favourite may survive repetition better than an intense but exciting dish.強く刺激的な料理より、安定した好物のほうが繰り返しに耐えられるかもしれません。
Pleasure changes when variety disappears and the choice can no longer be refused.種類がなくなり、その食べ物を断れなくなると、楽しさの質も変わります。
Here, getting sick of a food means becoming bored with it, not becoming physically ill.ここで get sick of は食べ物に飽きるという意味で、体調を崩すという意味ではありません。
Repetition involves texture, aroma, temperature, timing, and memory as well as flavour.繰り返しには、味だけでなく食感、香り、温度、時間、記憶も影響します。
A rejected second choice often identifies the kind of variety we would miss first.最後に外した第二候補から、最初に恋しくなる種類の変化が分かります。
A food may be easy to surrender because of taste, access, cost, religion, allergy, or another private reason.味、入手しやすさ、費用、宗教、アレルギー、その他の私的な理由で、手放しやすい食べ物もあります。
A less exciting but flexible choice may be more sustainable than the food we love most today.刺激は少なくても応用しやすい食べ物のほうが、今いちばん好きな物より続けやすいかもしれません。sustainable続けやすい
Medical, religious, sensory, and ethical dietary boundaries shape which food choices are sustainable.医療、宗教、感覚、倫理上の食事制限によって、続けられる食べ物の選択は変わります。
The question reveals how variety, freedom, and scarcity shape desire more than it identifies a perfect food.この問いは完璧な食べ物を探すより、種類、自由、不足が欲求をどう変えるかを示します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One food (一つの食べ物)

WARM-UP 1Which food would be hardest for you to get tired of?どの食べ物なら、最も飽きにくいですか？

Follow up
  • Would rice, noodles, or bread be hard to get tired of?
  • How could you serve it in different ways?

How quickly it gets boring (飽きるまでの時間)

WARM-UP 2How many days would it take for a favourite food to get boring?好きな食べ物でも、何日で飽きると思いますか？

Follow up
  • Would the taste or repetition become boring first?
  • If you have no favourite food, which everyday food could you eat for a week?

Two hard choices (選びにくい二つの食べ物)

QUESTION 1Which two foods would be hardest to choose between?どの二つの食べ物なら、選ぶのが最も難しいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you compare taste, price, or health?
  • Would you choose pizza or noodles?

What you would miss (恋しくなる食べ物)

QUESTION 2What would you miss most if you ate the same food every day?毎日同じ食べ物を食べたら、何が一番恋しくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Would you miss something sweet, spicy, or crunchy?
  • When would you notice it most?

Easy to give up (なくても平気な食べ物)

QUESTION 3What food could you happily never eat again?もう二度と食べなくても平気な食べ物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would a strong-smelling food be a good example?
  • Could a different recipe change your answer?

The boring answer (退屈でも健康的な答え)

QUESTION 4Would you choose a healthy everyday food or an exciting food that's bad for you?健康的な日常の食べ物と、体によくなくてもわくわくする食べ物のどちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which choice would be easier to continue?
  • What would you miss about the other food?

Keeping it interesting (飽きないための工夫)

QUESTION 5What small change can keep a favourite food interesting?好きな食べ物に飽きないための小さな変化は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would a new sauce be enough?
  • How often would you need a bigger change?

Your favourite after a year (一年後も一番好きなもの)

QUESTION 6Would you still call it your favourite after eating it every day for a year?一年間毎日食べても、まだそれを一番好きな食べ物と呼べますか？

Follow up
  • What might change your answer?
  • How often would you need a different meal?
