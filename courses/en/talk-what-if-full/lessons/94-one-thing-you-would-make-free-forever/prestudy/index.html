One thing you would make free forever (一生無料にしたい一つのもの)

Let's choose one free service and explore how life would change. Please read the title aloud.一つのサービスを無料にして、生活がどう変わるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

When free changes behaviour (無料が行動を変えるとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Permanent free access can change frequency, habits, expectations, and value as well as cost.永久に無料になると、費用だけでなく頻度、習慣、期待、価値も変わります。
Removing price can increase use even when the additional use creates little benefit.価格をなくすと、追加の利用にほとんど利益がなくても使用が増える場合があります。
The most expensive option is not necessarily the most valuable if it rarely fits daily life.最も高価な選択肢でも、日常に合わなければ最も価値があるとは限りません。
A practical choice saves friction, while an aspirational choice may open experiences that were previously inaccessible.実用的な選択は手間を減らし、理想的な選択は今まで届かなかった経験を可能にします。
Sharing access, time, or experience can spread the benefit without turning it into a resale business.利用、時間、経験を共有すれば、転売せずに利益を広げられます。
Reselling free access raises legal, contractual, and fairness questions before it raises profit.無料の利用権を転売するなら、利益より先に法律、契約、公平さの問題が生まれます。
An unexciting essential may create more lasting value than a glamorous luxury.目立たない必需品のほうが、華やかなぜいたく品より長く価値を生む場合があります。
A zero price does not eliminate the opportunity costs of time, space, attention, and environmental impact.価格がゼロでも、時間、空間、注意、環境への影響という機会費用はなくなりません。opportunity cost機会費用
Permanent access can reveal whether genuine interest or scarcity created the original desire.永久に利用できると、本当の関心と希少性のどちらが、もともとの欲求を生んだのか分かることがあります。
The useful question is which ordinary limitation the free option would actually remove.大切なのは、その無料の選択が日常のどの制限を実際になくすかです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Free for life (一生無料)

WARM-UP 1If one everyday service were free for life, which would you choose?日常のサービスを一つ一生無料にできるなら、何を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose transport, delivery, or cleaning?
  • How often would you use it?

A service that should cost less (もっと安くするべきサービス)

WARM-UP 2Which everyday service would help people most if it cost less?どの日常サービスが安くなれば、人々の役に最も立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Would cheaper transport or childcare help more people?
  • What could make the lower price difficult?

A free commute (無料の通勤)

QUESTION 1How would one free everyday service change an ordinary week?日常サービスが一つ無料になったら、普通の一週間はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Which routine would change most?
  • Would free transport change your week?

Helping a whole group (みんなの役に立つこと)

QUESTION 2Which free service could help a whole family or group?どんな無料サービスなら、家族やグループ全体の役に立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Who else would benefit?
  • Would free childcare or free public transport help more people?

The biggest lifetime saving (一生で最も節約できるもの)

QUESTION 3Which boring service would save the most money over a lifetime?地味でも、一生で最もお金を節約できるサービスは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would cleaning, internet, or insurance save more over time?
  • Would you still prefer a more enjoyable service?

Giving it away (ほかの人に譲ること)

QUESTION 4Should someone be allowed to give their free service to another person?無料サービスをほかの人に譲ってもよいですか？

Follow up
  • When would giving it away be generous?
  • What rule could prevent unfair resale?

Still not free (残る負担)

QUESTION 5When can a free service cost too much in time, privacy, or inconvenience?無料のサービスでも、時間、プライバシー、不便さの負担が大きすぎるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What makes that cost hard to avoid?
  • When would paying be better?

Worth paying for (お金を払う価値)

QUESTION 6What extra quality would make a paid service worth choosing over a free one?無料のものより有料サービスを選ぶ価値を生む、追加のよさは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which matters more: faster service or better support?
  • What makes the free version enough?
