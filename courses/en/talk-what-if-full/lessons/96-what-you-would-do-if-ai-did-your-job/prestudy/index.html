WHAT IF

What you would do if AI did your job (AIが自分の仕事をしたらすること)

Let's imagine how we would use our time if AI handled routine tasks. Please read the title aloud.AIが日常の作業をしたら、時間をどう使うか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

When the task disappears but income remains (作業がなくなり収入が残るとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The thought experiment keeps resources stable while AI performs routine tasks, creating time without an immediate financial penalty.この想像では、生活の条件を安定させたままAIが日常の作業を行い、すぐに経済的な不利益を受けずに時間が生まれます。
The structure provided by a role may come from employment, study, care, domestic work, or community contribution.役割が生む生活の形は、雇用、勉強、ケア、家事、地域への貢献から得られることがあります。
The first unstructured Monday could feel liberating before the novelty disappears.予定のない最初の月曜日は、珍しさが消えるまで解放されたように感じるかもしれません。unstructured予定のない
After the initial relief, lost routine, social contact, progress, or responsibility may become more visible.最初の安心のあと、日課、社会的なつながり、成長、責任を失ったことが見えやすくなります。
Work identity describes how occupation, contribution, and expertise shape one's sense of self.work identity とは、職業、貢献、専門性が自己認識をどう形作るかを表します。
Meaning and identity may come primarily from relationships, care, community, learning, or creative activity.意味や自己認識は、人間関係、ケア、地域、学び、創作活動から主に得られる場合もあります。
Disclosing the arrangement could create curiosity, envy, disbelief, or questions about fairness.その状況を公にすると、好奇心、嫉妬、疑い、公平さへの疑問が生じる可能性があります。
A self-chosen project can replace some structure without copying the old job.自分で選んだ活動は、以前の仕事を再現せずに一部の構造を置き換えられます。
A return to doing the tasks yourself might be motivated by mastery, service, community, or responsibility rather than income.また自分で作業をする理由は、収入より熟達、奉仕、共同体、責任かもしれません。
The scenario distinguishes the tasks a role contains from the human needs the role happens to meet.この状況は、役割に含まれる作業と、その役割が満たしていた人間の必要を分けて考えさせます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Monday (月曜日)

WARM-UP 1If AI handled every routine task while resources stayed stable, what would give the new time direction?AIがすべての日常作業を行い、生活の条件が安定したままなら、新しい時間に方向を与えるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which activity would replace the first block of routine work?
  • Which source of structure would keep the day purposeful?

Month three (三か月目)

WARM-UP 2How could three months of unstructured time affect motivation, routine, and well-being?予定のない時間が三か月続くと、意欲、日課、幸福にどう影響しますか？

Follow up
  • Which self-chosen structure would have emerged?
  • Which missing function of the old role might become difficult?

Telling people (人に話すか)

QUESTION 1When should someone disclose that AI performs most of a role's tasks?AIが役割のほとんどの作業を行うことを、どんなときに公表すべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which stakeholders would be affected by the arrangement?
  • What degree of transparency would be proportionate?

What work gave you (仕事が与えていたもの)

QUESTION 2Which need met by the old work, study, care, or routine might remain unmet?以前の仕事、勉強、ケア、または日課が満たしていたどんな必要が、満たされないままになりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which new source of structure would be enough?
  • How did the old role satisfy it?

Starting something (始めること)

QUESTION 3Which self-directed project could replace the structure once supplied by routine tasks?日常の作業が与えていた生活の形を、どんな自主的なプロジェクトが代わりに作れますか？

Follow up
  • Which value would give the project direction?
  • How would you balance the project with genuinely unstructured time?

Work identity (仕事を通じた自己認識)

QUESTION 4How strongly does work identity—or identity through another role—shape how you see yourself?仕事を通じた自己認識、または別の役割を通じた自己認識は、自分の見方をどのくらい形作っていますか？

Follow up
  • Which other source dominates?
  • Which part would survive the loss of tasks?

Going back (戻るか)

QUESTION 5Which tasks retain enough non-financial value for people to perform them voluntarily?どんな作業なら、金銭以外の価値が十分にあり、人が自ら続けますか？

Follow up
  • Which non-financial value comes from direct participation?
  • Where could AI support the task without replacing that value?

What stays human (人に残したいこと)

QUESTION 6Which task should remain meaningfully human even if automation becomes capable?自動化が可能になっても、どの作業は人が担う意味を残すべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which relational or ethical value would disappear otherwise?
  • Could a shared human-AI arrangement preserve it?
