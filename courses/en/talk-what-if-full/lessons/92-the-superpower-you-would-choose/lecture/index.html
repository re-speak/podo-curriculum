The superpower you would choose (選びたい超能力)

Let's choose a superpower and examine its real consequences. Please read the title aloud.超能力を一つ選び、実際にどんな結果が生まれるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Every power changes the problem (どの力も問題を変える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A superpower appeals because it suspends an ordinary human constraint.超能力は、普通の人間の制約を一時的になくすため魅力があります。
The imagined first use exposes the desire beneath the impressive ability.最初にどう使うかには、目立つ能力の奥にある願いが表れます。
The more useful a power becomes, the harder it may be to refuse responsibility for using it.力が役立つほど、それを使う責任を断りにくくなるかもしれません。
Secrecy may protect the user while preventing open cooperation or accountability.秘密は使う人を守る一方、協力や責任の共有を難しくします。
Disclosure could create trust, but also surveillance, pressure, and competing demands.公表すると信頼が生まれる一方、監視、圧力、対立する要求も生じ得ます。surveillance監視
An ability that sounds liberating may become exhausting when it can never be switched off.自由に見える能力でも、止められなければ疲れるものになり得ます。
Declining a power may be rational when its cost, temptation, or responsibility is too high.代償、誘惑、責任が大きすぎるなら、能力を断るのは合理的です。
A popular choice can reveal a common limitation people most want to escape.人気のある力は、多くの人が逃れたい共通の制約を示すことがあります。
Extraordinary ability rarely removes consequences; it redistributes them.特別な能力は結果をなくすのではなく、別の場所へ移すことが多いです。
Duration, control, visibility, and reversibility can completely change the choice.持続時間、制御、公に見えるか、元に戻せるかで、選択は完全に変わります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Three seconds (三秒で選ぶ)

WARM-UP 1Which superpower would create the most valuable change in ordinary life?どの超能力が普通の生活に最も価値のある変化をもたらしますか？

Follow up
  • Which ordinary constraint would it remove?
  • Which unintended consequence would need attention first?

The first use (最初の使い方)

WARM-UP 2Which first use would reveal whether a superpower could be handled responsibly?どんな最初の使い方なら、超能力を責任を持って扱えるか分かりますか？

Follow up
  • Who, if anyone, would be affected by that response?
  • Which safeguard would make it responsible?

Telling people (人に話すか)

QUESTION 1Which risks could justify keeping a superpower undisclosed?どんな危険があれば、超能力を公表しないことが正当化されますか？

Follow up
  • Who could claim a legitimate need to know?
  • Which foreseeable harm could non-disclosure prevent?

The downside (困る点)

QUESTION 2Which unintended consequence could outweigh a superpower's apparent benefit?どんな予期しない結果が、超能力の見かけの利点を上回りますか？

Follow up
  • How much control would you have over it?
  • Which predictable risk is decisive?

A power to refuse (断りたい力)

QUESTION 3Which apparently useful superpower would you decline?一見役に立ちそうでも、どの超能力なら断りますか？

Follow up
  • Which cost or temptation makes it unacceptable?
  • Would a strict limit make it tolerable?

Most people's choice (多くの人の選択)

QUESTION 4Which power would probably attract the largest number of people?最も多くの人を引きつけそうな力はどれですか？

Follow up
  • Which common frustration makes it attractive?
  • How might real use differ from stated intentions?

One rule (一つのルール)

QUESTION 5Which principle should govern the possession and use of extraordinary power?特別な力を持ち、使うことを導くべき原則は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How could that principle be maintained?
  • Which predictable temptation or risk is it designed to prevent?

Ordinary skill (普通の能力)

QUESTION 6Which ordinary human ability would remain more valuable than a superpower?どんな普通の人間の能力が、超能力より価値を持ち続けますか？

Follow up
  • Which real consequence makes it more valuable?
  • How can that ability be strengthened without magic?
