WHAT IF

Someone you would trade lives with for a day (一日だけ人生を入れ替えたい人)

Let's explore what one day in another life could teach us. Please read the title aloud.別の人生を一日体験すると何が分かるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What trading lives would reveal (人生を入れ替えると見えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
To trade lives is to inhabit another person's ordinary constraints, access, and routine for a day.人生を入れ替えるとは、別の人の日常の制約や機会、習慣を一日体験することです。
The person need not be famous; an unfamiliar role can be just as revealing.有名人である必要はなく、知らない役割も同じくらい多くを教えてくれます。
A fictional figure, public role, or anonymous type can keep the thought experiment respectful.架空の人物、公的な役割、匿名のタイプを選べば、思考実験を配慮あるものにできます。
The attraction may be access, expertise, responsibility, pressure, or a completely different routine.魅力は、機会、専門性、責任、重圧、まったく違う日課にあるかもしれません。
A day can expose constraints without granting enough context to judge an entire life.一日で制約は見えても、その人生全体を判断できるほどの背景は得られません。
Spending the day on pleasure would answer a different question from using it for investigation.楽しみのために一日を使うのと、調べるために使うのでは、答える問いが異なります。
Curiosity does not justify invading private relationships, messages, or records, even in a hypothetical.仮定の話でも、好奇心を理由に私的な関係、メッセージ、記録へ立ち入るべきではありません。
Disclosure afterward could turn private curiosity into a shared conversation or an unwanted intrusion.あとで知らせると、個人的な好奇心が共有の会話にも、望まれない侵入にもなり得ます。intrusion立ち入り
Returning willingly can reveal which ordinary parts of one's own life are valuable.喜んで自分の人生へ戻ることから、普段の生活の価値に気づくことがあります。
The chosen life often reveals the experience or knowledge we currently feel we lack.選んだ人生には、自分に今欠けていると感じる経験や知識が表れます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Whose day (誰の一日)

WARM-UP 1Which person's or role's daily life would be most revealing to experience for one day?どの人、または役割の日常を一日体験すると、最も多くのことが分かりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of that daily life would be most revealing?
  • Which assumption might the experience challenge?

Why that life (その人生を選ぶ理由)

WARM-UP 2Which feature can make another person's or role's daily life especially instructive?別の人や役割の日常を特に学びの多いものにする特徴は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which access or ability would become available?
  • Which constraint would you want to understand from inside?

Using the day (一日の使い方)

QUESTION 1How should someone use the first hour of a one-day life exchange?一日だけ人生を入れ替えたとき、最初の一時間をどう使うべきですか？

Follow up
  • Would the priority be experience, investigation, or enjoyment?
  • Which boundary would you refuse to cross?

What to learn (知りたいこと)

QUESTION 2Which question could a one-day exchange answer better than outside observation?一日の人生交換によって、外から見るよりよく答えられる疑問は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could a single day provide enough context?
  • Which evidence could you gather without invading privacy?

The unexpected part (意外な部分)

QUESTION 3Which hidden constraint could make an apparently attractive life difficult?一見魅力的な生活を難しくする、見えない制約は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which responsibility might only become visible from inside?
  • Which assumed difficulty might prove easier?

Telling them (本人に伝えるか)

QUESTION 4Which insight from a one-day exchange could be shared without violating privacy?一日の人生交換で得たどんな気づきなら、プライバシーを侵さずに共有できますか？

Follow up
  • How could you state the insight without exposing private information?
  • Which audience could use that insight responsibly?

Coming back (戻ること)

QUESTION 5Which ordinary feature of one's own life might gain value after a one-day exchange?一日の人生交換の後、自分の生活のどんな普通の特徴が大切に感じられそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which ordinary part would gain value?
  • Which feature of the borrowed life would be hardest to surrender?

Someone choosing you (自分が選ばれるなら)

QUESTION 6Which assumption might someone revise after experiencing one ordinary day in your life?あなたの普通の一日を体験すると、どんな思い込みを見直すかもしれませんか？

Follow up
  • Which ordinary routine could still be revealing?
  • Which conclusion would one day not justify?

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
