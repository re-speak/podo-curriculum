WHAT IF

Someone you would trade lives with for a day (一日だけ人生を入れ替えたい人)

Let's explore what one day in another life could teach us. Please read the title aloud.別の人生を一日体験すると何が分かるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What trading lives would reveal (人生を入れ替えると見えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
To trade lives is to inhabit another person's ordinary constraints, access, and routine for a day.人生を入れ替えるとは、別の人の日常の制約や機会、習慣を一日体験することです。
The person need not be famous; an unfamiliar role can be just as revealing.有名人である必要はなく、知らない役割も同じくらい多くを教えてくれます。
A fictional figure, public role, or anonymous type can keep the thought experiment respectful.架空の人物、公的な役割、匿名のタイプを選べば、思考実験を配慮あるものにできます。
The attraction may be access, expertise, responsibility, pressure, or a completely different routine.魅力は、機会、専門性、責任、重圧、まったく違う日課にあるかもしれません。
A day can expose constraints without granting enough context to judge an entire life.一日で制約は見えても、その人生全体を判断できるほどの背景は得られません。
Spending the day on pleasure would answer a different question from using it for investigation.楽しみのために一日を使うのと、調べるために使うのでは、答える問いが異なります。
Curiosity does not justify invading private relationships, messages, or records, even in a hypothetical.仮定の話でも、好奇心を理由に私的な関係、メッセージ、記録へ立ち入るべきではありません。
Disclosure afterward could turn private curiosity into a shared conversation or an unwanted intrusion.あとで知らせると、個人的な好奇心が共有の会話にも、望まれない侵入にもなり得ます。intrusion立ち入り
Returning willingly can reveal which ordinary parts of one's own life are valuable.喜んで自分の人生へ戻ることから、普段の生活の価値に気づくことがあります。
The chosen life often reveals the experience or knowledge we currently feel we lack.選んだ人生には、自分に今欠けていると感じる経験や知識が表れます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Whose day (誰の一日)

WARM-UP 1Whose daily life would you be curious to experience for one day?誰の日常生活を一日体験してみたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a famous person, a doctor, or someone from another country?
  • Which part of their day would you want to see?

Famous or very different (有名な人か全く違う人か)

WARM-UP 2Would you choose someone famous or someone with a very different ordinary life?有名な人と、全く違う普通の生活をする人のどちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • What would fame let you experience?
  • What could an ordinary life teach instead?

Using the day (一日の使い方)

QUESTION 1What would you do in the first hour of another person's life?別の人の人生を体験する最初の一時間に何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Would you explore their home, work, or neighbourhood?
  • What would you avoid doing?

What to learn (知りたいこと)

QUESTION 2What would you want to learn that an interview couldn't tell you?インタビューでは分からない何を知りたいですか？

Follow up
  • What could one day show you?
  • What would still be impossible to know?

The unexpected part (意外な部分)

QUESTION 3Which attractive life might be much harder in private?魅力的に見えても、実際にはずっと大変そうな生活は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would a celebrity's life or a luxury lifestyle be a good example?
  • What hidden responsibility might appear?

Private boundaries (プライバシーの境界)

QUESTION 4What would be unfair to look at or learn during the life swap?人生を交換している間でも、見たり知ったりするのは不公平だと思うものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would private messages be off-limits?
  • Would you want the same rule for your own life?

Coming back (戻ること)

QUESTION 5Which part of your own life might you appreciate more afterward?その後、自分の生活のどんな部分をもっと大切に感じそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would you appreciate your home, free time, or relationships more?
  • What might you miss from the other life?

What someone might misunderstand (誤解されそうなこと)

QUESTION 6What might another person misunderstand after one day in your life?あなたの生活を一日体験した人は、何を誤解しそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which routine might surprise them?
  • What would take longer than one day to understand?
