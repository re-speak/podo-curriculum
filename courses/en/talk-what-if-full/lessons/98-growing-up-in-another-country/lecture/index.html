WHAT IF

Growing up in another country (別の国で育っていたら)

Let's imagine how a different upbringing could shape a person. Please read the title aloud.別の環境で育つと、人がどう変わるか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

How upbringing shapes possibilities (育った環境が可能性を形作る方法)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Upbringing includes the relationships, institutions, language, expectations, and material conditions that shape childhood.upbringing には、子ども時代を形作る人間関係、制度、言語、期待、生活条件が含まれます。
No country provides one uniform childhood across region, class, family, generation, or community.どの国でも、地域、階層、家族、世代、共同体によって子ども時代は一様ではありません。
A useful counterfactual names a place and conditions without pretending to predict a national personality.役立つ反実仮想では、国民性を予測したふりをせず、場所と条件を具体的にします。counterfactual反実仮想
Growing up through another language could alter access, humour, memory, and social belonging.別の言語で育つと、機会、ユーモア、記憶、社会への所属感が変わる可能性があります。
Education systems distribute opportunity and define success in different, internally varied ways.教育制度は、国内でも多様な形で機会を分け、成功を定義します。
National context interacts with family, community, disability, wealth, and chance rather than replacing them.国の背景は、家族、地域、障害、経済状況、偶然と関わり、置き換わるわけではありません。
Some preferences might shift while temperament or central values remain recognizable.好みが変わっても、気質や中心的な価値観は分かる形で残るかもしれません。
What someone would miss may be a person, language, food, season, routine, or nothing obvious.恋しくなるものは、人、言語、食べ物、季節、日課、または特にないことかもしれません。
A childhood abroad would shape language, memory, and identity differently from a migration decision made later in life.海外で過ごす子ども時代は、後の人生で移住を決めることとは異なる形で、言語、記憶、アイデンティティを形作ります。
The value of the question lies in separating circumstance, adaptation, chance, and deliberate choice.この問いの価値は、状況、適応、偶然、意識的な選択を分けて考えることにあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Another country (別の国)

WARM-UP 1If you had grown up in another country, which one would you choose?別の国で育っていたとしたら、どの国を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a country for its schools, nature, or daily life?
  • What would you want to learn before choosing?

A different daily life (違う日常生活)

WARM-UP 2Which part of everyday childhood changes most across countries?国によって、子どもの日常生活のどの部分が最も変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Would school, home, or free time change most?
  • Which difference would a child notice first?

School in that country (その国の学校)

QUESTION 1In that country, what might school have been like for you?その国で育っていたら、学校生活はどんなものだったと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Would classes feel stricter or more relaxed?
  • What might surprise you on the first day?

What you would miss (恋しくなるもの)

QUESTION 2Which food or family custom would you miss from your actual childhood?実際の子ども時代の食べ物や家族の習慣で、何が恋しくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would you miss a food, holiday, or family routine?
  • What do people often miss from childhood?

What you would leave behind (置いていきたいもの)

QUESTION 3What part of your actual upbringing would you be happy to leave behind?実際に育った環境のどんな部分なら、なくてもよいと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Would you change school pressure, rules, or expectations?
  • If you would change nothing, what would you keep?

You in another language (別の言語での自分)

QUESTION 4What changes about you when you speak another language?別の言語を話すとき、自分のどんなところが変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Are you quieter, funnier, or more direct?
  • Does that change feel good?

Easier as a child (子どものほうが簡単なこと)

QUESTION 5What would be easier to learn as a child than after moving there as an adult?子どもの頃に学ぶほうが、大人になって移住してからより簡単なことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • The language or local manners?
  • What might an adult notice more clearly?

What stays yours (変わらず自分のもの)

QUESTION 6What part of your personality would probably stay the same anywhere?どこで育っても、自分の性格のどんな部分は同じだと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that feel less shaped by place?
  • Could another environment still change how it appears?
