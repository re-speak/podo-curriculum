Safety before possessions (持ち物より安全)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The scenario begins only after every person and pet is safely outside.この想像は、人もペットも全員安全に外へ出たあとから始まります。
No possession justifies re-entering smoke or fire; emergency instructions take priority.持ち物のために煙や火の中へ戻るべきではなく、緊急時の指示が最優先です。
Immediate evacuation takes priority over retrieving even an irreplaceable possession.かけがえのない持ち物であっても、取りに行くことより、すぐ避難することが優先です。
Only an object already on the safe exit route belongs in the thought experiment.安全な避難経路にすでにある物だけが、この思考実験の対象です。
An irreplaceable object carries information, history, or meaning that no new copy can restore.irreplaceable な物には、新しい物では戻せない情報、歴史、意味があります。
The most expensive possession may matter less than a cheap object connected to memory or identity.最も高価な持ち物より、記憶や自分らしさと結びついた安い物のほうが大切な場合があります。
Backups can protect photographs and documents, although the original object may still carry meaning.バックアップで写真や書類を守れますが、元の物に意味が残る場合もあります。
A choice that looks strange from outside may be coherent once its personal history is known.外から不思議に見える選択でも、その個人的な歴史を知れば筋が通る場合があります。
Losing something before may increase care, or may teach that memory can survive without the object.以前何かを失った経験から、より大切に守ることも、物がなくても記憶は残ると学ぶこともあります。
Backups, inventories, alarms, and evacuation plans matter more than deciding what to retrieve during danger.危険なときに何を取りに行くかより、バックアップ、一覧、警報、避難計画のほうが重要です。inventory一覧

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One thing (一つの物)

WARM-UP 1After every person and pet is safe, which possession would carry the greatest irreplaceable value?人もペットも全員安全になった後、どの持ち物が最もかけがえのない価値を持ちますか？

Follow up
  • Which form of memory, history, or identity gives it that value?
  • Which preparation could protect part of that value before an emergency?

Why that one (それを選ぶ理由)

WARM-UP 2Which forms of memory or meaning can make a possession irreplaceable?どんな記憶や意味が、持ち物をかけがえのないものにしますか？

Follow up
  • Does the value come from information, history, identity, or use?
  • Which part of that value could documentation preserve?

Your own answer (自分の答えへの驚き)

QUESTION 1Which category of possession can reveal an unexpected priority?どんな種類の持ち物が、意外な優先事項を示しますか？

Follow up
  • Why can that category appear less important at first?
  • Which hidden priority does it represent?

A different choice (ほかの人と違う選択)

QUESTION 2Which commonly valued possession might someone else take even if you would leave with nothing?自分は何も持ち出さなくても、ほかの人ならどの一般的に大切な持ち物を持ち出すかもしれませんか？

Follow up
  • Which history could make it important to someone else?
  • Which safety or value makes leaving it coherent for you?

A past loss (過去に失った物)

QUESTION 3How can the loss of an ordinary possession affect identity or memory?普通の持ち物を失うことが、アイデンティティや記憶にどう影響しますか？

Follow up
  • Which connection to identity or memory could make the loss significant?
  • How might the meaning survive without the original possession?

What loss teaches (失うことから学ぶこと)

QUESTION 4What can the loss of a possession reveal about value and memory?持ち物を失うことから、価値や記憶について何が分かりますか？

Follow up
  • How might it change later preparation?
  • Under what condition can meaning survive without the original object?

Preparing safely (安全な準備)

QUESTION 5Which preparation can protect irreplaceable information or possessions without delaying evacuation?避難を遅らせずに、かけがえのない情報や持ち物を守るには、どんな備えができますか？

Follow up
  • Would duplication, documentation, or protected storage help most?
  • Which evacuation step is both realistic and urgent?

What cannot burn (燃えないもの)

QUESTION 6Which valuable part of life exists beyond any possession?どんな大切なものが、持ち物を超えて存在しますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of care protects it?
  • How can an object support it without becoming the value itself?
