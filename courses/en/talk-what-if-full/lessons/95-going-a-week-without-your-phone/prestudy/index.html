What a phone-free week would test (携帯電話なしの一週間で試されること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A digital detox is a deliberate period in which discretionary digital-device use is reduced.デジタルデトックスとは、自由に減らせるデジタル機器の利用を意識的に減らす期間です。
For some people, much phone use is convenient or recreational rather than essential.人によっては、携帯電話の多くの利用は必要不可欠ではなく、便利さや娯楽のためです。
For others, a phone provides accessibility, medical support, safety, paid work, or care coordination.別の人には、アクセシビリティ、医療支援、安全、有給の仕事、ケアの調整に必要な道具です。accessibilityアクセシビリティ
Here, what breaks first refers to the first routine or system that fails, not physical damage.ここで「最初に駄目になるもの」とは、物理的な破損ではなく、最初に機能しなくなる日課や仕組みを指します。
Modern infrastructure has concentrated navigation, payment, tickets, authentication, and communication in one device.現代の仕組みでは、移動、支払い、切符、認証、連絡が一つの機器に集中しています。
The hardest loss may be one function, one person, or one source of reassurance rather than the device itself.最も困るのは機器そのものではなく、一つの機能、一人との連絡、安心感かもしれません。
Fewer alerts could improve concentration, sleep, presence, or boredom tolerance.通知が減ると、集中、睡眠、目の前への注意、退屈への耐性がよくなる可能性があります。
A workable experiment may rely on replacement devices, printed information, or agreed human support.実行できる実験には、代わりの機器、紙の情報、合意した人の支援が必要な場合があります。
A period offline can distinguish essential access from convenient or habitual use.オフラインの時間を過ごすと、必要な利用と、便利さや習慣による利用を区別できます。
A responsible digital detox distinguishes discretionary habits from access and care needs.責任あるデジタルデトックスは、自由に減らせる習慣と、アクセスやケアの必要を区別します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Seven days (七日間)

WARM-UP 1Which phone feature would you miss most for one week?一週間、携帯電話のどの機能が最も恋しくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Would you miss maps, messages, or payments most?
  • What could you use instead?

How the day begins (一日の始まり方)

WARM-UP 2What do you usually use your phone for first?普段、最初にスマートフォンを何に使いますか？

Follow up
  • Could that first task be done another way?
  • What happens on a day when you do not use it?

Easiest to live without (なくても困りにくい機能)

QUESTION 1Which useful phone feature could you give up with the least trouble?便利なスマートフォンの機能のうち、最も困らずに手放せるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you use instead?
  • Why is another feature harder to lose?

What might improve (よくなりそうなこと)

QUESTION 2What might improve, if anything, after three days with less phone use?スマホをあまり使わずに三日過ごしたら、何かよくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would sleep, focus, or free time change?
  • What could become harder with less phone use?

Leaving the phone behind (スマホを別の部屋に置くこと)

QUESTION 3When is it easiest to leave your phone in another room?携帯電話を別の部屋に置きやすいのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Would meals, exercise, or bedtime be easiest?
  • What makes a phone hard to leave?

Reachable in an emergency (緊急時につながる方法)

QUESTION 4How could you stay reachable in an emergency while using your phone less?携帯電話を減らしながら、緊急時に連絡を受けられるようにするにはどうしますか？

Follow up
  • Who would need another way to contact you?
  • Which emergency feature would stay available?

The parts you would keep (残す機能)

QUESTION 5Which parts of your phone would you still use during the break?スマホを休む間でも、どの機能は使い続けますか？

Follow up
  • Would you keep calls, maps, or payments?
  • Which use would you limit first?

One habit to change (変えたい一つの習慣)

QUESTION 6Which phone habit would you most like to change?どの携帯電話の習慣を最も変えたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you change late-night use or constant checking?
  • Which phone habit is worth keeping?
