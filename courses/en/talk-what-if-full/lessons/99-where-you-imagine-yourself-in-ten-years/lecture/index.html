WHAT IF

Where you imagine yourself in ten years (十年後に想像する自分)

Let's imagine an ordinary day ten years from now. Please read the title aloud.十年後の普通の一日を想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

A direction, not a prediction (予測ではなく方向)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A ten-year projection expresses a possible life direction rather than a prediction or commitment.十年後の予想図は、予測や約束ではなく、あり得る人生の方向を表します。
Life direction names what feels worth moving toward even when the route remains unclear.life direction は、道筋が不明でも、どこへ進む価値を感じるかを示します。
A future image may take shape through a concrete setting, an atmosphere, or the values present in an ordinary day.将来のイメージは、具体的な場所、雰囲気、または普通の一日にある価値観から形になることがあります。
Uncertainty can make underlying values more visible even when the setting and route remain undefined.場所や道筋が決まっていなくても、不確かさによって基本的な価値観がよりはっきり見えることがあります。
Paid work, study, care, rest, community, creativity, and ordinary routine can all organize a future day.有給の仕事、勉強、ケア、休息、地域、創作、普通の日課のどれも未来の一日を形作れます。
The balance of connection and solitude influences how a future ordinary day feels and functions.人とのつながりと一人の時間のバランスは、将来の普通の一日の感じ方や過ごし方に影響します。
Continuity can protect an identity, relationship, habit, or place that already works.継続することは、すでにうまくいく自分らしさ、関係、習慣、場所を守れます。continuity継続
A future can diverge from the picture without becoming a failure, while one missing value may still matter deeply.未来が予想図と違っても失敗とは限りませんが、一つの大切な価値が欠けることは重大かもしれません。
A proportionate experiment can test a possible direction without turning a projection into a fixed commitment.無理のない試みをすると、将来のイメージを固定した約束にせず、可能な方向を確かめられます。
A flexible projection can guide choices without trapping the future self inside an outdated plan.柔軟な予想図は、未来の自分を古い計画に閉じ込めずに、選択を導けます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Ten years on (十年後)

WARM-UP 1Which features would define a satisfying ordinary day ten years from now?十年後の満足できる普通の一日を決める特徴は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which feature would contribute most to satisfaction?
  • What would make the day sustainable as a normal routine?

What you are doing (していること)

WARM-UP 2Which purpose could give a future ordinary day structure and meaning?どんな目的が、将来の普通の一日に形と意味を与えますか？

Follow up
  • Which quality would you want the day to have?
  • Would its structure come from paid work, learning, care, rest, community, or another source?

Who is around (周りにいる人)

QUESTION 1Which balance between connection and solitude would support a satisfying future life?どんなつながりと一人の時間のバランスが、満足できる将来の生活を支えますか？

Follow up
  • Which quality of the relationship matters?
  • What gives the solitude meaning or ease?

What stays the same (変わらないこと)

QUESTION 2Which part of your current life, if any, would you want to preserve?今の生活で残したい部分があるとすれば、どこですか？

Follow up
  • Which value makes that continuity important?
  • Which uncertainty prevents a choice?

What would disappoint you (失望すること)

QUESTION 3Which absent value or condition, if any, would make the future disappointing?欠けていたら未来に失望する価値や条件があるとすれば、何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why is it central rather than optional?
  • Which openness keeps the projection flexible?

Life direction (人生の方向性)

QUESTION 4Which underlying values can reveal direction when a ten-year projection remains uncertain?十年後の予想が不確かでも、どんな基本的な価値観が方向を示しますか？

Follow up
  • Which underlying value gives it force?
  • Which uncertainty should remain open?

This year (今年できること)

QUESTION 5Which proportionate action this year could test or clarify a possible future direction?今年どんな適切な行動をすると、将来の方向を試したり明確にしたりできますか？

Follow up
  • What makes it proportionate and realistic?
  • Which uncertainty should resolve first?

Changing the picture (未来像を変えること)

QUESTION 6Which new evidence or opportunity could justifiably overturn a ten-year projection?どんな新しい根拠や機会があれば、十年後の予想を正当に変えられますか？

Follow up
  • Which opportunity could outweigh it?
  • Which experience or information could add definition?
