Why first earnings stay memorable (初めての収入が記憶に残る理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A first paycheck can turn the abstract idea of paid work into a concrete experience.初めての給料によって、有給の仕事という抽象的な考えが具体的な経験になることがあります。
First earnings may come from formal employment, a one-off task, creative work, or a sale.最初の収入は、正式な雇用、単発の作業、創作活動、販売など、さまざまな形から生まれます。
The emotional significance of first earnings may be much greater than the amount itself.初めての収入は、金額そのものより感情的な意味のほうが大きい場合があります。first earnings最初の収入
Some people celebrate by spending their first earnings, while others save or share them.初めての収入を使って祝う人もいれば、貯めたり誰かと分けたりする人もいます。
An object bought with first earnings can preserve the memory of the effort that produced them.初めての収入で買った物が、それを得るまでの努力の記憶を残すことがあります。
Sharing the news with someone can turn a private milestone into a recognized achievement.誰かに知らせることで、個人的な節目が周りにも認められた達成になります。
A meaningful account does not require recalling the exact amount, employer, or date.意味のある話をするために、正確な金額、雇い主、日付まで思い出す必要はありません。
The first decision made with earned money can become a lasting marker of independence.自分で得た収入について最初に下した判断は、自立を示す長く残る節目になることがあります。
Later priorities can change what we imagine doing with the same first payment.その後の優先順位によって、同じ初収入の使い道を今なら違って考えることがあります。
The first experience of earning can reveal whether money represents freedom, security, generosity, or reward.初めて収入を得る経験は、お金が自由、安心、分かち合い、報酬のどれを意味するかを示すことがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

First paid work (初めてお金をもらった仕事)

WARM-UP 1What was the first thing you ever got paid to do?初めてお金をもらったのは、どんなことをしたときですか？

Follow up
  • How old were you?
  • If you've never been paid, what small job would you try?

First paycheck (初めての給料)

WARM-UP 2What did you do—or what would you like to do—with your first paycheck?初めての給料で、実際にしたこと、またはしてみたいことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you buy first?
  • Would you save any of it?

Yourself or someone else (自分か誰かのためか)

QUESTION 1Would you spend your first paycheck on yourself or someone else?初めての給料は、自分のためと誰かのためのどちらに使いたいですか？

Follow up
  • What would you choose?
  • Why that person?

Saving part of it (一部を貯める)

QUESTION 2What would you want to save part of your first paycheck for?初めての給料の一部を、何のために取っておきたいですか？

Follow up
  • How much would you keep?
  • When would you use it?

Who you tell first (最初に話す相手)

QUESTION 3Who would you tell first about your first paycheck?初めて給料をもらったら、最初に誰に話したいですか？

Follow up
  • How would they react?
  • Why might someone keep it private?

Still special (少なくても特別)

QUESTION 4Would a very small first paycheck still feel special?初めての給料なら、金額がとても少なくても特別に感じますか？

Follow up
  • What would make it special?
  • When would the amount matter?

Your own money (自分で稼ぐお金)

QUESTION 5What changes when you start earning your own money?自分でお金を稼ぎ始めると、何が変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Do prices feel different?
  • What new choice becomes possible?

First-paycheck advice (初めての給料への助言)

QUESTION 6What advice would you give someone about their first paycheck?初めて給料をもらう人に、どんな助言をしますか？

Follow up
  • What advice should they ignore?
  • Would you follow your own advice?
