A different career you might have chosen (選んでいたかもしれない別のキャリア)

Let's explore what makes another career interesting. Please read the title aloud.別のキャリアが興味深く感じられる理由を考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

An alternative path can clarify present values (別の進路から今の価値観が見える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Considering an alternative career does not imply dissatisfaction or an intention to change.別のキャリアを考えることは、不満や転職の意図を意味しません。
A career path can combine paid employment with study, care, creative practice, public service, or enterprise.キャリアの進路は、有給の仕事に加えて、勉強、ケア、創作活動、公共への貢献、事業などを組み合わせることができます。
The attraction may lie in daily tasks, social value, autonomy, learning, or working conditions.魅力は、日々の業務、社会的価値、自律性、学び、労働条件にあるかもしれません。
A familiar, public, or fictional trajectory can replace personal career disclosure.個人的なキャリアを話さずに、身近な人、公人、架空の人物の歩みを使えます。
A credible comparison recognizes constraints and losses as well as attractive possibilities.信頼できる比較では、魅力的な可能性だけでなく、制約や失うものも考えます。
Access, location, money, responsibility, and timing shape which careers are genuinely available.機会、場所、お金、責任、時期によって、実際に選べるキャリアは変わります。
An unrealized interest may reappear through hobbies, volunteering, study, or a smaller role.実現しなかった関心が、趣味、ボランティア、学習、小さな役割として現れることがあります。
People may feel curiosity, relief, gratitude, indifference, or mixed feelings about an alternative.別の進路には、好奇心、安堵、感謝、無関心、複雑な気持ちなどもあり得ます。
Different occupations may express the same underlying values in different ways.異なる職業でも、同じ根本的な価値を別の形で表せます。
A counterfactual career can identify a present need without demanding a complete reinvention.仮定のキャリアから、全面的にやり直さなくても、今必要なことが見つかる場合があります。counterfactual事実と異なる仮定

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Another path (別の進路)

WARM-UP 1Which alternative career would be most interesting to try for one year?どの別のキャリアを一年間試すと最も面白そうですか？

Follow up
  • When did this alternative first become worth considering?
  • Does the comparison evoke curiosity, regret, relief, or none of these?

An ordinary day (普通の一日)

WARM-UP 2Which activities and responsibilities would define an ordinary day in that career?その仕事の普通の一日を形作る活動や責任は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which task would occupy the largest share of time?
  • Which responsibility might be less attractive than it first appears?

Why it appeals (魅力を感じる理由)

QUESTION 1What is the strongest source of attraction in that career?その仕事の最も強い魅力は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Does it concern the work, social contribution, autonomy, learning, or conditions?
  • Which underlying value does it express?

What made it possible (可能にする条件)

QUESTION 2Which conditions would make that career genuinely available?どのような条件があれば、そのキャリアが本当に選べるものになりますか？

Follow up
  • Would timing, access, resources, location, or responsibility matter most?
  • Could one element of the path be tested without a complete transition?

Someone who changed paths (進路を変えた人)

QUESTION 3Who offers an instructive example of a major career change?大きなキャリアチェンジの参考になる例は誰ですか？

Follow up
  • Which condition enabled the transition?
  • Which meaningful benefit or security did the choice put at risk?

How it feels now (今の気持ち)

QUESTION 4What emotions can an unrealized career path produce over time?実現しなかったキャリアは、時間とともにどんな感情を生み得ますか？

Follow up
  • Which comparison produces that response?
  • How has the response evolved over time?

A value to use now (今生かせる価値)

QUESTION 5Which value or activity from another career can be incorporated without a complete transition?完全に転職せずに、別のキャリアのどの価値や活動を取り入れられますか？

Follow up
  • Could it take the form of study, volunteering, a project, or a smaller responsibility?
  • What makes it inseparable from a complete transition?

Advice to a younger person (若い人への考え)

QUESTION 6Which question would help a younger person evaluate a career path without pretending the choice is permanent?選択が永遠に変えられないと思い込まずに、若い人がキャリアの進路を考えるための問いは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which attractive assumption should be tested?
  • Whose experience could reveal the less visible parts of the path?
