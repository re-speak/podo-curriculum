What makes completion feel meaningful (やり終えることに意味が生まれる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Completing a demanding task can make a person feel proud of the effort even without public recognition.大変な課題を完了すると、人に認められなくても、その努力を誇りに思うことがあります。
The source of pride may be persistence, growth, quality, or keeping a commitment.誇りの源は、粘り強さ、成長、質、約束を果たしたことなどさまざまです。
An achievement can be personally significant without being large, public, or competitive.達成は、大規模、公的、競争的でなくても、本人にとって大切なものになれます。
Invisible preparation often receives less attention than the final result.目に見えない準備は、最終的な結果ほど注目されないことがよくあります。
External recognition can reinforce pride without becoming its sole foundation.周囲からの評価は誇りを強めますが、唯一の土台になる必要はありません。recognition評価
Cultural expectations can make direct expressions of pride feel boastful even when they are accurate.文化的な期待によって、正直な誇りの表現でも自慢のように感じられることがあります。
An imperfect outcome can coexist with pride in the process or persistence.不完全な結果でも、その過程や粘り強さを誇りに思うことはできます。
Shared effort and close observation can make another person's achievement a genuine source of pride.努力を共にしたり近くで見たりすると、ほかの人の達成も心から誇りに感じられます。
The achievements we value reveal standards that matter more deeply than status.大切にする達成から、地位より深い自分の基準が見えてきます。
Reflecting on a completed effort can identify the next meaningful direction without diminishing the achievement.完了した努力を振り返ると、その価値を損なわずに次の意味ある方向を見つけられます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A finished effort (やり終えた努力)

WARM-UP 1Which feels more satisfying: completing a long project or finally fixing a small annoyance?長い仕事を終えることと、小さな困りごとをやっと直すことでは、どちらがより満足できますか？

Follow up
  • What is the clearest sign that the work is complete?
  • Who benefits from the finished result?

Taking longer (時間がかかったこと)

WARM-UP 2What's something you finished that took longer than you expected?思ったより時間がかかったけれど、最後まで終えたことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What slowed you down?
  • What almost made you stop?

Pride without praise (ほめられなくても感じる誇り)

QUESTION 1Would you still feel proud if nobody noticed?誰にも気づかれなくても、誇らしく感じますか？

Follow up
  • What would still make it worthwhile?
  • Whose praise would matter most?

Saying you're proud (誇りを口にすること)

QUESTION 2Why can saying "I'm proud of this" feel uncomfortable?「これを誇りに思う」と言うことが、気まずく感じられるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • Are people afraid of sounding boastful?
  • How else can they show pride?

Why it mattered (大切だった理由)

QUESTION 3Why can one finished task matter much more than another?一つの完了した努力が、別の努力より大きな意味を持つのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which mattered most: the effort, the result, or keeping a promise?
  • What personal quality does finishing the work show?

A result without pride (誇れなかった結果)

QUESTION 4What can someone finish without feeling proud of it?完了しても誇りにつながらない結果には、どんなものがありますか？

Follow up
  • What might be disappointing about the result?
  • Could the effort still deserve praise?

What to be known for (何で知られたいか)

QUESTION 5What kind of finished work would you want people to remember you for?どんな完成した仕事や貢献と、自分の名前を結びつけたいですか？

Follow up
  • What quality should people notice?
  • Would the size of the project matter?

Recognizing effort (努力を認める)

QUESTION 6How can we praise real effort without sounding empty?曖昧なお世辞ではなく、努力に基づく誇りを支えるにはどうすればよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the effort deserves attention?
  • What question would help the person talk about the work?
