What a real lunch break looks like where you live (住んでいる場所のリアルな昼休み)

Let's describe real lunch-break habits and discuss what makes the break useful and fair. Please read the title aloud.実際の昼休みの習慣を説明し、役立つ公平な休憩について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Is lunch break really a break? (昼休みは本当に休憩？)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
At 12:10, three coworkers leave the office to buy noodles from a small shop nearby.十二時十分、三人の同僚が近くの小さな店へ麺を買いにオフィスを出ます。
Another coworker eats a sandwich at her desk while answering messages.別の同僚は、メッセージに返信しながら机でサンドイッチを食べます。
Both meals happen during lunch break, but only one may feel like a real break.どちらも昼休み中の食事ですが、本当の休憩に感じられるのは片方だけかもしれません。
Lunch habits depend on local food, prices, school or workplace rules, and the time available.昼食の習慣は、地域の食べ物や値段、学校や職場の規則、使える時間によって変わります。
A one-hour break can become much shorter when buying food requires a long queue.一時間の休憩でも、食べ物を買うために長く並べば、実際の時間はずっと短くなります。
Eating with other people can build relationships, while eating alone can provide quiet time.誰かと食べることは関係づくりに役立ち、一人で食べることは静かな時間になります。
Leaving the building helps some people reset, but bad weather or a busy afternoon may keep them inside.建物を出ると気分を切り替えられる人もいますが、悪天候や忙しい午後のために中に残ることもあります。
Problems begin when a break exists on the schedule but work continues through it.予定表には休憩があっても、その間ずっと仕事が続けば問題になります。
An urgent message may need an answer, but not every message should be called urgent.緊急のメッセージには返信が必要かもしれませんが、すべてを緊急扱いにするべきではありません。
A better lunch break may require more time, easier food choices, or simply permission to disconnect.よりよい昼休みには、長い時間、選びやすい食事、あるいは仕事から離れてよいという雰囲気が必要かもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A usual lunch (普段の昼食)

WARM-UP 1What do people usually eat for lunch on a work or school day where you live?住んでいる場所では、仕事や学校のある日に何を食べることが多いですか？

Follow up
  • Do they bring food or buy it?
  • What is a quick lunch there?

The real length (実際の長さ)

WARM-UP 2How long does a real lunch break usually last?実際の昼休みは、普通どのくらいありますか？

Follow up
  • Is that enough time?
  • How much of it is spent getting food?

Alone or together (一人か一緒か)

QUESTION 1Do people usually eat alone or with coworkers or classmates?一人で食べることと、同僚やクラスメートと食べることでは、どちらが多いですか？

Follow up
  • Which do you prefer?
  • When is eating alone useful?

A real break (本当の休憩)

QUESTION 2What makes a lunch break feel like a real break?昼休みが本当の休憩に感じられるのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Does leaving the building help?
  • What ruins the break?

Skipping lunch (昼食を抜くとき)

QUESTION 3When do people skip lunch or eat at their desk?人が昼食を抜いたり、机で食べたりするのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Is it usually a choice?
  • How does it affect the afternoon?

Another country's habit (別の国の習慣)

QUESTION 4What lunch habit from another country would you like to try?別の国の昼食習慣で、試してみたいものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • A longer lunch or eating outside?
  • What might not work where you live?

Messages during lunch (昼休みのメッセージ)

QUESTION 5Should people be expected to answer work messages during lunch?昼休みに仕事のメッセージへ返信することを求められるべきですか？

Follow up
  • What if it is urgent?
  • Who decides what counts as urgent?

One better change (改善する一つの変更)

QUESTION 6What one change would improve lunch breaks where you live?住んでいる場所の昼休みをよくする変更を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would benefit most?
  • What would make the change difficult?
