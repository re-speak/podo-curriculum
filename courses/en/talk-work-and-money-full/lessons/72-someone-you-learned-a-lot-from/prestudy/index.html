We learn from how people guide, not only what they know (知識だけでなく導き方から学ぶ)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Useful guidance can come from formal authority, a peer, a public figure, or a fictional case.役立つ導きは、正式な権限を持つ人、仲間、公人、架空の事例からも得られます。formal authority正式な権限
Formal authority and instructional skill are separate qualities.正式な権限と指導力は別の性質です。
Feedback becomes actionable when standards and purposes are visible in advance.基準と目的が事前に明確なら、フィードバックを行動に移しやすくなります。
Effective guidance changes from explanation to practice, feedback, and greater independence.効果的な指導は、説明から練習、フィードバック、より大きな自立へと変化します。
Direct feedback can remain specific and demanding without humiliation or contempt.直接的なフィードバックも、屈辱や軽蔑なしに具体的で厳しくできます。
Ineffective or unfair leadership can clarify practices the learner does not want to repeat.効果のない、または不公平な指導から、自分が繰り返したくない行動が明確になることがあります。
A composite, anonymous, public, or fictional example can protect privacy and power-sensitive relationships.複数を組み合わせた例、匿名、公の例、架空の例なら、プライバシーや上下関係を守れます。
A principle learned from guidance may outlast the role, course, team, or contact.指導から得た原則は、役割、講座、チーム、関係が終わったあとも残ることがあります。
Respect for a mentor does not require adopting every habit, belief, or method.指導者を尊敬しても、すべての習慣、考え、方法を受け入れる必要はありません。
The strongest long-term evidence of guidance is a learner who can make sound decisions without constant supervision.指導の最も強い長期的な証拠は、常に監督されなくても適切に判断できる学習者です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Someone you learned from (多くを学んだ人)

WARM-UP 1Who have you learned a lot from, at work, at school, or elsewhere?仕事、学校、それ以外で、多くを学んだ人は誰ですか？

Follow up
  • What did you learn?
  • If no one person stands out, which type of person helps you learn?

What they did (行動)

WARM-UP 2What does a good teacher, manager, or coach actually do?よい先生、上司、コーチは実際に何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Did the person show, explain, or let you practise?
  • Which part of the experience proves the guidance worked?

How feedback worked (フィードバックの方法)

QUESTION 1How should a good guide turn a mistake into a useful next step?よい指導者は、間違いを役立つ次の一歩にどう変えますか？

Follow up
  • What made the response helpful or harmful?
  • Did it give the learner a clear next step?

Safe questions (質問しやすい雰囲気)

QUESTION 2What can a person in charge do to make questions feel safe?責任のある立場の人は、質問しやすくするために何ができますか？

Follow up
  • What reaction should they avoid?
  • How should they respond to "I don't know"?

What remained (残った学び)

QUESTION 3What lesson from a good guide tends to stay useful for years?効果的な指導から得た原則の中で、時間がたっても役立ちやすいのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which current problem could the old lesson help with?
  • What would need to change before using it in a new setting?

Telling them (本人に伝えること)

QUESTION 4When someone's advice helps you, do you usually tell them?誰かの助言が役に立ったとき、その人に伝えることが多いですか？

Follow up
  • Why or why not?
  • Is it ever too late to thank them?

Learning to decide (自分で判断する)

QUESTION 5How can a good guide help someone become more independent?よい指導者は、学ぶ人がもっと自分で判断できるようにどう助けられますか？

Follow up
  • What is the first choice the learner could make alone?
  • Which support should remain available afterward?

The guide you would be (自分ならどう導くか)

QUESTION 6What quality would you want people to remember about your guidance?自分の指導がどんな性質で知られてほしいですか？

Follow up
  • What action would show that quality?
  • How would you know the guidance helped?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
