A near-change reveals what a decision must protect (変えかけた経験から守るべきものが見える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
People may change careers through a shift in occupation, sector, responsibility, identity, schedule, or degree of paid work.職業、業界、責任、自己認識、勤務形態、有給労働の程度を変えることで、キャリアを変える人もいます。
Career transitions include movement among education, care, paid employment, self-employment, and retirement.キャリアの移行には、教育、ケア、有給雇用、自営業、退職の間の移動も含まれます。
A period of serious consideration may end in change, staying, postponement, or no final decision.真剣に検討した結果は、変更、継続、延期、未決定のいずれにもなり得ます。
Meaning, growth, flexibility, or relief may compete with income, identity, access, and responsibility.意味、成長、柔軟性、安堵が、収入、自己認識、機会、責任と競合することがあります。
Remaining in a role can reflect informed priorities rather than fear or inertia.今の役割に残ることは、恐れや惰性ではなく、十分に考えた優先順位を表す場合があります。
Training, experiments, reduced hours, projects, or role adjustments can test a direction before a full transition.研修、試行、時間の短縮、プロジェクト、役割調整によって、全面移行前に方向を試せます。transition移行
Career decisions may need confidentiality when disclosure could affect security, reputation, or identity.キャリアの決定は、開示によって安全、評判、立場に影響が出る場合、秘密を守る必要があります。
Encouragement becomes unhelpful when it ignores dependants, health, immigration, discrimination, or local opportunity.扶養、健康、在留資格、差別、地域の機会を無視する励ましは役立たなくなります。
Uncertainty may reflect missing evidence rather than indecision or weakness.不確実さは、優柔不断や弱さではなく、必要な情報がまだないことを示す場合があります。
A credible transition begins with a defined problem or value rather than the appeal of novelty alone.信頼できる転換は、新しさの魅力だけでなく、解決したい問題や実現したい価値から始まります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A possible change (考えた変更)

WARM-UP 1When is changing careers worth serious thought?どんなときに、キャリアチェンジを真剣に考える価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Would the change affect the field, role, schedule, or all three?
  • What is the smallest version of that career change?

What pushed the question (考えるきっかけ)

WARM-UP 2Which pressure is most likely to turn career-change thoughts into action?どんなプレッシャーが、キャリアチェンジの考えを行動に変えやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Has the pressure lasted days, months, or years?
  • Which part of the situation must improve soon?

What pulled the other way (新しい方向の魅力)

QUESTION 1What can make another career direction especially attractive?別のキャリアの方向に強くひかれるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Is the main attraction the work, pay, freedom, growth, or routine?
  • Which benefit would affect daily life most?

What was at risk (懸念したこと)

QUESTION 2What can make changing careers genuinely risky?どのような制約が、キャリアの移行を本当にリスクのあるものにしますか？

Follow up
  • Which risk is biggest: money, training, or family duties?
  • Could changing one step at a time reduce it?

What happened (その後)

QUESTION 3Why can waiting to change careers be a sensible choice?キャリアの移行を延期することが、合理的な決定になり得るのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • Is money, timing, or a family duty holding the decision back?
  • Which new fact could turn waiting into action?

A smaller test (小さく試す)

QUESTION 4What small, low-risk test could show what a new career is really like?新しいキャリアの実際を知るために、どんな小さくてリスクの低い試し方ができますか？

Follow up
  • What question would the test answer?
  • How could someone keep the test cheap and easy to stop?

Who knew (誰に話したか)

QUESTION 5Who is worth talking to before a major career change?大きなキャリアの移行の前に、誰の視点が最も役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What useful question could an experienced person answer?
  • Which personal facts would you avoid sharing?

What a change should improve (変更でよくしたいこと)

QUESTION 6What needs to get better before a career change is worth the risk?キャリアチェンジがリスクに見合うには、何がよくなる必要がありますか？

Follow up
  • Which must improve most: pay, time, interest, or health?
  • Which good part of the current career should remain?
