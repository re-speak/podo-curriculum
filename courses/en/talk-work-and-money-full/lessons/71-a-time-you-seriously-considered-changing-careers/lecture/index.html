A good benefit on paper and in real life (書類上でも実生活でもよい福利厚生)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
One company offers free lunch, a gym, and games in the office.ある会社は、無料の昼食、ジム、オフィス内のゲームを提供しています。
Its employees say the benefit they value most is being able to choose when they start work.しかし社員が最も大切だと言うのは、仕事を始める時間を選べることです。
A benefit has little value if it does not fit people's ordinary lives.福利厚生は、人々の日常生活に合わなければ、あまり価値がありません。
Free food may save money, while flexible hours may save a difficult journey during rush hour.無料の食事はお金を節約し、柔軟な勤務時間は混雑する時間の大変な通勤を避ける助けになります。
A predictable schedule can be more useful than flexibility for people who arrange childcare or other work.育児や別の仕事の予定を立てる人には、柔軟さより予測しやすい勤務予定のほうが役立つことがあります。
A four-day workweek sounds attractive, but lower pay or longer days can change the choice.週休三日は魅力的ですが、給料が下がったり一日の勤務が長くなったりすれば、選択は変わります。
Some benefits also look generous because they encourage people to stay at work longer.中には、社員をより長く職場にいさせるために、気前よく見える福利厚生もあります。
Free dinner is less appealing when it is offered because everyone works late.全員が遅くまで働くために提供される無料の夕食は、それほど魅力的ではありません。
Pay matters, but respect, useful time off, and supportive coworkers can also keep someone in a job.給料は大切ですが、尊重されること、使いやすい休暇、支えてくれる同僚も、仕事を続ける理由になります。
The best benefit is usually one people can use without feeling guilty or asking for special permission.最もよい福利厚生は、罪悪感を持ったり特別な許可を求めたりせずに使えるものです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Three benefits (三つの福利厚生)

WARM-UP 1Which would you choose: more paid vacation, flexible hours, or free lunch?有給休暇の増加、柔軟な勤務時間、無料の昼食なら、どれを選びますか？

Follow up
  • Why that one?
  • Which would you use most often?

A better week (一週間をよくするもの)

WARM-UP 2Which job benefit would improve your ordinary week most?普段の一週間を最もよくしてくれる福利厚生は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would change on a normal day?
  • Would it save time or money?

Good but unused (よくても使わないもの)

QUESTION 1Which job benefit sounds good but you probably wouldn't use?よさそうでも、実際にはあまり使わなそうな福利厚生は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why would it go unused?
  • Who might value it more?

A four-day week (週休三日)

QUESTION 2Would you accept slightly lower pay for a four-day workweek?週休三日のためなら、少し給料が下がっても受け入れますか？

Follow up
  • How much lower would be too much?
  • What would you do with the extra day?

Flexible or predictable (柔軟さか予測しやすさか)

QUESTION 3Which matters more: flexible hours or a predictable schedule?柔軟な勤務時間と予測しやすい予定では、どちらが大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which fits your life now?
  • Who might choose differently?

A standard benefit (標準にすべき福利厚生)

QUESTION 4What is one benefit every job should offer?どんな仕事にもあるべき福利厚生を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why should it be standard?
  • Who would pay for it?

A hidden problem (隠れた問題)

QUESTION 5Can a good job benefit ever be a sign of a bad workplace?よい福利厚生が、悪い職場のサインになることはありますか？

Follow up
  • How?
  • What problem would make the benefit meaningless?

A reason to stay (続ける理由)

QUESTION 6Besides pay, what would make you stay at a job longer?給料以外で、仕事を長く続けたいと思わせるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would people or flexibility matter more?
  • What would still make you leave?
