SMALL THINGS

A rule that only made sense in your family (自分の家でだけ通じた決まり)

Let's talk about why household rules make sense in one home and seem strange in another. Please read the title aloud.家庭の決まりが、ある家では自然で別の家では不思議に見える理由について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Every home makes a small culture (どの家庭にも小さな文化がある)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Every household develops rules that feel obvious inside it.どの家庭にも、その中では当然に感じられる決まりがあります。household rule家庭の決まり
Some rules solve a practical problem that outsiders cannot see.家の外の人には見えない実用的な問題を解決する決まりもあります。
Others continue long after the original reason disappears.もともとの理由がなくなったあとも続く決まりもあります。
Children often assume every home works the same way.子どもは、どの家も同じようにしていると思いがちです。
A visit to another home can reveal the difference suddenly.ほかの家を訪れると、その違いに急に気づくことがあります。
The discovery can be funny, embarrassing, or surprisingly emotional.その発見は、面白かったり、恥ずかしかったり、意外に感情的だったりします。
We may keep a rule even after we stop agreeing with its reason.理由に納得しなくなっても、その決まりを続けることがあります。
Another family's strange rule may be perfectly sensible in context.別の家庭の変わった決まりも、背景を知れば納得できるかもしれません。
Passing a rule on is a choice, not an automatic duty.決まりを次に伝えることは、当然の義務ではなく選択です。
The rules people remember become part of a family's story.覚えている決まりは、その家庭の物語の一部になります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A household rule (家庭の決まり)

WARM-UP 1What's a household rule that makes sense in one family but seems strange elsewhere?ある家庭では自然でも、別の場所では不思議に見える決まりは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What problem might it solve?
  • Why might an outsider misunderstand it?

How it works (決まりの内容)

WARM-UP 2How would an unusual household rule work day to day?家庭の変わった決まりは、日常の中でどのように使われますか？

Follow up
  • Who would be expected to follow it?
  • What might happen when someone forgets?

Discovering differences (違いに気づく時)

QUESTION 1How do people discover that other households have different rules?人は、ほかの家庭に違う決まりがあると、どのように気づくのでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Which situation reveals the difference fastest?
  • What reaction might follow?

Understanding the rule (決まりを理解する)

QUESTION 2What makes an unfamiliar household rule feel reasonable?知らない家庭の決まりが、納得できるようになるのはどのようなときですか？

Follow up
  • Which missing context matters most?
  • What value might the rule protect?

Worth keeping (残す価値)

QUESTION 3Which family rules are worth keeping as an adult?どのような家庭の決まりは、大人になっても残す価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • What purpose do they still serve?
  • How might they need updating?

Where rules differ (決まりが違う分野)

QUESTION 4Which area of home life creates the most different rules?家庭生活のどの分野で、いちばん違う決まりが生まれますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that area vary so much?
  • Which difference could cause conflict?

Explaining it (説明の仕方)

QUESTION 5How should a household explain a rule to a guest?家庭の決まりを、来客にどのように説明するとよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which rule needs advance warning?
  • Where should the household stay flexible?

Known for (覚えられる決まり)

QUESTION 6What small rule should a household be known for?家庭は、どのような小さな決まりで知られるとよいですか？

Follow up
  • What value would the rule express?
  • How could it stay flexible?
