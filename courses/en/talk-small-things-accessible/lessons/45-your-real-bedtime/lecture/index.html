Your real bedtime (実際に寝る時間)

Let's talk about what shapes your real bedtime and the rest you get. Please read the title aloud.実際に眠る時間と、取れる休息を左右するものについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

The gap before sleep (眠るまでのずれ)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The time we plan to sleep and the time we actually fall asleep are often different.寝ようと思う時間と、実際に眠りにつく時間は、よくずれます。
The last hour of the day can disappear into one more small task.一日の最後の一時間は、もう一つの小さな用事で消えることがあります。
Phones make it easy to continue a day that has already ended.スマートフォンがあると、もう終わった一日を簡単に続けられます。
Late evening may be the first time that feels completely our own.夜遅くが、初めて完全に自分の時間だと感じることがあります。
That freedom makes going to bed feel like giving something up.その自由があると、寝ることが何かを手放すように感じられます。
A late night matters differently before a free day and a busy one.夜更かしの影響は、翌日が休みか忙しいかで違います。
Trying to fix sleep with a strict rule can work briefly and then fail.厳しい決まりで睡眠を直そうとしても、短期間で終わることがあります。
A small routine can help us wind down before sleep.眠る前の小さな習慣があると、ゆっくり気持ちを落ち着けやすくなります。wind downゆっくり一日を終える
The useful change may be earlier in the evening, not at bedtime.役立つ変化は、寝る時間ではなく、もっと早い夜の時間にあるかもしれません。
The real goal is enough rest, not a perfect-looking routine.本当の目標は、完璧に見える習慣ではなく、十分に休むことです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Last night's bedtime (昨夜の就寝時間)

WARM-UP 1What time did you go to bed last night?昨夜は何時に寝ましたか？

Follow up
  • What time did you want to sleep?
  • What decided what actually happened?

The last hour (最後の一時間)

WARM-UP 2How did you spend the last hour before trying to sleep?眠ろうとする前の最後の一時間を、どのように過ごしましたか？

Follow up
  • Did the activity have a clear stopping point?
  • Why was it easy or hard to stop?

Your sleep pattern (睡眠のパターン)

QUESTION 1How predictable is your bedtime from day to day?毎日の就寝時間は、どのくらい予測できますか？

Follow up
  • What causes the biggest variation?
  • Which part stays fairly stable?

Does it matter? (問題になる？)

QUESTION 2How does going to bed late affect the next day?遅く寝ると、次の日にどんな影響がありますか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the day changes first?
  • When does a late night matter less?

An earlier bedtime (以前の就寝時間)

QUESTION 3Have you always gone to bed around this time?以前から、だいたいこの時間に寝ていましたか？

Follow up
  • What was different five years ago?
  • What changed your bedtime?

What gets in the way (邪魔するもの)

QUESTION 4What most often delays or shifts your sleep?何が原因で、眠る時間が遅れたりずれたりしますか？

Follow up
  • Is it a task, a habit, or needed private time?
  • Which part could happen at a different time?

A useful change (役立つ変化)

QUESTION 5Which small change seems most likely to improve your rest?どの小さな変化が、休息をいちばん改善できそうですか？

Follow up
  • Why does it seem realistic?
  • What obstacle would it need to overcome?

A realistic bedtime routine (続けられる就寝前の習慣)

QUESTION 6What's a bedtime routine you could actually stick to?実際に続けられそうな、寝る前の習慣はどんなものですか？

Follow up
  • When would you start winding down?
  • What would you stop doing before bed?
