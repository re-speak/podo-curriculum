GOAL

Check in and request a seat

Today, you'll practice checking in and asking for the seats you want. Please read the title aloud.今日は、チェックインをして希望の席を頼む練習をします。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

The scene (今日の場面)

Let's role-play checking in. You're the passenger; I'll be the agent. Let's begin.チェックインのロールプレイをしましょう。あなたは乗客、私は係員です。始めましょう。

Open with the first Agent line and keep the exchange moving.

Native tip (ネイティブのひとこと)

“A window seat” names the type of seat. “By the window” describes its location.「a window seat」は席の種類を表します。「by the window」は場所を表します。

Have the learner read both examples, then ask them to match each one to its situation label.
A named seat type席の種類Could we have a window seat?窓側の席をいただけますか？
Choose by meaning 伝えたい意味で選ぶ
Describing the location位置を伝えるCould we have a seat by the window?窓のそばの席をいただけますか？
