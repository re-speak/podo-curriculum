Tell needs a person (tell には人が必要)

Tell needs a person: told me. Then use would, often contracted to 'd, for the later action.tell には人が必要なので 「told me」 とします。その後の行動は would（通常 'd）で表します。

He told me + he'd + 動詞Report a later action後の行動を伝えるHe told me he'd call back.She told me she'd help.
