BALANCE GAME

Life in the city or the countryside? (都会暮らし、それとも田舎暮らし？)

Let's compare city life and country life, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.都会の生活と田舎の生活を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A short distance can matter more than a large home when essential places are used every day.毎日使う場所が近いなら、広い家より短い移動のほうが大切な場合があります。
Quiet surroundings do not guarantee access to health care, shops, or community.静かな環境でも、医療、店、地域の人々に近いとは限りません。
City access means reaching many needs without planning a long trip.都会の利用しやすさとは、長い移動を計画せずに多くの用事を済ませられることです。city access都会の利用しやすさ
A car can expand rural access while adding cost and dependence.車があれば田舎で行ける場所は増えますが、費用と依存も増えます。
Cities make work, transport, health care, and culture easier to reach.都会では、仕事、交通、医療、文化にアクセスしやすくなります。
That access often comes with higher housing costs and less private space.その便利さには、住居費の高さや私的な空間の少なさが伴うことがあります。
The countryside can offer quiet and space without guaranteeing an easy life.田舎には静けさと空間がありますが、生活が必ず楽になるわけではありません。
A small community can offer strong local ties while making privacy harder.小さな地域では人とのつながりが強い一方、プライバシーを保ちにくいことがあります。
Remote work can change where someone is able to live, but not every other obligation.在宅勤務で住める場所は増えますが、ほかの義務までなくなるわけではありません。
The right place often changes with health, family, transport, and life stage.健康、家族、交通、人生の段階によって、合う場所は変わります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

What you want nearby (近くにほしいもの)

WARM-UP 1What is the most important thing to have within walking distance of your home?家から歩いて行ける範囲にあると最も大切なものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How often would you use it?
  • Would a ten-minute walk feel close enough?

The perfect view (理想の眺め)

WARM-UP 2What would be the perfect view from your window?窓からどんな景色が見えたら理想的ですか？

Follow up
  • Would you accept a longer commute for that view?
  • Would you rather see trees or city lights?

City or quiet town (都会か静かな町)

QUESTION 1For daily life, would you rather live in a busy city or a quiet town?日常生活なら、にぎやかな都会と静かな町のどちらに住みたいですか？

Follow up
  • What would you use most in your chosen place?
  • Which would you miss more: quiet or convenience?

What you would miss (恋しくなるもの)

QUESTION 2What would you miss first after moving from a city to a quiet town?都会から静かな町へ移ったら、最初に何が恋しくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Which daily routine would become harder?
  • Shopping, transport, or seeing friends?

Advantages of country life (田舎暮らしのよさ)

QUESTION 3What advantages does living in the countryside have over living in a city?都会で暮らす場合と比べて、田舎暮らしにはどんなよさがありますか？

Follow up
  • Would you live in the countryside if you had your dream job there?
  • Do you think most people prefer living in big cities or small towns?

An acceptable distance (受け入れられる距離)

QUESTION 4How far would you be willing to travel to a hospital, supermarket, or station?病院、スーパー、駅までなら、どのくらい遠くても受け入れられますか？

Follow up
  • Which one must be closest?
  • Would a beautiful home change your limit?

Services or quiet (便利さか静けさ)

QUESTION 5For everyday life, which matters more: nearby services or a quiet home?日常生活では、近くの便利なサービスと静かな家のどちらが大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which service would you want nearby?
  • Would you accept more noise to live near a station?

Anywhere in the world (世界のどこにでも住めるなら)

QUESTION 6If you had enough money to live anywhere in the world, where would you choose to live, and why?世界のどこにでも住めるだけのお金があるとしたら、どこを選び、その理由は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would you want to live there forever, or just for a few years?
  • What would you miss about your current home?

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
