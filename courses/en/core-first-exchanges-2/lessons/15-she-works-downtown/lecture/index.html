Change the verb with he or she (he・she では動詞を変える)

With he or she, change the present-tense verb: usually add -s, with spelling changes such as study to studies.he や she では現在形の動詞を変え、通常は -s をつけ、study は studies のように綴りも変えます。

She works or she's working? (She works と She's working)

Use the simple present for a regular situation and the -ing form for now or a temporary situation.ふだんのことには現在形、今または一時的なことには -ing 形を使います。
