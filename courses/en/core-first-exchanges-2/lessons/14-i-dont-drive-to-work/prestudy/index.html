PART 1

I don't drive to work.

First, let's practice this pattern.まず、このパターンを練習しましょう。

Introduce Part 1 with this one line, then move on.

Today's pattern (今日のパターン)

Meaning & use 意味・使い方Use this to say that something is not part of your routine.あることをふだんしないと伝えるときに使います。

Briefly explain the meaning, then have the learner read each example aloud.
I don't drive to work.車で通勤しません。
I don't eat breakfast.朝ごはんを食べません。
I don't work on Sundays.日曜日は働きません。

Read the sentences (文を読もう)

Let's read each sentence aloud.一文ずつ声に出して読んでみましょう。

I don't drive to work.車で通勤しません。
I don't eat breakfast.朝ごはんを食べません。
I don't work on Sundays.日曜日は働きません。
I don't drink tea.お茶は飲みません。

Put don't before the action (動作の前に don't)

Use don't before the plain verb to say what you do not do.しないことを言うときは、動詞の原形の前に 「don't」 を置きます。

I + don't + 動詞の原形Negative routineしない習慣I don't drive to work.I don't eat breakfast.

Choose the pattern (パターンを選ぼう)

Choose the answer that matches the Japanese, then read the complete sentence aloud.日本語に合う答えを選び、完成した文を声に出して読んでください。

After each choice, have the learner read the entire completed sentence before moving on.
私は車で通勤しません。I don't/doesn't drive to work.
彼女は朝ごはんを食べません。She doesn't/don't eat breakfast.
私は日曜日は働きません。I don't/doesn't work on Sundays.
彼はお茶を飲みません。He don't/doesn't drink tea.

Don't or don't usually? (don't と don't usually)

Use “don't” for a general no. Add “usually” when there are exceptions.基本的にしないなら「don't」を使います。例外があるなら「don't usually」を使います。

General no基本的にしないI don't drive to work.車で通勤しません。
Pick by meaning 意味で選ぶ
There are exceptions例外があるI don't usually drive to work.ふだんは車で通勤しません。
