First, let's practice saying when your workday starts.まず、仕事を始める時刻を言う練習をしましょう。

Today's pattern (今日のパターン)

Meaning & use 意味・使い方Use this to say the exact time your workday begins.仕事を始める正確な時刻を伝えるときに使います。

Briefly explain the meaning, then have the learner read each example aloud.

Read the sentences (文を読もう)

Use at with an exact time (正確な時刻には at)

Keep "start work" together, and put "at" directly before the clock time.「start work」をひとまとまりにして、時計の時刻のすぐ前に「at」を置きます。

I + start work + at + 時刻Exact clock timeぴったりの時刻I start work at nine.I start work at ten.

PART 2

I usually ___ around ___.

Next, let's practice saying when something usually happens.次に、ふだん何時ごろするかを言う練習をしましょう。

Introduce Part 2 with this one line, then move on.

Today's pattern (今日のパターン)

Meaning & use 意味・使い方Use this to say what normally happens at about a certain time.ふだん何をだいたい何時ごろするかを伝えるときに使います。

Briefly explain the meaning, then have the learner read each example aloud.
I usually finish work around six.ふだん6時ごろ仕事を終えます。
I usually eat lunch around twelve.ふだん12時ごろ昼食をとります。
I usually have dinner around seven.ふだん7時ごろ夕食をとります。

Read the sentences (文を読もう)

Let's read each sentence aloud.一文ずつ声に出して読んでみましょう。

I usually finish work around six.ふだん6時ごろ仕事を終えます。
I usually eat lunch around twelve.ふだん12時ごろ昼食をとります。
I usually have dinner around seven.ふだん7時ごろ夕食をとります。
I usually start work around nine.ふだん9時ごろ仕事を始めます。

Put each word in its place (usually と around の位置)

Put "usually" before the action and "around" before the approximate time.「usually」は動作の前に、「around」はだいたいの時刻の前に置きます。

I + usually + 動作 + around + 時刻Usual action and approximate timeふだんの動作＋だいたいの時刻I usually finish work around six.I usually eat lunch around twelve.
