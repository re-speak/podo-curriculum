Let's discuss what financial security needs to make possible. Please read the title aloud.経済的な安心によって何が可能になる必要があるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Enough is a moving line (「十分」の基準は動く)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Any threshold for enough depends on household needs, location, obligations, and public support.「十分」の基準は、家庭の必要、地域、責任、公的支援によって変わります。
A financial cushion can absorb shocks, although it cannot remove uncertainty from life.金銭的な余裕は突然の負担を吸収できますが、人生の不確かさまではなくせません。uncertainty不確かさ
The most meaningful early threshold may be resilience to a foreseeable expense rather than a dramatic fortune.最初に意味を持つ基準は、大きな財産より、予想できる出費に耐えられることかもしれません。
Additional income has its strongest effect where housing, health, food, or safety remain unstable.住居、健康、食事、安全が不安定なほど、収入の増加は大きな効果を持ちます。
Beyond basic security, money often changes autonomy and exposure to stress rather than guaranteeing happiness.基本的な安心を超えると、お金は幸福を保証するより、自由やストレスへのさらされ方を変えることが多いです。
Rising resources may shift comparison and expectation, moving the line of enough again.使える資源が増えると、比較や期待が変わり、「十分」の線もまた動くことがあります。
Two people with similar resources may face different risks, responsibilities, and senses of security.同じような資源を持つ二人でも、危険、責任、安心感は異なることがあります。
Income reveals little about health, relationships, debt, care burdens, or private fear.収入だけでは、健康、人間関係、借金、ケアの負担、個人的な不安はほとんど分かりません。
Financial security may matter partly because it releases attention previously consumed by uncertainty.経済的な安心の価値は、不確かさに奪われていた注意を取り戻せることにもあります。
A defensible answer may be a range tied to security and choice rather than a universal figure.納得できる答えは、全員に共通する数字ではなく、安心と選択に結びついた幅かもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

What enough money can cover (十分なお金で払えるもの)

WARM-UP 1When someone says, ‘I have enough money,’ what do you imagine they can afford?誰かが「十分なお金がある」と言うとき、何を無理なく払えると想像しますか？

Follow up
  • Which cost matters most?
  • Would a home and emergency savings count as enough?

The first money worry to remove (最初になくしたいお金の心配)

WARM-UP 2Which money worry would you remove first?お金の心配を一つなくせるなら、最初にどれをなくしますか？

Follow up
  • How would removing that worry change daily life?
  • Which is more stressful: an unexpected bill or rising monthly costs?

Outside money's reach (お金だけでは解決できないこと)

QUESTION 1What's something important that more money can't fix?お金が増えても解決できない大切なことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could money still help indirectly?
  • Can money buy trust?

Different needs (異なる必要額)

QUESTION 2What can make the same income feel comfortable for one person and stressful for another?同じ収入でも、一人には余裕があり、別の人には苦しく感じられるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • Which cost varies most?
  • Who usually needs more money: one person or a family of four?

When extra money matters most (お金が最も役立つとき)

QUESTION 3When does extra money make the biggest difference?お金が増えることが最も大きな違いを生むのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Which need is being met?
  • Does extra money matter more for rent or entertainment?

Space after money worries (お金の心配が減った後の余裕)

QUESTION 4If money worries took less of your attention, what would you focus on instead?お金の心配に注意を取られなくなったら、代わりに何に集中したいですか？

Follow up
  • Why would that deserve more attention?
  • Would you use the extra focus for rest, family, or learning?

Savings or steady income (貯金か安定した収入か)

QUESTION 5Which creates a stronger feeling of security: savings or reliable monthly income?貯金と毎月の安定した収入のどちらが、より強い安心を生みますか？

Follow up
  • Why does your choice feel safer?
  • What's one bill people need to plan for every month?

Security beyond money (お金以外の安心)

QUESTION 6What kind of security matters most besides money?お金以外では、どんな安心が最も重要ですか？

Follow up
  • How can that kind of security help?
  • Which gives more security: good health or people you can rely on?
