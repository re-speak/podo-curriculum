Let's design a date that would feel enjoyable, relaxed, and right for you. Please read the title aloud.自分にとって楽しく、気楽に過ごせるデートについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes a date enjoyable (楽しいデートを作るもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Two people meet at a quiet café, and within ten minutes they can hear each other and choose whether to stay.二人が静かなカフェで会えば、最初の10分で相手の話を聞きながら、もう少しいたいか考えられます。
A good date needs enough of a plan to feel intentional, but not so much that every minute is fixed.よいデートには気持ちが伝わる程度の計画が必要ですが、一分ごとに予定を決める必要はありません。
The place matters because noise, cost, travel time, and an easy exit all affect how relaxed people feel.場所は大切です。騒音、費用、移動時間、帰りやすさのすべてが、二人の気楽さに影響します。
An activity can remove the pressure to talk constantly, but it can also leave too little room for conversation.何かの活動があれば話し続ける負担は減りますが、会話する時間が少なすぎることもあります。
Deciding together can reveal whether both people listen, compromise, and show interest in each other's comfort.一緒に決めることで、お互いに話を聞き、譲り合い、相手の心地よさを大切にできるかが分かります。
An expensive plan is not automatically thoughtful, and a simple plan is not automatically boring.高価な計画が必ず思いやりのあるものとは限らず、簡単な計画が必ず退屈とも限りません。
A remembered preference or a well-chosen detail can make an ordinary date feel personal.相手の好みを覚えていたり、一つのことを丁寧に選んだりすると、普通のデートも特別に感じられます。
Some awkwardness is normal at first, and one quiet moment does not prove that two people have no connection.最初に少し気まずくなるのは普通で、一度の沈黙だけで二人に相性がないとは言えません。
Rudeness or feeling unsafe can be a reason to leave early, while a lack of chemistry is a different kind of problem.失礼な態度や危険を感じることは早く帰る理由になりますが、気が合わないことは別の問題です。
A successful date does not have to promise a future; it can simply help both people learn whether they want another one.よいデートは将来を約束する必要はなく、もう一度会いたいかを二人が知る機会になれば十分です。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A first-date plan (初デートの案)

WARM-UP 1Which sounds better for a first date: coffee, a meal, a walk, or an activity?初デートなら、コーヒー、食事、散歩、何かの体験のどれがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Why does that one suit you?
  • Which option would you least enjoy?

A relaxed atmosphere (くつろげる雰囲気)

WARM-UP 2What would make that date feel relaxed rather than awkward?そのデートが気まずくならず、くつろげるものになるには何が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • How much do the place and noise level matter?
  • What would help in the first ten minutes?

Talk or activity (会話と活動)

QUESTION 1Would you rather talk the whole time or have an activity to focus on?ずっと話すのと、何かの活動をしながら過ごすのでは、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which activity would make conversation easier?
  • Why might constant conversation feel tiring?

Planning together (二人で決めること)

QUESTION 2Would you want the other person to plan everything, or decide together?相手に全部計画してほしいですか、それとも一緒に決めたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which part would you happily leave to them?
  • Which choice would you want to make yourself?

A memorable detail (心に残る工夫)

QUESTION 3What's one small detail that could make the date memorable?デートを心に残るものにする小さな工夫は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could it be inexpensive?
  • How could it show that the person paid attention?

An early ending (早く帰りたい理由)

QUESTION 4What would make you want to end the date early?何があったら、デートを早く切り上げたくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Is there a difference between a red flag and no chemistry?
  • How would you leave politely?

Romance or fun (ロマンスと楽しさ)

QUESTION 5Does a good date need romance, or can it simply be fun?よいデートにはロマンチックさが必要ですか、それとも楽しいだけでもよいですか？

Follow up
  • What feels romantic to you?
  • When is having fun enough?

A terrible date idea (自分には合わないデート)

QUESTION 6Now the opposite: what popular date idea would be terrible for you?では逆に、人気はあっても自分には最悪だと思うデート案は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would crowds, cost, or the activity be the problem?
  • What would you choose instead?
