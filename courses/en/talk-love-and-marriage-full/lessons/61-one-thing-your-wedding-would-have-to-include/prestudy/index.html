What makes a wedding feel personal (自分らしい結婚式を作るもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A wedding can bring a private commitment into a public ritual shared with other people.結婚式は、二人の私的な約束を、ほかの人と共有する公の儀式にすることがあります。
A single symbolic element may carry more personal meaning than an elaborate venue.豪華な会場より、一つの象徴的な要素のほうが個人的な意味を持つことがあります。symbolic象徴的な
Guest expectations, budget, family wishes, and tradition can create competing demands.招待客の期待、予算、家族の希望、伝統から、互いに競合する要求が生じることがあります。
A wedding tradition can be kept, adapted, or removed rather than treated as an unquestioned obligation.結婚式の伝統は、疑わない義務ではなく、残す、変える、省くという選択ができます。obligation義務
Removing a familiar element can preserve time, money, or attention for the event's real priority.よくある要素を一つなくすと、本当の優先事項に時間、費用、注意を使えることがあります。
Wedding decisions often expose how authority is shared among the couple, families, and community.結婚式の決定から、二人、家族、地域の間で決定権がどう分かれているかが見えます。
A memorable wedding moment often depends more on connection than expense or perfection.心に残る結婚式の瞬間は、費用や完璧さより、人とのつながりから生まれることがあります。
For some people, a private legal ceremony—or no wedding event—is more authentic than a celebration.人によっては、祝宴より、二人だけの法的な手続きや式をしない選択のほうが自分らしく感じられます。
Imagining the event can clarify values about family, privacy, and celebration without implying a plan to marry.結婚の予定を前提にしなくても、式を想像すると、家族、私生活、祝い方への価値観が明確になります。
A wedding feels coherent when its choices express the people involved rather than a borrowed checklist.借りた条件表ではなく、関わる人を表す選択がそろうと、結婚式に一貫した自分らしさが生まれます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One thing to include (欠かせない一つ)

WARM-UP 1If you had a wedding tomorrow, what one thing would have to be there?もし明日結婚式を開くなら、どの一つの要素が必須ですか？

Follow up
  • How would guests notice that element?
  • Which family member might value it too?

A wedding tradition (残したい結婚式の習慣)

WARM-UP 2Which wedding tradition would you definitely keep?結婚式の習慣の中で、絶対に残したいものはどれですか？

Follow up
  • Why does it matter to you?
  • How would you make it fit the couple?

What goes (省くもの)

QUESTION 1If one common wedding tradition had to go, which would you choose?よくある結婚式の習慣を一つなくすなら、どれを選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which source of stress would disappear?
  • Would the couple, family, or guests miss it most?

Who decides (決める人)

QUESTION 2How should a couple and their families share wedding decisions?結婚式の決定権は、二人と家族の間でどう分けるべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which decision could legitimately involve family?
  • Which choice should remain private to the couple?

A memorable wedding (心に残る結婚式)

QUESTION 3What makes one wedding detail truly memorable?結婚式の一つの演出を本当に心に残るものにするのは、どんな特徴ですか？

Follow up
  • Could the element feel special without costing much?
  • How would it work with only a few guests?

The worst part (大変な部分)

QUESTION 4What part of a wedding creates the most unnecessary stress?結婚式で、いちばん余計なストレスを生むのはどの部分ですか？

Follow up
  • Who carries most of that stress?
  • Which one change would lower it fastest?

Skipping the event (式をしない選択)

QUESTION 5Can a private ceremony feel more personal than a large public celebration?私的な式のほうが、大規模な公の祝いより個人的に感じられますか？

Follow up
  • What would become easier at a private ceremony?
  • Which part of a large celebration would be hardest to give up?

What the day is for (式の目的)

QUESTION 6What should be the main purpose of a wedding?結婚式を開く人たちにとって、どんな中心的な目的が式を導くべきですか？

Follow up
  • Should the couple, the families, or the guests come first?
  • Which wedding decision would make that priority visible?
