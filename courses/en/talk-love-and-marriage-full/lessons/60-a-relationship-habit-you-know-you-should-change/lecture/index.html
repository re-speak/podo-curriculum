Changing a relationship pattern (関係の癖を変える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Relationship patterns often become most visible when stress reduces our patience and flexibility.ストレスで忍耐力や柔軟性が低下すると、関係の中の傾向が最も見えやすくなります。
Under pressure, one person may interrupt, another may shut down, and another may repeatedly seek reassurance.プレッシャーの中では、話を遮る人、心を閉ざす人、安心の確認を繰り返す人がいます。
Change begins by identifying the trigger and the first moment the pattern appears.変化は、きっかけと、その傾向が最初に表れる瞬間を見つけることから始まります。
A partner's feedback can provide evidence without becoming a complete verdict on our character.パートナーの意見は、人柄全体への判決ではなく、行動を考える手がかりとして使えます。
When feedback produces shame, defensiveness may protect the habit rather than correct it.指摘によって恥ずかしさが強まると、防御的な反応がその癖を直すどころか守ってしまうことがあります。
A specific replacement behaviour is more workable than a broad promise to become a better partner.よりよいパートナーになるという広い約束より、具体的な代替行動のほうが実行しやすくなります。replacement behaviour代替行動
Improvement is rarely linear; an old pattern may reappear when circumstances become difficult.改善は一直線ではなく、状況が厳しくなると古い傾向が再び表れることがあります。
A behaviour that one person barely notices may feel deeply disruptive to another.ある人がほとんど気にしない行動でも、別の人には関係を大きく乱すものに感じられます。
Naming an unhelpful pattern in advance supports honesty, but it does not remove responsibility for its impact.役立たない傾向を事前に伝えることは誠実さにつながりますが、その影響への責任を消すものではありません。
Recognizing healthy habits prevents reflection from collapsing into a catalogue of personal faults.健全な習慣も認めることで、振り返りが自分の欠点を並べるだけになるのを防げます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A hard habit to change (変えにくい癖)

WARM-UP 1Which relationship habit is usually hardest to change?人間関係の癖の中で、普通いちばん変えにくいものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What usually brings the habit out?
  • Why can it be hard to change?

When people notice it (癖に気づくとき)

WARM-UP 2When do people usually notice a bad relationship habit in themselves?人は普通、いつ自分のよくない関係の癖に気づきますか？

Follow up
  • What result makes the habit clear?
  • Why can it be hard to notice?

What others say (人からの指摘)

QUESTION 1How can someone raise a relationship habit without turning it into a fight?関係の癖を、口論にせずどう話題にできますか？

Follow up
  • What exact behaviour should they mention?
  • When would the conversation go best?

Changing the reaction (反応を変える)

QUESTION 2What could someone do instead of reacting badly during an argument?口論中によくない反応をする代わりに、何ができますか？

Follow up
  • What could remind them to use the new response?
  • Why might it be hard in the moment?

A habit you dislike (相手の気になる癖)

QUESTION 3Which relationship habit is hardest to live with, and why?一緒に過ごす上でいちばん受け入れにくい人間関係の癖と、その理由は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What effect can it have on the other person?
  • Can someone have the habit without noticing it?

Explanation or excuse (説明か言い訳か)

QUESTION 4When does explaining a bad relationship habit start to sound like an excuse?人間関係のよくない癖を説明することが、言い訳に聞こえ始めるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the problem is still the person's responsibility?
  • What follow-up action would make the explanation believable?

A better response (よりよい対応)

QUESTION 5What response could replace shutting down during conflict?対立中に心を閉ざす反応を、何に置き換えられますか？

Follow up
  • What makes the better response difficult in the moment?
  • Would a reminder, another person, or more time help most?

A healthy habit (続けたいよい癖)

QUESTION 6Which healthy relationship habit matters most but often goes unnoticed?大切なのに気づかれにくい、人間関係のよい癖は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How does the habit help both people?
  • Why might people fail to notice it?
