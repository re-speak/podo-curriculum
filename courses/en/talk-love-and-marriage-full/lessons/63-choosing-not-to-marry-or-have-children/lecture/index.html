LOVE AND MARRIAGE

Choosing not to marry or have children (結婚や子どもを持たない選択)

Let's talk about different choices around marriage and children. Please read the title aloud.結婚や子どもについてのさまざまな選択を話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

More than one life path (一つではない生き方)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Marriage and parenthood are distinct decisions, even when social expectations link them.社会の期待で結びつけられていても、結婚と親になることは別々の決定です。parenthood親になること
A person may want marriage and children, one without the other, neither, or more time before deciding.結婚と子どもの両方、一方だけ、どちらも望まない、または決めるまで時間が必要という人がいます。
Choices may reflect desire, health, finances, caregiving, freedom, relationships, or deeply held values.選択には、希望、健康、経済状況、誰かを支える責任、自由、人間関係、強い価値観が関わります。
Because the decision can involve intimate circumstances, a person does not owe others a full justification.その決定には非常に私的な事情が関わるため、ほかの人に完全な説明をする義務はありません。
The same family question may feel caring in one context and coercive in another.同じ家族からの質問でも、ある状況では思いやりに、別の状況では強制的な圧力に感じられます。coercive強制的な
Family concern may focus on future loneliness, regret, care, or continuity across generations.家族は、将来の孤独、後悔、ケア、家族が次の世代へ続くかどうかを心配することがあります。
Concern can be genuine while still leaving the final decision with the adult whose life it shapes.心配が本物でも、その人生を生きる大人本人に最終判断を残すことはできます。
Views about marriage or children may evolve, remain consistent, or stay genuinely unresolved.結婚や子どもについての考えは、変化する、安定している、または本当に未決定のままということがあります。
Material conditions and public policy shape available choices, but they do not reduce every decision to economics.生活条件や公共政策は選べる道に影響しますが、すべての決定を経済だけで説明できるわけではありません。
A neutral conversation leaves room for multiple life paths and does not recruit the other person to a position.中立的な会話は、複数の生き方を認め、相手を特定の立場へ引き込もうとしません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Someone's decision (誰かの選択)

WARM-UP 1Why might someone choose not to marry or become a parent?どのような価値観や状況が、結婚も親になることも選ばない決定につながるのでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Which reasons do people often forget about?
  • Why are marriage and children separate choices?

Their reason (その理由)

WARM-UP 2Does anyone owe others an explanation for not wanting marriage or children?結婚や子どもを望まない理由を、ほかの人に説明する義務はありますか？

Follow up
  • Which questions cross a privacy line?
  • What wording would make honest curiosity feel respectful?

What people say (周りの反応)

QUESTION 1How can someone ask about a choice not to marry or have children without sounding judgmental?結婚しない、または子どもを持たない選択について、批判的に聞こえずにどう質問できますか？

Follow up
  • Which question would feel respectful?
  • How can someone ask without trying to change the person's mind?

Your view (自分の考えの変化)

QUESTION 2What can change someone's views on marriage or children over time?どのような経験や条件が、結婚や親になることへの考えを変えるのでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Which change could matter most: money, health, or a relationship?
  • Is ‘I haven't decided’ a complete answer?

Family expectations (家族の期待)

QUESTION 3How can family expectations affect decisions about marriage or children?家族や社会の期待は、結婚や親になることへの決定をどう形作りますか？

Follow up
  • Which family comment creates the strongest pressure?
  • What is one question relatives should avoid?

With no pressure (圧力がないなら)

QUESTION 4If social pressure disappeared, what should guide a choice about marriage or children?社会的な圧力がなくなったら、結婚や子どもについての選択を何が導くべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which should guide the choice first: personal wishes, health, or money?
  • Which real-life limit still deserves attention?

Two different decisions (二つの別の決定)

QUESTION 5Why should marriage and having children be treated as separate decisions?なぜ、結婚と親になることは別々の人生の決定として扱うべきですか？

Follow up
  • How do the daily duties of marriage and parenting differ?
  • What makes people talk about the two decisions together?

Responding with respect (敬意のある応じ方)

QUESTION 6How can people discuss marriage and children without assuming one right choice?結婚や子どもについて、一つの正解を決めつけずにどう話せますか？

Follow up
  • Which private reason should nobody demand proof of?
  • What could someone say to welcome more than one life path?
