PEOPLE AND TIES

How accurate is your MBTI? (MBTIはどのくらい当たっている？)

Let's talk about what MBTI can—and cannot—tell us about people. Please read the title aloud.MBTIから人について分かることと、分からないことについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Introduce today's topic, then have the learner read the title aloud.

A useful label—or a fixed box? (役立つラベル、それとも固定された箱？)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
MBTI starts with people's own answers about how they usually think, decide, and spend time with others.MBTIは、普段どのように考え、決め、人と過ごすかについての自分の答えから始まります。
The test puts the answers into four groups and gives a four-letter type.テストは答えを四つの分類に分け、四文字のタイプを示します。
Many people like the description because it gives words to things they already know about themselves.自分についてすでに知っていることを言葉にしてくれるため、説明が好きな人は多くいます。
A general description can feel very personal when someone remembers the parts that fit.合う部分をよく覚えていると、一般的な説明でもとても自分らしく感じられます。
People may not notice the parts that do not fit as clearly.当てはまらない部分には、あまりはっきり気づかないこともあります。
People also act differently in different places, such as at work, at home, or with close friends.人は、職場、家、親しい友人といるときなど、場所によって違う行動をします。
Mood, experience, and the meaning of a question can change a later test result.気分、経験、質問の意味の受け取り方で、あとのテスト結果が変わることがあります。
Friends or family may see habits that the person taking the test does not see.友人や家族は、テストを受ける本人には見えない習慣に気づくことがあります。
A type can help people start a conversation about personality.タイプは、性格について会話を始めるのに役立つことがあります。
A problem starts when the label becomes the person's whole identity or an excuse.ラベルがその人のアイデンティティのすべてや言い訳になると、問題が始まります。
Knowing a person's type may make us expect something before we know them well.相手をよく知る前に、タイプによって何かを期待してしまうことがあります。
MBTI may be most useful as a way to talk, not as a set of rules for judging people.MBTIは、人を判断する規則ではなく、話すための方法として使うと最も役立つかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

What the type gets right (タイプが当てていること)

WARM-UP 1What does your MBTI type get right about you?自分のMBTIタイプは、自分のどんなところを正しく表していますか？

Follow up
  • Which example shows it best?
  • If you do not know your type, would you want to take the test?

Your type in action (タイプらしい行動)

WARM-UP 2When do you act most like that type?どんなときに、そのタイプらしく行動しますか？

Follow up
  • Is it more visible at home, at work, or with friends?
  • When do you act completely differently?

Why it fits (当てはまる理由)

QUESTION 1Why do you think that part fits you?その部分は、なぜ自分に当てはまると思いますか？

Follow up
  • Has it always been true?
  • What habit makes it visible?

Other people's view (周りの見方)

QUESTION 2Who sees a personality more clearly: the person or a close friend?性格をより正確に見ているのは、本人と親しい友人のどちらだと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Which patterns might an observer recognize?
  • Why might people see themselves differently from how others see them?

What the result misses (結果に表れない一面)

QUESTION 3What is one part of you that an MBTI result might miss?MBTIの結果では表しきれない自分の一面は何ですか？

Follow up
  • In which situation does that side appear?
  • Would a close friend agree?

Asking other people (人に聞くこと)

QUESTION 4Is MBTI a good topic for starting a conversation?MBTIは会話を始めるのによい話題ですか？

Follow up
  • When can it open a worthwhile discussion?
  • What quick judgments should people avoid?

Change over time (時間と変化)

QUESTION 5Why might someone's MBTI result change over time?人のMBTI結果が時間とともに変わるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • How might experience change the meaning of a question?
  • Which parts of a personality may stay fairly stable?

Labels and treatment (ラベルと接し方)

QUESTION 6What can go wrong when people rely too much on someone's MBTI?人のMBTIを頼りすぎると、どんな問題が起こることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Which wrong expectation could affect how the person is treated?
  • What question would help you see the individual?
