What keeps a friendship going (友情を続けるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Long friendships rarely stay exactly the same.長い友情も、ずっと同じ形のままとは限りません。
People change jobs, homes, routines, and priorities.仕事や住む場所、生活習慣、優先することは変わります。
A friendship lasts when both people make room for those changes.お互いの変化を受け入れられると、友情は続きやすくなります。
Frequent contact helps, but it is not the only measure of closeness.よく連絡を取ることは役立ちますが、親しさを測る唯一の基準ではありません。
Some friends reconnect easily after months apart.何か月も会わなくても、すぐ元のように話せる友人もいます。
Shared history gives them context that new friends do not have.共通の思い出があると、新しい友人にはない理解が生まれます。
History is not enough if one person stops listening or showing care.ただし、どちらかが話を聞かず、気にかけなくなれば、思い出だけでは続きません。
Clear limits also protect a friendship from quiet resentment.はっきりした限界があると、言えない不満から友情を守れます。resentment言えない不満
People sometimes drift apart without either person doing anything wrong.どちらも悪くなくても、自然に疎遠になることがあります。drift apart疎遠になる
A lasting friendship is built by what both people keep bringing to it.長く続く友情は、お互いが関係に与え続けるもので作られます。

WARM-UP 2What kind of ordinary meeting can grow into a strong friendship?どんな普通の出会いが、強い友情に育つことがありますか？

Follow up
  • What gives the two people something to talk about?
  • When do they start feeling like close friends?

Changing over time (時間による変化)

QUESTION 1How can a friendship change without losing its strength?友情は、強さを失わずにどのように変化していけますか？

Follow up
  • Which changes can be healthy?
  • What should still feel familiar?

Staying connected (つながりを保つ)

QUESTION 2When friends are busy, what kind of contact keeps them close without creating pressure?友人同士が忙しいとき、プレッシャーを生まずに親しさを保つのは、どんな連絡ですか？

Follow up
  • How much contact keeps closeness?
  • What shows care without feeling like a duty?

What damages it (友情を傷つけるもの)

QUESTION 3Which behaviour hurts a friendship?友情に最も大きな損害を与えるのは、どのような行動ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does it hurt the friendship?
  • What would the person need to do next?

Drifting apart (疎遠になる)

QUESTION 4Why do some friendships fade even when neither person is at fault?どちらも悪くなくても、友情が薄れていくのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • Which life change most quickly reduces contact?
  • When is growing apart simply a normal change?

Starting again (関係を戻す)

QUESTION 5When is a friendship worth trying to revive?友情をもう一度よみがえらせる価値があるのは、どのようなときですか？

Follow up
  • What could reopen contact naturally?
  • How can renewed contact avoid creating pressure?

What you bring (自分が与えるもの)

QUESTION 6What do you bring to a strong friendship?強い友情に、自分は何を与えていますか？

Follow up
  • How would close friends describe what you bring to the relationship?
  • What would you still like to improve?
