Why first impressions change (第一印象が変わる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A first impression forms before we know much about a person.第一印象は、相手をよく知る前に生まれます。
A voice, expression, or short reply can feel like strong evidence.声や表情、短い返事が、確かな証拠のように感じられることがあります。
But the person may be tired, nervous, busy, or out of place.しかし、その人は疲れていたり、緊張していたり、忙しかったりしただけかもしれません。
One unusual moment can therefore create an unfair picture.そのため、普段と違う一瞬から、不公平なイメージができることがあります。
Repeated contact gives us more situations to compare.何度も会うと、違う場面での行動を比べられます。
A generous action or honest conversation may change the picture quickly.親切な行動や正直な会話で、印象がすぐ変わることもあります。
Sometimes the change is slower and comes from a pattern.一方で、何度かの行動を見て、ゆっくり印象が変わることもあります。
A positive first impression can also turn out to be wrong.よい第一印象も、間違っていたと分かることがあります。turn out実際には〜だとわかる
Remembering our mistakes can make us slower to judge others.自分の思い違いを覚えておくと、人をすぐに判断しにくくなります。
Other people are also forming incomplete first impressions of us.同時に、ほかの人も私たちについて不完全な第一印象を作っています。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A wrong first impression (間違った第一印象)

WARM-UP 1Has anyone ever made a completely wrong first impression on you?誰かに対する第一印象が、まったく間違っていたことはありますか？

Follow up
  • What did you think at first?
  • If not, which is easier to misread: a quiet person or a talkative person?

The deciding detail (印象を決めた点)

WARM-UP 2What small detail shaped that first impression?どんな小さな点が、その第一印象を作りましたか？

Follow up
  • Was it their voice, expression, clothes, or something else?
  • Why did you notice it?

A change of mind (考えが変わったきっかけ)

QUESTION 1What happened that changed your mind?何があって、その人への考えが変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • Was one action enough, or did it take time?
  • If you have no example, what would make you change a first impression?

Fast or slow (速い変化と遅い変化)

QUESTION 2Why do some first impressions change quickly while others stick?すぐ見直される第一印象と、なかなか変わらない第一印象があるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • Why might someone ignore new information?
  • What could help the impression change?

Missing context (足りない背景)

QUESTION 3What could explain someone's bad first impression?誰かの悪い第一印象は、何が原因かもしれませんか？

Follow up
  • Could tiredness or nervousness explain the behaviour?
  • What would you want to know before judging?

The other direction (逆の変化)

QUESTION 4What warning sign can completely reverse a good first impression?どんな危険信号が、よい第一印象を完全に変えることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Which warning sign is commonly overlooked?
  • How much new information would make the change fair?

Judging quickly (すぐ判断すること)

QUESTION 5When can a quick first impression be useful?すぐに持つ第一印象が役立つのは、どのようなときですか？

Follow up
  • When can a quick impression become unfair?
  • What should make us wait before judging?

How others misread you (誤解されやすいところ)

QUESTION 6What do people often misread about you at first?初対面で、自分のどんなところを誤解されやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What do they assume instead?
  • How long does it usually take them to notice?
