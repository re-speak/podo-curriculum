What an emergency contact represents (緊急時の連絡先が表すもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
An emergency call asks for more than sympathy.緊急時の電話では、共感だけでなく実際の助けも求めます。
The right contact depends on the kind of problem.誰に連絡するかは、問題の種類によって変わります。
A trusted person may know our needs but lack the right skill.信頼する人は自分を分かっていても、必要な技能がない場合があります。
A professional service may be the safest first call.専門サービスや緊急サービスが最初の連絡先として安全なことがあります。
After that, personal support can help with decisions and next steps.そのあと、身近な人が判断や次の行動を支えてくれます。
In the first few minutes, reliability can matter more than emotional closeness.最初の数分は、心の近さより、確実に対応してくれることが大切な場合があります。
Distance limits physical help, but advice, coordination, and reassurance can still reach us.距離があると直接の助けは限られますが、助言や手配、安心感は届けられます。
A move, illness, or relationship change can alter which people and services belong in a support network.引っ越し、病気、人間関係の変化で、支援体制に含まれる人やサービスも変わります。
Some people do not have one obvious personal contact, and a service may be part of the answer.すぐ思い浮かぶ身近な人がいなくても、サービスを選ぶ方法があります。
A support network of people and services is stronger when roles and contact details are clear before a crisis.緊急事態の前に役割や連絡先が分かっていると、人やサービスを含む支援体制はもっと確かになります。support network人やサービスを含む支援体制

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your first call (最初に連絡する相手)

WARM-UP 1Away from home, if you lost your phone and wallet, who would you contact first?外出先で携帯電話と財布をなくしたら、まず誰に連絡しようとしますか？

Follow up
  • How could you reach them without your phone?
  • When would you contact a professional service first?

Why that person (その人を選ぶ理由)

WARM-UP 2Why would you choose that person?なぜ、その人を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Are they calm, practical, nearby, or something else?
  • What could they do first?

Your first words (最初に伝えること)

QUESTION 1What would you say first once you reached them?連絡がついたら、最初に何を伝えますか？

Follow up
  • Which location detail would matter?
  • How briefly could you explain the problem?

A different emergency (別の緊急事態)

QUESTION 2What kind of emergency would make you call someone else?どんな緊急事態なら、別の人に連絡しますか？

Follow up
  • Would you call a professional service or another trusted person?
  • What different help would you need?

Emergency services first (緊急サービスへの連絡)

QUESTION 3When should you call emergency services before someone you know?知り合いより先に、緊急サービスへ連絡すべきなのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What is one clear example?
  • Who would you contact after help was on the way?

A changing first choice (変わった第一連絡先)

QUESTION 4Has your first-choice contact changed over time?最初に連絡したい相手は、以前と変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • Who would you have called before?
  • What caused the change?

Help from far away (遠くからの助け)

QUESTION 5Would you still call them if they lived far away?その人が遠くに住んでいても、連絡しますか？

Follow up
  • What help could they give from a distance?
  • Who could help nearby?

A backup plan (予備の連絡先)

QUESTION 6Does that person know you'd call them in an emergency?その人は、緊急時に自分が連絡するつもりだと知っていますか？

Follow up
  • Should you tell them?
  • Who would be your backup contact?
