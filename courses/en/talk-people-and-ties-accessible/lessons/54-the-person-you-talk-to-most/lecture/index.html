Let's talk about how contact patterns shape our relationships. Please read the title aloud.連絡のしかたが人間関係に与える影響について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

What frequent contact reveals (頻繁な会話から分かること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The person we talk to most is not always the person we feel closest to.いちばんよく話す人が、いちばん親しい人とは限りません。
Work, home, and daily routines shape who is available.仕事、家庭、日課によって、話せる相手が決まります。
Some frequent conversations are practical rather than personal.よくする会話でも、個人的ではなく用事だけの場合があります。
Others move easily between small updates and serious worries.日常の小さな話から深い悩みまで、自然に話せる関係もあります。
Messaging makes frequent contact possible without sharing a schedule.メッセージなら、同じ時間が空いていなくても頻繁に連絡できます。
Calls or face-to-face talks may carry more tone and detail.電話や対面の会話では、声の調子や細かな情報が伝わります。
The main person can change when a job, home, or relationship changes.仕事、住む場所、関係が変わると、よく話す相手も変わります。
Losing regular contact can reveal how much support it provided.日常の連絡がなくなると、それがどれだけ支えだったか分かることがあります。
Staying in touch requires a rhythm both people can maintain.連絡を続けるには、二人が無理なく続けられる頻度が必要です。stay in touch連絡を取り続ける
The best measure may be not frequency but whether real things can be said.大切なのは頻度より、本当のことを話せるかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your main contact (いちばん話す相手)

WARM-UP 1Who do you talk to most these days?最近、いちばんよく話す相手は誰ですか？

Follow up
  • Do you mostly talk in person or by message?
  • Is it a family member, friend, coworker, or someone else?

Your usual topics (いつもの話題)

WARM-UP 2What do you usually talk about with them?その人とは、普段どんなことを話しますか？

Follow up
  • Is it mostly everyday updates or bigger problems?
  • Which subject do you avoid?

Short or long (短いやり取りと長い会話)

QUESTION 1Do you mostly exchange short messages or have long conversations?短いメッセージをやり取りすることと、長く話すことでは、どちらが多いですか？

Follow up
  • Which one keeps you closer?
  • When does frequent contact feel like pressure?

Deeper connection (関係を深める話題)

QUESTION 2What kinds of conversation make people feel closer over time?どのような会話が、時間をかけて関係を深めますか？

Follow up
  • Do deeper conversations always require personal details?
  • How can ordinary daily talk build trust?

Easy and difficult topics (話しやすい話題と難しい話題)

QUESTION 3Why is one subject easy to discuss with one person but hard with another?同じ話題でも、相手によって話しやすさが変わるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • What topics need more trust?
  • When can timing matter more than the topic?

A changing pattern (変わった連絡の形)

QUESTION 4Have they always been the person you talk to most?その人は、以前からいちばんよく話す相手でしたか？

Follow up
  • When did the pattern change?
  • What brought you closer?

Five years ago (5年前の相手)

QUESTION 5Who did you talk to most five years ago?5年前、いちばんよく話していた相手は誰ですか？

Follow up
  • Why did that person matter then?
  • Do you still keep in touch?

Staying close (親しさを保つ方法)

QUESTION 6If you couldn't talk often, what would help you stay close?あまり話せなくなったら、親しさを保つために何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of contact would matter most?
  • How long could the silence last without feeling awkward?
