Your plans for this weekend (今週末の予定)

Let's talk through your weekend plan and what could change it. Please read the title aloud.週末の予定と、それが変わる可能性について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What a weekend plan does (週末の予定がしてくれること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Weekend plans can give shape to the week by creating something concrete to anticipate.週末に具体的な楽しみがあると、一週間の流れに区切りが生まれます。anticipate楽しみに待つ
Some people recover through solitude, while others regain energy through company and activity.静かな時間で回復する人もいれば、人と過ごすことで元気になる人もいます。
A satisfying plan often balances genuine rest with one activity that feels meaningful.よい予定には、休息と意味のある活動の両方を入れられます。
Shared plans require coordination around time, place, cost, and different expectations.人と一緒の予定では、時間、場所、費用、期待の違いを調整する必要があります。
Weather, health, and unexpected responsibilities can disrupt even a carefully arranged day.天気、体調、予想外の用事で、よく考えた予定も変わることがあります。
A realistic backup plan can make an unexpected change feel less disappointing.予備の案があると、変更があってもがっかりしにくくなります。
Filling every hour can turn leisure into another schedule that has to be managed.すべての時間を埋めると、週末も仕事のように感じることがあります。
Leaving some time uncommitted creates room for rest or an unplanned opportunity.少し時間を空けておくと、休んだり予定外のことをしたりできます。
When an original plan falls through, the improvised alternative can unexpectedly become the best part.予定がだめになったとき、代わりにしたことが一番よい思い出になることもあります。
The weekend we want often reveals what is missing from the rest of our life at that moment.望む週末には、その時の生活に欠けているものが表れることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

This weekend (今週末)

WARM-UP 1How are you feeling about this weekend?今週末について、今どんな気持ちですか？

Follow up
  • Are you hoping to rest or do something?
  • What would make it feel different from a weekday?

A good weekend (よい週末)

WARM-UP 2What would make this weekend feel well spent?今週末を有意義だったと感じるには、何があればよいですか？

Follow up
  • What would leave you feeling refreshed?
  • What would make the weekend feel wasted?

The full plan (全体の予定)

QUESTION 1Do you like weekends planned or open?週末は予定を決めたいですか、それとも空けておきたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which plans benefit from structure?
  • When does an open day feel better?

Who with (誰と)

QUESTION 2Which sounds better this weekend: time alone or time with someone?今週末は、一人の時間と誰かと過ごす時間のどちらがよさそうですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of company would fit your mood?
  • How much time alone would you still want?

Looking forward (楽しみなこと)

QUESTION 3What's one simple thing you would enjoy this weekend?今週末に楽しめそうな、簡単なことを一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does that sound good right now?
  • Who, if anyone, would you invite?

What could change (変わる可能性)

QUESTION 4What usually makes you change a weekend plan?週末の予定を変えるのは、ふつうどんな理由からですか？

Follow up
  • Which change is easy to accept?
  • When does a change improve the weekend?

Still undecided (まだ決めていないこと)

QUESTION 5What part of a weekend is worth planning ahead?週末のどんなことは、前もって決める価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Which decision becomes harder at the last minute?
  • What is better left spontaneous?

If it falls through (予定がなくなったら)

QUESTION 6If a plan gets canceled, what's your best backup?予定が中止になったら、いちばんよい代わりの過ごし方は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could the backup be better than the original plan?
  • How quickly would you decide what to do?
