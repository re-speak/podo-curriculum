A small win nobody noticed (誰にも気づかれなかった小さな成功)

Let's notice a small success and explain why it mattered. Please read the title aloud.小さな成功に目を向け、なぜ大切だったか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Progress without applause (褒められなくても進んでいること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A great deal of meaningful progress produces no visible result for other people to recognize.大切な進歩のすべてが、他人に見える結果になるわけではありません。
Completing a delayed task or responding more calmly to a difficult day can represent genuine progress.先延ばしにしたことを終えたり、難しい一日にうまく対応したりするのも本当の成功です。
Such wins often look completely ordinary to anyone who cannot see the effort behind them.その努力を知らない人には、そのような成功はまったく普通に見えることがあります。
External praise feels good, but it is an unreliable measure of whether an achievement mattered.人から褒められるのはうれしいものですが、それで達成の価値を正確に測れるとは限りません。
Constant comparison can make gradual personal progress almost impossible to notice.絶えず他人と比べていると、自分の少しずつの進歩にほとんど気づけなくなります。
A brief record of completed actions can make slow, private improvement more visible over time.終えたことを短く記録すると、静かな成長が見えやすくなります。
A small success and a serious ongoing difficulty can both be true during the same month.小さな成功と、今も続く深刻な問題は、同じ月に両方ありえます。
Sharing a quiet achievement with one trusted person can validate it without turning it into a performance.信頼できる人に静かな達成を話すと、人に見せる行為に変えずに、その価値を認められます。validate価値を認める
A useful definition of success responds to our actual circumstances rather than an ideal version of life.成功の基準は、理想ではなく今の状況に合っている必要があります。
Recognizing small wins gives us evidence that change is occurring before the final outcome arrives.小さな成功に気づくと、最終結果の前から変化が起きていると分かります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

This week's small win (今週の小さな成功)

WARM-UP 1What's one small thing you did well this week?今週、自分でうまくできたと思う小さなことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why did it matter to you?
  • Did anyone else notice?

Unseen effort (見えない努力)

WARM-UP 2What effort do people often make without getting credit?人はどんな努力をしても、なかなか評価されないことがありますか？

Follow up
  • What makes the effort almost invisible?
  • How could people recognize it better?

Quiet progress (気づかれにくい進歩)

QUESTION 1What progress have you made lately that someone else might miss?最近の進歩で、ほかの人には気づかれにくそうなものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What small sign shows the change?
  • When did you first notice it?

Who understands (分かってくれる人)

QUESTION 2How can you tell when someone has been working hard quietly?人が人知れず頑張っていたことは、どうすれば分かりますか？

Follow up
  • What small changes can show the effort?
  • How should someone respond when they notice?

Celebrate a win (小さな成功を祝う)

QUESTION 3How do you celebrate a small win?小さな成功を、どのように祝いますか？

Follow up
  • When is private satisfaction enough?
  • How can celebrating help the next step?

Private or shared? (話す？自分だけにする？)

QUESTION 4When is keeping a small win private better than sharing it?小さな成功を人に話さず、自分だけにしておくほうがよいのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Can sharing change the meaning of the win?
  • When does telling someone make it more valuable?

Praise and motivation (褒め言葉とやる気)

QUESTION 5Which helps more: praise from someone else or noticing your own progress?ほかの人に褒められることと、自分の進歩に気づくことでは、どちらがより役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Which kind of motivation lasts longer?
  • How can outside praise become less important?

A good month (よい一か月)

QUESTION 6What small sign can show that a month went well?一か月がうまくいったと分かる、小さなサインは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that sign meaningful?
  • Did you have more good days or bad days this month?
