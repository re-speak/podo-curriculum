A show you can't stop watching (見るのをやめられない作品)

Let's talk about what keeps you watching a show. Please read the title aloud.つい見続けてしまう作品について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why we keep watching (見続けたくなる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Streaming services give us far more shows than we could realistically finish.配信サービスには、見終えられないほど多くの作品があります。
With so much choice, deciding what to start can be harder than deciding what to continue.何を見るか決めるほうが、続きを見るか決めるより難しいことがあります。
A strong opening episode creates an unanswered question that pulls us into the next one.よい第1話は、答えを知りたくなる疑問を残します。
Compelling characters can sustain our interest even when the plot develops slowly.物語の進みが遅くても、登場人物の魅力で見続けることがあります。
A personal recommendation also carries someone else's enthusiasm and gives us a reason to keep watching.人からのおすすめには、その人の熱意も含まれていて、見続ける理由になります。
Binge-watching can feel satisfying in the moment while quietly taking time from sleep and other plans.何話も一気見するのは楽しい一方、睡眠やほかの予定の時間を奪うこともあります。binge-watch一気見する
Abandoning a show halfway is reasonable when continuing feels more like an obligation than entertainment.見続けることが娯楽より義務のように感じられたら、途中でやめるのは自然な判断です。obligation義務
The right show often depends on our mood and how much attention we can give it that day.その日に合う作品は、気分や集中できる度合いによって変わります。
A satisfying—or disappointing—ending can reshape how we remember the entire series.結末に満足できるかどうかで、作品全体の記憶が変わることがあります。
After a series ends, we often search for the same emotional experience rather than an identical story.一つ見終えると、同じ物語より、同じ気持ちを味わえる次の作品を探すことが多いです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Watching now (今見ているもの)

WARM-UP 1Are you watching any shows at the moment?今、何か作品を見ていますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of show fits your mood right now?
  • What usually makes you start one?

How many episodes? (何話まで見る？)

WARM-UP 2How many episodes do you give a show before you quit?作品を見るのをやめる前に、何話くらいまで見ますか？

Follow up
  • What has to happen before you commit to a series?
  • What can make you leave after one episode?

A slow story (ゆっくり進む物語)

QUESTION 1What can keep you watching even when a story is slow?物語の進みが遅くても、見続けたくなるのはどんな要素ですか？

Follow up
  • Which character or detail can carry a slow episode?
  • When does slow become boring?

Friend or app? (友人かアプリか)

QUESTION 2Which recommendation do you trust more: a friend or an app?作品のおすすめは、友人とアプリのどちらをより信頼しますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of recommendation feels personal?
  • What does an app often misunderstand about your mood?

Stopping halfway (途中でやめる理由)

QUESTION 3What makes you stop watching a show halfway?作品を途中で見るのをやめるのは、どんな理由からですか？

Follow up
  • What can ruin a good series?
  • What could make you try it again?

One more episode (もう一話見る？)

QUESTION 4When is watching one more episode a bad idea?もう一話見るのがよくないのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Which next-day responsibility would make you stop?
  • What happens when you ignore that limit?

A good ending (よい結末)

QUESTION 5What makes an ending satisfying?どんな結末なら満足できますか？

Follow up
  • Which loose ends need an answer?
  • When can an open ending work better?

Alone or together? (一人か誰かと？)

QUESTION 6Do you enjoy a show more when you watch it alone or with someone else?作品は一人で見るほうが楽しめますか、それとも誰かと一緒に見るほうが楽しめますか？

Follow up
  • When does company improve the experience?
  • Which kind of show would you rather watch alone?
