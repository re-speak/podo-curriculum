What first times teach us (初めての体験が教えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Daily routines create useful stability, but they can gradually narrow the range of experiences we consider.日課は生活を楽にしますが、自分の世界を小さくすることもあります。
A first attempt forces us to pay attention because familiar shortcuts no longer work.初めての挑戦では、慣れたやり方が通用しないため、もう一度よく注意することになります。
Before a new experience, we build expectations from other people's stories and incomplete information.新しい体験の前には、人の話や不完全な情報から予想を作ります。
Nervousness does not always signal danger; it can show that the outcome matters to us.緊張は必ずしも危険のサインではなく、結果が自分にとって大切だという表れの場合もあります。
Clear guidance or a supportive companion can reduce the uncertainty of a first attempt.分かりやすい説明や支えてくれる人がいると、最初の一歩が楽になります。
The actual experience often differs from our imagined version in both easier and harder ways.実際の体験は、想像より楽な点も難しい点もあることがよくあります。
Feeling like a beginner can be uncomfortable when we are used to being capable in daily life.普段はできることが多い人にとって、初心者の感覚は居心地が悪いことがあります。beginner初心者
A second attempt reveals whether the activity has lasting value or was exciting only because it was novel.二度目に試すと、その活動に長く続く価値があるのか、新しいから楽しかっただけなのかが分かります。
Remembering recent firsts reminds us that personal growth does not end at a particular age.最近の初めてを思い出すと、成長は特定の年齢で終わるわけではないと分かります。
One successful first experience often expands the range of things we are willing to try next.初めての体験がうまくいくと、次に挑戦してみようと思えることの幅が広がります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Something new (初めてのこと)

WARM-UP 1What's something you tried for the first time this year?今年、初めてやってみたことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • When was that?
  • What did you do last weekend that was new?

Something new together (誰かと試すこと)

WARM-UP 2What's something new you'd enjoy trying with another person?誰かと一緒に新しく試してみたいことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would you invite?
  • Why would company make it better?

Nerves or excitement (緊張かわくわくか)

QUESTION 1Before trying something new, do you usually feel more nervous or excited?新しいことを始める前は、緊張とわくわくのどちらを強く感じますか？

Follow up
  • What makes that feeling stronger?
  • When do you nearly cancel?

Different from expected (予想との違い)

QUESTION 2What's something that turned out very different from what you expected?想像していたものと大きく違った体験は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What surprised you most?
  • Was it better or worse?

A complete beginner (まったくの初心者)

QUESTION 3When was the last time you felt like a complete beginner?最後に、まったくの初心者だと感じたのはいつですか？

Follow up
  • What was hardest at first?
  • Who or what helped?

Worth another try (もう一度やりたいこと)

QUESTION 4What's something you tried once and would happily do again?一度試して、喜んでもう一度やりたいと思ったことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you do differently next time?
  • Who would you take?

Next one (次は)

QUESTION 5What do you want to try next?次は何をやってみたいですか？

Follow up
  • What's stopping you?
  • When could you actually do it?

The opposite (逆に)

QUESTION 6Now the opposite — what did you try once and never again?逆に、一度やって二度とやらなかったことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What put you off?
  • Would you try it with a different person?
