Just about or easily? (just about と easily)

Use “just about” for a tiny margin, while “easily” says there is comfortable extra capacity.余裕がほぼないなら「just about」、十分な余裕があるなら「easily」を使います。

Tiny margin余裕がほぼないThat should just about cover it.それでぎりぎり足りるはずです。
Pick by meaning 意味で選ぶ
Comfortable margin十分な余裕That should easily cover it.それで十分余裕をもって足ります。
