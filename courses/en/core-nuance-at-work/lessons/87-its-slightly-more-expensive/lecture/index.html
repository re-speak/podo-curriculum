Slightly or a little? (slightly と a little)

Both mark a small difference. “Slightly” is common in precise or professional comparisons; “a little” is more conversational.どちらも小さな差です。「slightly」は正確・仕事上の比較でよく使い、「a little」はより会話的です。

Precise or professional正確・仕事上It's slightly more expensive.少しだけ高いです。
