As soon as or the moment? (as soon as と the moment)

As soon as is neutral and clear. The moment adds stronger immediacy in conversation.「as soon as」 は中立的で明確です。「the moment」 は会話で、さらに強い即時性を加えます。

Neutral中立的I'll call as soon as it arrives.届いたらすぐ電話します。
Pick by meaning 意味で選ぶ
More immediateさらに即時I'll call the moment it arrives.届いた瞬間に電話します。
