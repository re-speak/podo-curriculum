BALANCE GAME

Being great at one thing or good at many? (一つに秀でる、それとも多くをこなす？)

Let's compare deep expertise in one skill and broad ability across many skills, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.一つの技能の深い専門性と多くの技能にわたる幅広い能力を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A skill learned deeply can make related skills easier to understand.一つの技能を深く学ぶと、関連する技能も理解しやすくなることがあります。
Breadth helps a person notice when a problem belongs to another field.幅広い知識があると、問題が別の分野に関係すると気づきやすくなります。
A generalist can connect problems that specialists see separately.幅広い技能を持つ人は、専門家が別々に見る問題をつなげられます。generalist幅広い技能を持つ人
Skills fade without use, so maintaining many abilities also takes time.技能は使わないと薄れるため、多くの能力を保つにも時間がかかります。
Deep expertise grows through long practice and repeated feedback.深い専門性は、長い練習と繰り返すフィードバックで育ちます。
Broad skills help when a problem crosses several fields.幅広い技能は、問題が複数の分野にまたがるときに役立ちます。
A team can combine specialists instead of asking one person to know everything.チームなら、一人にすべてを求めず、複数の専門家を組み合わせられます。
Trying several skills can reveal which one deserves deeper study.いくつかの技能を試すことで、深く学ぶべき一つが見つかります。
A specialist still needs enough range to explain and apply their knowledge.専門家にも、知識を説明し活用するための幅広さが必要です。
The useful shape may be broad foundations with one or two deep strengths.幅広い基礎と、一つか二つの深い強みを組み合わせる方法もあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Recent progress (最近の上達)

WARM-UP 1Which skill, if any, have you improved recently?最近、上達した技能があるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • What helped you improve?
  • Which is easier to improve: cooking, exercise, or language?

A task that needs range (幅広さが必要な課題)

WARM-UP 2What skills do you need to plan a good trip?よい旅行を計画するには、どんな技能が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which skill is hardest to replace?
  • Which part is hardest: choosing a place, budgeting, or organizing?

Depth or breadth (深さか幅広さ)

QUESTION 1Would you rather master one skill or become good at many?一つの技能を極めるのと、多くの技能が得意になるのと、どちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which skill or group of skills do you imagine?
  • What skill would you happily practise every week for a year?

One year to learn (学ぶ一年)

QUESTION 2With one year to learn, would you study one skill deeply or try five?学ぶ時間が一年あるなら、一つを深く学ぶのと五つを試すのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • How would you measure progress?
  • Would you rather practise daily or try something new each week?

A strong team (強いチーム)

QUESTION 3On a small team, which is more useful: one specialist or one person who can do several jobs?小さなチームでは、一人の専門家と、いくつもの仕事ができる人のどちらが役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Why would that person help the team?
  • Can a flexible worker help when a teammate is absent?

A new field (新しい分野)

QUESTION 4Which new skill would you rather learn: cooking, photography, or first aid?新しく学ぶなら、料理、写真、応急手当のどれがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Why would that skill be useful?
  • Which is easiest to practise at home: cooking, photography, or first aid?

A goal that rewards range (幅広さが役立つ目標)

QUESTION 5When is being good at several skills better than mastering one?複数の技能が得意なことは、どんなときに一つを極めるよりよいですか？

Follow up
  • Where would deep expertise still help?
  • When is broad knowledge more useful: planning or fixing one technical problem?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • Which skill would your tutor choose to master?
  • Would six months to learn change the answer?
