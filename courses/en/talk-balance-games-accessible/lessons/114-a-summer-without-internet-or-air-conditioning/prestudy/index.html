A summer without internet or air conditioning? (ネットなしの夏、それともエアコンなし？)

Let's compare a summer without internet and a summer without air conditioning, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.インターネットなしの夏とエアコンなしの夏を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Indoor heat can become unsafe before it feels unbearable.室内の暑さは、耐えられないと感じる前に危険になることがあります。
Internet access carries work, school, banking, messages, and emergency information.インターネットは、仕事、学校、銀行、連絡、緊急情報にも使われます。
Cooling needs change with shade, insulation, airflow, and humidity.冷房の必要性は、日陰、断熱、風通し、湿度で変わります。cooling涼しくすること
A power cut can remove internet and air conditioning at the same time.停電すると、インターネットとエアコンの両方が使えなくなることがあります。
Children, older people, and some medical conditions increase the risk from indoor heat.子ども、高齢者、特定の病気がある人は、室内の暑さによる危険が高くなります。
Mobile data, home internet, and public Wi-Fi do not fail in exactly the same way.モバイル通信、家庭のネット、公共Wi-Fiは、同じように止まるとは限りません。
Fans help the body release heat, but they cannot make every hot room safe.扇風機は体の熱を逃がしますが、どんな暑い部屋でも安全にできるわけではありません。
Libraries, community centres, and other cool spaces can change the choice.図書館や公共施設など涼しい場所を使えるかどうかで、選択は変わります。
A day offline can create rest, but forced disconnection can also block important duties.一日ネットを離れると休めますが、大切な用事ができなくなることもあります。
The honest answer depends on season, safety, building quality, and what the internet is needed for.答えは、季節、安全、建物、ネットの用途によって変わります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One hour offline (一時間オフライン)

WARM-UP 1What becomes difficult after one hour without internet?インターネットが一時間使えないと、最初に何が困りますか？

Follow up
  • What could still be done offline?
  • Which would you miss first: messages, maps, or payments?

A cool day offline (涼しい日のオフライン)

WARM-UP 2How would you spend half a cool day without internet?涼しい日にインターネットなしで半日過ごすなら、何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Which offline activity would you choose?
  • Would you choose a walk, a book, or a movie?

Lose one for summer (夏に一つを諦める)

QUESTION 1For one summer, would you give up internet or air conditioning?一夏の間、インターネットとエアコンのどちらかを諦めるなら、どちらにしますか？

Follow up
  • Would work, health, or family needs matter most?
  • Which is more essential in hot weather: cooling or online access?

One day without cooling (冷房なしの一日)

QUESTION 2What would be the first problem during one day without air conditioning?エアコンなしで一日過ごすと、最初に何が問題になりますか？

Follow up
  • What could make the day easier?
  • Which suffers first in heat: sleep or concentration?

A cool spring day (涼しい春の日)

QUESTION 3On a cool spring day, would you rather lose internet or air conditioning?涼しい春の日なら、インターネットとエアコンのどちらを諦めますか？

Follow up
  • What would you do offline?
  • Would you still need air conditioning on a cool day?

A nearby cool place (近くの涼しい場所)

QUESTION 4Would a safe, cool place nearby make a summer without air conditioning possible?近くに安全で涼しい場所があれば、エアコンなしの夏を過ごせますか？

Follow up
  • Would the safe place need to open at night?
  • Would a nearby library make the summer easier?

A hard summer activity (夏に大変な活動)

QUESTION 5Which summer activity is hardest without air conditioning?エアコンなしでは、夏のどんな活動が最も大変ですか？

Follow up
  • What cooler place could help?
  • Which is harder without cooling: sleeping or working?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • Which loss would be harder for your tutor?
  • Would a cooler climate change the choice?
