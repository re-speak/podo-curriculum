BALANCE GAME

Travelling alone or with other people? (一人旅、それとも誰かと一緒？)

Let's compare travelling alone and travelling with other people, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.一人で旅行することとほかの人と旅行することを比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Solo travellers make every decision, including the difficult ones.一人旅では、難しい判断も含めて、すべて自分で決めます。
A companion can share costs, attention, and responsibility.同行者がいれば、費用、気づかい、責任を分けられます。
A shared itinerary works only when both people can influence it.共有する旅程は、両方の人が意見を出せるときにうまくいきます。shared itinerary共有する旅程
Even people travelling together can plan a few hours apart.誰かと旅行していても、数時間は別々に過ごせます。
Solo travel gives one person control over pace, meals, and changes of plan.一人旅では、速さ、食事、計画変更を自分で決められます。
Company can make difficult moments safer and more enjoyable.同行者がいると、難しい場面がより安全で楽しくなります。
Travelling together requires negotiation about money, energy, and interests.誰かとの旅行では、お金、体力、関心について調整が必要です。
Being alone can feel peaceful in one place and isolating in another.一人でいることは、場所によって穏やかにも孤独にも感じられます。
Sharing a room can save money while reducing privacy.部屋を共有するとお金を節約できますが、プライバシーは減ります。
Shared travel creates memories that can be discussed long after the trip ends.誰かとの旅行では、旅のあとも一緒に話せる思い出ができます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The best part of solo travel (一人旅のよさ)

WARM-UP 1What's the best part of travelling alone?一人旅の最もよいところは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which freedom matters most?
  • Would you enjoy one day alone in a new city?

Share the view (景色を共有)

WARM-UP 2Would a beautiful view feel better alone or shared with someone?すてきな景色は、一人で見るのと誰かと共有するのと、どちらがよく感じられますか？

Follow up
  • What is good about your choice?
  • Would you send someone a photo right away?

A solo or shared trip (一人旅か誰かとの旅)

QUESTION 1When visiting somewhere new, would you rather travel alone or with someone?初めての場所を訪れるなら、一人で行くのと誰かと行くのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • What would be easier alone?
  • When would company help?

The hard part of company (誰かとの旅行の難しさ)

QUESTION 2What's the hardest part of travelling with other people?ほかの人と旅行する一番難しいところは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What could companions agree on before leaving?
  • Which causes more conflict: money, food, or timing?

Enjoying time alone (一人の時間を楽しむ)

QUESTION 3What would make three days alone somewhere new enjoyable?初めての場所で一人で過ごす三日間を、楽しくするものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • When might company still help?
  • Would knowing the local language make solo travel easier?

Someone else plans (ほかの人が計画)

QUESTION 4How would a companion who planned everything change a trip?すべてを計画する同行者がいると、旅行はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • What would you still decide yourself?
  • Would you let a close friend plan the whole weekend?

Better with company (誰かと行くほうがよい場所)

QUESTION 5What kind of destination is better with company?どんな旅先なら、誰かと一緒のほうがよいですか？

Follow up
  • What makes company useful there?
  • Which feels safer with company: a city or a remote area?

Time alone (一人で過ごす時間)

QUESTION 6Now the opposite: what part of a trip would you rather not share with anyone?では反対に、旅行のどんな時間は誰とも共有せずに過ごしたいですか？

Answer first, then follow up
  • One meal or one afternoon?
  • Why would that time alone help?
