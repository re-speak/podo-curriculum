BALANCE GAME

Studying in a café or at home? (カフェで勉強、それとも家？)

Let's compare studying in a café and studying at home, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.カフェで勉強することと家で勉強することを比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A familiar place can become a cue that tells the brain it is time to study.慣れた場所は、勉強を始める合図になることがあります。
Short walks and planned breaks can reset attention in either place.短い散歩や決めた休憩は、どちらの場所でも集中を戻す助けになります。
Other people studying nearby can create gentle accountability.近くで勉強する人がいると、自然に続けやすくなることがあります。gentle accountability自然な継続のしやすさ
A public study space limits private calls, speaking practice, and confidential work.公共の勉強場所では、私的な通話、声を出す練習、秘密の作業がしにくくなります。
A little background activity helps some people begin, while it distracts others.少し周りに動きがあると始めやすい人も、気が散る人もいます。
Leaving home can create a useful ritual for starting work.家を出ることが、作業を始めるよい習慣になる場合があります。
A café costs money and may not guarantee a seat, power, or quiet.カフェではお金がかかり、席、電源、静けさが必ずあるとは限りません。
Home gives access to books, equipment, food, and privacy.家では、本、設備、食事、プライバシーを利用しやすいです。
Comfort at home can make it easier to postpone the task.家で快適すぎると、作業を先延ばしにしやすくなります。
The best place can change with the task: reading, writing, memorising, and meeting are different.読む、書く、覚える、話し合うでは、合う場所が違います。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A place for focus (集中できる場所)

WARM-UP 1What makes a place easy to concentrate in?集中しやすい場所には、どんな特徴がありますか？

Follow up
  • Do you focus better alone or around other people?
  • Which helps more: silence or a little background noise?

One important hour (大切な一時間)

WARM-UP 2Where would you sit to finish one important task in an hour?一時間で大切な作業を終えるなら、どこに座りますか？

Follow up
  • What important task would you choose?
  • Would you choose a desk at home or a quiet cafe?

Café or home (カフェか家)

QUESTION 1For two hours of serious study, would you choose a café or home?二時間しっかり勉強するなら、カフェと家のどちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • What would you bring?
  • Which place has fewer distractions for you?

The hard part of a café (カフェの難しさ)

QUESTION 2What's hardest about studying in a café?カフェで勉強する一番難しいところは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes the cafe worth using anyway?
  • Which cafe noise is hardest to ignore: music or conversations?

Buying a drink (飲み物を買う)

QUESTION 3Would you keep using a quiet café if you had to buy a drink every time?静かなカフェでも毎回飲み物を買うなら、通い続けますか？

Follow up
  • Would you stay longer to make the drink feel worth it?
  • Would a free library be a better choice?

A better home desk (よりよい家の机)

QUESTION 4Would a good desk and lighting at home make you stop using cafés?家に良い机と照明があれば、カフェを使わなくなりますか？

Follow up
  • What else would the room need?
  • Would you still visit a cafe for a change of mood?

A task for home (家でする課題)

QUESTION 5What kind of task is best done at home?どんな課題が、家でするのに最も合っていますか？

Follow up
  • Why does home suit that task?
  • Which needs more privacy: writing or a video call?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • Which study task would your tutor take to a cafe?
  • Would a video call change the choice?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
