BALANCE GAME

A hundred friends or three close ones? (友人百人、それとも親しい友人三人？)

Let's compare a hundred friends and three close friends, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.百人の友人と三人の親しい友人を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A broad social circle brings different information, ideas, and introductions.広い人間関係からは、さまざまな情報、考え、紹介を得られます。
Close friends can notice a problem before someone asks for help.親しい友人は、助けを求める前に問題に気づくことがあります。
An inner circle is small enough for trust to grow through repeated contact.親しい仲間の輪は小さいからこそ、繰り返す関わりの中で信頼が育ちます。inner circle親しい仲間の輪
Every friendship needs some attention, so a larger network changes how time is divided.どの友情にも気づかいが必要なので、人間関係が広いほど時間の使い方も変わります。
Being known by many people can bring more invitations and more obligations.多くの人に知られると、誘いが増える一方、応える必要も増えます。
Close friendship needs repeated time, attention, and trust.親しい友情には、繰り返す時間、気づかい、信頼が必要です。
A hundred connections can still feel lonely when none is safe enough for honesty.知り合いが百人いても、正直に話せる相手がいなければ孤独を感じます。
Three close friends cannot meet every social or practical need.親しい友人三人だけで、すべての交流や実用的な必要を満たせるとは限りません。
Moving, changing jobs, or entering a new community can make wider connections more valuable.引っ越し、転職、新しい地域への参加では、広いつながりの価値が高まります。
Many people need a small trusted core and a wider circle around it.多くの人には、信頼できる小さな中心と、その周りの広いつながりの両方が必要です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A long conversation (長く話せる相手)

WARM-UP 1Who, if anyone, is easy for you to talk to for hours?何時間でも話しやすい相手がいるとしたら、誰ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes a long conversation easy or difficult?
  • Is a long conversation easier in person or on the phone?

Meeting new people (新しい人と会う)

WARM-UP 2What makes it easy to meet new people?新しい人と知り合いやすくするものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What opening question works well?
  • Where is it easier to meet people: a class, a party, or online?

Choose a circle (人間関係を選ぶ)

QUESTION 1For one difficult week, would you rather have a hundred friendly contacts or three close friends?大変な一週間なら、顔見知りの友人百人と親しい友人三人のどちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of support would your choice provide?
  • Who helps more in a crisis: close friends or many contacts?

Many contacts, no close friend (知り合いは多いが親友はいない)

QUESTION 2What would be missing if you knew many people but had no close friends?知り合いは多くても親しい友人がいないとしたら、何が足りませんか？

Follow up
  • Who would you call after a bad day?
  • Would you rather share good news with one person or a large group?

A wide circle helps (広い人間関係が役立つとき)

QUESTION 3When is a wide circle more useful than a few close friends?少数の親友より、広い人間関係のほうが役立つのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of support would a wide circle provide?
  • Which helps more when job hunting: close friends or many contacts?

Starting in a new city (新しい町で始める)

QUESTION 4After moving to a new city, would you build a wide circle or one close friendship first?新しい町へ移ったら、広い人間関係と一つの親しい友情のどちらを先に作りますか？

Follow up
  • How would you meet people?
  • Where would you start: work, a class, or a hobby group?

What a wide circle offers (広い人間関係のよさ)

QUESTION 5What can a wide circle of friends offer that three close friends may not?広い友人関係には、三人の親しい友人にはないどんなよさがありますか？

Follow up
  • Which opportunity is a good example?
  • Which brings more new information: one close friend or many contacts?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • What mattered most in your tutor's choice?
  • Which is easier to build first: one close friendship or many contacts?
