BALANCE GAME

More money or more time? (もっとお金、それとももっと時間？)

Let's compare more money and more time, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.もっとお金ともっと時間を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Money is most useful when it solves a cost that cannot wait.お金は、待てない支出を解決するときに最も役立ちます。
Free time matters most when sleep, health, or relationships are being squeezed.自由な時間は、睡眠、健康、人間関係が圧迫されるときに特に重要です。
A time-poor person may need free hours more than extra income.時間に余裕がない人には、収入より自由な時間が必要な場合があります。time-poor時間に余裕がない
Predictable hours can be more valuable than a larger number of uncertain free hours.自由な時間は、量だけでなく、予測して使えるかどうかも大切です。
A higher income often comes with longer hours or more responsibility.収入が増えると、労働時間や責任も増えることがあります。
Extra time can save money when it makes cooking, comparing prices, or doing repairs possible.時間に余裕があると、料理、価格比較、修理などでお金を節約できます。
Money can also buy time through transport, delivery, or paid help.お金があれば、交通、配達、有料の助けによって時間を作ることもできます。
Free hours do not feel free when worry or exhaustion fills them.心配や疲れでいっぱいなら、自由な時間があっても自由には感じられません。
Health, family, and long-term plans can change which resource feels scarce.健康、家族、長期的な計画によって、どちらが不足しているかは変わります。
The useful goal is often enough money and enough time, not the maximum amount of either.大切なのは、どちらかを最大にすることより、十分なお金と十分な時間を確保することです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Time costs money (時間で節約)

WARM-UP 1Would you take a slower route to save money?お金を節約するためなら、時間のかかる道を選びますか？

Follow up
  • How much extra time would be acceptable?
  • Which usually costs less: walking or public transport?

Three free hours (自由な三時間)

WARM-UP 2What would you do with three unexpected free hours?突然、自由な時間が三時間できたら、何をしますか？

Follow up
  • What would you do first?
  • Which sounds better today: rest, errands, or fun?

Choose one (どちらを選ぶ)

QUESTION 1If tomorrow came with one extra hour or fifty extra dollars, which would help more?明日、自由な時間が一時間増えるか、五十ドル増えるなら、どちらがより役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What would your choice help you do?
  • Which disappears faster in a busy week: money or free time?

Money this month (今月のお金)

QUESTION 2What everyday cost could fifty extra dollars help with this month?今月、追加の五十ドルでどんな日常の支出を助けられますか？

Follow up
  • How urgent would that cost be?
  • What's one everyday expense that often surprises people?

More free time (自由な時間)

QUESTION 3What part of life would extra time improve?自由な時間が増えたら、生活のどの部分がよくなりますか？

Follow up
  • How would the extra time help?
  • Which helps more after a tiring week: extra time or extra money?

Ten extra hours (週に十時間)

QUESTION 4Would you take a pay raise if it meant working ten more hours each week?給料が上がっても毎週十時間多く働くなら、引き受けますか？

Follow up
  • What would those ten hours replace?
  • Would you give up one evening a week for higher pay?

When time wins (時間を選ぶとき)

QUESTION 5When might someone choose extra time even if money was tight?お金に余裕がなくても、どんなときに時間を選ぶでしょうか？

Follow up
  • What would the extra time protect?
  • Which would you protect first: sleep or income?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • Which part of your tutor's reason made sense to you?
  • Which option would help more on a very busy day?
