BALANCE GAME

Do it yourself or pay someone? (自分でやる？お金を払って頼む？)

Let’s compare doing a job yourself with paying someone to do it, then find the point where the better choice changes. Please read the title aloud.自分でする場合と、お金を払って人に頼む場合を比べ、どんな条件で選択が変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

When help is worth paying for (人に頼む価値があるとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A table in a box may need an hour, a screwdriver, and more patience than expected.箱で届いたテーブルの組み立てには、一時間とドライバー、そして予想以上の根気が必要かもしれません。
Doing a job yourself can save labour costs, but tools, materials, and mistakes still cost money.自分ですれば作業代は節約できますが、道具や材料、失敗にもお金がかかります。
Paid help becomes more attractive when a job is dangerous, urgent, or hard to fix.危険な作業、急ぎの作業、やり直しにくい作業では、お金を払って頼む価値が高まります。
Learning a skill can save time and money when the same job comes back often.同じ修理や家事を何度もするなら、技能を覚えるために使った時間は後で役立ちます。
A cheap attempt can become expensive if a mistake breaks something or makes it unsafe.安く済ませるつもりでも、物を壊したり危険を生んだりすれば、かえって高くつくことがあります。
Some people enjoy having control and finishing a practical job themselves.実用的な作業を自分の手で終えることに、満足感や自由を感じる人もいます。
Hiring someone saves effort, but finding the right person and arranging a visit also takes time.人に頼めば手間は減りますが、信頼できる人を探して訪問時間を決めるのにも時間がかかります。
Free help from a friend can still affect the friendship if the job takes too long or goes badly.友達の無料の手伝いでも、時間がかかったり失敗したりすれば、人間関係に負担がかかることがあります。
Income, health, free time, and confidence can all change which choice feels reasonable.収入、健康、使える時間、自信によって、どちらが妥当な選択かは変わります。
Many people do simple, low-risk jobs themselves and pay a professional when safety or quality matters more.多くの人は危険の少ない作業は自分で行い、安全や長く使える品質が重要なときは専門家に頼みます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A job you do yourself (自分でする作業)

WARM-UP 1What’s one job at home you usually do yourself?家で普段、自分でする作業は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How did you learn to do it?
  • Do you enjoy it?

A job worth paying for (お金を払いたい作業)

WARM-UP 2What’s one job you’d happily pay someone else to do?お金を払ってでも人に頼みたい作業は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it difficult or annoying?
  • How often does it need doing?

Furniture in a box (組み立て家具)

QUESTION 1If some furniture arrived in a box, would you build it yourself or pay for help?組み立て式の家具が届いたら、自分で作りますか、それともお金を払って頼みますか？

Follow up
  • What tools or skills would you need?
  • How much would you pay?

Time worth buying (お金で買う時間)

QUESTION 2When is paying someone worth the time it saves?どんなときなら、時間を節約するために人へお金を払う価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Would saving one hour be enough?
  • What would you do with that time?

A skill that saved money (節約から覚えた技能)

QUESTION 3What’s something you learned because you didn’t want to pay for help?人に頼むお金を節約したくて、自分で覚えたことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How long did it take to learn?
  • Would you use that skill again?

A job for a professional (専門家に頼む作業)

QUESTION 4Which job would you never try without a professional?専門家なしでは絶対にやらない作業は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Is safety or quality the bigger reason?
  • Would you want to watch and learn?

Help from a friend (友達の手伝い)

QUESTION 5If a friend offered free help, what would you still prefer to do yourself?友達が無料で手伝うと言っても、自分でやりたいことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why would you want control of it?
  • What help would you accept?

The first job to hand over (最初に任せる作業)

QUESTION 6If you suddenly earned more, what would you stop doing yourself first?収入が急に増えたら、最初に自分でやめる作業は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How often would you pay for it?
  • What would you continue doing yourself?
