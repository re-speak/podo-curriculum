A book you chose for someone else (誰かのために選んだ本)

Let's tell the story behind a book chosen for another person. Please read the title aloud.誰かのために選んだ本にある物語を話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Choosing a book for someone (誰かのために本を選ぶこと)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Giving a book away means making a guess about another person's interests.本を贈ることは、相手の興味を想像して選ぶことです。give away人に贈る
A book we love is not automatically the right book for someone else.自分にとって大切な本が、必ずしも相手に合うとは限りません。
The best choice often connects with a conversation we have already had.よい選択は、二人が以前に話したことから生まれることが多いです。
A short note can explain why the giver thought of that person.短いメモなら、なぜその人を思い出したか伝えられます。
The recipient may feel pressure if the giver keeps asking whether they finished it.贈った人から読み終えたか何度も聞かれると、受け取った人はプレッシャーを感じることがあります。
Some people enjoy receiving a recommendation more than owning another object.人によっては、本という物より、おすすめそのもののほうがうれしい場合があります。
A book can continue a conversation long after it is given.本は、贈ったあとも長く会話を続けてくれます。
Not knowing whether it was read can be part of giving without conditions.読まれたか分からないことを受け入れるのも、条件をつけずに贈ることの一部です。
The book that helped us may help another person for a completely different reason.自分を助けた本が、別の人にはまったく違う理由で届くことがあります。
Choosing for someone reveals how we see them as well as what we like.誰かのために本を選ぶと、自分の好みだけでなく、その人をどう見ているかも表れます。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A book to give away (人に贈る本)

WARM-UP 1Who would you enjoy choosing a book for?誰のためなら、楽しく本を選べそうですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of book might suit them?
  • Is a book easier to choose for a close friend or a coworker?

The safest gift (いちばん無難な贈り物)

WARM-UP 2Would you give someone a book you love or choose one from their wish list?自分の好きな本を贈りますか、それとも相手の欲しい本のリストから選びますか？

Follow up
  • Why would you choose that way?
  • When might the other choice be better?

Know the reader (読む人を知る)

QUESTION 1What should you know about someone before choosing a book for them?その人のために本を選ぶ前に、相手について何を知っておくとよいですか？

Follow up
  • Does their free time matter?
  • Which interest helps most?

A good choice (よい選択だった証拠)

QUESTION 2How can you tell that a book matches someone?本がその人に合っているかは、どうすれば分かりますか？

Follow up
  • What reaction would you hope for?
  • Would finishing the book matter?

An unread book (読んでいない本)

QUESTION 3Would you give someone a book you haven't read?自分が読んでいない本を、人に贈りますか？

Follow up
  • What would make you trust it?
  • What would you say about the choice?

Recommend or give? (おすすめか贈り物か)

QUESTION 4When is recommending a book better than buying it as a gift?本を贈るより、おすすめするだけのほうがよいのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What pressure can a gift create?
  • When is buying it more thoughtful?

A note inside (本に添える言葉)

QUESTION 5What note would you write inside a gift book?本を贈るとき、中にどんなメッセージを書きますか？

Follow up
  • Would you explain why you chose it?
  • How personal would the note be?

A book for me (私に選ぶ一冊)

QUESTION 6Ask me what I enjoy reading, then choose a book for me.私に好きな読書について聞いてから、本を一冊選んでください。

Follow up
  • Why does it fit my answer?
  • What might surprise me?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
