The problem with a hidden gem (隠れた名店を教える難しさ)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A favorite restaurant can feel personal even though it is a public place.誰でも入れる店でも、お気に入りのレストランは個人的な場所のように感じられます。
We may value the staff, pace, and atmosphere as much as the food.料理だけでなく、店員、時間の流れ、雰囲気も大切かもしれません。
A regular order creates comfort because we know exactly what will arrive.いつもの注文には、何が来るか分かる安心感があります。
Finding a place by chance makes it feel like a discovery we earned.偶然見つけた店は、自分で発見した特別な場所のように感じられます。
Taking one trusted person there can deepen the connection.信頼する人を一人連れて行くと、その店とのつながりが深まることがあります。
Word-of-mouth helps a good business survive.口コミは、よい店が続いていく助けになります。word-of-mouth口コミ
Too much attention can also bring lines, noise, and a different atmosphere.注目されすぎると、行列や騒音で雰囲気が変わることもあります。
Keeping a place secret protects our experience but does not support the owner.店を秘密にすれば自分の体験は守れますが、店主の助けにはなりません。
A hidden gem is often special because it fits one person's needs, not everyone's.隠れた名店は、誰にでも最高だからではなく、ある人に合うから特別なことがあります。hidden gem隠れた名店
The best recommendation tells the right person why the place suits them.いちばんよいおすすめは、その店に合う人を考え、理由を伝えることです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A place you protect (教えたくない店)

WARM-UP 1Would you tell everyone about a great small restaurant, or keep it quiet?すてきな小さなレストランを見つけたら、みんなに教えますか、それとも秘密にしますか？

Follow up
  • What would you want to protect about the place?
  • When would sharing it help the restaurant?

The kind of place (どんな店？)

WARM-UP 2What makes a small restaurant feel special?小さなレストランが特別に感じられるのは、どんなところですか？

Follow up
  • Which detail cannot be copied easily?
  • How much does the owner matter?

Where it is (場所)

QUESTION 1Where do the best hidden restaurants usually appear?よい穴場のレストランは、どんな場所にあることが多いですか？

Follow up
  • What makes the location part of the experience?
  • Would a central location change the feeling?

Your order (選びたい注文)

QUESTION 2What's one dish that can make a restaurant worth returning to?その一品のためにまた行きたくなる料理は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which detail keeps it memorable?
  • Can one great dish carry an average menu?

How it is found (見つける方法)

QUESTION 3Which recommendation do you trust most: a friend, reviews, or walking past?友人、口コミ、店の前を通った印象の中で、どれをいちばん信頼しますか？

Follow up
  • What information does that source give you?
  • Which source is easiest to fake?

Who you would take (連れて行きたい人)

QUESTION 4Who is the best person to take to a tiny favorite restaurant?お気に入りの小さなレストランに連れて行くなら、どんな人がいちばんよいですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of guest would understand the place?
  • Who might accidentally spoil the experience?

If it became popular (人気になったら)

QUESTION 5What changes when a small restaurant becomes popular?小さなレストランが人気になると、何が変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Can the place grow without losing its character?
  • Which disadvantage would be fair to accept?

Worth keeping quiet (秘密にする価値)

QUESTION 6When does keeping a great restaurant secret become unfair to the owner?すてきなレストランを秘密にすることが、店主に不公平になるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • When does secrecy stop helping the owner?
  • Would you tell one friend or post online?
