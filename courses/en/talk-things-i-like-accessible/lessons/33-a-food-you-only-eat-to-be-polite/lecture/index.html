Kindness when the food is not for you (好みでない料理への思いやり)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Food offered by another person can carry effort, welcome, and pride.人から出された料理には、手間、歓迎の気持ち、誇りが込められていることがあります。
That meaning can make an honest dislike difficult to express.その意味があるため、嫌いだと正直に伝えにくくなることがあります。
Eating a small amount may show courtesy without pretending to love the food.少し食べることで、大好きなふりをせずに礼儀を示せます。courtesy礼儀
A warm comment about the effort or another dish can still be sincere.手間や別の料理について温かく伝えることは、十分に誠実です。
Diet, allergies, and strong physical reactions require clearer boundaries.食事上の制限、アレルギー、強い身体反応がある場合は、より明確に断る必要があります。
The relationship and the setting change how directly we can speak.相手との関係や場面によって、どのくらい直接言えるかが変わります。
Hosts often prefer useful information to praise or enthusiasm that is obviously false.もてなす人は、明らかに本心ではない褒め言葉や反応より、役立つ情報を好むことがあります。
A food that is loved by everyone else can make our own reaction feel strange.ほかの人がみんな大好きな料理だと、自分の反応が変に感じられることがあります。
Taste can change, but repeated polite eating does not guarantee that it will.好みは変わることがありますが、礼儀のために何度も食べても、変わるとは限りません。
Good table manners protect both the relationship and a person's real limits.よい食卓の礼儀は、人間関係も、その人の本当の限界も守ります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Eating to be polite (礼儀のために食べるもの)

WARM-UP 1Is there something you eat to be polite?礼儀のために食べるものはありますか？

Follow up
  • Which food comes closest?
  • What would make it hard to refuse?

An awkward table (困る食卓)

WARM-UP 2In what situation might food you do not want end up on your plate?食べたくないものが皿にのるとしたら、どんな場面ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would have served it?
  • Why would refusing feel awkward?

The food (その食べ物)

QUESTION 1What food would you only eat out of politeness?礼儀のためだけなら食べるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which taste or texture would be difficult?
  • Could you enjoy any part of it?

Where it happens (食べる場面)

QUESTION 2Where would you be most likely to have to eat it?それを食べる可能性がいちばん高いのは、どんな場面ですか？

Follow up
  • How formal would the situation be?
  • Would your response change somewhere else?

Would anyone notice? (気づかれる？)

QUESTION 3Would people notice that you did not like it?好きではないことに、周りの人は気づくと思いますか？

Follow up
  • What might reveal your reaction?
  • How could another person respond?

What you would say (やさしく伝える)

QUESTION 4How would you tell someone kindly that you did not like the food?その料理が好きではなかったことを、やさしくどう伝えますか？

Follow up
  • Which words would soften it?
  • When might you need to be more direct?

Everyone else loves it (みんなが好きなもの)

QUESTION 5What food does everyone else seem to love that you do not get?ほかの人はみんな好きそうなのに、自分にはよさが分からない食べ物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What do people praise about it?
  • Have you ever tried a better version?

Honesty or feelings (正直さか気持ちか)

QUESTION 6Which matters more at dinner: honesty or protecting the host's feelings?食事の場では、正直さと相手の気持ちを守ることのどちらが大切ですか？

Follow up
  • When should honesty win?
  • What would you want a guest to tell you?
