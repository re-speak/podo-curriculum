When two foods meet (二つの食べ物が出会うとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A food pairing can feel completely ordinary in one culture and deeply strange in another.ある文化では普通の食べ合わせが、別の文化ではとても奇妙に感じられます。
Sweet and salty flavors often work together because contrast makes both more noticeable.甘さと塩気は、対比によって互いをはっきりさせるため、よく合います。
Texture can be just as decisive as flavor when two foods are brought together.二つの食べ物を合わせるとき、食感も味と同じくらい重要です。
A combination can sound worse than it tastes because its description creates resistance before the first bite.説明を聞いただけで、ひと口目の前に抵抗感が生まれ、実際の味より悪く聞こえる組み合わせもあります。resistance抵抗感
Trying one well-made version provides better evidence than judging the whole idea from a poor example.上手に作られた一つを試すほうが、よくない一例から全体を判断するより、よい根拠になります。
An acquired taste may become enjoyable only after familiarity replaces the initial surprise.慣れて好きになる味は、最初の驚きが薄れてから楽しめるようになります。
People often defend familiar combinations because they carry memory and identity as well as flavor.なじみのある組み合わせには、味だけでなく思い出や自分らしさも含まれています。
Refusal may come from smell, texture, or a specific ingredient rather than simple closed-mindedness.食べ物を断る理由は、偏見ではなく、匂いや食感、特定の材料かもしれません。
Trust in the person offering the food and the setting can change our willingness to experiment.すすめる人への信頼や場面によって、試す気持ちは変わります。
A fair food conversation makes room for genuine curiosity as well as a firm personal limit.公平な食べ物の会話では、好奇心にも、はっきり断ることにも余地があります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Pineapple on pizza (パイナップルのピザ)

WARM-UP 1Pineapple on pizza — yes or no?パイナップルのピザは、ありですか、なしですか？

Follow up
  • Would you eat one slice right now?
  • Would your answer change if someone else ordered it?

Sweet, salty, fruit, meat (甘い・塩味・果物・肉)

WARM-UP 2Which would you rather try: sweet-and-salty popcorn or meat with fruit?甘じょっぱいポップコーンと果物を使った肉料理なら、どちらを試したいですか？

Follow up
  • What sounds good about it?
  • What could make the other one work?

The strangest pairing (いちばん変わった組み合わせ)

QUESTION 1What's the strangest food combination you've actually tried?実際に食べた中で、いちばん変わった食べ合わせは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who suggested it?
  • Was it better or worse than expected?

Your unusual combination (自分の変わった組み合わせ)

QUESTION 2What two foods do you combine that other people might find strange?ほかの人には変わって見えそうでも、自分では一緒に食べる二つの食べ物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When did you first try them together?
  • Who else likes the combination?

Hard to understand (理解しにくい組み合わせ)

QUESTION 3Which popular food pairing is hardest for you to understand?人気があっても、いちばん理解しにくい食べ合わせは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Is the problem flavor, texture, or the idea?
  • What do fans probably enjoy?

A good surprise (おいしい驚き)

QUESTION 4Which strange food combination surprised you by being good?意外においしくて驚いた、変わった食べ合わせは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What made the flavors work?
  • Would you order it again?

A firm no (絶対に無理)

QUESTION 5What food combination would you refuse to try?絶対に試したくない食べ合わせは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Where does curiosity stop for you?
  • Is the limit about safety or simply how it feels?

One strange bite (変わった一口)

QUESTION 6Who would you trust to choose one strange bite for you?変わった食べ物を一口だけ選んでもらうなら、誰を信頼しますか？

Follow up
  • Would you want a safe surprise or a real challenge?
  • Would liking one bite be enough?
