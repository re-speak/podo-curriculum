Let's describe the place at home where you feel most comfortable and why. Please read the title aloud.家でいちばん居心地よく感じる場所と、その理由について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

How a corner becomes your place (家の一角が自分の場所になるまで)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A favorite place at home may be nothing more than one chair beside a window.家のお気に入りの場所は、窓のそばにある椅子一脚だけかもしれません。
Comfort comes from small details such as light, temperature, sound, and where useful things are kept.居心地のよさは、光や温度、音、必要な物の置き場所といった小さな点から生まれます。
The best-looking part of a room is not always the place where someone wants to stay.部屋でいちばん見栄えのよい場所が、いつも長くいたい場所とは限りません。
A corner becomes personal through repeated actions: reading, eating, calling someone, or doing nothing.家の一角は、読書や食事、電話、何もしない時間を繰り返すことで、自分らしい場所になります。
One object can give a room character without being valuable or impressive.一つの物が、高価でも目立つ物でもないのに、部屋らしさを作ることがあります。
A shared home requires compromise because one person's calm space may be another person's busy space.共同で暮らす家では、一人の落ち着く場所が、別の人には忙しい場所かもしれません。
The same room can feel open in morning light and private after dark.同じ部屋でも、朝の光の中では開放的に、夜には自分だけの場所のように感じられます。
A small change in lighting or furniture can improve daily life more than a complete redesign.照明や家具を少し変えるだけで、部屋全体を作り直すより日常が快適になることがあります。
A comfortable place supports what we need, but it can also make it harder to get up and begin something else.居心地のよい場所は必要なことを支えてくれますが、立ち上がって別のことを始めにくくすることもあります。
The first detail we mention when describing a room often reveals what the space means to us.部屋を説明するとき最初に話す点には、その場所が自分にとって何を意味するかが表れます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your usual seat (いつもの席)

WARM-UP 1Where do you sit most often at home?家では、どこに座っていることがいちばん多いですか？

Follow up
  • What do you usually do there?
  • How comfortable is it?

A comfortable corner (居心地のよい一角)

WARM-UP 2What makes one corner of a home feel comfortable?家の一角が居心地よく感じられるのは、どんなものがあるからですか？

Follow up
  • Does light or furniture matter more?
  • What sound fits the space?

The view (そこからの眺め)

QUESTION 1What can you see from your favorite spot at home?家のお気に入りの場所から、何が見えますか？

Follow up
  • Does the view change during the day?
  • What do you enjoy noticing?

A place to pause (休む場所)

QUESTION 2Where do you go at home when you need a break?家で休みたいとき、どこへ行きますか？

Follow up
  • What do you do there?
  • How long do you usually stay?

A personal object (自分らしい物)

QUESTION 3What object makes your room feel like yours?どんな物があると、自分の部屋らしいと感じますか？

Follow up
  • Where did it come from?
  • Would the room feel different without it?

One room change (部屋で変えたいこと)

QUESTION 4What's one thing you would change about your room?自分の部屋で一つ変えるなら、何を変えますか？

Follow up
  • Why that change first?
  • Could you do it easily?

Morning and night (朝と夜)

QUESTION 5Which room feels most different in the morning and at night?朝と夜でいちばん雰囲気が変わる部屋はどこですか？

Follow up
  • What creates the difference?
  • Which time do you prefer there?

The first detail (最初に話すこと)

QUESTION 6If you described your room to me, what would you mention first?私に自分の部屋を説明するなら、最初に何を話しますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that detail represent the room?
  • What might surprise me?
