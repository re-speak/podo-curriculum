Let's explore what makes a gift feel exactly right for one person. Please read the title aloud.ある人にとってプレゼントがぴったりに感じられる理由を話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes a gift feel right (プレゼントがぴったりに感じられる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The right gift is sometimes the small thing a person mentioned once and forgot about.ぴったりのプレゼントは、相手が一度だけ話して忘れていた小さな物かもしれません。
Its value comes from attention, not necessarily from its price.その価値は、必ずしも値段ではなく、相手への気づきから生まれます。
A useful gift says that the giver understands someone's daily life.実用的な贈り物は、贈る人が相手の日常を理解していることを表します。
A surprising gift can show imagination, but it also carries a greater risk of being wrong.意外な贈り物は想像力を示せますが、相手に合わない可能性も高くなります。
Asking what someone wants removes some surprise while protecting them from an unwanted object.欲しい物を聞くと驚きは少し減りますが、いらない物を贈ることは避けられます。
Guessing feels more personal when the giver truly knows the person's taste.贈る人が相手の好みを本当によく知っているなら、考えて選ぶことはより特別に感じられます。
An expensive gift can create obligation when the receiver cannot respond in the same way.高価な贈り物は、受け取った人が同じように返さなければならないと感じさせることがあります。
A cheap gift can still feel generous when its timing and meaning are right.値段の安い贈り物でも、タイミングと意味が合っていれば、心のこもったものになります。
Even a gift that misses the mark can preserve a kind intention or a funny story.少し合わなかった贈り物でも、優しい気持ちや面白い思い出が残ることがあります。
A gift feels exactly right when it fits both the person and the moment.プレゼントは、その人とその瞬間の両方に合うと、ぴったりだと感じられます。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your last gift (最後にもらった物)

WARM-UP 1What's the last gift you received?最後にもらったプレゼントは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who gave it to you?
  • What was the occasion?

A gift you use (今も使う贈り物)

WARM-UP 2What's a gift you still use?今も使っているプレゼントは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How often do you use it?
  • Did you expect it to be so useful?

An easy recipient (選びやすい相手)

QUESTION 1Who is easiest for you to choose a gift for?あなたにとって、プレゼントを選びやすいのは誰ですか？

Follow up
  • Why do you know their taste well?
  • What would you choose today?

A personal gift (自分らしい贈り物)

QUESTION 2What's a gift that showed someone really knew you?相手が自分をよく分かっていると感じたプレゼントは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which detail made it feel personal?
  • Were you surprised?

Useful or surprising (実用的か意外か)

QUESTION 3Would you rather receive something useful or something surprising?実用的な物と意外な物なら、どちらをもらいたいですか？

Follow up
  • What is a good example?
  • When would you choose the other kind?

A low-cost gift (お金をかけない贈り物)

QUESTION 4What's a good gift that doesn't cost much?あまりお金をかけなくても喜ばれるプレゼントは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it thoughtful?
  • Who would appreciate it?

Asking or guessing (聞くか考えるか)

QUESTION 5When is it better to ask what someone wants?相手の欲しい物を聞いたほうがよいのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Does asking remove the surprise?
  • When is guessing more fun?

A gift for me (私への贈り物)

QUESTION 6What small gift would you choose for me?私に小さなプレゼントを選ぶなら、何にしますか？

Follow up
  • What in our conversation guided you?
  • When would you give it?
