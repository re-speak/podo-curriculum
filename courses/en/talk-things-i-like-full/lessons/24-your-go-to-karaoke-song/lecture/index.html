A song for the room (その場に合う一曲)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The strongest karaoke choice is not necessarily the song we admire most privately.カラオケでいちばんよい選曲は、自分がいちばん好きな曲とは限りません。
The singer needs a melody and vocal range that feel manageable.歌う人には、無理のないメロディーや音域が必要です。vocal range音域
The room matters too, because certain songs invite other people to join the moment.みんなが一緒に楽しめる曲もあるため、その場の雰囲気も大切です。
A recognizable opening lets everyone identify the song immediately.なじみのあるイントロなら、みんながすぐに何の曲か分かります。
A long, restrained song may be beautiful yet poorly suited to a lively group.長く静かな曲は美しくても、にぎやかなグループには合わないことがあります。
A dependable backup song can rescue the mood after an ambitious first choice goes badly.難しい最初の曲がうまくいかなかったとき、頼れる予備の曲が場を救います。
People often inherit a go-to song through friends, family, or repeated social occasions.定番曲は、友人や家族、何度もの集まりを通して身につくことがあります。
Some songs remain off-limits because their lyrics or associations feel too personal to share.歌詞やその曲との結びつきが人前で共有するには個人的すぎて、避ける曲もあります。
Recommending a song for someone else requires attention to their voice, taste, and confidence.ほかの人にすすめるときは、その人の声や好み、自信を考える必要があります。
The best karaoke memories usually come from a shared moment rather than flawless singing.最高のカラオケの思い出は、完璧な歌より、みんなで共有した瞬間から生まれることが多いです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your first song (最初の一曲)

WARM-UP 1What song would you choose first at karaoke?カラオケで最初にどの曲を選びますか？

Follow up
  • What makes it a reliable choice?
  • Would you sing it alone or with a group?

Your ideal karaoke (理想のカラオケ)

WARM-UP 2What kind of karaoke night would you enjoy most?どんなカラオケなら、いちばん楽しめそうですか？

Follow up
  • Who would be there?
  • What would make the room feel comfortable?

Why that song (その曲を選ぶ理由)

QUESTION 1Why does that song work for you?なぜその曲は自分に合いますか？

Follow up
  • Which part feels easiest?
  • What makes it enjoyable to sing?

How you learned it (覚えたきっかけ)

QUESTION 2Who or what taught you that song?その曲は誰から、または何から覚えましたか？

Follow up
  • What do you remember about learning it?
  • Has the song changed meaning for you?

Your backup (予備の曲)

QUESTION 3What would your backup song be if your first choice felt wrong?最初の曲が合わないとき、予備の曲に何を選びますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of room would suit it?
  • How would it differ from your first choice?

Never at karaoke (絶対に歌わない曲)

QUESTION 4What would you never sing at karaoke?カラオケで絶対に歌わない曲は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it a bad choice for you?
  • Who could sing it well instead?

A song for the group (みんなのための曲)

QUESTION 5What makes a song good for a whole group?みんなで楽しめる曲には、何が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Does everyone need to know it?
  • When can a quiet song still work?

A song for me (私へのおすすめ)

QUESTION 6Ask me what I enjoy singing, then recommend one karaoke song.私が歌って楽しめるものを聞いてから、カラオケの曲を一つすすめてください。

Follow up
  • Why does that song fit what I told you?
  • What should my backup be?
