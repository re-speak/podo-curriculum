BALANCE GAME

Learn on your own or take a class? (独学する？教室で学ぶ？)

Let’s compare learning independently with taking a class, then explain when feedback, structure, or freedom matters most. Please read the title aloud.独学と授業で学ぶ場合を比べ、フィードバック、決まった進め方、自由さのどれが重要になるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What each way of learning gives you (それぞれの学び方で得られるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Someone starting guitar tonight could open a free video or arrive at a teacher’s studio with no idea how to hold the instrument.今夜ギターを始める人は、無料動画を開くことも、持ち方も分からないまま先生の教室へ行くこともできます。
Learning alone lets a person pause, repeat, skip familiar material, and choose the time of each session.独学なら、止めたり繰り返したり、知っている部分を飛ばしたり、学ぶ時間を自分で決めたりできます。
A class provides a fixed appointment, a sequence to follow, and other people who notice whether someone returns.授業には決まった時間と進める順番があり、また来たかどうかに気づく人もいます。
Feedback matters most when a learner cannot easily see or hear their own mistake.自分の間違いを見たり聞いたりしにくい技能では、フィードバックが特に重要です。
Pronunciation, swimming, and physical technique can feel correct while an important detail is still wrong.発音、水泳、体の動かし方は、大切な部分が間違っていても正しくできているように感じることがあります。
Independent study works better when the learner can choose the next step instead of collecting unrelated lessons.独学は、関係のない教材を集めるのではなく、次に何をするか選べるときにうまく進みます。
A free course removes the price barrier, but it does not create a deadline or a reason to continue after a difficult week.無料講座なら費用の問題はなくなりますが、締め切りや、大変な一週間の後も続ける理由までは生まれません。
A class is worth more when the teacher adapts, answers questions, and gives useful feedback rather than only presenting information.先生が情報を伝えるだけでなく、学ぶ人に合わせ、質問に答え、役立つ助言をくれる授業には、より大きな価値があります。
Other learners can add encouragement and practice, although comparison with the group can also weaken confidence.ほかの受講者から励ましや練習相手を得られる一方、周りとの比較で自信を失うこともあります。
Many learners combine both methods: they practise alone, then use a teacher or class when progress stalls.多くの人は二つを組み合わせ、一人で練習し、上達が止まったときに先生や授業を利用します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A self-taught skill (独学で身につけた技能)

WARM-UP 1What’s one skill you taught yourself?独学で身につけた技能は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How did you learn it?
  • If none, what would you like to learn alone?

A worthwhile class (受けてよかった授業)

WARM-UP 2What’s one class that was worth your time?受けてよかったと思う授業や講座は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What made the teacher or group useful?
  • If none, what class would you try?

A new skill (新しい技能)

QUESTION 1If you started a new language or instrument, would you learn alone or take a class?新しい言語や楽器を始めるなら、独学しますか、それとも授業を受けますか？

Follow up
  • Which one are you imagining?
  • What would you do in the first week?

Learning at your own pace (自分のペースで学ぶこと)

QUESTION 2Which part of learning is easier to do alone?学ぶ過程のどんな部分は、一人でするほうが簡単ですか？

Follow up
  • Does learning at your own speed help?
  • Would you use a book or a video?

Feedback you need (必要なフィードバック)

QUESTION 3What skill needs feedback from another person?どんな技能には、ほかの人からのフィードバックが必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Pronunciation, sport, or something else?
  • What might be hard to notice alone?

Three months online (オンラインで学ぶ三か月)

QUESTION 4Would a free online course keep you motivated for three months?無料のオンライン講座だけで、三か月間やる気を保てますか？

Follow up
  • What routine would help?
  • What might make you quit?

A class worth paying for (お金を払う価値のある授業)

QUESTION 5When is a class worth paying for?どんなときなら、授業にお金を払う価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Is feedback or a fixed schedule more valuable?
  • How much would feel reasonable?

A reason to switch (学び方を変える理由)

QUESTION 6What would make you switch from one learning method to the other?どんなことがあれば、もう一方の学び方に変えますか？

Follow up
  • Would slow progress make you switch?
  • Would one excellent teacher change your mind?
