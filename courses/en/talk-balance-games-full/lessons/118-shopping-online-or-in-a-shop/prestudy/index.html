BALANCE GAME

Shopping online or in a shop? (オンライン、それとも店で買い物？)

Let's compare shopping online and shopping in a shop, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.オンラインで買うことと店舗で買うことを比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Seller identity and accountability matter when platforms mix established retailers with unknown third parties.知らない出品者より、知っている店のほうが信頼しやすいことがあります。
Digital purchases create records that may be shared across payment, advertising, and delivery systems.オンラインの買い物では、購入や閲覧の情報が複数の会社に残ることがあります。
Online ordering can remove transport and mobility barriers that make physical retail inaccessible.配送は、店へ行くのが難しい人の買い物を助けます。
Physical access does not guarantee range, especially for specialised sizes, parts, or products.店へ行きやすくても、地域の在庫は少ない場合があります。
Reviews broaden evidence while remaining vulnerable to selection bias, manipulation, and differences in personal use.オンラインのレビューは情報を増やしますが、サイズや品質の不確かさは残ります。
Physical inspection provides sensory evidence that specifications and images cannot fully reproduce.店舗では、手触り、サイズ、色、使い心地を直接比べられます。
Total purchase cost includes shipping, waiting, return friction, travel, and the risk of a poor match.表示価格が最安でも、配送や返品まで含めると総額は安くない場合があります。
Digital convenience reduces physical travel while potentially expanding comparison and decision time.オンラインは移動時間を減らしますが、探す時間が増えることもあります。
In-person retail may add after-sales service and accountability that are hard to value before a problem occurs.近くの店では、すぐに相談、修理、交換ができる場合があります。
Purchase channel should match the information needed and the consequences of a mistaken choice.よい買い方は、商品、急ぎ、価格、買い間違いの負担で変わります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The product photo (商品写真)

WARM-UP 1Which evidence makes an online listing reliable enough to buy from?オンラインの商品を信頼するには、何が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which evidence reduces uncertainty without relying on popularity?
  • How would you verify the seller rather than only the product?

Needed today (今日必要)

WARM-UP 2If an item were essential today, which buying channel would you trust first?今日必要な物なら、最初にどこを探しますか？

Follow up
  • Which failure—delay, wrong fit, or higher price—is least acceptable today?
  • Would confirmed local stock outweigh a wider online range?

Online or in person (ネットか店頭)

QUESTION 1For a purchase that really matters, would you trust an online order or an in-person shop more?本当に大切な買い物なら、オンライン注文と実店舗のどちらをより信頼しますか？

Follow up
  • Which product feature needs direct inspection before purchase?
  • What total cost would make the other channel more attractive?

Only one method (一つの買い方)

QUESTION 2What convenience and information would disappear if only one purchase channel remained?選んだ買い方しか使えなければ、買い物はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Which missing source of information would cause the first mistake?
  • How would accessibility differ for someone without easy transport?

Cheaper but hard to return (安いが返品困難)

QUESTION 3How large must the online saving be to justify difficult returns?ネットのほうが安くても返品が難しいなら、買いますか？

Follow up
  • What probability of a poor fit would erase the saving?
  • Which return condition must be known before ordering?

Free delivery today (当日無料配送)

QUESTION 4If delivery were free and immediate, which value would physical shops still provide?無料で当日に届くなら、店へ行く理由は残りますか？

Follow up
  • Which service or expertise would still justify the physical shop?
  • Would privacy or seller accountability affect the answer?

A product that changes the method (買い方を変える商品)

QUESTION 5What combination of price, urgency, and uncertainty would reverse your choice?どんな商品なら、反対の買い方を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which combination of urgency and uncertainty changes the channel?
  • Would the cost of a wrong choice matter more than the price?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between shopping online and shopping in a shop, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターにオンラインで買うことと店舗で買うことのどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • How does the tutor price uncertainty and return friction?
  • Which service or delivery condition would reverse their channel?
