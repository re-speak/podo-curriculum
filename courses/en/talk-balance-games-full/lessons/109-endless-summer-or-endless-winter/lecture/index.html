BALANCE GAME

Endless summer or endless winter? (終わらない夏、それとも冬？)

Let's compare endless summer and endless winter, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.終わらない夏と終わらない冬を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Thermal comfort depends on humidity, wind, shade, and night-time cooling as well as temperature.湿度が高いと、それほど高くない気温でも厳しく感じることがあります。
Winter risk is shaped by wind chill, road conditions, housing quality, and access to heat.寒さは、風、氷、住まいの状態で移動が難しくなると、より危険になります。
Seasonal extremes expose the limits of both options once comfort becomes a question of health and infrastructure.季節の極端さによって、どちらも快適ではなく大変になることがあります。
A year without seasonal change would affect agriculture, water systems, ecosystems, and seasonal employment.一つの季節が続けば、食べ物、水、植物、地域の仕事も変わります。
Once temperatures become extreme, the comparison shifts from comfort to health and safety.厳しい暑さも寒さも、健康上の危険になることがあります。
Insulation, cooling, heating, and transport determine how strongly outdoor weather shapes daily life.建物や公共交通によって、季節の大変さは変わります。
Extended daylight can support activity while making rest and temperature control harder.夏の長い日は外で過ごす時間を増やしますが、睡眠を妨げることもあります。
Winter supports some forms of rest and recreation but can restrict mobility and social contact.冬には静けさや雪の活動がありますが、移動が制限されることもあります。
Permanent heating or cooling demand would make infrastructure and household cost central to the choice.一つの季節が続くなら、冷暖房のエネルギー費用が重要になります。
Climate preference changes with work, health, housing, transport, and the activities a person needs to maintain.よい気候は、好きな天気だけでなく、そこで何をする必要があるかで決まります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A difficult weather day (大変な天気)

WARM-UP 1Which practical burden makes extreme heat or cold hardest to manage?暑い日や寒い日を大変にするものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which burden comes from temperature and which from infrastructure?
  • Who would face the greatest health or mobility risk?

Perfect weather (完璧な天気)

WARM-UP 2How would you use a full day with your ideal temperature and light?完璧な天気の日なら、外で何をしたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which activity depends most on temperature rather than daylight?
  • How would the ideal change if the day had to be productive?

One season forever (一つの季節)

QUESTION 1If the same season continued all year, would you prefer permanent summer or permanent winter?同じ季節が一年中続くなら、終わらない夏と終わらない冬のどちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which daily system would decide the climate choice?
  • What seasonal experience would be hardest to lose?

Everyday life (毎日の生活)

QUESTION 2Which routine would change most under a permanent summer or winter?選んだ季節が終わらなければ、日常はどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • How would food, transport, and sleep adapt over time?
  • Which change would become expensive rather than merely inconvenient?

Perfect indoors (家の中は快適)

QUESTION 3If indoor temperature were always controlled, which outdoor difference would decide the choice?建物や交通機関がいつも快適でも、答えは同じですか？

Follow up
  • Which outdoor condition would remain impossible to control?
  • Would reliable transport remove the stronger objection?

Hours of daylight (日の長さ)

QUESTION 4How much of your preference comes from daylight rather than temperature?夏と冬の日の長さが同じなら、選択は変わりますか？

Follow up
  • How much of the preference is actually about sleep or mood?
  • Would equal daylight make humidity or ice more decisive?

When you switch (答えが変わる条件)

QUESTION 5Which exact temperature, rain, or snow condition would reverse your choice?どんな天候なら、反対の季節を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which exact weather threshold would change the answer?
  • Would the threshold differ for work and leisure?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between endless summer and endless winter, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターに終わらない夏と終わらない冬のどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • Does the tutor weigh climate or infrastructure more heavily?
  • Which temperature or daylight threshold reverses their answer?
