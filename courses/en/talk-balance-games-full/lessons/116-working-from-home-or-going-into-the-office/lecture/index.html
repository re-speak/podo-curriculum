Working from home or going into the office? (在宅勤務、それとも出社？)

Let's compare working from home and going into the office, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.在宅で働くことと職場へ行くことを比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Working remotely can reduce informal visibility even when measured output remains strong.在宅勤務では、静かに行う努力が上司や同僚に見えにくくなることがあります。
Sensitive work can depend on controlled devices, networks, and spaces that are not equally available everywhere.家と職場では、通信や情報の安全性が違う場合があります。
Location flexibility can support accessibility and care, although the right accommodation differs by person.場所を選べることは、障害や介護の必要がある人にとって働きやすさを高めることがあります。
Remote work can transfer part of the employer's workspace cost to the worker's household.在宅勤務では、冷暖房、電気、家具、通信の費用を家庭が負担することがあります。
Location should follow task design: individual focus and rapid collaborative feedback need different conditions.静かな集中が必要な作業もあれば、短い会話でよくなる作業もあります。
Commute burden depends on duration, predictability, crowding, cost, and whether the time can be used.短い通勤と、長く不安定な通勤では負担が違います。
Remote work shifts the cost of space, connectivity, ergonomics, and interruption into the home.在宅勤務には、合う空間、設備、生活との境界が必要です。
Co-location particularly benefits informal learning when experienced colleagues are accessible rather than merely present.職場では、助けてくれる人が近くにいると学びやすくなります。
Office attendance has limited value when schedules, roles, or norms still prevent meaningful interaction.同じ建物にいても、必ずよい協働が生まれるわけではありません。
Effective hybrid work coordinates location around purpose instead of assigning days without a reason.よいハイブリッド勤務は、一緒にする作業と静かにする作業を分けます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

At-home distraction (家で気が散るもの)

WARM-UP 1What's the biggest distraction when working or studying at home?家で仕事や勉強をするとき、いちばん邪魔になるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How could the space be changed?
  • Which distracts more at home: noise, chores, or your phone?

No commute (通勤なし)

WARM-UP 2How would you use the time saved by removing a commute?通勤時間がなくなったら、その時間をどう使いますか？

Follow up
  • Would you use the time the same way every day?
  • Would you use the saved commute time for rest, exercise, or something social?

A place to focus (集中する場所)

QUESTION 1For focused work, would you choose home or a shared workplace?集中して作業するなら、家と共有の職場のどちらを選びますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of work are you imagining?
  • Which place is quieter for you?

A different workday (変わる一日)

QUESTION 2What changes most in your day when you work at home instead of an office?職場ではなく家で働くと、一日の中で何が最も変わりますか？

Follow up
  • Does it become easier or harder?
  • Focus, communication, or breaks?

A quiet office nearby (近くて静かな職場)

QUESTION 3Would a quiet office fifteen minutes away be better than working at home?十五分の場所にある静かな職場は、在宅勤務よりよいですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the office would help most?
  • Would a short commute make the office more attractive?

Worth meeting in person (会う価値のある作業)

QUESTION 4Which task is worth meeting a team in person for?どんな作業なら、チームが直接会う価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • What would the team need to do together?
  • Which is easier in person: planning or solving a disagreement?

Feeling connected at home (在宅でもつながりを感じること)

QUESTION 5What can make a workday at home feel less lonely?在宅勤務の日に孤独を感じにくくするには、何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Would a short call with a coworker help?
  • Which would help more: lunch outside or an online chat?

A reason to switch (働く場所を変える理由)

QUESTION 6What would make you switch to the other workplace?どんな条件なら、もう一方の働く場所に変えますか？

Follow up
  • A shorter commute or a private room?
  • Which problem would need to disappear?
