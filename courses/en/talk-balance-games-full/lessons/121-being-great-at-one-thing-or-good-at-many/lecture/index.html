BALANCE GAME

Being great at one thing or good at many? (一つに秀でる、それとも多くをこなす？)

Let's compare deep expertise in one skill and broad ability across many skills, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.一つの技能の深い専門性と多くの技能にわたる幅広い能力を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Deep knowledge can transfer through shared principles, patterns, and methods of practice.一つの技能を深く学ぶと、関連する技能も理解しやすくなることがあります。
Cross-domain literacy improves problem framing even when a specialist must provide the final answer.幅広い知識があると、問題が別の分野に関係すると気づきやすくなります。
A strong generalist can translate across fields and recognize relationships that narrow expertise may miss.幅広い技能を持つ人は、専門家が別々に見る問題をつなげられます。
Breadth carries a maintenance cost because unused competence decays and fields continue to change.技能は使わないと薄れるため、多くの能力を保つにも時間がかかります。
Specialisation requires sustained deliberate practice and access to increasingly demanding feedback.深い専門性は、長い練習と繰り返すフィードバックで育ちます。
Breadth becomes valuable when coordination and translation matter more than isolated technical depth.幅広い技能は、問題が複数の分野にまたがるときに役立ちます。
Organisations can distribute depth across specialists while relying on others to connect their work.チームなら、一人にすべてを求めず、複数の専門家を組み合わせられます。
Exploration is not wasted breadth when it helps identify the field in which deeper investment is worthwhile.いくつかの技能を試すことで、深く学ぶべき一つが見つかります。
Even narrow expertise depends on communication, judgment, and an understanding of adjacent fields.専門家にも、知識を説明し活用するための幅広さが必要です。
A T-shaped profile combines cross-domain literacy with selected areas of genuine mastery.幅広い基礎と、一つか二つの深い強みを組み合わせる方法もあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Recent progress (最近の上達)

WARM-UP 1Which skill, if any, have you improved recently?最近、上達した技能があるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • What helped you improve?
  • Which is easier to improve: cooking, exercise, or language?

One skill to master (極めたい一つの技能)

WARM-UP 2What’s one skill you would love to be great at?とても上手になりたい技能を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why that one?
  • How often would you practise it?

Depth or breadth (深さか幅広さ)

QUESTION 1Would you rather become excellent at one skill or good at many?一つの技能の深い専門性と、多くの技能にわたる幅広い能力のどちらを伸ばしたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which skill or group of skills do you imagine?
  • What skill would you happily practise every week for a year?

One year to learn (学ぶ一年)

QUESTION 2With one year to learn, would you study one skill deeply or try five?学ぶ時間が一年あるなら、一つを深く学ぶのと五つを試すのと、どちらがよいですか？

Follow up
  • How would you measure progress?
  • Would you rather practise daily or try something new each week?

A strong team (強いチーム)

QUESTION 3On a small team, who helps more: a specialist or someone who can do several jobs?小さなチームでは、一人の専門家と、いくつもの仕事ができる人のどちらが役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Why would that person help the team?
  • Can a flexible worker help when a teammate is absent?

A new field (新しい分野)

QUESTION 4Which new skill would you rather learn: cooking, photography, or first aid?新しく学ぶなら、料理、写真、応急手当のどれがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Why would that skill be useful?
  • Which is easiest to practise at home: cooking, photography, or first aid?

When range helps (幅広さが役立つとき)

QUESTION 5When would several good skills help more than being excellent at that one?その一つを極めるより、いくつかの技能が得意なほうが役立つのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • At home or at work?
  • Where would a specialist still be better?

A good-enough skill (平均で十分な技能)

QUESTION 6Which skill would you be happy to stay only average at?平均くらいのままでも構わない技能は何ですか？

Answer first, then follow up
  • Why is “good enough” enough?
  • Would you still enjoy learning it?
