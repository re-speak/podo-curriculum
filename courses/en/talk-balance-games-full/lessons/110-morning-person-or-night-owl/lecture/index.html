What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Light exposure is one of the strongest signals that shifts circadian timing.朝の光は、その日の体内時計を整える助けになります。
Evening alertness does not remove the sleep debt created by fixed early obligations.夜に集中できても、翌朝早く始まれば睡眠不足になることがあります。
A person's body clock may conflict with institutional schedules without reflecting laziness or discipline.体内時計は、従わなければならない予定と合わないことがあります。
Large differences between workday and free-day sleep create a form of social jet lag.週末に睡眠時間が大きくずれると、月曜日に時差のような負担を感じます。
Circadian timing affects attention, appetite, temperature, and the natural onset of sleep.体内時計は、集中しやすい時間や眠くなる時間に影響します。
An early schedule is not healthy by itself if it consistently reduces total sleep.早起きか遅起きかだけでなく、睡眠時間の長さも重要です。
Institutional timetables and care responsibilities can override a person's preferred rhythm.学校、仕事、交通、家族の予定が、好みより先に生活時間を決めることがあります。
Regular sleep timing often supports recovery better than alternating sharply between early and late routines.大きく変わる生活より、一定した生活時間のほうが体には楽です。
Different rhythms in one household create practical negotiations over light, noise, meals, and shared time.同居する人の生活時間が違うと、静かにする時間も反対になることがあります。
The workable answer balances biological preference with fixed commitments and enough restorative sleep.よい生活時間は、体のリズムと動かせない予定の両方に合うものです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Feeling ready to think (考える準備が整うとき)

WARM-UP 1What usually helps your mind become clear and ready to work?頭がすっきりして作業の準備が整うには、普段何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What makes it harder to feel alert?
  • Which wakes you up faster: light, food, or movement?

No schedule tomorrow (明日は予定なし)

WARM-UP 2When would you sleep and wake if tomorrow had no schedule?明日何も予定がなければ、何時に寝て起きますか？

Follow up
  • How much sleep would that give you?
  • Would you sleep later on a completely free day?

Early or late (朝型か夜型)

QUESTION 1When the work matters most, do you function better early in the morning or late at night?重要な作業では、朝早くと夜遅くのどちらのほうが力を発揮できますか？

Follow up
  • When would you do your hardest work?
  • Which is quieter for you: early morning or late night?

Changing a routine (日課を変える)

QUESTION 2Which is hardest to change: sleep, meals, or social plans?睡眠、食事、人と会う予定のうち、最も変えにくいのはどれですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that hardest to change?
  • Would changing your sleep schedule affect your mood?

Opposite schedules (反対の生活時間)

QUESTION 3What rule helps two people with opposite sleep schedules share a home?睡眠時間が反対の二人が一緒に暮らすには、どんなルールが役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • When would the home need to be quiet?
  • Would you set quiet hours at night or in the morning?

No morning duties (朝の予定がない生活)

QUESTION 4Would you stay up later if nothing required you to wake early?早起きする必要が何もなければ、もっと夜更かししますか？

Follow up
  • Could staying up late affect your health?
  • Would you still wake early on weekends?

A schedule that forces change (変化を求める予定)

QUESTION 5Which important responsibility can force someone to change their sleep schedule?どんな大切な責任が、睡眠時間を変えるきっかけになりますか？

Follow up
  • Why could that responsibility change a schedule?
  • Which changes sleep faster: a new job or a new baby?

Hear your tutor's choice (チューターの選択を聞く)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor which option they would choose and why.チューターならどちらを選ぶか、その理由も聞いてください。

Answer first, then follow up
  • When does your tutor think most clearly?
  • Would a free weekend change the answer?
