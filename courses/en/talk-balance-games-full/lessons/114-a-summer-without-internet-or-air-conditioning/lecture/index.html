A summer without internet or air conditioning? (ネットなしの夏、それともエアコンなし？)

Let's compare a summer without internet and a summer without air conditioning, then explain what could change your choice. Please read the title aloud.インターネットなしの夏とエアコンなしの夏を比べ、何が変われば選択も変わるか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes the choice change (選択を変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Heat illness risk can rise indoors before discomfort alone signals the danger.室内の暑さは、耐えられないと感じる前に危険になることがあります。
Connectivity now supports essential systems as well as entertainment and convenience.インターネットは、仕事、学校、銀行、連絡、緊急情報にも使われます。
Building design and local climate determine how much mechanical cooling is necessary.冷房の必要性は、日陰、断熱、風通し、湿度で変わります。
Grid failure exposes the dependence of both cooling and connectivity on resilient infrastructure.停電すると、インターネットとエアコンの両方が使えなくなることがあります。
Heat exposure is not equally dangerous; age, health, medication, and mobility all affect risk.子ども、高齢者、特定の病気がある人は、室内の暑さによる危険が高くなります。
Different connection routes create partial backups, although coverage, cost, and power still limit them.モバイル通信、家庭のネット、公共Wi-Fiは、同じように止まるとは限りません。
Air movement improves comfort, yet extreme heat and humidity can exceed what fans can safely solve.扇風機は体の熱を逃がしますが、どんな暑い部屋でも安全にできるわけではありません。
Public cooling spaces can reduce individual risk only when they are accessible during the hottest hours.図書館や公共施設など涼しい場所を使えるかどうかで、選択は変わります。
Disconnection may restore attention while simultaneously excluding people from essential systems.一日ネットを離れると休めますが、大切な用事ができなくなることもあります。
A responsible comparison separates optional convenience from essential cooling and essential connectivity.答えは、季節、安全、建物、ネットの用途によって変わります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One hour offline (一時間オフライン)

WARM-UP 1Which essential task fails first during a short internet outage?インターネットが一時間使えないと、最初に何が困りますか？

Follow up
  • Which essential system depends on the connection?
  • At what duration would inconvenience become a safety issue?

A cool day offline (涼しい日のオフライン)

WARM-UP 2What would make a cool offline half-day restorative rather than inconvenient?涼しい日にスマホなしで半日過ごすなら、何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Which offline activity would restore rather than consume attention?
  • What communication duty would still need a backup?

Choose one loss (一つを諦める)

QUESTION 1If you had to lose one for a summer, would you choose internet or air conditioning?一夏の間どちらかを失うなら、インターネットとエアコンのどちらにしますか？

Follow up
  • Which loss creates the more serious health or access risk?
  • What alternative infrastructure would make the other loss manageable?

The first problem (最初に困ること)

QUESTION 2Which ordinary system would fail first after a full day without the service you gave up?選んだものなしで一日過ごすと、最初に何が困りますか？

Follow up
  • Who besides you would be affected by the first failure?
  • Which backup would delay that failure longest?

A cool spring day (涼しい春の日)

QUESTION 3If heat were not a safety issue for one day, which inconvenience would you accept?涼しい春の日に一日だけなら、どちらを諦めますか？

Follow up
  • Does removing heat risk reveal a different priority?
  • Which online duty would be impossible to postpone?

A safe cool place (安全に涼める場所)

QUESTION 4If reliable public cooling were available all day, would the balance change?近くに安全で涼しい場所があれば、夏の答えは変わりますか？

Follow up
  • What hours and transport would make public cooling genuinely usable?
  • Which household member would still be unable to rely on it?

Season and safety (季節と安全)

QUESTION 5What temperature or access condition would reverse the choice?どんな季節や安全条件なら、反対を選びますか？

Follow up
  • Which temperature or outage duration is the real threshold?
  • How would humidity or medical risk change that threshold?

Compare your answers (答えを比べる)

QUESTION 6Ask your tutor to choose between a summer without internet and a summer without air conditioning, then compare the reasons behind both answers.チューターにインターネットなしの夏とエアコンなしの夏のどちらを選ぶか聞き、お互いの理由を比べてください。

Follow up
  • How does the tutor separate convenience from essential infrastructure?
  • Which safety condition would reverse their summer choice?
