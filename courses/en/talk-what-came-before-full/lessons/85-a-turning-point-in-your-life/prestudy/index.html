How a turning point becomes visible (転機が見えるまで)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A turning point redirects which futures remain available or imaginable.転機は、どんな将来が可能で想像できるかを変えます。
Its importance may remain invisible until later events reveal the new direction.その重要性は、後の出来事が新しい方向を示すまで見えないことがあります。
Decisions, accidents, relationships, losses, and opportunities can all redirect a life.決断、事故、人間関係、喪失、機会はどれも人生の方向を変え得ます。
Some turning points express agency; others force a response to circumstances.主体的に選ぶ転機もあれば、状況への対応を迫る転機もあります。agency主体性
Advice or pressure may shape a choice without fully determining it.助言や圧力が、完全に決めることなく選択を形作る場合があります。
The life not taken can be considered, but never known with certainty.選ばなかった人生は考えられても、確実に知ることはできません。
Chance creates conditions, while decisions shape the response to them.偶然が条件を作り、決断がそれへの対応を形作ります。
Later gratitude does not erase uncertainty or loss during the transition.後の感謝が、転機の不安や喪失を消すわけではありません。
An adverse result does not automatically make the earlier decision irrational.悪い結果でも、以前の決断が不合理だったとは自動的に言えません。
Calling something a turning point gives structure to a life that felt uncertain while lived.転機と呼ぶことで、当時は不確かだった人生に構造を与えられます。

Noticing a turning point (転機に気づくこと)

Follow up

A small opportunity (小さな機会)

QUESTION 2Which opportunity can matter even when it does not look important at first?最初は重要に見えなくても、大きな意味を持つ機会は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why might someone almost miss it?
  • Could a class, trip, or new friendship open a new path?

Someone who changes a life (人生を変える人)

QUESTION 3How can one person's small action redirect someone else's life?一人の小さな行動が、ほかの人の人生をどう変えることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Could a friend, teacher, or stranger do it?
  • Would they know they changed the person's life?

The role of luck (運が果たす役割)

QUESTION 4At a turning point, what comes from luck and what comes from choice?転機では、何が運から生まれ、何が選択から生まれますか？

Follow up
  • What can the person control?
  • Would meeting the right person be luck or choice?

Changing direction later (後で方向を変えること)

QUESTION 5When is changing direction wiser than staying on the same path?同じ道を進み続けるより、方向を変えるほうが賢明なのはいつですか？

Follow up
  • What makes staying feel safer?
  • Would boredom or a new opportunity be enough reason to change?
