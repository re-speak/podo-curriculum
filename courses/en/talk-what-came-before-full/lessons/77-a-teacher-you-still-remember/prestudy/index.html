Why some teachers remain memorable (記憶に残る先生の理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A memorable teacher is not necessarily the warmest, easiest, or most admired one.記憶に残る先生が、最も温かく接しやすく、尊敬されていたとは限りません。
Memory may preserve a teacher's voice, standards, humour, patience, or unpredictability.記憶には、先生の声、基準、ユーモア、忍耐強さ、意外な一面が残ることがあります。
Strictness can feel intimidating in the moment yet reasonable in retrospect.厳しさはその場では威圧的でも、振り返ると妥当だったと思えることがあります。
Discipline remains harmful when it relies on humiliation rather than clear standards.明確な基準ではなく屈辱に頼る指導は、後になっても有害なままです。humiliation屈辱
One precise act of attention may matter more than an inspirational speech.的確な気づかいが、感動的な演説より大きな意味を持つことがあります。
A teacher can recognize potential before the learner has language for it.先生が、学ぶ側がまだ言葉にできない可能性を先に見つけることもあります。
Lessons about effort, fairness, or curiosity can outlast the course content itself.努力、公平さ、好奇心についての教えは、授業内容そのものより長く残ることがあります。
Classroom memory is partly collective, shaped by stories students repeat to one another.教室の記憶は一部共有され、生徒同士が繰り返す話によって形作られます。
Distance may soften fear without excusing conduct that crossed a line.時間が恐怖を和らげても、限度を越えた行動まで正当化されるわけではありません。
Revisiting a teacher's influence can reveal the kind of guidance we now value.先生の影響を振り返ると、今の自分がどんな導き方を大切にするかが見えてきます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A memorable teacher (記憶に残る先生)

WARM-UP 1Which real or fictional teacher has stayed in your mind?実在でも作品の中でも、今も心に残っている先生は誰ですか？

Follow up
  • What did that teacher do?
  • Which teacher from a film or book would be another good choice?

A teacher who makes learning easier (学びやすくする先生)

WARM-UP 2What does a good teacher do when a student makes a mistake?よい先生は、生徒が間違えたときに何をしますか？

Follow up
  • How can the teacher keep the student interested?
  • Would you prefer a hint or a clear correction?

A small act students remember (生徒が覚えている小さな行動)

QUESTION 1What small act from a teacher can matter to a student years later?何年後にも生徒にとって大切に思える、先生の小さな行動は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why can a small act stay in a student's memory?
  • Would remembering a name or giving encouragement be a good example?

Strict but good? (厳しくてもよい先生か)

QUESTION 2When does a strict teacher go too far?厳しい先生が行き過ぎるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What effect does it have on students?
  • Which is worse: shouting at students or giving too much homework?

Beyond the subject (教科以外の学び)

QUESTION 3What's an important lesson a teacher can leave beyond the subject itself?先生が教科そのものを超えて残せる大切な学びは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How could that lesson help later?
  • Could a teacher show kindness or confidence without teaching it directly?

Understanding a teacher later (後で分かる先生のこと)

QUESTION 4Why can a teacher's choices make more sense years after the class ends?授業が終わって何年かしてから、先生の選択に納得できることがあるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • What can people understand later?
  • Can you think of a rule that seemed unfair at school?

What you would say (今伝えるなら)

QUESTION 5What would you like to tell a former teacher?昔の先生に何を伝えたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would it be thanks, a question, or criticism?
  • What should teachers hear from former students?

When the style does not fit (教え方が合わないとき)

QUESTION 6How can a student keep learning when a teacher's style does not work for them?先生の教え方が自分に合わなくても、生徒はどう学び続けられますか？

Follow up
  • What could the student try first?
  • Could another teacher, classmate, or online lesson help?
