What childhood ambitions reveal (子どもの夢が表すもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A childhood ambition often grows from one vivid but incomplete image of adult work.子どもの夢は、大人の仕事についての鮮明だが不完全なイメージから生まれます。
A uniform, special tool, admired skill, or public attention may carry the appeal.制服、特別な道具、尊敬される技術、人からの注目が魅力になることがあります。
The ordinary routine, training, and compromise behind the role are usually invisible.その役割の裏にある日常、訓練、妥協はたいてい見えません。
Family examples, school experiences, fiction, and media expand or narrow imaginable futures.家族の例、学校での経験、作品、メディアが、想像できる将来を広げたり狭めたりします。
An ambition may fade when reality becomes clearer or another identity becomes possible.現実が明確になったり別の自分を想像できたりすると、夢が薄れることがあります。
A childhood ambition may survive indirectly through a related skill, value, or hobby.子どもの夢が、関連する技術、価値観、趣味として間接的に残ることもあります。
Some people had no fixed ambition, changed it constantly, or simply do not remember.決まった夢がなかった人、何度も変えた人、覚えていない人もいます。
A satisfying adult life need not confirm a prediction made with childhood knowledge.満足できる大人の生活は、子どもの知識で立てた予想どおりになる必要はありません。
The old title may conceal a durable wish to help, create, explore, or be recognized.昔の職業名には、人を助けたい、作りたい、探検したい、認められたいという願いが隠れていることがあります。
Even when the occupation changed, the underlying value may connect childhood and adulthood.職業が変わっても、その根底の価値が子ども時代と大人の今を結ぶことがあります。underlying value根底の価値

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A childhood ambition (子どもの夢)

WARM-UP 1Which childhood dream job sounds most fun to you now?今聞いて最も楽しそうな子どもの頃の夢の仕事は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would astronaut, chef, or athlete sound fun now?
  • Which part might be less fun in real life?

What appealed (魅力だった点)

WARM-UP 2What part of a dream job does a child usually see first?子どもは夢の仕事のどんな部分を最初に見ますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that part look exciting to a child?
  • What difficulty do children often miss?

Where it came from (きっかけ)

QUESTION 1Who or what has the strongest influence on a child's dream job?子どもの夢の仕事に最も強く影響する人やものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How might that influence shape the dream?
  • Can a film make a difficult job look easy?

When it changed (変わった時期)

QUESTION 2What new knowledge most often changes a childhood career dream?どんな新しい知識が、子どもの頃の仕事の夢を変えやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the dream might still survive?
  • Could the child find a related job instead?

A skill that carries over (後にも役立つ技能)

QUESTION 3How can a skill from a childhood dream help in a very different career?子どもの頃の夢から得た技能は、大きく違う仕事でどう役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Would creativity, confidence, or teamwork be a good example?
  • Where else could that skill help?

Still connected (今とのつながり)

QUESTION 4How can an abandoned childhood dream still shape an adult's choices?諦めた子どもの頃の夢は、大人の選択にどう影響し続けますか？

Follow up
  • Could the old dream become a hobby?
  • Where might it appear in adult life?

The realistic view (現実的な見方)

QUESTION 5Which hidden part of a dream job becomes clear only with adult experience?夢の仕事のどんな見えない部分が、大人の経験を通して初めて分かりますか？

Follow up
  • Which part was missing from the childhood picture?
  • Would you still try it for one day?

A child's surprise (子どもの自分が驚くこと)

QUESTION 6What would your childhood self misunderstand about the life you have now?子どもの頃の自分は、今の生活について何を誤解しそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would your work, home, or technology surprise them most?
  • What would still feel familiar?
