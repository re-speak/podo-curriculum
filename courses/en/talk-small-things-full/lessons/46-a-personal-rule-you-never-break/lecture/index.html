A personal rule you never break (絶対に破らない自分のルール)

Let's talk about how personal rules can make repeated decisions easier. Please read the title aloud.自分のルールが、繰り返す判断をどのように楽にするか話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Rules we choose for ourselves (自分で選ぶルール)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A personal rule converts a recurring decision into a boundary that no longer needs daily debate.自分のルールは、何度も繰り返す判断を、毎日迷わなくてよい境界へ変えます。
Such a boundary may protect time, money, health, relationships, dignity, or peace of mind.そのような境界は、時間、お金、健康、人間関係、尊厳、心の平穏を守ることがあります。
Many strict rules are written in response to one experience whose cost we refuse to repeat.厳しいルールの多くは、二度と同じ代償を払わないために、ある経験をきっかけに作られます。
The rule reduces decision fatigue, yet its clarity can harden into unnecessary rigidity.そのルールは判断の疲れを減らす一方で、その明確さが不要な融通のなさへ変わることがあります。decision fatigue判断の疲れ
A carefully chosen exception may clarify the rule's purpose rather than weaken it.慎重に選んだ例外は、ルールを弱めるのではなく、その目的を明確にする場合があります。
A boundary can look excessive to people who cannot see the experience behind it.その背景にある経験が見えない人には、境界が行きすぎに見えることがあります。
Explaining a boundary can help cooperation, although its full personal history remains private.境界を説明すれば協力しやすくなりますが、その個人的な背景をすべて明かす必要はありません。
Some personal rules remain long after the risk, relationship, or stage of life has changed.危険や人間関係、人生の段階が変わったあとも、個人的なルールが残ることがあります。
Reconsidering a rule deliberately is different from abandoning it under temporary pressure.ルールを意識して考え直すことは、一時的な圧力で捨てることとは違います。
A durable personal rule remains connected to a purpose we can still name and defend today.長く続ける価値のある自分のルールは、今も言葉にして説明できる目的と結びついています。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your personal rule (自分のルール)

WARM-UP 1What's one personal rule you try never to break?できるだけ破らないようにしている自分のルールは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Is it about money, time, health, or other people?
  • When did you start following it?

Your level of strictness (守り方の厳しさ)

WARM-UP 2How strict are you about that rule?そのルールを、どのくらい厳しく守っていますか？

Follow up
  • What exception would be reasonable?
  • When did you last break it?

The reason behind it (ルールにした理由)

QUESTION 1What made you turn it into a rule?何がきっかけで、それをルールにしたのですか？

Follow up
  • Was it one event or a repeated problem?
  • What does the rule prevent?

A reasonable exception (納得できる例外)

QUESTION 2When is breaking a personal rule reasonable?どのようなときは、自分のルールを破ってもよいですか？

Follow up
  • How can you tell if it is an honest exception or just an excuse?
  • What should happen afterward?

Other people's view (ほかの人からの見え方)

QUESTION 3Would your rule seem strange to anyone else?そのルールを変わっていると思う人はいますか？

Follow up
  • Who might misunderstand it?
  • What missing detail would explain it?

Explain or keep private (説明する？)

QUESTION 4If a personal rule affects someone else, how much should you explain?自分のルールがほかの人に影響するなら、どこまで理由を説明すべきですか？

Follow up
  • What do they genuinely need to know?
  • Which detail can remain private?

A useful rule (役立つルール)

QUESTION 5How can you tell whether the rule still helps you?そのルールが今も役立っていると、どう判断できますか？

Follow up
  • What problem does it solve now?
  • What would show that it is no longer useful?

A former rule (やめたルール)

QUESTION 6Now the opposite: what personal rule have you stopped following?では逆に、守るのをやめた自分のルールは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why did you stop?
  • If none comes to mind, which common rule would you never follow?
