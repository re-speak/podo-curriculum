What you do on a rainy day (雨の日にすること)

Let's build a rainy day from the first drop to the perfect ending. Please read the title aloud.雨の降り始めから理想の終わりまで、雨の日を組み立てて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What rain changes (雨が変えるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Rain quietly changes the pace, route, and atmosphere of an ordinary day.雨は、普通の一日のペースや道順、雰囲気を静かに変えます。
Outdoor plans may be shortened, moved indoors, postponed, or abandoned altogether.屋外の予定は短縮されたり、室内へ移されたり、延期されたり、完全に中止されたりします。
With no urgent plans, the sound of rain can create a rare sense of permission to slow down.急ぐ予定がなければ、雨音によって、ゆっくりしてよいという珍しい安心感が生まれます。
The same rain becomes far less romantic when we have to commute, carry things, or arrive on time.通勤したり、荷物を運んだり、時間どおりに着く必要があると、同じ雨でもずっと現実的な負担になります。
Warm food, dry clothes, and a sheltered room gain extra emotional value on a wet day.雨の日には、温かい食べ物、乾いた服、雨をしのげる部屋に、いつも以上の安心感が生まれます。shelter雨宿り
Rain can provide a socially acceptable excuse to return to neglected indoor hobbies.雨は、しばらく離れていた室内の趣味に戻る、自然な言い訳を与えてくれます。
It can also multiply the stress of a delay by making every part of the journey less predictable.一方で、移動のあらゆる部分が予測しにくくなり、少しの遅れのストレスを増幅させることもあります。
A downpour often remains memorable because of one vivid sound, smell, route, or place where we sheltered.土砂降りは、鮮明な音や匂い、通った道、雨宿りした場所とともに記憶に残ることがよくあります。downpour激しい雨
The ideal rainy day depends on comfort, safety, and the absence of an urgent obligation.理想の雨の日には、心地よさと安全、そして急いで果たす義務がないことが欠かせません。
Weather does not determine the whole day, but it reshapes which choices feel easy, difficult, or inviting.天気が一日すべてを決めるわけではありませんが、どの選択が楽か、難しいか、魅力的かを変えます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

No plans, just rain (予定のない雨の日)

WARM-UP 1It's raining and you have no plans. What happens?雨が降っていて、予定はありません。どう過ごしますか？

Follow up
  • What do you do first?
  • What would make you leave home anyway?

The atmosphere (雨の日の雰囲気)

WARM-UP 2On a rainy day, which sets the mood most: the sound, smell, or view?雨の日の雰囲気を最も作るのは、音、匂い、景色のどれですか？

Follow up
  • Which detail feels most relaxing?
  • Which one could become annoying?

How rain feels (雨への気持ち)

QUESTION 1What do you enjoy—or dislike—about rainy days?雨の日のどのようなところが好き、または苦手ですか？

Follow up
  • How does the feeling change when you have to travel?
  • Which kind of rain affects your mood most?

Rainy-day food (雨の日の食べ物)

QUESTION 2What's the best food for a rainy day?雨の日にいちばん合う食べ物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it fit the weather?
  • Would you cook it or get it elsewhere?

What gets cancelled (中止になること)

QUESTION 3What kind of plan would you cancel because of rain?雨なら、どのような予定を中止しますか？

Follow up
  • What amount of rain would change your decision?
  • Could the plan move indoors instead?

A difficult situation (大変な雨の場面)

QUESTION 4Which rainy-day problem would be hardest to deal with?雨の日のどのような状況が、いちばん大変そうですか？

Follow up
  • What would make it manageable?
  • Which detail would stay in your memory?

A better day (よくなる一日)

QUESTION 5How can rain improve an ordinary day?雨は、普通の一日をどのようによくすることがありますか？

Follow up
  • Which plan might become better indoors?
  • Who might enjoy the change most?

The perfect rainy day (理想の雨の日)

QUESTION 6What would your ideal rainy day look like?理想の雨の日は、どのような一日ですか？

Follow up
  • How much company would fit the day?
  • What one thing would ruin it?
