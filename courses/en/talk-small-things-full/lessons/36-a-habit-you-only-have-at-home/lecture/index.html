Why home changes our behaviour (家で行動が変わる理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
At home, we can relax the constant work of managing other people's impressions.家では、他人からどう見られるかを絶えず管理する負担から解放されます。
That privacy produces habits we would edit or suppress in public.その私的な空間によって、人前なら直したり抑えたりする習慣が生まれます。suppress抑える
Some private habits provide comfort; others survive because they are harmless and amusing.安心感を与える私的な習慣もあれば、害がなく面白いから残っているものもあります。
A home habit can remain invisible to us until someone else witnesses it.家での習慣は、ほかの人に目撃されるまで、自分には見えないままのことがあります。
People who share a home gradually create private routines and unspoken rules.同じ家で暮らす人たちは、少しずつ独自の習慣や暗黙のルールを作ります。
A routine feels universal until another household reveals a completely different norm.別の家庭でまったく違う基準を知るまでは、自分の習慣を誰にでも共通だと思いがちです。
Privacy gives us room to rehearse, experiment, and fail before anyone is watching.私的な空間なら、誰かに見られる前に練習し、試し、失敗できます。
Private habits often disappear when living arrangements or daily routines change.住まい方や日々の流れが変わると、私的な習慣も消えることがよくあります。
Other habits persist because they have become part of our personal recovery ritual.一方で、自分なりの回復の儀式になっているため、続く習慣もあります。
The useful boundary is not normal versus strange, but harmless privacy versus behaviour that affects others.大切な境界は、普通か変かではなく、害のない私的な習慣か、他人に影響する行動かという点です。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your at-home habit (家でだけの習慣)

WARM-UP 1What's one habit you only have at home?家でだけする習慣を一つ挙げるとしたら、何ですか？

Follow up
  • Do you talk to yourself, eat from the pan, wear old clothes, or do something else?
  • Why do you only do it at home?

A typical moment (いつもの場面)

WARM-UP 2What does a typical moment with that habit look like?その習慣が出るのは、普段どんな場面ですか？

Follow up
  • When does it usually happen?
  • What would another person notice first?

How it begins (始まり方)

QUESTION 1How can a home-only habit begin?家でだけする習慣は、どのように始まるのでしょうか？

Follow up
  • What need might it meet?
  • What could make it last for years?

Someone sees it (見られたとき)

QUESTION 2How might a visitor react to an unusual home habit?家での変わった習慣を見たら、訪問者はどう反応しそうですか？

Follow up
  • What could make the reaction kind instead of judgmental?
  • What would be worth explaining?

Shared routines (一緒に暮らす人の習慣)

QUESTION 3Why do people living together develop unusual routines?一緒に暮らす人たちは、なぜ独特の習慣を作るのでしょうか？

Follow up
  • What problem might a routine solve?
  • How can it become invisible to the people doing it?

A household surprise (家庭の意外な習慣)

QUESTION 4What's a household habit that could seem unusual somewhere else?別の家庭では変わって見えそうな、家の習慣は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What situation would make it understandable?
  • Which differs more across homes: eating, cleaning, or sleeping habits?

A changing routine (変わる習慣)

QUESTION 5What home habit might disappear if someone moved?引っ越したら、どんな家での習慣がなくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would living with someone new change it?
  • Which changes fastest after a move: eating, cleaning, or sleeping habits?

Keep it private (私的なままにする)

QUESTION 6When is it reasonable to keep a harmless private habit to yourself?害のない私的な習慣を人に見せないほうがよいのは、どのようなときですか？

Follow up
  • Would you tell a visitor about the habit?
  • When could the habit affect someone else?
