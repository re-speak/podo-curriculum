Why some praise stays with us (心に残る褒め言葉)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A compliment can be only a few words and still stay with us for years.褒め言葉は短くても、何年も心に残ることがあります。
Specific praise often feels more believable than a general compliment.具体的な褒め言葉は、誰にでも当てはまる一般的な褒め言葉より信じやすく感じます。
It matters when the speaker noticed effort that other people missed.ほかの人が気づかなかった努力を見てくれたとき、その言葉は特に大切になります。
Many people feel awkward and quickly reject praise.褒められると気まずく感じ、すぐ否定してしまう人も多いです。
A simple thank-you accepts the kindness without claiming too much.簡単にお礼を言えば、自慢せずに相手の好意を受け取れます。
Criticism can be easier to remember because our minds watch for danger.人は危険に注意しやすいため、批判のほうを覚えやすいことがあります。criticism批判
Saving a meaningful compliment can balance that negative habit.意味のある褒め言葉を覚えておくと、その悪い傾向とのバランスを取れます。
Giving good praise also requires attention to another person's real work.よい褒め言葉を伝えるには、相手の本当の努力を見る必要があります。
The best time is often soon after the action, while the detail is clear.具体的なことを覚えているうちに伝えると、褒め言葉はより効果的です。
One honest sentence can change how someone sees their own ability.たった一つの正直な言葉で、自分の能力についての見方が変わることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A lasting compliment (心に残る褒め言葉)

WARM-UP 1What's a compliment you still remember?今でも覚えている褒め言葉は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who said it?
  • Why did it stay with you?

Awkward praise (気まずい褒め言葉)

WARM-UP 2What kind of compliment can feel awkward instead of nice?うれしいより、気まずく感じる褒め言葉はどんなものですか？

Follow up
  • Does the speaker, timing, or wording matter most?
  • Which is more awkward: praise from a stranger or praise in public?

The right time (ちょうどよいタイミング)

QUESTION 1Who gave you a compliment at exactly the right time?ちょうど必要なときに褒めてくれた人は誰ですか？

Follow up
  • What did they notice?
  • Did it change what you did next?

Did you believe it? (信じられた？)

QUESTION 2What makes a compliment feel believable?どんな褒め言葉なら信じられますか？

Follow up
  • Which details show that the person truly noticed?
  • Why can good praise still be hard to accept?

Your response (そのときの返事)

QUESTION 3What's your natural reaction when someone praises you?褒められたとき、自然にどんな反応をしますか？

Follow up
  • Does your reaction change with the person?
  • What response feels warm without rejecting the praise?

What stays longer (長く残るもの)

QUESTION 4Which stays with you longer: a compliment or criticism?褒め言葉と批判では、どちらが長く心に残りますか？

Follow up
  • Why can one comment outweigh many others?
  • How do you stop an unfair comment from taking over?

Praise you gave (自分が伝えた言葉)

QUESTION 5What do you notice that makes you want to compliment someone?人のどんなところに気づくと、褒めたくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Which quiet effort deserves more recognition?
  • What makes a compliment useful rather than polite?

More everyday praise (もっと伝えたい褒め言葉)

QUESTION 6What everyday compliment should people give more often?日常で、もっと多く伝えるとよい褒め言葉は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What could that compliment help someone notice?
  • Which feels better: 'Good job' or praise about one specific thing?
