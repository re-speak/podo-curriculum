A song you keep playing (何度も聴いている曲)

Let's talk about a song that has become part of your life lately. Please read the title aloud.最近の生活の一部になっている曲について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why one song stays on repeat (一曲を繰り返し聴く理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Repeating a song lets us enjoy something familiar while still noticing new details.同じ曲を繰り返すと、安心感を楽しみながら新しい細部にも気づけます。
Sometimes the sound matters more than the meaning of the words.歌詞の意味より、音そのものが大切なこともあります。
A song can quickly become connected to a place, person, or period of life.曲はすぐに、場所や人、人生のある時期と結びつくことがあります。
Hearing it later can bring back details we thought we had forgotten.あとで聴くと、忘れたと思っていた細かなことが戻ってくることがあります。
We also choose music to change or support our mood.気分を変えたり、その気分を保ったりするために音楽を選ぶこともあります。
A song for work may be different from a song for walking home.仕事に合う曲と、帰り道に合う曲は違うかもしれません。
Overplaying a song can temporarily weaken its emotional effect.聴きすぎると、その曲が持っていた感情的な力が一時的に弱くなることがあります。
After a break, the same song may feel fresh again.しばらく離れると、同じ曲がまた新鮮に感じられます。
A personal playlist can become a record of a year without using words.自分のプレイリストは、言葉を使わずに一年の記録になります。playlistプレイリスト
The song we repeat now may later become the fastest way back to this moment.今繰り返している曲が、将来この時期を思い出す一番早い方法になるかもしれません。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A current song (最近の曲)

WARM-UP 1Is there a song you've been playing a lot lately?最近よく聴いている曲はありますか？

Follow up
  • Which artist or sound fits your mood lately?
  • When do you usually want to hear it?

One detail (一つの細部)

WARM-UP 2Which matters more in a song: lyrics, voice, or beat?曲では、歌詞、声、リズムのどれがいちばん大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Which element creates the first impression?
  • Which one keeps the song interesting?

How long it lasts (どのくらい続く？)

QUESTION 1When do you get tired of a song you love?好きな曲に飽きるのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Does overplaying change the song or your mood?
  • How long does a song need to feel fresh again?

A memory (思い出すこと)

QUESTION 2What kind of memory can a song bring back instantly?曲を聴くと、どんな思い出がすぐによみがえりますか？

Follow up
  • Why does music hold that memory so strongly?
  • Can the feeling change over time?

When you play music (音楽を聴くとき)

QUESTION 3What activity is better with music?音楽があるともっとよくなる活動は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How does the music change your pace or mood?
  • Which task needs silence instead?

An old repeat (昔繰り返した曲)

QUESTION 4What can make a favorite song hard to hear?好きな曲でも、聴くのがつらくなるのはどんな理由からですか？

Follow up
  • Can a song belong too strongly to one period?
  • What might help someone enjoy it again?

Sharing music (音楽を共有すること)

QUESTION 5What song would you send someone who needed energy?元気が必要な人に、どんな曲を送りますか？

Follow up
  • Would the lyrics or sound matter more?
  • When you need energy, do you choose a fast song or a familiar one?

Your year as a playlist (一年のプレイリスト)

QUESTION 6Which song belongs on a playlist about your year?今年を表すプレイリストに、どんな曲を入れますか？

Follow up
  • What story would the song tell?
  • Would your year need a happy song or a calm one?
