One thing you always carry (いつも持ち歩いているもの)

Let's use what's in your bag to talk about your daily life. Please read the title aloud.かばんの中身から、日常生活について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

The life inside a bag (かばんの中にある生活)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A bag holds the things we expect to need away from home.かばんには、外出先で必要だと思う物を入れます。
The things we use every day are usually easy to reach.毎日使う物は、たいてい取り出しやすい場所に入っています。
Other items stay there for an emergency that may never happen.ほかの物は、起こらないかもしれない緊急時のために入っています。emergency緊急時
Receipts and forgotten objects can stay in a bag for months because we never decide to remove them.レシートや忘れた物は、出すか決めないまま何か月もかばんに残ることがあります。
A heavy bag can show how many different situations we are trying to prepare for.かばんが重いと、いろいろな状況に備えようとしていることが分かります。
The contents change when our work, health, or responsibilities change.仕事や健康、責任が変わると、中身も変わります。
Phones have replaced many separate tools, but they have also added chargers.スマートフォンは多くの道具に取って代わりましたが、今度は充電器を持つ必要が生まれました。
Some objects are useful mainly because they make us feel prepared.実際に役立つより、持っていると安心する物もあります。
Cleaning a bag forces us to decide what still belongs in our routine.かばんを整理すると、今の生活にまだ必要な物か決めることになります。
One ordinary object can tell a surprisingly detailed story about a person's day.普通の一つの物から、その人の一日について意外に詳しいことが分かります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A bag today (今日のかばん)

WARM-UP 1Which everyday item would be most inconvenient to forget?日常の持ち物で、忘れるといちばん困る物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What need does it meet?
  • How easy would it be to replace?

What you carry (持ち歩く物)

WARM-UP 2What are the three things you usually take when you go out?出かけるとき、ふつう持っていく三つの物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which item earns its place every day?
  • What changes on a longer day out?

What's inside (入っている物)

QUESTION 1Which bulky everyday item is still worth carrying?かさばっても持ち歩く価値がある日用品は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When is it most useful?
  • Would you carry an umbrella, a water bottle, or something else?

What you use (実際に使う物)

QUESTION 2What do you reach for most when you're away from home?外出中に、いちばんよく手に取る物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of your day depends on it?
  • Could another item replace it?

Ready for an emergency (もしものための持ち物)

QUESTION 3What emergency item do people carry but rarely use?人が持ち歩いていても、めったに使わない非常用の物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How serious would the problem need to be?
  • When does being prepared become too much?

Always forgotten (いつも忘れる物)

QUESTION 4What do people forget most often when they leave home?家を出るとき、人は何をいちばん忘れやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that item easy to forget?
  • How does forgetting it change the day?

How it changed (変わった持ち物)

QUESTION 5Which things did phones replace in people's pockets?スマートフォンは、人が持ち歩いていたどんな物の代わりになりましたか？

Follow up
  • Which replacement made daily life easier?
  • What was lost when the old item disappeared?

What it says (持ち物が語ること)

QUESTION 6What can you guess about someone from what's in their bag?バッグの中身から、その人について何が分かると思いますか？

Follow up
  • Which item tells you the most?
  • What could make you guess wrong?
