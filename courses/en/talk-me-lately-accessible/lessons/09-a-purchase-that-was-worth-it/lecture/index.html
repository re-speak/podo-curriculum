A purchase that was worth it (買ってよかったもの)

Let's talk about a purchase that was worth the money. Please read the title aloud.お金を出す価値があった買い物について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What makes a purchase worth it? (買い物の価値)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A purchase can be worth the money for many reasons, not only because of its price.買い物に価値がある理由はたくさんあり、値段だけでは決まりません。
Even an expensive item can feel worth the price over time if someone uses it every day.高いものでも、毎日使えば、時間がたつと値段に見合うと感じられることがあります。
A product can be useful because it solves the same small problem again and again.商品は、同じ小さな問題を何度も解決することで役に立つことがあります。
The excitement of buying something may disappear, but its usefulness can stay.何かを買ったときのわくわくは消えても、使いやすさは残ることがあります。
Good quality can mean that something lasts, feels comfortable, or saves time.よい品質とは、長く使えること、快適なこと、時間を節約できることかもしれません。
A high price does not always mean good value, but a product that works well may be worth more.高いものがいつもよい価値を持つとは限りませんが、よく働く商品なら多く払う価値があるかもしれません。
After buying something, repairs, storage, or monthly payments can become extra costs.買ったあとに、修理、保管、毎月の支払いが追加費用になることがあります。
The time of the purchase matters too, because a good product may be wrong for someone's life at that moment.買う時期も大切で、よい商品でもそのときの生活には合わないことがあります。
People often regret a purchase when they buy too quickly or expect too much.急いで買ったり、期待しすぎたりすると、買い物を後悔することがよくあります。regret後悔する
Repairing or selling a product can help it stay useful for longer.商品を修理したり売ったりすると、もっと長く役立てることができます。
Some purchases save time, while others are connected to a good memory or more confidence.時間を節約する買い物もあれば、よい思い出や自信につながる買い物もあります。
A person's best purchase often shows what they want to improve in daily life.一番よかった買い物には、その人が日常生活で何をよくしたいかが表れます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your best buy (買ってよかったもの)

WARM-UP 1What's something you bought that was worth the money?買ってよかったと思う物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What made it worth the price?
  • Which usually feels more worth it: something useful or something fun?

Use or durability? (よく使う？長く使う？)

WARM-UP 2Which matters more in a good purchase: using it often or keeping it for years?よい買い物では、よく使うことと長く使えることのどちらが大切ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does that matter more?
  • When would the other one win?

The decision (決めた理由)

QUESTION 1What makes you wait before buying something?物を買う前に待つのは、どんな理由からですか？

Follow up
  • How long do you usually wait?
  • What can make you decide sooner?

In your routine (日常の中で)

QUESTION 2What purchase can make daily life noticeably easier?日常生活をはっきり楽にしてくれる買い物は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which routine does it improve?
  • Which saves more time: a kitchen tool or a phone app?

Price and value (値段と価値)

QUESTION 3When can paying more save money later?いつなら、多く払うことであとからお金を節約できますか？

Follow up
  • What example comes to mind?
  • When is the cheaper choice still enough?

A regret (後悔した買い物)

QUESTION 4What kind of purchase most often becomes a regret?どんな買い物が、いちばん後悔につながりやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Which warning sign gets ignored?
  • What can someone learn from the mistake?

The limit (価値がなくなる条件)

QUESTION 5What can make a useful item stop feeling worth it?役に立つ物でも、価値を感じなくなるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • When do repair costs become too much?
  • How can a better replacement change the judgment?

Buying a replacement (買い替える前に)

QUESTION 6What do you check before buying a replacement?買い替える前に、何を確認しますか？

Follow up
  • Which improvement would matter most?
  • What would make you choose another brand?
