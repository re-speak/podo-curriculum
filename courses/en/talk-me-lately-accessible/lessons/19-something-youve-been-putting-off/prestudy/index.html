Why we keep delaying things (先延ばしを続ける理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Putting something off creates a gap between what we intend and what we do.先延ばしをすると、やりたいことと実際の行動の間に差ができます。put off先延ばしにする
The task is often large, unclear, boring, or emotionally difficult.その課題は大きすぎたり、不明確だったり、退屈だったり、気持ちの面で難しかったりします。
Saying we have no time can hide a problem with the first step.時間がないと言いながら、実は始め方が分からない場合もあります。
Avoiding the task gives quick relief but keeps the worry alive.避けると一時的に楽になりますが、不安は残ります。
The longer we wait, the task can start to feel like a judgment about us.長く待つほど、その課題が自分の性格への評価のように感じられることがあります。
Self-criticism usually adds more emotion without making the next action easier.自分を責めると、次の行動が楽になるどころか、気持ちの負担が増えることが多いです。
A step small enough to finish in ten minutes can get us moving again.10分で終えられる小さな行動を決めると、また動き始められます。
Another person or a fixed time can make starting easier.決まった時間やほかの人の助けがあると、始めやすくなります。
Remembering a task we finally completed can show what helped before.以前やっと終えたことを思い出すと、何が役立ったか分かります。
Sometimes the honest solution is to decide not to do the task at all.ときには、もうしないと決めることが正直な解決になる場合もあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A delayed task (先延ばしの用事)

WARM-UP 1What's one task you've been putting off lately?最近、先延ばしにしていることを一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it easy to delay?
  • When will it become urgent?

What you do instead (代わりにすること)

WARM-UP 2What do you usually do instead of the task you're avoiding?やるべきことを避けているとき、代わりに何をしますか？

Follow up
  • What feeling are you trying to avoid?
  • When does the distraction create more stress?

Starting or finishing? (始める？終わらせる？)

QUESTION 1Which gets harder after a delay: starting or finishing?先延ばしにすると、始めることと終わらせることのどちらが難しくなりますか？

Follow up
  • How does the delay change the task in your mind?
  • What breaks that pattern?

The real barrier (本当の障害)

QUESTION 2Which task is easiest to put off: boring, difficult, or unclear?退屈な仕事、難しい仕事、はっきりしない仕事の中で、いちばん先延ばしにしやすいのはどれですか？

Follow up
  • Which emotion comes with that kind of task?
  • What first step reduces the resistance?

A tiny overdue task (小さなやり残し)

QUESTION 3What's a tiny task you've delayed for far too long?小さいのに、長く先延ばしにしてしまった用事は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why is it still unfinished?
  • Could you finish it in ten minutes?

Starting right away (すぐ始めるきっかけ)

QUESTION 4What can make you start an annoying task right away?面倒な用事でも、すぐに始められるのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Which outside pressure is actually useful?
  • How can you make the first step smaller?

After a delay (先延ばしのあと)

QUESTION 5How do you reward yourself after finishing something annoying?面倒なことを終えたあと、どのように自分にご褒美をあげますか？

Follow up
  • Does the reward improve motivation or only relief?
  • When is finishing itself enough?

Restarting (再開するコツ)

QUESTION 6What's one trick that makes a delayed task easier to restart?先延ばしにしたことを再開しやすくするコツは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How could you make the task feel easier?
  • How much would you try to finish on the first day back?
