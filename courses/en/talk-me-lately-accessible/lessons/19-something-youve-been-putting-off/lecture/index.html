Let's understand one delayed task and make the next action clear. Please read the title aloud.先延ばしにしていることを理解し、次の行動を明確にしましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Why we keep delaying things (先延ばしを続ける理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Putting something off creates a gap between what we intend and what we do.先延ばしをすると、やりたいことと実際の行動の間に差ができます。put off先延ばしにする
The task is often large, unclear, boring, or emotionally difficult.その課題は大きすぎたり、不明確だったり、退屈だったり、気持ちの面で難しかったりします。
Saying we have no time can hide a problem with the first step.時間がないと言いながら、実は始め方が分からない場合もあります。
Avoiding the task gives quick relief but keeps the worry alive.避けると一時的に楽になりますが、不安は残ります。
The longer we wait, the task can start to feel like a judgment about us.長く待つほど、その課題が自分の性格への評価のように感じられることがあります。
Self-criticism usually adds more emotion without making the next action easier.自分を責めると、次の行動が楽になるどころか、気持ちの負担が増えることが多いです。
A step small enough to finish in ten minutes can get us moving again.10分で終えられる小さな行動を決めると、また動き始められます。
Another person or a fixed time can make starting easier.決まった時間やほかの人の助けがあると、始めやすくなります。
Remembering a task we finally completed can show what helped before.以前やっと終えたことを思い出すと、何が役立ったか分かります。
Sometimes the honest solution is to decide not to do the task at all.ときには、もうしないと決めることが正直な解決になる場合もあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Still not done (まだしていないこと)

WARM-UP 1Which kind of task are you most likely to put off?あなたはどんな課題を、いちばん先延ばしにしやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it easy for you to delay?
  • When does it become urgent?

What you do instead (代わりにすること)

WARM-UP 2When you avoid a task, what do you tend to do instead?課題を避けるとき、代わりに何をすることが多いですか？

Follow up
  • Does that activity actually feel relaxing?
  • When does the task return to your mind?

How long (どのくらい)

QUESTION 1When a task gets delayed, how long does it usually stay undone?課題を先延ばしにすると、普段どのくらい終わらないままですか？

Follow up
  • What makes the delay grow?
  • How much does a deadline change it?

The real barrier (本当の障害)

QUESTION 2What usually stops you from starting?普段、何が始めることを止めますか？

Follow up
  • Is the first step usually clear?
  • Which feeling comes up most often?

If it never gets done (ずっとしなかったら)

QUESTION 3What can happen when a task never gets done?課題をずっと終えないと、何が起こりえますか？

Follow up
  • Who else might have a problem?
  • When might it be better to stop?

A past delay (以前の先延ばし)

QUESTION 4What could finally make you start a task you had put off?先延ばしにしていた課題を、あなたがついに始めるきっかけは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which pressure or reward could help you?
  • What might end a long delay?

After a delay (先延ばしのあと)

QUESTION 5If you finish something late, how do you usually feel?遅れて何かを終えたとき、普段どんな気持ちになりますか？

Follow up
  • Does the result match the worry?
  • What feeling arrives first when it is done?

The next decision (次の判断)

QUESTION 6If something gets delayed again, what could you do next?また何かを先延ばしにしたら、次に何ができますか？

Follow up
  • What is a ten-minute next step?
  • When is dropping the task the better decision?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
