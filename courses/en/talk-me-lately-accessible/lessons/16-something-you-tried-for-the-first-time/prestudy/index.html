What first times teach us (初めての体験が教えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Daily routines become easier, but they can make our world feel smaller.日課は生活を楽にしますが、自分の世界を小さくすることもあります。
Trying something new makes us pay attention again.初めてのことをすると、もう一度よく注意するようになります。
We usually imagine the experience before we actually have it.新しい体験の前には、実際にするより先に頭の中で想像します。
Nervousness can mean that the result matters to us.緊張は、その結果を大切に思っているしるしかもしれません。nervousness緊張
Clear instructions or a supportive person can make the first step easier.分かりやすい説明や支えてくれる人がいると、最初の一歩が楽になります。
The real experience is often different from the version in our head.実際の体験は、頭の中で考えていたものと違うことがよくあります。
Being bad at first can feel uncomfortable when we are used to being capable.普段はできることが多い人にとって、初心者の感覚は居心地が悪いことがあります。
Trying it again shows whether the experience was only exciting because it was new.もう一度すると、新しいから楽しかっただけかどうかが分かります。
Remembering first times helps us notice that we are still changing.最近の初めてを覚えておくと、自分がまだ変わっていると気づけます。
One new experience often points toward the next thing we want to try.一つの新しい体験が、次に挑戦したいことにつながることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Something new (試してみたいこと)

WARM-UP 1What new experience would you be willing to try this month?今月、どんな新しいことなら試してみたいですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it worth trying?
  • How might it feel to be new at it?

Feeling new (初めての感覚)

WARM-UP 2Which is harder: starting something new or being bad at it?新しいことを始めることと、うまくできないことでは、どちらが難しいですか？

Follow up
  • What makes that part uncomfortable?
  • What could make it easier?

Why people try (試す理由)

QUESTION 1What gives people the final push to try something new?新しいことを試す最後のきっかけになるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Does another person's help matter?
  • Can waiting make it harder?

Expectation and reality (予想と実際)

QUESTION 2What's something that is often easier than people expect?人が思っているより簡単なことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does it look difficult at first?
  • What helps a beginner?

First-time stories (初めての体験談)

QUESTION 3What makes a first try worth repeating?初めて試したことを、もう一度やりたいと思うのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What would you change the second time?
  • Does company make a difference?

The first before that (その前の初めて)

QUESTION 4Which first-time experiences make the best stories?どんな初体験が、いちばん面白い話になりますか？

Follow up
  • Which detail makes them memorable?
  • Can an awkward start improve the story?

Staying a beginner (初心者でいること)

QUESTION 5What helps you enjoy being a beginner?初心者であることを楽しむには、何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What kind of progress feels good?
  • Who makes learning more fun?

The next first (次の初めて)

QUESTION 6What would be fun to try with no pressure to be good at it?上手にできなくてもよいなら、何を試すと楽しそうですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it appealing?
  • Would you try it alone or with someone?
