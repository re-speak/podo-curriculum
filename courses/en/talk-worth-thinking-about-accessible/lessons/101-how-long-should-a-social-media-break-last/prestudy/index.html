Let's examine what a meaningful break from social media would change. Please read the title aloud.SNSを意味のある期間休むと何が変わるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What a break actually tests (SNSを休むと分かること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
A break from social media can mean an hour, a week, or a permanent choice.SNSを休む期間は、一時間、一週間、またはずっと離れるという選択もあります。
The difficult part is different for each person.何が難しいかは人によって違います。
Some people would miss messages more than public posts.公開投稿よりも、メッセージが使えないことのほうが困る人もいます。
An app can be useful and still become a time sink.便利なアプリでも、時間を奪うものになることがあります。time sink時間を奪うもの
Deleting an app does not remove every reason to reach for it.アプリを消しても、開きたくなる理由がすべてなくなるわけではありません。
Work, family, safety, or community may make one service hard to leave.仕事、家族、安全、地域とのつながりのため、やめにくいサービスもあります。
A clear limit may work better than an all-or-nothing rule.全部やめるより、明確な制限のほうが合う人もいます。
Young users need guidance as well as limits.若い利用者には、制限だけでなく使い方の助言も必要です。
People who manage social media well often know why they are opening it.SNSを上手に使う人は、何のために開くのか分かっていることが多いです。
The useful question is not only how long we can stop, but what we want back.大切なのは、何日やめられるかだけでなく、何を取り戻したいかです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A meaningful break (意味のある休み)

WARM-UP 1How long does a social-media break need to be before it changes anything?SNSを休むことで何かが変わるには、どのくらいの期間が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of change would count?
  • Would a shorter break reveal anything?

The first missing function (最初に困る機能)

WARM-UP 2Which function would people miss first?人はどの機能が使えないと最初に困るでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Is it communication, information, or entertainment?
  • What already serves the same purpose?

After deleting an app (アプリを消したあと)

QUESTION 1Which habit usually changes after someone deletes or mutes an app?アプリを削除したり通知を切ったりすると、どの習慣が変わりやすいですか？

Follow up
  • What becomes easier?
  • What tends to bring the person back?

Hard to replace (代えにくい価値)

QUESTION 2What does social media provide that is hardest to replace?SNSが与えるもので、最も代えにくいものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why is another tool less effective?
  • Who benefits most from that value?

Useful or a time sink (役立つか時間を奪うか)

QUESTION 3When does social media become a time sink instead of a useful tool?SNSはいつ、役立つ道具ではなく時間を奪うものになりますか？

Follow up
  • Which kind of checking has no clear purpose?
  • What limit could restore a purpose?

Healthy use (健全な使い方)

QUESTION 4What habits show a healthy relationship with social media?どんな習慣が、SNSとの健全な関係を示しますか？

Follow up
  • How does the person decide when to open it?
  • Which habit would be hardest to copy?

Advice at fourteen (十四歳への助言)

QUESTION 5What would you tell a fourteen-year-old about using social media?十四歳の人にSNSの使い方について何と伝えますか？

Follow up
  • Which rule would be realistic?
  • What should adults understand too?

One function to keep (残したい一つの機能)

QUESTION 6Which one social-media function is most worth keeping?SNSの機能を一つだけ残すなら、どれが最も価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Why that function?
  • What could replace the others?
