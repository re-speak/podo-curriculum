What AI gets wrong about human work (AIが人の仕事を誤解するところ)

Let's examine where AI misses the human context around work. Please read the title aloud.AIが仕事を取り巻く人間の文脈をどこで見落とすか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Capability is not the whole job (能力だけでは仕事にならない)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
AI can perform a task without understanding the whole job around it.AIは作業をこなせても、その周りにある仕事全体を理解しているとは限りません。
Visible output is often only one part of good work.目に見える成果は、よい仕事の一部にすぎないことがあります。
A fast answer can still be wrong for the situation.答えが速くても、その状況には合わないことがあります。
People also make mistakes, so human control is not automatically perfect.人も間違えるので、人が管理すれば必ず完璧というわけではありません。
Every tool has a blind spot that users need to understand.どんな道具にも、利用者が知るべき見落としやすい点があります。blind spot見落としやすい点
AI is often strongest when the task has clear examples and a clear check.明確な例と確認方法がある作業では、AIが力を発揮しやすいです。
It is weaker when the real question is what should matter.何を大切にすべきかが本当の問いなら、AIは苦手です。
Handing over a task can make accountability less clear.作業を任せると、誰が結果に責任を持つのか分かりにくくなることがあります。
Five years from now, jobs may change in different ways rather than simply disappear.五年後、仕事は単になくなるのではなく、さまざまな形に変わるかもしれません。
A good question is not whether AI is good or bad, but who checks what.大切なのはAIがよいか悪いかではなく、誰が何を確認するかです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A human context AI misses (AIが見落とす人の文脈)

WARM-UP 1Which part of human work is easiest for AI to misunderstand?人の仕事のどの部分を、AIは最も誤解しやすいですか？

Follow up
  • Which role gives us a clear example?
  • Why does that role matter?

The missing context (欠ける文脈)

WARM-UP 2In one role, what important context would AI miss?一つの役割で、AIはどんな重要な文脈を見落としますか？

Follow up
  • Does the task depend on trust, timing, or history?
  • Who understands that context best?

A visible mistake (見える失敗)

QUESTION 1What kind of AI mistake would make that blind spot visible?どんなAIの誤りなら、その見落としがはっきり分かりますか？

Follow up
  • What would the output get wrong?
  • How could someone check it?
