What speaking English has made you notice (英語を話して気づいたこと)

Let's reflect on what speaking English makes us notice about language. Please read the title aloud.英語を話すことで言語について何に気づくか振り返りましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

A new language changes attention (新しい言語で気づき方が変わる)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Learning to speak English can change what a person notices in Japanese.英語を話す学習によって、日本語での話し方に気づくことがあります。
Some differences are grammatical, but many are about tone and expectation.文法の違いもありますが、口調や期待の違いも多くあります。
New word choice can make the speaker consider an idea from another angle.新しい言葉の選び方によって、別の角度から考えることがあります。
That does not mean the language creates a completely different personality.だからといって、言語が全く別の性格を作るわけではありません。
A learner may feel more direct in English because the available phrases are direct.知っている英語表現が直接的なため、英語ではより直接的だと感じる人もいます。
Speaking English may expand who someone can talk to without changing their closest relationships.英語を話すことで話せる相手が増えても、大切な人間関係は変わらないことがあります。
The hardest part may be speed, confidence, listening, or saying a precise thought.難しいのは、速さ、自信、聞き取り、考えを正確に言うことかもしれません。
Progress often appears first in a small moment rather than a dramatic breakthrough.進歩は大きな成功より、小さな瞬間に最初に表れることがあります。
Some learners notice no change yet, and that is also useful evidence.まだ変化を感じない学習者もいて、それも大切な情報です。
Looking back helps a learner decide what kind of English speaker to become next.振り返ることで、次にどんな英語話者になりたいかを決められます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A difference you notice (気づく違い)

WARM-UP 1What difference between English and Japanese has become more noticeable since you started speaking English?英語を話し始めてから、英語と日本語のどんな違いが以前より目立つようになりましたか？

Follow up
  • What first made the difference noticeable?
  • Which language makes it clearer?

One clear example (一つの明確な例)

WARM-UP 2Which example shows that difference most clearly?どの例が、その違いを最もはっきり示しますか？

Follow up
  • What would someone say in English?
  • How would Japanese handle it?

Words, tone, timing, or silence (言葉、口調、間、沈黙)

QUESTION 1Does the difference involve words, tone, timing, or silence?その違いは、言葉、口調、間、沈黙のどれに関係しますか？

Follow up
  • Which part is hardest to translate?
  • What might a listener misunderstand?

A wider conversational world (広がる会話の世界)

QUESTION 2How does English change the range of people a learner can talk to?英語は、学習者が話せる人の範囲をどう変えますか？

Follow up
  • Which conversation becomes possible?
  • What still limits the connection?

The hardest adjustment (最も難しい調整)

QUESTION 3What is the hardest adjustment when speaking English?英語を話すとき、最も難しい調整は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Has that difficulty changed?
  • What support actually helps?

Compared with expectations (予想との違い)

QUESTION 4How did speaking English compare with what you expected?英語を話す経験は、予想と比べてどうでしたか？

Follow up
  • Which expectation was accurate?
  • Which one changed most?

Expression and identity (表現と自分らしさ)

QUESTION 5How can different words change expression without changing who someone is?違う言葉は、その人らしさを変えずに表現をどう変えられますか？

Follow up
  • Which word choice shows the difference?
  • What remains consistent across languages?

Would you start again? (もう一度始めるか)

Follow up

  • What would you change from the beginning?
