BETWEEN TWO COUNTRIES

A place you would show a visitor (旅行者に案内したい場所)

Let's plan one day somewhere meaningful, away from the famous sights. Please read the title aloud.有名な観光地ではない、大切な場所での一日を考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

The places a guidebook misses (ガイドブックが見落とす場所)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Famous sights help visitors recognize a city, but ordinary places help them understand it.有名な観光地は街を知る助けになりますが、普通の場所は街を理解する助けになります。
A neighborhood market, small park, or local café can show how people spend real time.近所の市場、小さな公園、地元のカフェは、人々の実際の過ごし方を見せてくれます。
These places matter because someone has a personal reason to return to them.そうした場所が大切なのは、誰かが個人的な理由で何度も戻るからです。
The best time of day may change the sounds, people, and atmosphere completely.いちばんよい時間帯によって、音、人、雰囲気がまったく変わることがあります。
A visitor may miss the point unless the local person explains what to notice.地元の人が見るべき点を説明しなければ、旅行者は魅力に気づかないかもしれません。
That explanation can reveal a memory, a routine, or a side of the city that is disappearing.その説明から、思い出、日課、消えつつある街の一面が見えることがあります。disappear消えつつある
Showing a place also means deciding how much of it should stay private.場所を案内することは、どこまで秘密にしておくかを決めることでもあります。
Too much attention can change the quiet character that made the place special.注目されすぎると、その場所を特別にした静かな個性が変わることがあります。
A thoughtful host chooses a place that fits the visitor, not simply their own favorite.思いやりのある案内役は、自分の一番好きな場所ではなく、相手に合う場所を選びます。thoughtful思いやりのある
The result is a shared day rather than a list of sights.その結果、観光地のリストではなく、一緒に過ごした一日になります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Start here (ここから)

WARM-UP 1If I visited Japan for one day, where would you take me besides the famous places?私が一日だけ日本を訪れたら、有名な場所以外のどこへ連れて行きますか？

Follow up
  • What side of Japan would the place show?
  • Would you plan food, nature, or shopping first?

Local or famous? (地元か有名な場所か)

WARM-UP 2What makes a local place better than a famous tourist spot?地元の場所が有名な観光地よりよいのは、どんなところですか？

Follow up
  • Which ordinary detail makes it meaningful?
  • When is the famous place still worth choosing?

Your place (あなたの場所)

QUESTION 1Where would you take a visitor who likes quiet places?静かな場所が好きな旅行者を、どこへ連れて行きますか？

Follow up
  • What would make the place memorable?
  • Would they enjoy a park, a café, or a temple most?

Everyday life (日常が見える場所)

QUESTION 2What place shows everyday life in your area?あなたの地域の日常生活が見える場所はどこですか？

Follow up
  • Which routine would be easiest to see?
  • Where is daily life easiest to see: a station, a market, or a park?

The best time (いちばんよい時間)

QUESTION 3When is the best time to see a familiar place?身近な場所を見るのにいちばんよい時間はいつですか？

Follow up
  • How do the people, sounds, or light change?
  • Do you prefer familiar places in the morning or at night?

For a food lover (食べ物が好きな人へ)

QUESTION 4Which local place would you show a food lover?食べ物が好きな人に、地元のどこを案内しますか？

Follow up
  • What does the place show about local food?
  • Would a food lover prefer a market or a restaurant?

What I might miss (見落としそうなこと)

QUESTION 5What do guidebooks often fail to explain about local places?ガイドブックは、地元の場所について何を説明できていないことが多いですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that hard to explain?
  • Do guidebooks miss local routines or local stories more often?

Turn the trip around (旅を逆にする)

QUESTION 6What kind of place would you want me to show you in my country?私の国では、どんな場所を案内してほしいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you prefer daily life or a special place?
  • Would you rather explore one area or move around?
