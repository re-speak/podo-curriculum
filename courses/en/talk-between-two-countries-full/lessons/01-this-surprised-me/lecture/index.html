Why small surprises matter (小さな驚きが大切な理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
People often picture another country long before they visit it, through films, social media, and stories from friends.人は実際に訪れるずっと前から、映画やSNS、友人の話を通して、ほかの国を思い描いていることがよくあります。
Those early pictures are often incomplete, but they can still feel surprisingly certain.そうした最初のイメージは不完全なことが多いのに、なぜか確かなもののように感じられます。
The surprise begins when ordinary routines do not match the image they brought with them.日常の習慣が、自分の中にあったイメージと合わないとき、驚きが生まれます。
A small experience—how people queue, share food, or speak to strangers—can stand out more than a famous landmark.列の作り方や食べ物の分け方、知らない人への話し方といった小さな体験が、有名な観光名所より強く印象に残ることがあります。
Because it is ordinary, the detail can feel personal and stay in our memory longer.日常の一部だからこそ、その細かな違いは身近に感じられ、長く記憶に残ることがあります。
At first, we may judge the difference by treating habits from home as the standard.最初は、自国の習慣を基準にして、その違いを判断してしまうことがあります。
That first judgment can be useful if it becomes a question rather than a final conclusion.最初の判断も、最終的な結論ではなく問いに変えられれば、役に立つことがあります。
But one encounter rarely represents a whole country; the same custom can vary by region, generation, or situation.しかし、一度の出会いが国全体を表すことはほとんどなく、同じ習慣でも地域や世代、状況によって異なります。
Talking with different people or seeing the same situation again can change our first explanation.違う人と話したり、同じ状況をもう一度見たりすると、最初の説明が変わることがあります。
Asking why something surprised us can reveal both the new culture and the assumptions we brought with us.なぜ驚いたのかを考えると、新しい文化だけでなく、自分が持ち込んだ思い込みも見えてきます。assumption思い込み
The comparison can also make familiar habits at home easier to notice.比べることで、自国の慣れ親しんだ習慣にも気づきやすくなります。
Over time, that surprise may become curiosity and change how we see familiar life at home.時間がたつと、その驚きは好奇心に変わり、慣れ親しんだ自国の日常の見え方まで変えることがあります。

Conversation style (会話スタイル)

Please choose your preferred discussion style.希望する会話の進め方を選んでください。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Caught off guard (思いがけなかったこと)

WARM-UP 1What's one thing about another country that genuinely surprised you?ほかの国について、本当に驚いたことを一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Did you notice it in person, online, or somewhere else?
  • Which kinds of cultural differences are easiest to notice?

Tiny differences (小さな違い)

WARM-UP 2What's one small everyday difference between cultures that you find interesting?文化による日常の小さな違いで、面白いと感じるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does it stand out to you?
  • Where do you notice cultural differences most: food, manners, or daily life?

Through a visitor's camera (旅行者のカメラ)

QUESTION 1What do visitors often photograph in Japan that locals barely notice?日本を訪れた人がよく写真を撮る一方で、日本人はあまり気に留めないものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Where do they usually see it?
  • Why does it stand out to visitors?

See everyday life (日常を見てみたい国)

QUESTION 2Which country would you most like to visit to see what everyday life is really like?実際の日常生活を見るために、最も訪れてみたい国はどこですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of ordinary life would you want to see?
  • Would you rather learn about a place through a local person, a documentary, or a book?

Easy to misunderstand (誤解しやすい習慣)

QUESTION 3Which Japanese custom would be easiest for a visitor to misunderstand?日本の習慣の中で、訪れた人が最も誤解しやすそうなものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What might they assume?
  • Which is easier to explain: bowing or taking off your shoes?

A habit worth trying (取り入れてみたい習慣)

QUESTION 4Which custom from another culture would fit easily into your life?ほかの文化の習慣で、自分の生活に無理なく取り入れられそうなものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would make it easy to keep doing?
  • Would you rather try another culture's food, celebration, or daily habit?

Hard to adapt (慣れるのが難しいこと)

QUESTION 5Which cultural difference would take you the longest to get used to?あなたにとって、慣れるまでに最も時間がかかりそうな文化の違いは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which part would challenge your normal routine?
  • Which adjustment is usually hardest: food, language, or daily routines?

A habit you'd miss (恋しくなる習慣)

QUESTION 6What everyday Japanese habit would you miss if you lived abroad?海外で暮らしたら恋しくなりそうな、日本の日常の習慣は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When would you notice its absence?
  • Could you recreate it there?
