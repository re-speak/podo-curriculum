The first hour shapes the trip (最初の1時間が旅を決める)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
The doors open, and a new country first reaches you through its sounds, signs, and air.ドアが開くと、音や標識、空気を通して新しい国を最初に感じます。
Before sightseeing begins, you may need to find your bag, connect your phone, and choose a way into town.観光が始まる前に、荷物を受け取り、携帯をつなぎ、町までの行き方を選ぶ必要があるかもしれません。
Simple tasks feel harder when the language, money, and transport system are unfamiliar.言葉やお金、交通の仕組みに慣れていないと、簡単なことも難しく感じられます。
Some travelers want familiar food after a long journey, while others want their first local taste immediately.長旅のあとは慣れた食べ物が欲しい人もいれば、すぐに現地の味を試したい人もいます。
Learning one useful phrase can turn the first conversation from a test into a welcome.役に立つひと言を覚えると、最初の会話が試験ではなく歓迎のように感じられます。
A translation app is quick, but a helpful person may give you information the screen cannot.翻訳アプリは速いですが、親切な人は画面では得られない情報を教えてくれることがあります。
The first hour can feel exciting and tiring at the same time, so small comforts matter.最初の1時間はわくわくすると同時に疲れることもあるため、小さな安心が大切です。
First impressions are vivid, but one confusing station or friendly stranger should not define a whole country.第一印象は強く残りますが、一つの分かりにくい駅や親切な人だけで国全体を決めるべきではありません。
A personal arrival routine can create calm before the traveler starts exploring.自分なりの到着後の習慣があると、町を歩き始める前に落ち着くことができます。
The most useful local advice often answers one immediate question: what should I do first?いちばん役立つ地元の助言は、「最初に何をすればよいか」という目の前の質問に答えてくれます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

First impressions (最初の印象)

WARM-UP 1When you arrive in a new country, what do you notice first?初めての国に着いたとき、最初に何に気づきますか？

Follow up
  • Do you notice people, sounds, or signs first?
  • What did you notice on your last trip?

After the journey (長旅のあと)

WARM-UP 2What's the first thing you want to eat or drink after a long trip?長旅のあと、最初に何を食べたり飲んだりしたいですか？

Follow up
  • Do you want something familiar or local?
  • Where would you look for it?

First local phrase (最初の現地語)

QUESTION 1What's the first local phrase you try to learn?現地の言葉で最初に覚えたいひと言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When would you use it?
  • Would you practice it before arriving?

Arrival confusion (到着時の戸惑い)

QUESTION 2Which part of arriving feels most confusing: signs, transport, or money?到着したとき、標識、交通、お金のどれがいちばん分かりにくいですか？

Follow up
  • What information would help?
  • Who would you ask?

Settling in (ほっとすること)

QUESTION 3What small thing helps you relax after arriving?到着後、どんな小さなことでほっとできますか？

Follow up
  • Does contacting someone help?
  • Do you unpack or go out first?

Asking for help (助けを求める)

QUESTION 4Who would you ask first if you needed help?助けが必要なら、最初に誰に聞きますか？

Follow up
  • What would make someone approachable?
  • Would you use a translation app first?

Japan's first hour (日本での最初の1時間)

QUESTION 5What might surprise a visitor during their first hour in Japan?日本に着いて最初の1時間で、外国人が驚きそうなことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Where would they notice it?
  • What would you explain?

My town tonight (今夜、私の町で)

QUESTION 6If I arrived in your town tonight, what should I do first?今夜私があなたの町に着いたら、最初に何をするといいですか？

Follow up
  • Where should I go?
  • What should I avoid doing?
