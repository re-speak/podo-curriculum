Something Japan does especially well (日本が特に得意なこと)

Today, we'll choose one everyday service Japan does especially well and explain why. Please read the title aloud.今日は、日本が特に得意な日常サービスを一つ選び、その理由を話します。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What does “best” mean? (「一番」の意味)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Japan is often praised for everyday services such as convenience stores, trains, taxis, and delivery.日本は、コンビニ、電車、タクシー、宅配などの日常サービスでよく評価されています。
Calling one of them “the best” depends on the problem a person needs to solve.そのどれかを「一番」と呼ぶかどうかは、その人が解決したい問題によって変わります。
Trains can stand out for their frequency and predictability, especially during a daily commute.電車は、特に毎日の通勤では、本数の多さと予測しやすさが際立つことがあります。predictability予測しやすさ
Convenience stores combine prepared food, bill payment, ticketing, and parcel services in one small place.コンビニは、調理済みの食事、支払い、チケット、荷物のサービスを一つの小さな場所にまとめています。
Taxis are valued for cleanliness and reliability, although the price can limit how often people use them.タクシーは清潔さと信頼性で評価されますが、料金のため頻繁には使いにくいことがあります。
Delivery services save time by offering narrow time windows and flexible ways to receive a package.宅配サービスは、細かな時間指定や柔軟な受け取り方によって時間を節約します。
Visitors may notice impressive speed, while residents often value consistency more.旅行者は速さに感心するかもしれませんが、住民はむしろ安定性を重視することがあります。
A strong service can create expectations that become difficult to give up when someone moves abroad.優れたサービスは、海外へ引っ越したときに手放しにくい期待を生むことがあります。
Its success may also depend on workers, infrastructure, and costs that customers rarely see.その成功は、利用者には見えにくい働く人、インフラ、費用にも支えられているかもしれません。
Even an excellent system can have flaws, and a famous strength may sometimes feel overrated.優れた仕組みにも欠点があり、有名な長所が評価されすぎていると感じることもあります。overrated評価されすぎた
Comparisons become more useful when they focus on one concrete situation instead of national pride.国への誇りではなく、一つの具体的な場面に注目すると、比較はもっと役に立ちます。
The service a person chooses as Japan's best often reveals what they value in daily life.日本で一番だと選ぶサービスには、その人が日常生活で何を大切にしているかが表れます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Your pick (あなたの一番)

WARM-UP 1Convenience stores, trains, taxis, or delivery—pick the one Japan does best.コンビニ、電車、タクシー、宅配の中で、日本が一番得意だと思うものを選んでください。

Follow up
  • Which strength makes it stand out?
  • What weakness could change your choice?

A real scene (実際の場面)

WARM-UP 2On a busy day, which Japanese service is most useful?忙しい日に、どの日本のサービスがいちばん役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Which problem does it solve first?
  • What detail makes the biggest difference?

Service that helped (助けになったサービス)

QUESTION 1When did a Japanese service last make your day easier?日本のサービスのおかげで最後に一日が楽になったのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What happened?
  • Which small detail made it go smoothly?

For a visitor (旅行者にとって)

QUESTION 2Which Japanese service helps visitors the most?旅行者にいちばん役立つ日本のサービスは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which situation makes it especially valuable?
  • What might still confuse a first-time user?

Another country (ほかの国)

QUESTION 3What everyday service does another country do better than Japan?日常のサービスで、ほかの国のほうが日本より上手にしているものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What do they do differently?
  • Which matters more in a service: speed or friendliness?

Living abroad (海外で暮らすなら)

QUESTION 4What everyday service would you miss most while living abroad?海外で暮らすなら、どんな日常のサービスがいちばん恋しくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which routine would change first?
  • Which is harder to replace: trains or convenience stores?

Overrated? (評価されすぎ？)

QUESTION 5Which Japanese service may get a little too much praise?日本のサービスの中で、少しほめられすぎているものはどれですか？

Follow up
  • What problem do people overlook?
  • Which matters more: good reviews or your own experience?

Make it better (もっとよくする)

QUESTION 6What one change would improve trains, taxis, convenience stores, or delivery?電車、タクシー、コンビニ、宅配のどれかをよくするなら、何を一つ変えますか？

Follow up
  • Who would gain the most from the change?
  • Which new cost or difficulty might appear?
