What gets lost between languages (言語の間で失われるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A dictionary can give an equivalent word without giving the same feeling.辞書は対応する言葉を示せても、同じ感覚までは伝えられないことがあります。equivalent対応するもの
Meaning also depends on who is speaking, their relationship, and the situation.意味は、話す人、相手との関係、状況にも左右されます。
One Japanese word may therefore need a full English sentence in some contexts.そのため、日本語の一語が、場面によっては英語の一文を必要とすることがあります。
The closest translation may be correct but sound warmer, colder, stronger, or more formal.いちばん近い訳でも、より温かく、冷たく、強く、または堅く聞こえることがあります。
Speakers often solve the gap by explaining the situation around the word.話し手は、その言葉が使われる状況を説明して差を埋めることがよくあります。
Examples show when the word is natural and when it would feel strange.例があると、その言葉が自然な時と、不自然に感じる時が分かります。
Tone, timing, and shared knowledge can carry as much information as the word itself.口調、タイミング、共有している知識は、言葉そのものと同じくらい情報を伝えます。
That is why a technically accurate subtitle can still feel wrong.そのため、技術的には正しい字幕でも、違和感があることがあります。technically厳密には
Learning these gaps helps us notice assumptions built into our own language.こうした違いを学ぶと、自分の言語に含まれる前提にも気づけます。
Translation is not only replacing words; it is choosing what the listener most needs to receive.翻訳は単語を置き換えるだけでなく、聞き手に最も必要なものを選ぶことです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Start here (ここから)

WARM-UP 1Which English word would you like to understand better?もっとよく理解したい英単語は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which use of the word confuses you?
  • Do new words stick better from songs, shows, or conversations?

Hard to explain (説明しにくい言葉)

WARM-UP 2Which is harder to translate: a joke, a feeling, or a polite phrase?冗談、気持ち、丁寧な表現の中で、いちばん訳しにくいのはどれですか？

Follow up
  • Which part depends on the situation?
  • Which is easier to translate: facts or feelings?

Missing in English (英語にない表現)

QUESTION 1What's a Japanese expression you wish English had?英語にもあればいいのにと思う日本語の表現は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When do you use it?
  • What does a direct translation miss?

What it really means (本当の意味)

QUESTION 2Which Japanese word needs a full English sentence to explain?英語では一文使わないと説明しにくい日本語は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When does the word feel natural?
  • Which is harder to explain in English: a food name or a feeling?

A strange translation (不自然な翻訳)

QUESTION 3What's a translation that sounded funny or wrong to you?面白く聞こえたり、不自然に聞こえたりした翻訳は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Where did you see or hear it?
  • What do you think it was meant to say?

Friends and work (友人と職場)

QUESTION 4What's a word you use differently with friends and at work?友人に対してと職場で、使い方が変わる言葉は何ですか？

Follow up
  • How does it change?
  • Which version feels more natural to you?

A funny mistake (笑ってしまう翻訳ミス)

QUESTION 5What kind of translation mistake can make a serious scene funny?どんな翻訳ミスで、真面目な場面が面白くなってしまいますか？

Follow up
  • Can a word-for-word translation cause it?
  • Have subtitles ever made you laugh?

Meaning or feeling? (意味か感覚か)

QUESTION 6Which matters more in translation: exact meaning or the same feeling?翻訳では、正確な意味と同じ感覚のどちらが大切ですか？

Follow up
  • What should a translator never lose?
  • How could they keep both?
