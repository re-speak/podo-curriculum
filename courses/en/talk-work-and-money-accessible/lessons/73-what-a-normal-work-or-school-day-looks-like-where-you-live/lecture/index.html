Official time and real time (決まった時刻と実際の時刻)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Official finishing time and real finishing time can differ.決まった終了時刻と実際に終わる時刻は、違うことがあります。
The pattern varies by role, place, day, and season.終わる時間は、役割、場所、曜日、季節によって変わります。
Some people do not have a fixed work or school day.決まった仕事や学校の一日がない人もいます。
The choice to leave on time can be normal in one place and difficult in another.定時に帰ることが普通の場所も、難しい場所もあります。leave on time定時に帰る
Staying late does not always mean more useful work.遅くまでいることが、必ずしも役立つ仕事の多さを意味しません。
Early departure may be possible because work is flexible.柔軟な働き方によって、早く帰れることがあります。
A local pattern should not be treated as true for everyone.地域の一つの習慣を、全員に当てはめるべきではありません。
The journey home can make the real day longer.帰宅までの移動で、実際の一日が長くなることがあります。
Predictable endings help people plan the rest of life.終わる時間が予測できると、その後の生活を計画しやすくなります。
A fair system makes exceptions visible rather than permanent.例外的な延長と、毎日の暗黙の残業は違います。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

When the day really ends (実際に終わる時間)

WARM-UP 1What time do people usually finish work or school where you live?住んでいる場所では、人は普通何時に仕事や学校を終えますか？

Follow up
  • Is there one common time or wide variation?
  • Which group or setting does your example describe?

Expected or unusual (普通か例外か)

WARM-UP 2How different are official and real finishing times where you live?住んでいる場所では、公式の終了時刻と実際の時刻はどのくらい違いますか？

Follow up
  • Is there an official time to compare with it?
  • What signals the end?

Leaving before others (先に帰ること)

QUESTION 1How are people usually treated when they finish or leave before others?ほかの人より先に仕事や勉強を終えて帰る人は、普通どのように受け止められますか？

Follow up
  • Is the reaction supportive, neutral, mixed, or negative?
  • Does the person's role change the reaction?

A local expression (地域の呼び方)

QUESTION 2How does your language describe someone who always works or studies late?いつも遅くまで働いたり勉強したりする人を、あなたの言語ではどう表しますか？

Follow up
  • Is it praise, criticism, or neutral?
  • How is the pattern described instead?

My reaction and example (先生の反応と例)

QUESTION 3I'll share a real finishing-time pattern from my experience. What seems surprising or familiar about it?私の経験から、実際の終了時刻のパターンを一つ話します。どこが意外で、どこが身近に感じますか？

Follow up
  • What would you like to ask about my example?
  • Which detail seems most different from your setting?

Compare the two examples (二つの例を比べる)

QUESTION 4How can finishing times differ across two places you know about?知っている二つの場所で、終了時刻はどう異なり得ますか？

Follow up
  • What is one clear similarity?
  • What is one difference, if any?

A fairer ending (より公平な終わり方)

QUESTION 5What one change could make finishing times more predictable or fair?終了時刻をより予測しやすく、公平にする変更を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would need to support it?
  • What exception would still be reasonable?

A good end to the day (よい一日の終わり)

QUESTION 6Besides the time, what helps a work or school day end well?時刻以外に、仕事や学校の一日をよく終えるために何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Is it a clear handover, a finished task, or something else?
  • What can prevent unfinished work from following someone home?
