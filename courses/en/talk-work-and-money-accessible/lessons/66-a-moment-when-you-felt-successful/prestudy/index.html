A moment when you felt successful (うまくいっていると感じた瞬間)

Let's talk about ordinary moments that can feel like success. Please read the title aloud.成功のように感じられる日常の瞬間について話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Success is often a moment, not a definition (成功は定義より一つの瞬間)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A small success can matter before anyone else notices it.小さな成功でも、周りが気づく前から大切な意味を持つことがあります。
Success may feel like relief, confidence, or quiet satisfaction.成功は、安心、自信、静かな満足として感じられることがあります。
The scene makes the word success more specific.具体的な場面があると、成功という言葉が分かりやすくなります。
One solved problem can make a whole day feel different.一つの問題が解決すると、一日全体の感じ方が変わることがあります。
Other people may be present without causing the feeling.周りに人がいても、その人たちが成功の感覚を決めるとは限りません。
A successful feeling may last minutes or change later.成功した気持ちは数分だけ続き、あとで変わることもあります。
Comparing several small successes can reveal what progress means to a person.いくつかの小さな成功を比べると、その人にとっての進歩が見えてきます。small success小さな成功
What counts as success can change with circumstances.何を成功と考えるかは、状況によって変わります。
A harder goal is not automatically a more meaningful success.難しい目標ほど意味のある成功になるとは限りません。
Naming one moment can reveal a practical definition of success.一つの瞬間を挙げると、自分にとっての成功が具体的に見えます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One successful moment (うまくいった瞬間)

WARM-UP 1Which small success feels best: arriving on time, cooking well, or finishing a task?時間どおりに着くこと、上手に料理すること、作業を終えることでは、どの小さな成功がいちばんうれしいですか？

Follow up
  • What was happening in that moment?
  • What made it count as a small success?

What was happening (何が起きていたか)

WARM-UP 2What small action can lead to a successful moment?どんな小さな行動が、成功した瞬間につながりますか？

Follow up
  • What problem could that action solve?
  • Would planning, practice, or asking for help come first?

Who was there (そこにいた人)

QUESTION 1Does a successful moment matter more when another person sees it?成功した瞬間は、誰かに見てもらうと、より大切に感じられますか？

Follow up
  • What might the other person notice?
  • How could they show that they noticed?

How it felt (どう感じたか)

QUESTION 2Does success feel more like excitement, relief, confidence, or calm to you?成功は、興奮、安心、自信、落ち着きのどれに近く感じますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that feeling fit?
  • Could another success feel different?

Did it last (続いたか)

QUESTION 3How long does a feeling of success usually last?成功した感覚は、普通どのくらい続きますか？

Follow up
  • What kept it going?
  • What remained important?

What would count now (今の成功)

QUESTION 4What would count as a small success for you or someone like you now?今の自分や似た状況の人にとって、小さな成功になるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why would it matter now?
  • What small sign would show progress?

A changing standard (変わる基準)

QUESTION 5How can a person's idea of success change over time?成功についての考えは、時間とともにどう変わり得ますか？

Follow up
  • What mattered before but matters less now?
  • What still matters?

Someone else's success (他の人の成功)

QUESTION 6How can you recognize someone else's small success without comparing it with yours?自分と比べずに、ほかの人の小さな成功をどう認められますか？

Follow up
  • What effort could you mention?
  • What comparison should you avoid?
