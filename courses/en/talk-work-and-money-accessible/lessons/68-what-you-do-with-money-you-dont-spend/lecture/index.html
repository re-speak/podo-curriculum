WORK AND MONEY

What you do with money you don't spend (使わなかったお金でしていること)

Let's talk about what money can do besides pay for things. Please read the title aloud.物を買う以外に、お金にできることについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Money can serve more than one purpose (お金には複数の役割がある)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
People may save, share, invest, repay debt, or simply keep money available.お金は、貯める、分ける、投資する、借金を返す、使える状態で持つなどに使われます。
Not everyone has money left after essential costs.必要な支出のあとにお金が残らない人もいます。
People may set aside a small amount to create flexibility later.少額でも取っておくと、あとで選べる余地が生まれます。set aside取っておく
Giving money can express care, duty, or shared responsibility.お金を渡すことは、思いやり、義務、共同の責任を表す場合があります。
A good choice depends on a person's circumstances.よい選択は、その人の状況によって異なります。
People can compare money choices by purpose instead of exact amounts.正確な金額ではなく、目的に注目してお金の選択を比べることができます。
Advice that fits one person may be unsuitable for another.一人に合う助言が、別の人にも合うとは限りません。
Dividing a sample amount among several goals can make priorities clearer.一定のお金をいくつかの目的に分けると、優先順位が明確になります。
Priorities can change after a new responsibility or loss.新しい責任や損失があると、お金の優先順位が変わることがあります。
The purpose of money may matter more than the method.お金の扱い方より、その目的のほうが重要なことがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Beyond spending (使う以外)

WARM-UP 1Besides spending it, what can people do with money?使う以外に、人はお金をどう扱えますか？

Follow up
  • Does it happen regularly or only sometimes?
  • Which future option would matter most?

One purpose (一つの目的)

WARM-UP 2Which non-spending use of money matters most?使う以外のお金の使い方で、最も大切なのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What need does it serve?
  • Is it for now, later, or both?

When it began (始めた時期)

QUESTION 1What usually helps someone begin setting money aside?お金を取っておき始めるのに、普通何が役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What can prompt the first step?
  • What needs to be true before it feels realistic?

How it is going (今の状況)

QUESTION 2What makes a saving or investment approach workable?貯蓄や投資の方法が無理なく続くのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What has helped so far?
  • What has made it difficult?

Where the idea came from (考えのきっかけ)

QUESTION 3What most influences how people handle money they do not spend?人が使わないお金をどう扱うかに、最も影響するのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What part did you accept?
  • How did the approach develop?

Would it suit someone else (人に合うか)

QUESTION 4Which money habits should be recommended only in some situations?どのお金の習慣は、限られた状況でだけ勧めるべきですか？

Follow up
  • What makes it suitable?
  • What risk or limit leads you to reject it?

A financial boundary (しないこと)

QUESTION 5Which financial action deserves the most caution?どの金融行動に、最も注意が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which value or risk creates that boundary?
  • What safeguard would make the action less risky?

More than money (お金以外)

QUESTION 6What important kind of support cannot be replaced by money?お金では代えられない大切な支援には何がありますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that support matter?
  • Can money still make it easier to provide?
