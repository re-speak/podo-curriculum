The work behind the results (結果を支える仕事)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Any two-hour window can reveal how a daily routine is really organised.どの2時間を選んでも、日常が実際にどう組み立てられているかが見えてきます。
Behind the scenes, people prepare, check, coordinate, and repair.表に見えないところで、人は準備、確認、調整、修復をしています。behind the scenes表に見えないところで
Care and domestic work can also be invisible.ケアや家の仕事も、見えにくいことがあります。
A smooth result can hide the effort that prevented problems.順調な結果の裏には、問題を防いだ努力が隠れていることがあります。
Invisible work may require judgment rather than many actions.見えない仕事には、多くの動作より判断が必要な場合があります。
Privacy may limit how much detail can be shared.プライバシーのため、詳しく話せない場合があります。
Recognition should not create more reporting work.見えない仕事を認めるために、報告の負担を増やすべきではありません。
Different roles hide different forms of effort.役割によって、見えにくい努力の形は異なります。
A fair handover names unfinished hidden work.公平な引き継ぎでは、終わっていない見えない仕事も伝えます。
Understanding hidden work can change how a result is valued.見えない仕事を知ると、結果の評価が変わることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The last two hours (直近の二時間)

WARM-UP 1Think about a recent two-hour stretch. What filled the time?最近の二時間を思い出してください。その時間を何に使いましたか？

Follow up
  • Which activity would have been visible to an observer?
  • What made that period typical or unusual?

Where the time goes (時間がかかること)

WARM-UP 2Which part of your day takes more time than other people might expect?一日の中で、ほかの人が思うより時間のかかることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which activity usually sets the order of everything else?
  • Which interruption usually changes that order?

The best part (いちばん好きな部分)

QUESTION 1Which part of your routine feels most satisfying?日課の中で、最も満足感があることは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What makes that part rewarding?
  • Would it still feel satisfying if you did more of it?

What drains your energy (エネルギーを使うこと)

QUESTION 2Which part of the day takes the most energy out of you?一日の中で、最もエネルギーを使うことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Is the strain physical, mental, or social?
  • What change would make it easier?

A small task that grows (大きくなる小さな作業)

QUESTION 3What's one small task that creates a surprising amount of extra work?小さな作業なのに、驚くほど多くの追加の仕事を生むものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why do other people not notice the work?
  • Which problem does that hidden work prevent?

Remove one task (一つなくす)

QUESTION 4If you could remove or shorten one task, which would you choose?一つの作業をなくすか短くできるなら、どれを選びますか？

Follow up
  • What would become easier in your routine if you removed or shortened it?
  • Who else might notice the change?

A smooth busy day (忙しい一日を順調に)

QUESTION 5What preparation helps the rest of a busy day run better?どんな準備をすると、忙しい一日の残りがうまく進みますか？

Follow up
  • When does the preparation happen?
  • Which problem does it prevent?

Swap routines for a day (一日日課を交換する)

QUESTION 6What would surprise someone who swapped routines with you for a day?あなたと一日だけ日課を交換した人は、何に驚くと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Which part would challenge them most?
  • What might they understand better afterward?
