Why repeated advice stays with us (繰り返された助言が残る理由)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Adults often repeat advice because they want to protect a child.大人は子どもを守りたくて、同じ助言を繰り返すことがあります。
A child may hear the words long before understanding the reason.子どもは理由を理解するよりずっと前に、その言葉を聞いていることがあります。
Some advice is practical, such as checking the road or saving money.道路を確認することや貯金など、実用的な助言もあります。
Other advice reflects the adult's own fears and experiences.別の助言には、大人自身の不安や経験が表れています。
Repeated words can feel like nagging when the reason is unclear.理由が分からないと、繰り返す言葉は口うるさく感じられます。nagging小言
People sometimes ignore good advice simply because they hear it too often.よい助言でも、何度も聞くため無視してしまうことがあります。
Its meaning may become clear only after a similar mistake.同じような失敗をして初めて、意味が分かることもあります。
Some people later repeat the same words to someone younger.後になって、同じ言葉を年下の人に言う人もいます。
Other advice deserves to be questioned or left behind.一方で、疑ったり手放したりしたほうがよい助言もあります。
Remembering the words lets us decide what to keep and what to change.その言葉を思い出すと、残すものと変えるものを選べます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The repeated line (何度も聞いた言葉)

WARM-UP 1What advice do adults often repeat to children?子どもに大人がよく繰り返す助言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who usually gives that advice?
  • What makes the line easy to remember?

The exact words (実際の言葉)

WARM-UP 2How is that advice usually worded?その助言は、ふつうどんな言い方をしますか？

Follow up
  • Was it short or explained?
  • Which word made it memorable?

Did it land? (聞き入れたか)

QUESTION 1Did the child in your example follow the advice, resist it, or react another way?その例の子どもは、その助言に従いましたか、反発しましたか、それとも別の反応をしましたか？

Follow up
  • What did the child do next?
  • How did that reaction shape the advice?

Why repeat it? (繰り返す理由)

QUESTION 2Did worry, experience, or tradition make the adult keep saying it?心配、経験、または習慣から、大人はそれを言い続けたのでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Which source best explains the repetition?
  • Was the reason clear at the time?

Were they right? (正しかったか)

QUESTION 3Looking back, was the advice useful, partly useful, or wrong?振り返ると、その助言は役立ちましたか、一部だけ役立ちましたか、それとも間違っていましたか？

Follow up
  • What changed your view?
  • Which part would you rewrite?

Passing it on (受け継ぐ言葉)

QUESTION 4Would you pass that advice on, change it, or stop repeating it?その助言を伝えますか、変えますか、それとも繰り返すのをやめますか？

Follow up
  • How would you explain it to a child?
  • Which words would you change?

Your own reminder (自分なら何を言うか)

QUESTION 5Which reminder matters enough for adults to repeat to children?大人が子どもに繰り返す価値のある注意は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does that reminder matter?
  • How can an adult say it without nagging?

Advice for adults (大人への助言)

QUESTION 6What advice do adults need to hear more often?大人がもっと聞く必要のある助言は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who could say it convincingly?
  • Why is it easy to ignore?
