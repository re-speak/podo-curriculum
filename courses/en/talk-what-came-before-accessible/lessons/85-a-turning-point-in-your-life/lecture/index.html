Family traditions do not stay still (家族の習慣も変わっていく)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Every New Year's Day, Emi's family used to meet at her grandparents' house.毎年元日、エミの家族は祖父母の家に集まっていました。
Her grandmother cooked the same soup, and everyone sat around one crowded table.祖母がいつも同じ汁物を作り、全員が一つのにぎやかな食卓を囲みました。
As the children grew up, some relatives moved away and the gathering became smaller.子どもたちが成長すると、親族の中には遠くへ引っ越す人もいて、集まりは小さくなりました。
The family now meets on a different day, and several people join by video.今では別の日に集まり、何人かはビデオ通話で参加します。
The tradition has changed, but the soup and one old family story remain.習慣は変わりましたが、その汁物と昔からの家族の話は残っています。
Traditions often depend on one person who plans, cooks, invites people, or remembers the details.家族の習慣は、計画したり、料理したり、人を招いたり、細かなことを覚えたりする一人の人に支えられていることがよくあります。
When that person stops, another relative may continue the tradition in a new way.その人がやめたとき、別の家族が新しい形で習慣を続けることがあります。
Not every part needs to continue; some parts may cost too much, take too much work, or feel unfair.すべての部分を残す必要はなく、お金や手間がかかりすぎたり、不公平だったりする部分もあります。
A smaller meal or a shorter visit can keep the meaning without keeping every old rule.食事を小さくしたり訪問を短くしたりしても、昔の決まりをすべて守らずに意味を残せます。
A new tradition becomes meaningful when people want to do it again, not simply because it is repeated.新しい習慣は、ただ繰り返すからではなく、人がまたやりたいと思うことで大切なものになります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A clear memory (はっきり覚えている習慣)

WARM-UP 1Which family tradition do you remember most clearly?最もはっきり覚えている家族の習慣は何ですか？

Follow up
  • When did it happen?
  • Would birthdays or holiday meals count?

One special detail (特別だった小さなこと)

WARM-UP 2What small detail made that tradition feel special?その習慣を特別にしていた小さなことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Was it a food, place, or phrase?
  • Who noticed that detail most?

Who kept it going (続けていた人)

QUESTION 1Who kept the tradition going?その習慣を続けていたのは誰ですか？

Follow up
  • What did that person do?
  • Did anyone else take over later?

How it changed (変わった形)

QUESTION 2How did the tradition change as people got older or moved away?家族が年を重ねたり離れて暮らしたりする中で、その習慣はどう変わりましたか？

Follow up
  • What became harder?
  • What stayed the same?

Worth keeping (残す価値のある部分)

QUESTION 3Which part of an old family tradition is worth keeping?昔からの家族の習慣で、残す価値があるのはどの部分ですか？

Follow up
  • Why does it still matter?
  • Could it work in a smaller form?

Ready to change (変えたい部分)

QUESTION 4What part would you happily change or stop?喜んで変えたり、やめたりしたい部分は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why no longer keep it?
  • Who might disagree?

A new tradition (新しい家族の習慣)

QUESTION 5Can a new family tradition become meaningful quickly?新しい家族の習慣でも、すぐに大切なものになれますか？

Follow up
  • What would help it last?
  • Does it need to happen every year?

A future tradition (これからの習慣)

QUESTION 6What tradition would you like to start for the future?これから始めたい習慣は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would you include?
  • What would make it easy to continue?
