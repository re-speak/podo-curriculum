What a phone-free week would test (携帯電話なしの一週間で試されること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A digital detox means choosing a period with less optional device use.デジタルデトックスとは、任意のデジタル機器の利用を減らす期間を選ぶことです。digital detoxデジタル機器から離れること
For some people, a phone is optional entertainment.人によっては、携帯電話は主に娯楽の道具です。
For others, it is an access, safety, work, or care tool.別の人には、アクセス、安全、仕事、ケアのための道具です。
In this topic, what breaks first means what stops working first.この話で「最初に駄目になるもの」とは、最初に機能しなくなるものを指します。
Maps, payments, tickets, and messages have moved into one device.地図、支払い、切符、メッセージが一つの機器に集まっています。
Missing the phone is not the same as missing every app.携帯電話が恋しいことと、すべてのアプリが必要なことは同じではありません。
Less interruption could improve focus or rest.中断が減ると、集中や休息がよくなるかもしれません。
A week without a phone may require another device or another person.携帯電話なしの一週間には、別の機器や人の助けが必要かもしれません。
Time offline can reveal which phone functions are truly important.携帯電話を使わない時間があると、本当に大切な機能が分かります。
A useful experiment removes what is optional and protects what is essential.よい実験は、不要なものを減らし、必要なものを守ります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Seven days (七日間)

WARM-UP 1Which part of a week with no optional phone use would be hardest?携帯電話の任意の利用をやめた一週間で、最も難しい部分は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which routine would need a new plan?
  • What could make that challenge manageable?

What stops working (最初に機能しなくなること)

WARM-UP 2Which daily routine would be most disrupted by less phone use?携帯電話の利用を減らすと、どの日課が最も影響を受けますか？

Follow up
  • Is it essential or only convenient?
  • What keeps the experiment bounded?

What you would miss (恋しくなるもの)

QUESTION 1Which useful phone function would be hardest to replace for a week?一週間、代わりを用意するのが最も難しい携帯電話の機能は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What need does that function meet?
  • What non-phone alternative could meet part of the need?

What might improve (よくなりそうなこと)

QUESTION 2What might improve during the week?その一週間で何がよくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the day might feel calmer?
  • What evidence would show a real improvement?

A past break (過去に離れた時間)

QUESTION 3When is the easiest time to put a phone away for a while?しばらく携帯電話を置いておくのに、最も簡単な時間はいつですか？

Follow up
  • What makes that time suitable for a break?
  • How long would the break need to be useful?

Making it work (実行するための支え)

QUESTION 4What support would make a reduced-phone week safe and practical?携帯電話を減らす一週間を安全で現実的にするには、どんな支えが必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Which tool or person could provide that support?
  • How would it protect essential access?

One use to keep (残す一つの使い方)

QUESTION 5Which phone function should remain available during a reduced-use week?携帯電話の利用を減らす一週間でも、どの機能は使えるようにしておくべきですか？

Follow up
  • Why should that function remain available?
  • Which optional use could be limited around it?

After the week (一週間のあと)

QUESTION 6Which phone habit would be most useful to redesign?どの携帯電話の習慣を見直すと、最も役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • What current habit works well?
  • What small change could last?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
