The superpower you would choose (選びたい超能力)

Let's choose a superpower and examine its real consequences. Please read the title aloud.超能力を一つ選び、実際にどんな結果が生まれるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Every power changes the problem (どの力も問題を変える)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A superpower is attractive because it removes an ordinary limit.超能力は、普通の制限をなくすので魅力的です。superpower超能力
The first use often reveals the real reason for choosing it.最初の使い方には、その力を選ぶ本当の理由が表れます。
A useful power can still create a difficult responsibility.役立つ力でも、難しい責任が生まれることがあります。
Keeping it secret protects privacy but limits help.秘密にすると自分を守れますが、助けられる範囲が狭くなります。
Telling people invites trust, pressure, and demands.人に話すと、信頼だけでなく圧力や要求も生まれます。
Every advantage has a downside when it is always available.いつでも使える利点には、必ず困る面もあります。
Refusing a power can be a reasonable choice.力を選ばないことも、もっともな選択です。
The power most people choose may reflect a shared frustration.多くの人が選ぶ力には、共通の不満が表れるかもしれません。
A power can solve one problem while moving another elsewhere.一つの問題を解決しても、別の問題が生まれることがあります。
The best choice depends on the limits placed on the power.いちばんよい力は、その力の制限によって変わります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A power for ordinary life (日常生活のための力)

WARM-UP 1Which superpower would make ordinary life most interesting?どの超能力が普通の生活を最も面白くしますか？

Follow up
  • Would flying, stopping time, or reading minds improve daily life most?
  • What problem could that power create?

The first use (最初の使い方)

WARM-UP 2If you got a superpower, what would you do on the first morning?超能力を得たら、最初の朝に何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Would you test it alone or use it to help someone?
  • What could go wrong?

Telling people (人に話すか)

QUESTION 1Would you tell someone close to you about a superpower?親しい人に超能力のことを話しますか？

Follow up
  • Would telling one person make the power safer?
  • What could secrecy protect?

The downside (困る点)

QUESTION 2Which superpower would be annoying in daily life?日常生活では面倒になりそうな超能力は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would reading minds or hearing everything become annoying?
  • Could you switch it off?

A power to refuse (断りたい力)

QUESTION 3Which useful-looking superpower would you refuse?役立ちそうでも断りたい超能力は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would reading minds or never sleeping be too costly?
  • Could a safe limit change your answer?

If everyone had it (みんなが持っていたら)

QUESTION 4Which superpower would cause the most trouble if everyone had it?全員が持つと最も大きな問題を起こす超能力は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Why would that power become dangerous on a large scale?
  • Would stopping time or reading minds cause more trouble?

One rule (一つのルール)

QUESTION 5What one rule would you set for using a superpower?超能力を使うために、どんなルールを一つ決めますか？

Follow up
  • What temptation would the rule control?
  • Should a superpower ever be used without permission?

Ordinary skill (普通の能力)

QUESTION 6What ordinary skill is better than a superpower in real life?現実では、どんな普通の能力が超能力より役立ちますか？

Follow up
  • Would patience, clear communication, or first aid be a good example?
  • How can someone improve that skill?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
