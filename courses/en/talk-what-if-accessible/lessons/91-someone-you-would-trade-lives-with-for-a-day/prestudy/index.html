WHAT IF

Someone you would trade lives with for a day (一日だけ人生を入れ替えたい人)

Let's explore what one day in another life could teach us. Please read the title aloud.別の人生を一日体験すると何が分かるか考えましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What trading lives would reveal (人生を入れ替えると見えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
To trade lives is to borrow another person's ordinary day.人生を入れ替えるとは、別の人の普通の一日を借りることです。trade lives人生を入れ替える
A famous person is only one possible choice.有名人だけが選択肢ではありません。
A fictional or anonymous person protects privacy.架空の人や匿名の人なら、プライバシーを守れます。
Curiosity may focus on freedom, pressure, skill, or routine.興味は、自由、プレッシャー、能力、日課に向くかもしれません。
One day is long enough to notice limits but not understand a life.一日あれば制限には気づけますが、その人生全体は理解できません。
Using the day for fun is different from using it to learn.楽しむために一日を使うのと、学ぶために使うのは違います。
Private information should remain private even in a fantasy.想像の中でも、個人的な情報は守るべきです。
Telling the person afterward could change the meaning of the day.あとで本人に話すと、その一日の意味が変わるかもしれません。
Wanting your own life back does not make the experiment pointless.自分の人生に戻りたいと思っても、その体験が無意味になるわけではありません。
The choice says as much about our questions as about the other person.誰を選ぶかは、その人だけでなく自分の疑問も表します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Whose day (誰の一日)

WARM-UP 1Whose daily life would you be curious to experience for one day?誰の日常生活を一日体験してみたいですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of that day would you most want to see?
  • What might surprise you about the routine?

Why that life (その人生を選ぶ理由)

WARM-UP 2What can make another person's daily life worth experiencing for one day?別の人の日常を一日体験する価値が生まれるのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which experience would become available?
  • Which pressure would you want to understand?

Using the day (一日の使い方)

QUESTION 1What would be the best use of the first hour in another person's life?別の人の人生を体験する最初の一時間を、どう使うのがよいですか？

Follow up
  • Would the first action be fun or useful?
  • What would you avoid doing with someone else's life?

What to learn (知りたいこと)

QUESTION 2What question could one day in another life help answer?別の人生を一日体験すると、どんな疑問の答えが分かりますか？

Follow up
  • Could one day really answer it?
  • What evidence would be fair to use?

The unexpected part (意外な部分)

QUESTION 3What part of another person's daily life might be harder than it looks?別の人の日常で、外から見るより大変そうな部分は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which hidden responsibility might appear?
  • What might be easier than expected?

Telling them (本人に伝えるか)

QUESTION 4Which lesson from a one-day life exchange would be appropriate to share afterward?一日の人生交換から得たどんな学びなら、その後、話してもよいですか？

Follow up
  • How could you describe the lesson without sharing private details?
  • Who might find the lesson useful?

Coming back (戻ること)

QUESTION 5What might make someone value their own ordinary life after a one-day exchange?一日の人生交換の後、自分の普通の生活を大切に感じるのはどんなことですか？

Follow up
  • What would you value more?
  • Which part of the borrowed life would be hard to leave?

Someone choosing you (自分が選ばれるなら)

QUESTION 6What could another person learn by experiencing one ordinary day in your life?別の人があなたの普通の一日を体験すると、何を学べますか？

Follow up
  • Which routine might still surprise them?
  • What would they misunderstand after only one day?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
