WHAT IF

What you would really do if you won the lottery (宝くじに当たったら本当にすること)

Let's imagine how a large lottery win could change everyday life. Please read the title aloud.宝くじで大当たりしたら、日常がどう変わるか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What a sudden win reveals (突然の大当たりで見えること)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A jackpot creates choices before it creates a plan.大当たりすると、計画ができる前に多くの選択が生まれます。jackpot大当たり
The first action often shows what feels urgent now.最初にすることには、今いちばん急いで解決したいことが表れます。
Some people would tell others; some would stay quiet.すぐ誰かに話す人もいれば、黙っている人もいます。
More money does not decide whether work still matters.お金が増えても、仕事がまだ大切かどうかは自動的には決まりません。
Buying something first is only one possible answer.最初に何かを買うことだけが答えではありません。
A fast decision can feel different after the excitement fades.興奮が落ち着くと、急いで決めたことの見え方が変わる場合があります。
Not everyone would regret the same kind of choice.何を後悔するかは人によって違います。
A new normal could arrive quickly or take years.新しい生活がすぐ普通になる人も、何年も落ち着かない人もいます。
Money can remove limits without choosing a direction.お金は制限を減らせても、進む方向までは選んでくれません。
The useful question is what kind of life the win should support.大切なのは、そのお金でどんな生活を支えたいかという問いです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

The first move (最初の行動)

WARM-UP 1Imagine you win a large lottery prize tomorrow. What would you do first?明日、宝くじで大当たりしたと想像してください。最初に何をしますか？

Follow up
  • Would you call someone, make a plan, or do nothing for a day?
  • What would you avoid doing immediately?

The first call (最初に電話する相手)

WARM-UP 2Whose advice, if anyone, would you want before making a big decision?大きな決断をする前に、相談したい相手はいますか？

Follow up
  • What could that person help you notice?
  • If you asked no one, how would you slow the decision down?

Who knows (誰に知らせるか)

QUESTION 1Who, if anyone, would need to know about the win?当選について、知らせる必要がある人がいるとすれば誰ですか？

Follow up
  • Who would have a practical reason to know?
  • What benefit could privacy provide?

What stays the same (変えずに残すもの)

QUESTION 2What part of your life would you want to keep exactly the same?当選しても、今の生活のどんな部分をまったく変えずに残したいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you keep your home, routine, or hobbies?
  • What might money change anyway?

Buying for someone else (誰かのために買う物)

QUESTION 3What's the first thing you would buy for someone else?ほかの人のために最初に買うものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a gift, a trip, or practical help?
  • If you bought nothing, how else could you share the money?

An easy mistake (起こしやすい失敗)

QUESTION 4What’s the easiest mistake for a lottery winner to make?宝くじに当たった人が、最もしやすい失敗は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Spending too fast or trusting the wrong person?
  • What would help someone avoid it?

Keeping life normal (日常を変えないこと)

QUESTION 5What would you want to keep normal after becoming rich?お金持ちになっても、何を普通のままにしておきたいですか？

Follow up
  • Why is that part worth protecting?
  • Would sudden wealth make ordinary friendships harder?

Money’s limit (お金の限界)

QUESTION 6What’s one problem that money could make easier but not solve?お金で楽にはできても、解決はできない問題を一つ挙げるとしたら何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would loneliness or poor health be a good example?
  • What else would help?

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
