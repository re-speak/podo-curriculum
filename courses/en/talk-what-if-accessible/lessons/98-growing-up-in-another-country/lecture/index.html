WHAT IF

Growing up in another country (別の国で育っていたら)

Let's imagine how a different upbringing could shape a person. Please read the title aloud.別の環境で育つと、人がどう変わるか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

How upbringing shapes possibilities (育った環境が可能性を形作る方法)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Upbringing means the environment and experiences that shape someone while growing up.育った環境とは、成長中に人を形作る環境や経験です。upbringing育った環境
A country is not one single childhood.一つの国に、同じ子ども時代が一つだけあるわけではありません。
The thought experiment needs a specific setting, not a stereotype.この想像には、固定観念ではなく具体的な環境が必要です。
Language could change how relationships and ideas are expressed.別の言語で育つと、人間関係や考えの表し方が変わるかもしれません。
School systems can reward different skills and behaviour.学校制度によって、評価される能力や行動が違います。
Family and community still matter inside any country.どの国でも、家族や地域の影響は残ります。
A different upbringing could change habits without changing every value.育った環境が違えば習慣は変わっても、すべての価値観が変わるとは限りません。
Missing the place where we actually grew up can be complex.実際に育った場所を恋しく思う気持ちは、単純ではありません。
Growing up abroad would shape a person differently from moving abroad as an adult.海外で育つことは、大人になってから海外へ移ることとは違う形で人をつくります。
The exercise shows which parts of identity feel shaped and which feel chosen.この想像から、自分のどの部分が環境で作られ、どの部分を選んだと感じるかが分かります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Another country (別の国)

WARM-UP 1Which country would offer the most interesting different upbringing?どの国で育つと、最も興味深い別の成長環境になりますか？

Follow up
  • Which specific part of the upbringing interests you?
  • What would you need to learn before making predictions?

Why there (そこを選ぶ理由)

WARM-UP 2Which feature makes a different place interesting for childhood?別の場所で子ども時代を過ごすことを面白くする特徴は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which opportunity or experience matters?
  • What do you not know enough about yet?

A different you (違う自分)

QUESTION 1How can growing up in another country shape a person's identity?別の国で育つと、人のアイデンティティはどう形作られますか？

Follow up
  • Which habit might change?
  • Which value might stay the same?

What you would miss (実際の育ちから恋しくなるもの)

QUESTION 2What might you miss about where you actually grew up?実際に育った場所について、何が恋しくなりそうですか？

Follow up
  • Which familiar routine might still matter?
  • Would you miss a person, place, language, or feeling?

What you would not miss (恋しくならないもの)

QUESTION 3What part of your actual upbringing might you not miss?実際に育った環境のどの部分なら、恋しくならないかもしれませんか？

Follow up
  • What do you value about it?
  • What might replace it?

Language and belonging (言語と所属感)

QUESTION 4How can the language of childhood shape expression and belonging?子ども時代の言語は、表現や所属感をどう形作りますか？

Follow up
  • What else would shape belonging?
  • What might become easier or harder to express?

Growing up versus moving (育つことと今移ること)

QUESTION 5Why can imagining a childhood abroad feel different from moving abroad now?海外で育つ想像と、今海外へ移ることが違って感じられるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • What makes the present move different?
  • Which non-private condition matters most?

What stays yours (変わらず自分のもの)

QUESTION 6What part of you do you think would stay the same anywhere?どこで育っても、自分のどの部分は同じだと思いますか？

Follow up
  • Why does that feel less shaped by place?
  • Could another environment still change how it appears?
