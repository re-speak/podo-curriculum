WHAT IF

What you would do if AI did your job (AIが自分の仕事をしたらすること)

Let's imagine how we would use our time if AI handled routine tasks. Please read the title aloud.AIが日常の作業をしたら、時間をどう使うか想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

When the task disappears but income remains (作業がなくなり収入が残るとき)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
In this scenario, AI handles routine tasks while resources remain stable.この想像では、生活の条件が安定したまま、AIが日常の作業を行います。
Daily roles include paid work, study, care, and unpaid contribution.日々の役割には、有給の仕事、勉強、ケア、無給の貢献があります。
The first free Monday might feel like a holiday.最初の自由な月曜日は、休日のように感じるかもしれません。
After a month, the missing routine may matter more.一か月後には、なくなった日課のほうが気になるかもしれません。
A work identity is the part of self-understanding connected to work.仕事を通じた自己認識とは、仕事と結びついた自分の見方です。work identity仕事を通じた自己認識
Not everyone builds identity through paid work.誰もが有給の仕事から自分らしさを得るわけではありません。
Telling people about the arrangement could invite envy or doubt.その状況を人に話すと、うらやましさや疑いが生まれるかもしれません。
Starting something new gives free time a direction.新しいことを始めると、自由な時間に方向ができます。
Doing the work yourself again may depend on meaning rather than money.また自分で仕事をする理由は、お金より意味かもしれません。
The question separates what we do from why it matters.この問いは、何をするかと、なぜ大切かを分けます。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

A week of extra time (自由な時間が増える一週間)

WARM-UP 1If AI did your usual tasks for a week, how would you use the extra time?AIが普段の作業を一週間してくれたら、空いた時間をどう使いますか？

Follow up
  • Would you rest, see people, or start a project?
  • Would the extra time feel relaxing or strange at first?

Giving free time some shape (自由時間に形を与えること)

WARM-UP 2How would you give a free week some structure without filling it with work?仕事で埋めずに、自由な一週間をどう組み立てますか？

Follow up
  • Would you make a plan or decide each morning?
  • What would you leave unplanned?

Saying AI did the work (AIがしたと伝えること)

QUESTION 1Who deserves to know when AI produced most of a result?AIが結果の大部分を作ったとき、誰に知らせる必要がありますか？

Follow up
  • Would a customer, teacher, or teammate need to know?
  • When would saying nothing be unfair?

What you would miss (恋しくなること)

QUESTION 2Which part of work, study, care, or daily routine would you miss?仕事、勉強、ケア、日課のどの部分が恋しくなりますか？

Follow up
  • Would you miss the people, the routine, or finishing something?
  • What would you happily stop doing?

A project to begin (始めたいプロジェクト)

QUESTION 3What project would you start with a month of free time?一か月の自由な時間があれば、どんなプロジェクトを始めますか？

Follow up
  • Would you learn something, make something, or travel?
  • How would you use the first free week?

A task with purpose (意味を感じる日課)

QUESTION 4Which daily task can make a person feel useful?どんな日々の作業が、その人自身を役立っていると感じさせますか？

Follow up
  • Could cooking, caring for someone, or fixing something do that?
  • Could AI help without taking over?

A task you would keep (自分で続けたいこと)

QUESTION 5What task would you still choose to do yourself if AI could do it?AIにできても、自分で続けたい作業は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Would you still cook, write, or make important decisions yourself?
  • When could AI still help?

A human decision (人が決めること)

QUESTION 6What should never be handed over completely to AI?AIに完全には任せるべきでないことは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Medical decisions, legal decisions, or something else?
  • How could AI still help?
