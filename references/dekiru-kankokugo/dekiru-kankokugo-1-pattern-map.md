# Dekiru Kankokugo 1 Pattern Map

Source reviewed in order: `page-wireframes-md/page_001.md` through `page-wireframes-md/page_137.md`.

This map lists the grammar/pattern blocks and closely related usage items by lesson. Page numbers below refer to the source markdown/PDF page number, e.g. `page_013.md` = PDF Page 13.

## Pre-Lesson: Classroom and Greetings

Pages: `page_001.md`-`page_006.md`

No formal `文型` grammar block appears here. These pages introduce classroom/greeting formulae:

1. Classroom commands and responses: `보세요`, `쓰세요`, `들으세요`, `읽으세요`, `따라 하세요`, `네`, `아뇨`, `알겠어요`, `모르겠어요`, `- 주세요`, `질문 있어요?`, `있어요/없어요`, `숙제입니다`, `수고하셨습니다` (`page_003.md`)
2. Greetings: `안녕하세요?`, `안녕하십니까?`, `오래간만입니다`, `오랜만이에요`, `잘 지내셨어요?`, `잘 지냈어요` (`page_004.md`)
3. Apology/thanks/mealtime/leaving formulae: `죄송합니다`, `미안합니다`, `괜찮아요`, `천만에요`, `감사합니다`, `고맙습니다`, `잘 먹겠습니다`, `잘 먹었습니다`, `다녀오겠습니다`, `다녀오세요`, `안녕히 가세요`, `안녕히 계세요` (`page_005.md`-`page_006.md`)

## Lesson 1: 저는 일본 사람입니다.

Pages: `page_007.md`-`page_010.md`

Goal: basic self-introduction and first-meeting language.

1. `-입니다（です）／-입니까?（ですか）` - noun copula and yes/no question form (`page_009.md`)
2. `-은/는（助詞「は」）` - topic particle; `-은` after a final consonant, `-는` after a vowel (`page_009.md`)
3. Practice frame: `저는 [N]입니다`, `[N]입니까?`, `앞으로 잘 부탁합니다` (`page_007.md`-`page_010.md`)

## Lesson 2: 일본 사람이 아닙니다.

Pages: `page_011.md`-`page_016.md`

Goal: saying what someone is not; asking/answering identity questions.

1. `-이/가 아닙니다（〜ではありません）` / `-이/가 아닙니까?（〜ではありませんか）` - formal noun negation (`page_013.md`)
2. `-이/가（助詞「が」）` - subject particle; `-이` after a final consonant, `-가` after a vowel (`page_013.md`)
3. Practice frame: `저는 한국 사람이 아닙니다`, `어느 나라 사람입니까?` (`page_014.md`-`page_015.md`)

## Lesson 3: 그건 뭐예요?

Pages: `page_017.md`-`page_022.md`

Goal: asking what something is in everyday polite style.

1. `-예요/이에요（〜です）` - informal polite noun copula; also used as a question by rising intonation (`page_019.md`)
2. `-이/가 아니에요（〜ではありません）` - informal polite noun negation (`page_019.md`)
3. Supporting usage: `이것/그것/저것`, contracted `이건/그건/저건`, `이게`, `뭐예요?`, and `-도` in object-identification practice (`page_020.md`-`page_021.md`)

## Lesson 4: 약속이 있습니다.

Pages: `page_023.md`-`page_028.md`

Goal: saying that something exists/does not exist; appointments with people.

1. `있습니다 / 있어요（あります・います）` - existence/possession, formal and informal polite forms (`page_025.md`)
2. `없습니다 / 없어요（ありません・いません）` - non-existence/non-possession, formal and informal polite forms (`page_025.md`)
3. Supporting usage: `-하고（〜と）`, `-에서（〜で）`, and appointment frames like `어디에서 누구하고 약속이 있습니까?` (`page_026.md`-`page_027.md`)

## Lesson 5: 회사는 어디에 있어요?

Pages: `page_029.md`-`page_034.md`

Goal: describing location.

1. `-에 있어요（〜にあります・います）` - location/existence pattern (`page_031.md`)
2. Position nouns: `위`, `밑/아래`, `옆`, `앞`, `뒤`, `안`, `밖`, `오른쪽`, `왼쪽`, `맞은편` (`page_031.md`)
3. Negative-location answers: `아무것도 없어요`, `아무도 없어요` (`page_031.md`)
4. Review page covers early particles: `-에서`, `-에`, `-하고`, `-도`, `-(이)라고`, `-이/가`, `-은/는` (`page_034.md`)

## Lesson 6: 주말에는 뭐 합니까?

Pages: `page_035.md`-`page_040.md`

Goal: formal polite verb/adjective sentences and connecting actions.

1. `-ㅂ/습니다（〜ます・です）` / `-ㅂ/습니까?（〜ますか・ですか）` - formal polite ending for verbs/adjectives; includes `ㄹ` drop (`page_037.md`)
2. `-고（並列：〜して、〜くて）` - connects parallel actions/states (`page_037.md`)
3. Supporting usage: `-을/를（〜を）`, time/location `-에`, `-에는`, and activity verbs (`page_036.md`-`page_040.md`)

## Lesson 7: 그렇게 멀지 않습니다.

Pages: `page_041.md`-`page_046.md`

Goal: negating verbs/adjectives; saying from-to ranges.

1. `안 - / -지 않습니다（否定文）` - short and long negation for verbs/adjectives (`page_043.md`)
2. `(時間) -부터 -까지（〜から〜まで）` - time range (`page_043.md`)
3. `(場所) -에서 -까지（〜から〜まで）` - place range (`page_043.md`)
4. Supporting usage: `-와/과（〜と）`, `-도（〜も）`, negative adverbs `별로`, `전혀` (`page_042.md`-`page_045.md`)

## Lesson 8: 언제 갑니까?

Pages: `page_047.md`-`page_052.md`

Goal: dates, numbers, prices, phone numbers, and exclamatory comments.

1. `漢字語数詞` - Sino-Korean numbers for dates, prices, numbers, floors, minutes (`page_049.md`-`page_050.md`)
2. Date patterns: `몇 월이에요?`, `며칠이에요?`, `-월`, `-일` (`page_049.md`)
3. Price/number patterns: `얼마예요?`, `-원`, `몇 번이에요?`, `몇 층이에요?`, `몇 분이에요?` (`page_049.md`-`page_050.md`)
4. `-네요（〜ですね・ますね）` - exclamation/agreement ending; includes `ㄹ` drop (`page_050.md`)

## Lesson 9: 부산까지 어떻게 가요?

Pages: `page_053.md`-`page_060.md`

Goal: everyday polite verb/adjective endings and means/comparison.

1. `-아/어요（〜ます・です）` - informal polite verb/adjective ending (`page_055.md`)
2. `-아/어요` conjugation cases: vowel harmony, contraction of `ㅏ/ㅓ`, compound vowels `ㅘ/ㅝ/ㅕ`, `되다 -> 돼요`, `내다 -> 내요`, `하다 -> 해요`, noun `-예요/-이에요` (`page_055.md`-`page_056.md`)
3. Supporting usage from vocabulary/practice: `-(으)로（手段：〜で）`, `-보다（〜より）`, `-의（〜の）`, `어떻게 가요?` (`page_054.md`-`page_059.md`)

## Lesson 10: 몇 시부터예요?

Pages: `page_061.md`-`page_066.md`

Goal: native Korean numbers, counters, and time schedules.

1. `固有語数詞` - native Korean numbers 1-99 (`page_063.md`)
2. Counter changes: `하나 -> 한`, `둘 -> 두`, `셋 -> 세`, `넷 -> 네`, `스물 -> 스무` (`page_063.md`)
3. Time/counter patterns: `몇 시예요?`, `-시`, `-번`, `-살`, `-명/사람`, honorific `-분`, `-개`, `-대`, `-장`, `-마리`, `-잔`, `-병`, `-권` (`page_063.md`-`page_064.md`)
4. Supporting schedule usage: `몇 시부터예요?`, `몇 시까지 해요?`, `일주일에 몇 번` (`page_061.md`-`page_066.md`)

## Lesson 11: 언제 일본에 왔어요?

Pages: `page_069.md`-`page_076.md`

Goal: past tense and talking about past experiences.

1. `過去形 -았/었습니다・았/었어요` - formal and informal polite past forms (`page_071.md`)
2. Past-tense conjugation cases: vowel harmony, contraction, compound vowels, `되다 -> 되었습니다/됐습니다`, `내다 -> 내었습니다/냈습니다`, `하다 -> 했습니다/했어요` (`page_071.md`-`page_072.md`)
3. Noun past: `-였습니다` after vowel-ending nouns, `-이었습니다` after consonant-ending nouns; informal `-였어요/-이었어요`; noun negation `아니었습니다/아니었어요` (`page_072.md`)
4. Supporting usage: time nouns such as `그저께`, `어제`, `오늘`, `내일`, `모레`, `지난주`, `이번 달`, `작년`; no `-에` after `그저께/어제/오늘/내일/모레` (`page_072.md`)

## Lesson 12: 성함이 어떻게 되세요?

Pages: `page_077.md`-`page_084.md`

Goal: honorific speech for asking about another person.

1. `敬語：-(으)십니다・-(으)세요` - honorific formal/informal polite forms; includes `ㄹ` drop (`page_079.md`)
2. Honorific special verbs: `먹다/마시다 -> 드시다`, `있다 -> 계시다`, `자다 -> 주무시다`, `죽다 -> 돌아가시다` (`page_079.md`)
3. Honorific noun sentences: `-(이)십니다`, `-(이)세요` (`page_079.md`)
4. Honorific particles: `-은/는 -> -께서는`, `-이/가 -> -께서`, `-에게 -> -께` (`page_080.md`)
5. Supplement: command/greeting `-(으)세요・-(으)십시오` (`page_084.md`)
6. Supporting usage: `성함/나이/가족/주소/전화번호이/가 어떻게 되세요?`, `-에게` (`page_078.md`-`page_081.md`)

## Lesson 13: 어디에 다녀오셨어요?

Pages: `page_085.md`-`page_090.md`

Goal: honorific past tense and travel experience.

1. `敬語の過去形：-(으)셨습니다・-(으)셨어요` - honorific past formal/informal polite forms (`page_087.md`)
2. Honorific past question forms: `-(으)셨습니까?`, `-(으)셨어요?`; includes `ㄹ` drop (`page_087.md`)
3. Honorific past special verbs: `드셨어요`, `계셨어요`, `주무셨어요`, `돌아가셨어요` (`page_087.md`)
4. Honorific past noun sentences: `-(이)셨어요?` (`page_087.md`)
5. Supporting usage: `-에 대하여（〜について）`, `-동안（〜間）`, `-한테/-한테서（人に/人から）`, direction `-(으)로（へ）` (`page_086.md`-`page_090.md`)

## Lesson 14: 한국과 일본은 비슷하지만 많이 다르지요?

Pages: `page_091.md`-`page_096.md`

Goal: confirming information and contrasting two facts.

1. `-지요?（確認：〜でしょう？、〜よね？）` / contracted `-죠?` - confirmation question; nouns use `-(이)지요?` (`page_093.md`)
2. `-지만（逆接：〜が、けれども）` - contrastive connector; nouns use `-(이)지만` (`page_093.md`)
3. Practice frame: `한국은 ...지만 일본은 ...` for comparing Korea/Japan (`page_094.md`-`page_096.md`)

## Lesson 15: 온천에 가고 싶어요.

Pages: `page_097.md`-`page_102.md`

Goal: expressing personal and third-person wants.

1. `-고 싶어요（希望：〜したいです）` - first/second-person desire; formal `-고 싶습니다`, question `-고 싶습니까?/-고 싶어요?` (`page_099.md`)
2. `-고 싶지 않아요（〜したくないです）` - negative desire (`page_099.md`)
3. `-고 싶어 해요（第3者の希望：〜したがっています）` - third-person desire (`page_099.md`)
4. `-고 싶어 하지 않아요（〜したがっていません）` - negative third-person desire (`page_099.md`)

## Lesson 16: 선물을 사서 돈을 많이 써요.

Pages: `page_105.md`-`page_110.md`

Goal: giving reasons/causes.

1. `-아/어서（理由・原因：〜ので）` - connects reason/cause to result (`page_107.md`)
2. Past restriction: do not attach past `-았/었` before `-아/어서` (`page_107.md`)
3. Noun reason forms: `-여서` after vowel-ending nouns, `-이어서` after consonant-ending nouns; reference forms `-라서/-이라서` (`page_107.md`)
4. `「으」불규칙활용（으不規則活用）` - `ㅡ` drops before `-아/어`; examples `바쁘다 -> 바빠요/바빠서`, `예쁘다 -> 예뻐요/예뻐서`, `쓰다 -> 써요/써서` (`page_107.md`-`page_108.md`)

## Lesson 17: 결혼식에 뭘 입고 가면 돼요?

Pages: `page_111.md`-`page_116.md`

Goal: asking permission and asking what one should do.

1. `-아/어도 돼요?（許可求め：〜してもいいですか）` - permission request (`page_113.md`)
2. Permission responses: `네, -아/어도 돼요`, `네, -(으)세요`, `안 돼요`, `-(으)면 안 돼요（〜してはいけません）` (`page_113.md`)
3. `-(으)면 돼요?（〜すればいいですか）` / `-(으)면 돼요（〜すればいいです）` (`page_113.md`)
4. `-(으)면（仮定・条件：〜ば、〜たら）` (`page_114.md`)
5. Supporting usage: `-만（〜だけ）`, compound action `입고 가다（着て行く）` (`page_112.md`)

## Lesson 18: 식사라도 같이 할까요?

Pages: `page_117.md`-`page_122.md`

Goal: making suggestions and stating purpose of movement.

1. `-(으)ㄹ까요?（勧誘：〜ましょうか）` - suggestion/invitation/asking intention; includes `ㄹ` drop (`page_119.md`)
2. `-(으)ㅂ시다（勧誘：〜ましょう）` - joint-action suggestion; not for superiors (`page_119.md`)
3. `-(으)러（目的：〜しに）` - purpose before motion verbs; `ㄹ` stems take `-러` (`page_119.md`)
4. Supporting usage: `-(이)라도（〜でも）`, `어때요?`, meeting-time/place invitation frames (`page_118.md`-`page_122.md`)

## Lesson 19: 사진 좀 찍어 주시겠어요?

Pages: `page_123.md`-`page_128.md`

Goal: polite requests.

1. `-아/어 주시겠어요?（依頼：〜していただけますか）` / `-아/어 주시겠습니까?` - polite request (`page_125.md`)
2. `-아/어 주세요（依頼：〜してください）` / formal `-아/어 주십시오` (`page_125.md`)
3. `-ㅂ/습니다만（〜ですが・ますが）` - often used as a request preface, e.g. `실례합니다만`, `미안합니다만` (`page_125.md`)
4. Supplement: difference between `-(으)세요` as polite instruction/command and `-아/어 주세요` as request (`page_128.md`)

## Lesson 20: 자전거를 탈 수 있어요?

Pages: `page_129.md`-`page_134.md`

Goal: ability/possibility, inability, and current progress.

1. `-(으)ㄹ 수 있어요（能力・可能：〜することができます）` - ability/possibility; written with spaces around `수` (`page_131.md`)
2. `못- / -(으)ㄹ 수 없어요（不可能：〜することができません）` - inability/impossibility (`page_131.md`)
3. `-고 있어요（現在進行：〜ています）` - ongoing action/current state (`page_131.md`)
4. Supporting usage: `잘`, `잘 못`, `못` in ability tables (`page_133.md`)
