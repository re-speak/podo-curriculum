# Contextual English semantic audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-13; naturalness and ownership follow-up applied 2026-08-14
**Scope:** all 60 Travel and Business lesson specifications
**Source of truth:** [`../tracks/2-contextual-english/table-of-contents.md`](../tracks/2-contextual-english/table-of-contents.md)

This audit asks whether a proficient speaker would plausibly say each line, whether the two learner
moves complete the task named by the title, whether the task earns a place in a 60-lesson applied
curriculum, and whether every Core reference genuinely supports the form. It is not evidence that a
line is the only natural choice or that every learner needs every specialised lesson.

## Pass standard

A row passes only when all five tests pass:

1. **Spoken naturalness:** the line works aloud in the stated relationship and register.
2. **Task unity:** title, can-do, both learner lines, and both replies serve one external result.
3. **Transfer value:** the frames can survive changed details rather than teaching one memorised script.
4. **Learner value:** the task is common in the domain or consequential for a clearly identified group.
5. **Honest ownership:** a Core number names a lesson that actually supports the form. A useful form
   without an exact owner is marked as a bounded `chunk`; similarity of difficulty is not ownership.

The target register is internationally intelligible spoken English with internally consistent
British-compatible spelling (`travelling`, `prioritise`, `recognise`, `itemised`, `lift`). Regionally
specific alternatives may be taught receptively, but model lines should not drift between dialects.

## Importance scale

- **Broad:** high-frequency or high-consequence for most learners choosing that domain.
- **Role/need:** important when the learner travels in that way or holds the relevant workplace role.
- **Extension:** a worthwhile advanced capstone; not a prerequisite for routine success.

`Role/need` does not mean filler. Accessibility advocacy, insurance reports, performance feedback,
and crisis communication may be infrequent across the whole market but critical for the learner who
selects that course. They belong in optional higher courses, not in the low-intermediate entry course.

## Findings and changes

The two-domain scope survived the audit. No lesson was retained merely to fill a six-row course.
The apparently social Travel rows all have a travel-specific result: joining an activity, making a
plan with fellow travellers, resolving an itinerary disagreement, or repairing a host interaction.
General friendship and everyday-life topics remain outside this track.

The audit did require substantive corrections:

- repaired spoken lines that were grammatical but not what a speaker would most naturally say;
- simplified CTX-45 so a B1–B2 recommendation does not depend on two C1-style chunks;
- removed ambiguity from action summaries and remedy requests;
- fixed partner replies that sounded translated or elliptical;
- replaced false Core references with genuine owners or explicit bounded chunks;
- fixed multi-owner parsing so `Core 36, Core 56` no longer silently loses the second owner.

Future edits must rerun this audit logic. A sentence is not approved merely because it is
grammatical, and an important situation does not rescue two unrelated target patterns.

### 2026-08-14 follow-up

The learner-view follow-up tightened CTX-9, 10, 11, 13, 17, 18, 21, 23, 25, 28, 36, 38 and 52.
The changes removed false Core ownership, bounded fixed service language as chunks, repaired
translated-sounding wording, and constrained slots so that changing them cannot silently break the
scene. The 60-lesson order and all stable IDs remain unchanged.

The final deep pass later that day tightened the whole-line prerequisite rule. It simplified early
Travel and Business patterns where later grammar added no practical value, raised the Meetings and
Negotiation course floors to Core 86 and Core 101, removed hidden third speech acts, and replaced
the propagated `On balance` conclusion frame. The shared parser now rejects any future-Core
reference above a course floor unless that learner line is explicitly marked as a bounded chunk.
See [`final-curriculum-deep-audit.md`](./final-curriculum-deep-audit.md).
