# Final English curriculum deep audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-14
**Source of truth:** the four `english/tracks/*/table-of-contents.md` files
**Production-pattern scope:** 122 Core lessons, 60 Contextual lessons and the borrowed moves in 121
Freetalking topics

**Interactive change proof:** [`final-deep-pass-review.html`](../final-deep-pass-review.html)

This pass asks three questions in lesson order:

1. Does every learner-produced form depend only on language already available at that point, or on
   an explicitly bounded chunk?
2. Do the two primary patterns cooperate to complete one can-do without hiding a third production
   objective inside either line?
3. Is each target a plausible spoken default with a useful, bounded slot, rather than merely a
   grammatical or formal written expression?

Pronunciation remains a planning-only contrast syllabus, not a two-pattern grammar track. Its 12
rows were checked for broad order, but this audit does not authorize decks or claim that their
eventual page-level load has been piloted.

## Hard gates used

- **One outcome:** the can-do names one observable exchange or task.
- **Two targets:** Core and Contextual have exactly two assessed learner patterns. Supporting
  expressions, partner replies and spiral review do not become extra production objectives.
- **Pair unity:** the second target answers, repairs, qualifies or advances the first.
- **Whole-line readiness:** fixed words, slot grammar and inflection all count toward load.
- **Honest prerequisite:** a Contextual Core reference above its course floor is a parse error unless
  the whole pattern is marked `chunk`.
- **Slot quality:** the frame has stable boundaries and supports several natural completions.
- **Spoken default:** a learner should sound like a person completing the task, not like a written
  report, debate transcript or translated textbook example.

## Core disposition

All 122 lessons still have one can-do and exactly two primary patterns. Stable IDs and unit order are
unchanged.

The pass corrected these consequential issues:

- **Can-do alignment:** Core 1 and 11 no longer promise productive outcomes their two patterns do not
  teach.
- **Prerequisite order:** Core 43 no longer produces a comparative before comparison begins in Core
  45.
- **Cognitive load:** Core 76 now keeps both targets inside the service causative
  (`have/get + thing + participle`) instead of adding a second person-causative system.
- **Model/frame integrity:** Core 87 and 97 now instantiate every slot shown in their frames.
- **Spoken naturalness:** Core 98, 102, 115, 116 and 117 replace written or debate-style defaults
  with spoken anchors while preserving the same communicative functions.
- **Usable slot boundaries:** Core 116, 117 and 121 now give the learner a real changeable
  proposition, reason or topic rather than an adverb-only or awkwardly positioned blank.

Core 117 is now `Overall, I'd say ___` plus `What it comes down to for me is ___`. `On balance`,
`net-net` and the old deciding-factor line are no longer production defaults.

## Contextual disposition

All 60 lessons still have one scene, one can-do, exactly two learner lines and a realistic reply to
each line. Stable IDs and the ten-course architecture are unchanged.

- Early Travel lines were simplified to already-owned language where the practical task did not
  need later grammar: CTX 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12 and 16.
- CTX 9 no longer embeds a dietary statement and a recommendation question inside one primary
  target. The two objectives are now state the restriction and check the recommendation.
- CTX 19, 22, 23, 25, 26 and 30 now have honest ownership, narrower can-dos or lower-load frames.
- Early Business lines in CTX 31, 35, 36 and 37 now use genuine earlier owners instead of loose or
  future-form analogies.
- The Meetings course now begins at **Core 86** and the Negotiation course at **Core 101**, matching
  the language their lessons actually retrieve.
- CTX 45 now teaches one bounded recommendation plus a known comparative reason; CTX 58 gives its
  two stakeholder lines distinct jobs.
- CTX 29 and 60 inherit the spoken Core 117 conclusion language.

The parser now rejects every unbounded Core reference above a Contextual course floor. This turns a
review judgment into a durable sequence invariant.

## Freetalking disposition

Freetalking does not teach a third grammar syllabus; its moves retrieve Core or remain explicitly
bounded chunks. The pass updated every Core 115/117 propagation, repaired awkward moves in FT 54,
65, 74, 94, 103 and 105, and converted all 15 Balance Game shorthand notes into complete spoken
opening lines. Those final openings are now regression-checked for quoted, sentence-complete copy.

## What this pass does not prove

This is an editorial and structural audit, not frequency evidence or a learner-performance study.
The live catalog still needs the owner's native dispositions, and the B1+–C1 rows still need the
planned corpus/evidence work and learner pilots. A valid parse proves count, structure and declared
prerequisites; it does not by itself prove acquisition or delayed retrieval.
