WHAT IF

Where you imagine yourself in ten years (十年後に想像する自分)

Let's imagine an ordinary day ten years from now. Please read the title aloud.十年後の普通の一日を想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

A direction, not a prediction (予測ではなく方向)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A ten-year picture is a direction, not a promise.十年後の姿は方向であり、約束ではありません。
A life direction can exist without a detailed plan.人生の方向性は、詳しい計画がなくても持てます。life direction人生の方向性
A future picture can begin with a place or with the feeling of the day.将来のイメージは、場所から始まることも、一日の気持ちから始まることもあります。
Uncertainty can show which values matter before the details are clear.細かいことが決まっていなくても、不確かさから大切な価値観が見えることがあります。
Work is only one part of a future day.仕事は未来の一日の一部にすぎません。
The amount of company and solitude changes the shape of a future day.人と過ごす時間と一人の時間の量で、将来の一日の形は変わります。
Keeping something the same can be as important as changing it.何かを変えないことも、変えることと同じくらい大切です。
Disappointment depends on values, not a perfect checklist.失望するかどうかは、完璧なリストより価値観で決まります。
A small experiment this year can test a possible future direction.今年、小さく試してみると、将来の方向を確かめられます。
The picture should be allowed to change as life changes.生活が変われば、未来の姿も変えてよいのです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Ten years on (十年後)

WARM-UP 1What would an ideal ordinary day look like ten years from now?十年後の理想的な普通の一日は、どんな一日ですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of the day would matter most?
  • What would make the day feel ordinary rather than exceptional?

What you are doing (していること)

WARM-UP 2Which activity or purpose could organize an ordinary future day?将来の普通の一日を形作る活動や目的は何ですか？

Follow up
  • What would you want the day to contain?
  • Would it be paid work, study, care, rest, or something else?

Who is around (周りにいる人)

QUESTION 1What balance of company and solitude would make a future day comfortable?将来の一日を快適にする、人との時間と一人の時間のバランスはどのようなものですか？

Follow up
  • What kind of connection matters?
  • What makes the scene comfortable or useful?

What stays the same (変わらないこと)

QUESTION 2Is there anything you would like to keep the same for ten years?十年間、変えずに保ちたいことはありますか？

Follow up
  • Why is it worth protecting?
  • Which uncertainty makes choosing difficult?

What would disappoint you (失望すること)

QUESTION 3What missing part would disappoint you, if any?欠けていたら失望するものがあるとすれば、何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which value is behind it?
  • What makes the picture flexible?

Life direction (人生の方向性)

QUESTION 4What can reveal a broader life direction even when details are unclear?細かいことが不明でも、人生の大きな方向を示すものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which value points that way?
  • What remains uncertain?

This year (今年できること)

QUESTION 5What small action this year could make a future direction clearer?今年どんな小さな行動をすると、将来の方向が明確になりますか？

Follow up
  • What makes it small enough to begin?
  • What would need to become clearer first?

Changing the picture (未来像を変えること)

QUESTION 6What new opportunity could make someone happily change a ten-year plan?どんな新しい機会があれば、十年計画を喜んで変えられますか？

Follow up
  • Which new opportunity could matter more?
  • Which experience or information could add clarity?

Today's feedback (今日のフィードバック)

Before we finish, here's one strength and one thing to focus on next.最後に、よかった点を一つと、次に意識する点を一つ伝えます。

Before closing, ask the learner to repeat one useful correction.
