WHAT CAME BEFORE

One day you would go back to (戻ってみたい一日)

Let's imagine one meaningful visit to the past. Please read the title aloud.意味のある過去への一日旅行を想像しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

What we seek in one past day (過去の一日に求めるもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
The wish to revisit a day may seek understanding or presence rather than revision.一日に戻りたい気持ちは、修正より理解やその場にいることを求める場合があります。
A second view could recover details that stress, youth, or distraction originally hid.もう一度見れば、緊張、若さ、気の散りで見えなかった細部を取り戻せるかもしれません。
A joyful day may be worth reliving simply because attention was divided the first time.最初は注意が散っていたため、幸せな一日をもう一度味わいたいことがあります。
A painful day may attract attention because one question or conversation remained unfinished.苦しい一日が気になるのは、疑問や会話が一つ終わらないままだからかもしれません。
Intervening in one event could alter consequences the visitor values now.一つの出来事に介入すると、今大切にしている結果まで変わる可能性があります。
Choosing only to observe does not remove the emotional cost of returning.観察するだけでも、戻ることの感情的な負担はなくなりません。
A bounded thought experiment becomes clearer when it fixes one day, one purpose, and no permanent stay.一日、一つの目的、残らないという条件を決めると、想像が明確になります。thought experiment思考実験
A historical or familiar public day can offer distance from private experience.歴史上または公に知られた一日なら、個人的な経験から距離を取れます。
Refusing the trip can express acceptance rather than lack of imagination.戻らない選択は、想像力の不足ではなく受け入れを表すことがあります。
The selected day often reveals which relationship, choice, or feeling still invites attention.選ばれた一日は、どんな関係、選択、感情が今も注意を求めているかを表します。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One day only (一日だけ)

WARM-UP 1If you could witness one day in the past, which day would be worth seeing?過去の一日を目撃できるなら、どの日を見る価値がありますか？

Follow up
  • Would you choose a personal day or a famous event?
  • Where would you arrive?

A day worth watching again (もう一度見たい一日)

WARM-UP 2Would an ordinary day or a famous day reveal more about the past?普通の一日と有名な日のどちらが、過去についてより多くを教えてくれますか？

Follow up
  • Would an ordinary family day or a famous event be more interesting?
  • What would you watch first?

Why return (戻る目的)

QUESTION 1What mystery could one day in the past help solve?過去の一日を見ることで、どんな謎を解けそうですか？

Follow up
  • Would you want to understand a person or an event?
  • What might a visitor still get wrong?

Change or observe (変えるか見るか)

QUESTION 2If a visit to the past allowed one action, would you observe or step in?過去への訪問で一つ行動できるなら、見るだけにしますか、それとも関わりますか？

Follow up
  • What could change if the visitor stepped in?
  • What could they learn by only watching?

One ordinary day (普通の一日)

QUESTION 3Who from the past would you most like to spend one ordinary day with?過去の誰と、普通の一日を一緒に過ごしたいですか？

Follow up
  • What would you want to notice?
  • Would you choose a relative or a public figure?

A question for the past (過去の人への質問)

QUESTION 4What would you ask someone from the past?過去の人に何を聞きたいですか？

Follow up
  • Would you ask about daily life or an important event?
  • What answer might surprise you?

Would it help? (役に立つか)

QUESTION 5What part of the present might feel different after seeing the past directly?過去を直接見た後、現在のどんな部分が違って感じられるでしょうか？

Follow up
  • Would the present seem easier or harder?
  • Could seeing an ordinary past day change your view?

A day best left alone (見ないでおきたい一日)

QUESTION 6What is one day in the past you would rather leave alone?過去の一日で、あえて見ないままにしておきたい日はありますか？

Follow up
  • Would it be personal or historical?
  • Why might not seeing it be better?
