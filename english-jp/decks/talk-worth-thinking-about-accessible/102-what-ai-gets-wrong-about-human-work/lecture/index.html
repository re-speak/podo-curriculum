A convenience you could live without (なくても暮らせる便利なもの)

Let's discuss which conveniences help, what they cost, and when a slower choice is better. Please read the title aloud.便利なサービスのよさや代償、遅い選択のほうがよいときについて話しましょう。タイトルを声に出して読んでください。

Have the learner read the title once, then move on.

Convenient, but at what cost? (便利さと引き換えにするもの)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article.
Ken orders dinner on his phone, and it arrives at his door twenty minutes later.ケンがスマートフォンで夕食を注文すると、二十分後に玄関まで届きます。
The service saves him time, but the meal comes with several plastic containers and bags.時間は節約できますが、食事にはいくつものプラスチック容器や袋が付いてきます。
Convenience usually solves one problem while creating a cost somewhere else.便利さは一つの問題を解決する一方で、別のところに負担を生むことがよくあります。
The cost may be money, waste, personal data, pressure on workers, or a skill people use less.その負担は、お金、ごみ、個人情報、働く人への圧力、あるいは使わなくなるスキルかもしれません。
Maps make travel easier, but some people become less confident about finding their own way.地図アプリで移動は簡単になりますが、自分で道を見つける自信がなくなる人もいます。
Same-day delivery helps in an emergency, yet it can make every purchase feel urgent.当日配送は緊急時に役立ちますが、すべての買い物を急ぎのように感じさせることもあります。
Streaming offers endless choice, although choosing can take longer than watching.動画配信には無数の選択肢がありますが、見る時間より選ぶ時間のほうが長くなることもあります。
Faster service can also create deadlines that are hard for workers to meet safely.より速いサービスは、働く人が安全に守るのが難しい締め切りを生むこともあります。
People may accept these costs when the service saves important time or helps someone who finds movement difficult.大切な時間を節約したり、移動が難しい人を助けたりするなら、こうした負担を受け入れる人もいます。
At other times, the slower choice offers better quality, less waste, or a more enjoyable experience.別の場面では、遅い選択のほうが質が高く、ごみが少なく、より楽しい経験になることがあります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of the conversation.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。会話の中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Use these pages as a flexible pool. Ask one question, react naturally, and share a brief real answer or relevant perspective of your own when it adds to the exchange. Skip freely and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

One week without it (一週間なくてもよいもの)

WARM-UP 1Which could you give up for a week: delivery, streaming, or same-day shipping?配達、動画配信、当日配送なら、一週間どれをやめられますか？

Follow up
  • Which would you miss first?
  • What would you do instead?

The biggest time-saver (最も時間を節約するもの)

WARM-UP 2Which everyday convenience saves you the most time?日常の便利なサービスで、最も時間を節約してくれるものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • How much time does it save?
  • What did people do before it?

Less enjoyable (楽しさを減らす便利さ)

QUESTION 1Which convenience sometimes makes things less enjoyable?ときどき物事を楽しくなくする便利なものは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Could faster travel make you miss something?
  • Which slow activity do you enjoy?

A fading skill (使わなくなるスキル)

QUESTION 2What can people forget how to do because an app or service does it for them?アプリやサービスが代わりにすることで、人がやり方を忘れてしまうのは何ですか？

Follow up
  • Does that skill still matter?
  • When might people need it again?

Unnecessary waste (不要なごみ)

QUESTION 3Which convenience creates the most unnecessary waste?どの便利なサービスが、最も不要なごみを生んでいますか？

Follow up
  • What gets thrown away?
  • What less convenient option creates less waste?

Pressure on workers (働く人への負担)

QUESTION 4When can faster service put too much pressure on workers?速いサービスが働く人に負担をかけすぎるのは、どんなときですか？

Follow up
  • What deadline seems unreasonable?
  • Would you accept slower service?

Too much personal data (多すぎる個人情報)

QUESTION 5Which convenience would you avoid if it needed too much personal data?個人情報を多く求められるなら、使わない便利なサービスは何ですか？

Follow up
  • What information would you refuse to share?
  • What benefit might still tempt you?

The slower option (遅いほうの選択)

QUESTION 6When would you choose the slower option even if both cost the same?値段が同じでも、遅いほうを選ぶのはどんなときですか？

Follow up
  • Would quality or enjoyment matter more?
  • Which choice would you make in a hurry?
