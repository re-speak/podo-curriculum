Official time and real time (決まった時刻と実際の時刻)

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
A stated schedule may not match when work, school, care, or another daily responsibility actually ends.決められた予定と、仕事、学校、ケアなどが実際に終わる時刻は一致しない場合があります。
Occupation, institution, household role, transport, workload, and season can all affect departure time.職種、組織、家庭での役割、交通、仕事量、季節によって、帰る時刻は変わります。
Carers, shift workers, freelancers, retired people, and others may have no single official finishing time.ケアをする人、交代勤務、フリーランス、退職した人などには、決まった終了時刻がない場合があります。
The meaning of the choice to leave on time depends on staffing, workload, authority, and local expectations.定時に帰ることの意味は、人員、仕事量、権限、地域の期待によって異なります。
Longer presence can reflect demand or pressure without guaranteeing productivity or quality.長くいることは需要や圧力を示しても、生産性や質を保証しません。
A person may leave earlier because of flexible hours, a completed task, care duties, or a different starting time.柔軟な時間、作業の完了、ケアの責任、開始時刻の違いによって、早く帰る場合があります。
A careful comparison avoids turning one workplace or school into a national stereotype.丁寧な比較では、一つの職場や学校を国全体の固定観念にしません。
Transport and unpaid preparation can extend the practical day beyond the official schedule.交通や無給の準備によって、実際の一日は公式の予定より長くなります。unpaid無給の
Reliable finishing times support care, rest, relationships, transport, and other commitments.終業時刻が安定すると、ケア、休息、人間関係、交通、ほかの予定を支えられます。
Occasional extra time differs from a culture in which hidden overtime is expected every day.時々の延長と、見えない残業を毎日期待する文化は異なります。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

When the day really ends (実際に終わる時間)

WARM-UP 1When do people actually finish work or school where you live?住んでいる場所では、人は実際に何時に仕事や学校を終えますか？

Follow up
  • Is there a typical time or substantial variation?
  • Which role, institution, or routine does the example represent?

Expected or unusual (普通か例外か)

WARM-UP 2How closely do official finishing times match actual practice where you live?住んでいる場所では、公式の終了時刻と実際の運用はどの程度一致しますか？

Follow up
  • How does any stated time compare with practice?
  • Which event or condition marks the end?

Leaving before others (先に帰ること)

QUESTION 1What response, if any, follows when someone finishes or leaves before others in that setting?その場で誰かがほかの人より先に終えたり帰ったりすると、どんな反応がありますか？

Follow up
  • Is the response supportive, neutral, mixed, or critical?
  • How does authority, role, or reason affect the response?

A local expression (地域の呼び方)

QUESTION 2How does local language describe someone who consistently works or studies late?いつも遅くまで働いたり勉強したりする人を、地域の言葉ではどう表しますか？

Follow up
  • Does it express approval, criticism, humor, or neutrality?
  • How is the behavior described?

My reaction and example (先生の反応と例)

QUESTION 3I'll share one concrete finishing-time pattern from my experience. Which detail deserves comparison?私の経験から、具体的な終了時刻のパターンを一つ話します。どの詳細を比較すべきですか？

Follow up
  • Which part of my example would you like me to clarify?
  • Which detail offers the strongest comparison with another setting?

Compare the two examples (二つの例を比べる)

QUESTION 4How can actual finishing times differ across two settings?二つの環境で、実際の終了時刻はどう異なり得ますか？

Follow up
  • Which similarity is most meaningful?
  • Which difference, if any, needs context rather than a national explanation?

A fairer ending (より公平な終わり方)

QUESTION 5Which practical change could make the end of the day more predictable or equitable?一日の終わりをより予測可能で公平にするために、どんな実際的な変更ができますか？

Follow up
  • Whose authority or cooperation would implementation require?
  • Which occasional exception would remain legitimate?

A good end to the day (よい一日の終わり)

QUESTION 6Beyond clock time, what makes the transition out of work, school, or another responsibility effective?時刻以外に、仕事、学校、その他の責任からうまく切り替えるには何が必要ですか？

Follow up
  • Does it require a handover, closure, planning, or a boundary?
  • What keeps unfinished demands from dominating the next part of the day?
