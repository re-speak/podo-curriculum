Where the solo line comes from (一人で行動する境界)

Did you have any questions about the article?記事について何か質問はありましたか？

Answer the learner's questions, then move on. If there are none—or the learner did not complete the pre-study—skip the article; do not spend class time reading it.
Solitude feels radically different depending on the activity.一人でいる感覚は、活動の種類によって大きく変わります。
Someone may dine alone comfortably yet find solo travel intimidating.一人での食事は平気でも、一人旅には不安を感じる人がいます。
The boundary often reflects safety, cost, unfamiliarity, and social expectations.その境界には、安全、費用、慣れのなさ、周囲の期待が関係します。
It also depends on whether companionship is central to the activity's meaning.誰かと一緒にいることが、その活動の意味の中心かどうかも関係します。
Acting alone offers autonomy over pace, timing, and decisions.一人で行動すると、速さ、時間、判断を自分で決められます。autonomy自分で決める自由
It can also concentrate every practical problem and decision on one person.一方で、実際の問題や判断がすべて一人に集中します。
One manageable success can expand a person's comfort zone.無理のない一度の成功で、安心してできる範囲が広がることがあります。
A frightening or exhausting experience can contract that boundary again.怖い経験や疲れる経験で、その境界が再び狭くなることもあります。
Seeing someone similar act alone can make the same choice feel attainable.自分に似た人が一人で行動するのを見ると、同じ選択が現実的に感じられます。
The useful question is not whether independence is superior, but where it genuinely serves us.大切なのは自立が優れているかではなく、どこまでなら本当に自分の役に立つかです。

8 QUESTION POOL

Let's talk (話そう)

We don't need to answer every question. Let's follow the most interesting parts of your answers.全部の質問に答える必要はありません。答えの中でいちばん面白いところを広げていきましょう。

Treat these pages as a pool, not a sequence. React or share briefly before choosing the next prompt, skip freely, and move to feedback with 2–3 minutes left.

Where the line is (一人でできる境界)

WARM-UP 1Which activity would feel hardest for you to do alone?一人でするとき、どの活動が自分の安心できる範囲から最も遠く感じますか？

Follow up
  • What makes it different from ordinary solo activities?
  • Is the main barrier safety, confidence, or enjoyment?

The hardest part (いちばん難しい部分)

WARM-UP 2What's the hardest part of doing that activity alone?その活動を一人でするとしたら、いちばん難しいのはどの部分ですか？

Follow up
  • Is the problem safety, confidence, or enjoyment?
  • Which moment would feel hardest?

Just outside the line (境界の少し外)

QUESTION 1Which solo plan would you try first: a restaurant meal, a movie, or a day trip?最初に一人で試すなら、レストランでの食事、映画、日帰り旅行のどれですか？

Follow up
  • Which moment would feel hardest?
  • What could make it enjoyable?

The best part (いちばんよい点)

QUESTION 2What would be the best part of doing it alone?それを一人でするなら、いちばんよい点は何ですか？

Follow up
  • Which decision would become easier?
  • What would you miss sharing with someone?

Comfort alone (一人で平気な理由)

QUESTION 3What helps some people feel comfortable doing more things alone?一人でもより自信を持って行動できる人がいるのはなぜですか？

Follow up
  • What skill makes going alone easier?
  • How can one good experience build confidence?

A moving line (変わった境界)

QUESTION 4How can a person's comfort with doing things alone change over time?一人で行動することへの気楽さは、時間とともにどう変わりますか？

Follow up
  • What experience could make going alone easier?
  • What could make it feel harder again?

A smaller step (小さな一歩)

QUESTION 5What small activity can prepare someone to do more things alone?一人でできることを増やすために、どんな小さな活動が準備になりますか？

Follow up
  • How could they make it feel safer?
  • What would show they are ready for more?

Getting comfortable alone (一人でも平気になること)

QUESTION 6Would you actually like to get comfortable doing it alone?実際に、それを一人でも平気でできるようになりたいですか？

Follow up
  • What would be the first small step?
  • When would company still be better?
